Who can apply

Educational qualification

This differs by post, and the Fire post is the one people get wrong.

PostQualification
Sub-Inspector (GD) in CAPFsBachelor’s degree in any stream from a recognised university.
Sub-Inspector in Delhi PoliceBachelor’s degree in any stream. Male candidates must also hold a valid light motor vehicle driving licence.
Sub-Inspector (Fire) in CISFBachelor’s degree in Science with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, or a degree or diploma in Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Automobile, Chemical, Mining, Aeronautical or Telecommunication Engineering.

Age and physical standards

  • Age as on 1 August 2026: 20 to 25 years for all posts except Sub-Inspector (Fire) in CISF, which is 18 to 30 years. Relaxation applies to reserved categories as per the notification.
  • Physical standards: height and chest requirements apply and differ by category and region. The full table is in the notification — check it before you pay the fee, because PST is where most applications end.

Revised exam pattern

This is the part worth reading twice. SSC has reworked both papers for 2026, and a study plan built on the old pattern will send you in the wrong direction.

One mark per correct answer and 0.25 deducted per wrong answer. Sectional timing is new here too — each section closes after its 15 minutes.

Paper 2 — 160 questions, 320 marks

Paper 2 used to be an English paper. It is not any more. It now covers four subjects — General Intelligence and Reasoning, General Knowledge and General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude and English Comprehension — at two marks per question, with 0.50 deducted per wrong answer. English still carries the largest share. The section-wise split and timing are set out in the notification.

Paper 2 is now the paper that decides your rank. Under the old pattern candidates treated it as an English formality after clearing Paper 1. With 320 marks across four subjects, that approach will cost you a posting. Start Paper 2 preparation alongside Paper 1, not after it.

Selection process

  1. Paper 1 — computer based, qualifying you for the physical stage.
  2. PST and PET — physical standard and endurance tests, qualifying in nature. Ex-Servicemen are exempt from PET but still take PST.
  3. Paper 2 — computer based, and the main scoring stage.
  4. Detailed Medical Examination.
  5. Final merit list, based on Paper 2 performance and your post and force preference.

There is no interview at any stage.

How to apply

  1. Go to ssc.gov.in and complete One Time Registration if you have not already.
  2. Log in with your registration ID and password.
  3. Open the SSC CPO 2026 application link and fill in your details.
  4. Upload your photograph and signature to the stated specifications.
  5. Pay the fee if it applies to you and confirm the status inside your login.
  6. Preview, submit, and save a printout of the confirmation page.

Applications go through the SSC portal or the official mySSC app only. Do not leave it to the last day — the portal is slowest in the final 48 hours and a failed payment can leave your form marked incomplete.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to apply?

11:00 PM on 30 September 2026. The fee can be paid until 11:00 PM on 1 October, and the correction window runs 8 to 10 October 2026.

How many vacancies are there?

1,871 tentative — 205 Delhi Police SI (male), 112 Delhi Police SI (female), 1,320 SI (GD) in CAPFs and 234 SI (Fire) in CISF.

Has the exam pattern really changed?

Yes, substantially. Paper 1 is down to 100 questions for 100 marks with a 15-minute sectional timer. Paper 2 is up to 160 questions for 320 marks and now covers four subjects rather than English alone.

What is the age limit?

20 to 25 years as on 1 August 2026 for most posts, and 18 to 30 years for Sub-Inspector (Fire) in CISF. Category relaxations apply.

When is the exam?

Not announced. Paper 1, PST/PET and Paper 2 dates will be notified separately by the commission.

Is there an interview?

No. Selection runs through Paper 1, PST/PET, Paper 2 and a detailed medical examination.
