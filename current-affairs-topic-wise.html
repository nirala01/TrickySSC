How to use it

Read by section, revise by month

SSC General Awareness picks current affairs from a fixed set of areas. Reading section by section shows you every summit, award or exercise of the year side by side, which makes it easier to remember who hosted what and when.

  • First pass – read one section a day, newest month first.
  • Fact boxes – revise the orange boxes in the last week; they hold the names, dates and headquarters exams ask.
  • Tests – attempt the section's tests after reading and re-read the points you got wrong.

Frequently asked questions

What is topic-wise current affairs?
It is the same year of current affairs arranged by section instead of by date: International, National, Economic, Science and Technology, Sports, Awards, Conferences, Appointments, Military Exercises and Important Days, each with notes for every month from Jan 2026 to Oct 2026.
Which exams is this useful for?
SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, SSC MTS, SSC CPO and SSC GD, and Railway exams such as RRB NTPC and Group D, where General Awareness asks the same kind of one-line facts.
How many practice questions are there?
1504 questions in all: 10 to 15 per section for every month, plus 50 each on the Economic Survey and the Budget. Every question comes from the notes of the same section.
Are the notes free?
Yes. All notes on these pages are free to read without login.
How often is it updated?
A new month is added as it ends, and the oldest month drops off so the window stays at the most recent ten to twelve months, which is the period SSC papers usually cover.
