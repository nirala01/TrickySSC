October 2026Monthly notes

RBI turns to tightening

  • Repo hike – on 7 October the MPC lifted the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50%, all members agreeing.
  • Stance – changed to 'calibrated tightening'; SDF now 5.25%, MSF and Bank Rate 5.75%.
  • Reason – costlier crude (above $100 a barrel) and rising prices.
  • Rupee shield – banks must keep 20% of large forex derivative deals (above $2 million) as a Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve with the RBI.
  • CRR – daily CRR maintenance raised from 90% to 99% from 16 October; the CRR itself stays at 3%.
Monetary Policy Committee: six members – three from the RBI including the Governor, who chairs it, and three named by the Centre; created in 2016 under the amended RBI Act, 1934; it meets at least four times a year.

Rabi MSP for 2027-28

  • Decision – the CCEA raised MSPs of all six mandated rabi crops on 30 September.
  • Wheat – ₹2,610 per quintal, a rise of ₹25.
  • Biggest jump – safflower, up ₹675 to ₹7,215; rapeseed-mustard up ₹413 to ₹6,613.
  • Others – lentil ₹7,390, gram ₹5,958 and barley ₹2,286.
  • Margin – expected return over cost is highest for wheat (106%), then rapeseed-mustard (96%).
MSP: suggested by the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (set up 1965) and approved by the CCEA; fixed for 22 crops, with an FRP for sugarcane.

Big Cabinet approvals

  • Green Energy Corridor III – ₹1,86,405 crore plan to carry about 135 GW of renewable power and add 50 GWh of battery storage by 2032-33.
  • PM Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana – ₹7,395.55 crore for 2026-27 to 2030-31 to build up panchayats and rural governance.
  • PM Samajik Sahayata Yojana – new name of the National Social Assistance Programme; ₹53,428 crore over five years.
  • Containers – ₹10,000 crore Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme to make shipping containers at home.
  • Sports goods – Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme worth ₹1,856.67 crore, with four manufacturing zones.

Scheme milestones

  • Make in India – turned 12 on 25 September; launched in 2014 and run by DPIIT; its symbol is a lion made of gears.
  • PM Vishwakarma – three years old on 17 September; runs under the MSME Ministry until 2027-28.
  • PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana – first year done on 11 October; it targets 100 low-yield farm districts over six years.
  • PM-SETU – one year old; ₹60,000 crore to upgrade 1,000 government ITIs on a hub-and-spoke pattern.
  • PM GatiShakti – the national master plan, launched in 2021, completed five years on 13 October.
PM Vishwakarma: launched on 17 September 2023 (Vishwakarma Jayanti) for artisans in 18 traditional trades; loans without collateral up to ₹3 lakh in two parts at 5% interest, plus a ₹15,000 toolkit incentive.

Money and farm data

  • Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 2026 – in force from 1 October; it replaces the 1891 law and got Presidential assent on 13 August.
  • Apple Pay – started in India on 30 September with Axis Bank credit cards; UPI and RuPay are not yet supported.
  • Farmer IDs – 11 crore digital Farmer IDs under AgriStack were created by 23 October, five months before the March 2027 deadline.
  • DA – Dearness Allowance for central staff raised from 60% to 63% from 1 July 2026.

September 2026Monthly notes

DDU-GKY 2.0

  • Target – from October 2026, 70,000 rural youth will get skill training under the revamped scheme.
  • Origin – launched on 25 September 2014 under DAY-NRLM; run by the Ministry of Rural Development.
  • Who – rural poor aged 15–35 (45 for women, persons with disabilities and special groups); 50% seats for SC/ST, 33% for women, 5% for PwD.
  • New placement rule – a job now counts only after 6 months of work (3 months earlier), including wage, self and gig work.
  • Migration centres – yearly fund per Migration Support Centre raised from ₹10 lakh to ₹30 lakh; trainees tracked on the Kaushal Panjee portal.
DDU-GKY: Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana – a placement-linked skilling scheme; part of DAY-NRLM; training lasts 3 to 12 months and is free.

DILRMP 3.0

  • Launch – Shivraj Singh Chouhan released guidelines for the Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme 3.0.
  • Type and money – a Central Sector scheme with ₹565.50 crore over 2026–31.
  • Land Stack – one GIS platform joining maps, records of rights, registration and related court cases.
  • ULPIN – the 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar number for every land parcel.
  • Towns – urban land records under the NAKSHA pilot, with UrPro cards.

Jobs and social security

  • EPFO ceiling – the Cabinet (16 September) raised the wage ceiling for mandatory EPFO cover from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month.
  • Impact – first change since September 2014; over 51 lakh more workers to be covered.
  • PLFS districts – MoSPI released the first district-level labour data, based on the new sample design from January 2025; the survey now follows the calendar year.
  • Women at work – Hamirpur (HP) had the highest female labour force participation among districts.
  • ASUSE 2025 – first district estimates for the unincorporated sector (757 districts); in every district of Mizoram at least half the units are women-owned.

Freight, ports and payments

  • DFC complete – PM Modi dedicated the last three Western DFC sections at Vadodara on 8 September; the full 2,843 km freight network now runs.
  • Two arms – Western DFC about 1,506 km (Dadri–JNPT); Eastern DFC about 1,337 km from Sahnewal (Punjab) to Sonnagar (Bihar).
  • Mega Ports – four notified on 25 September under Section 73 of the Indian Ports Act, 2025 – Deendayal (Kandla), Jawaharlal Nehru (Nhava Sheva), Paradip and Mundra.
  • Odd ones – Mundra (Adani) is the only non-major port in the list; Paradip is the only one on the east coast.
  • UPI MDR – from 15 October, 0.4% on merchant payments above ₹2,000 (capped at ₹300); merchants with up to ₹1 lakh UPI receipts a month are exempt; P2P stays free.
DFCCIL: Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, under the Ministry of Railways; the Western corridor was funded mainly by JICA, the Eastern by the World Bank.

Green fuels and EVs

  • e-Methanol – foundation laid on 26 September for the country's first e-methanol plant at a port – Deendayal Port, Kandla; 150 tonnes a day.
  • Partners – Deendayal Port Authority and Assam Petro-Chemicals, Namrup, in a 76:24 ratio; fuel for ships on Asia–Europe routes.
  • LNG train – India's first LNG-powered train was flagged off by Amit Shah from Sabarmati, Ahmedabad, on 27 September; dual-fuel power cars can cut diesel use by about 40%.
  • Soil carbon – first carbon-credit payment by DBT went to 2,550 smallholders in Punjab and Haryana, at Ludhiana.
  • EV index – NITI Aayog's India Electric Mobility Index 2025 (with WRI India) ranked Delhi first (84), then Maharashtra and Karnataka; MP rose from 23rd to 7th.

Shipyards, ratings and skills

  • Kochi ship repair – Drydocks World and Cochin Shipyard formed a 50:50 JV to run and expand the International Ship Repair Facility.
  • New shipyard – Mazagon Dock signed with NSHIP-AP for a greenfield yard at Dugarajapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.
  • JCR upgrade – Japan Credit Rating Agency lifted India from BBB+ to A-, outlook stable.
  • SHE-MARTs – launched by Amit Shah from Kollam, Kerala, on 26 September – community retail outlets for women SHGs under DAY-NRLM, a Budget 2026-27 idea.
  • WorldSkills 2026 – at Shanghai India finished 10th with 6 silvers and 20 medallions; Smriti Nambiar (bakery) was best of nation; the 2028 event goes to Aichi, Japan.

August 2026Monthly notes

Cabinet: energy schemes

  • GOBARdhan – on 6 August 2026 the Cabinet approved GOBARdhan as the National Circular Bioenergy Scheme, with Rs 23,731 crore for FY 2026-27 to 2035-36.
  • Nodal ministry – the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas runs it; the goal is to raise compressed biogas (CBG) output nearly ten times.
  • Blending rule – gas firms must mix CBG into CNG and piped gas: 3% in 2026-27, going up to 4% next year and 5% from 2028-29 onward.
  • Samudra Manthan – the National Offshore Exploration Scheme, cleared on 31 July with Rs 84,084 crore up to 2030-31, pushes deep and ultra-deep sea oil and gas search.
  • PM Surya Sarovar Yojana – Rs 5,070 crore under MNRE for floating solar plants (5,000 MW) with at least 10,000 MWh of battery storage.
GOBARdhan: Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan; began in 2018 under Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen) to turn cattle dung and farm waste into biogas and manure; now a national CBG scheme under MoPNG.

Farmers and food subsidy

  • PM-KISAN extended – the Cabinet on 31 July continued the scheme for 2026-27 to 2030-31 with Rs 3.15 lakh crore.
  • About PM-KISAN – started in February 2019; a central sector scheme paying Rs 6,000 a year in three instalments by DBT; open to all landholding farm families since June 2019.
  • Digital rupee for ration – on 14 August 2026 Chandigarh and Dadra and Nagar Haveli became the first UTs where PMGKAY food subsidy reaches all beneficiaries as programmable e₹ in CBDC wallets.
  • Earlier pilots – the CBDC food coupon was first tried in Gujarat on 15 February 2026, then in Puducherry on 26 February.
  • Popcorn seed – Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan launched India's first non-GMO popcorn maize hybrids, Krishna 459 and Godavari 234, at Musunuru (Eluru, AP), developed alongside ICAR-IIMR (Indian Institute of Maize Research).

Money, payments and welfare

  • FI-Index – RBI's Financial Inclusion Index improved in all three parts for the year to March 2026, with the biggest push from the Usage part.
  • MDR power – a change in the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 lets the government levy a Merchant Discount Rate on UPI and RuPay; UPI stays free for ordinary users.
  • Vishwas 2026 – an EPFO one-time scheme to settle penalty disputes with employers, open for six months from 29 June 2026.
  • PM e-DRIVE – the electric vehicle support scheme was extended by two years to 31 March 2028.
  • Jan Aushadhi – 20,149 Kendras were open by 30 June 2026, with a basket of 2,110 medicines plus 315 surgical items.
Financial Inclusion Index: launched by RBI in August 2021 on a 0–100 scale; built from three sub-indices – Access (35%), Usage (45%) and Quality (20%); released every July for the year ending March.

Power and infrastructure

  • 300 GW milestone – India's non-fossil power capacity crossed 300 GW by 31 July 2026, more than 54% of total installed capacity of about 552 GW.
  • Biggest source – solar leads clean capacity at about 165 GW, followed by wind and hydro.
  • Record year – FY 2025-26 saw a record 55.29 GW added, mostly solar.
  • Vishnugad-Pipalkoti – a 444 MW hydro project on the Alaknanda in Chamoli, Uttarakhand, built by THDC India.
  • Ship recycling – India topped the world in 2025, its share rising to 35.4% from 30.1%.

Industry, textiles and trade

  • Handloom CoE – on 3 August Textiles Minister Giriraj Singh opened the Centre of Excellence for Handloom Technology at IIT Delhi; it will build a national digital store of handloom knowledge.
  • Five launches – HandloomX, Handloom e-Vidya, the Handloom 4.0 app, Threads of Valour and Kalagriha (a Gen-Z design studio); a 'Handloom Haat' experience centre also opened in Delhi.
  • Bharat GI – the MSME Ministry and DPIIT signed an MoU to sell GI products under a 'Bharat GI' banner on ONDC and GeM.
  • Amul in Bengal – Amit Shah laid the foundation of the world's biggest curd plant at Sankrail Food Park, Howrah, for the Amul Bengal Dairy project.
  • Data for planning – MeitY tied up with PhonePe to use its Pulse Pro data platform for PM Gati Shakti.

Reports and deals

  • Family businesses – the Hurun India Most Valuable Family Businesses 2026 list, made with Barclays Private Clients, put the Ambani family on top.
  • Combined value – India's top 300 family firms are worth Rs 138 lakh crore; the Adani family leads among first-generation groups.
  • Income gap – MoSPI data show Sikkim has the highest per-capita income among states and Bihar the lowest.
  • Chennai loan – India and ADB signed a US$230 million loan for Chennai's climate-resilient water and sewerage project under AMRUT 2.0.
  • Mauritius – India and Mauritius signed a pact on oil and gas cooperation.

July 2026Monthly notes

Chips and mobile phones

  • Semicon 2.0 – the Union Cabinet cleared the second phase of the India Semiconductor Mission on 15 July 2026 with an outlay of Rs 1,27,500 crore.
  • Six pillars – support for chip design, fabs, ATMP/OSAT units, equipment and materials, R&D and talent.
  • Phase one record – under Semicon 1.0 (2021, Rs 76,000 crore), 12 units with investment of about Rs 1.64 lakh crore were approved.
  • MPMS – a Rs 62,500 crore Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme for 2026-27 to 2030-31 succeeds the PLI scheme for large-scale electronics, which ended in March 2026.
  • Incentives – a sales-linked reward of 2.25–5%, more for local parts and an extra 3% for Indian brands' design and R&D; output of about Rs 39 lakh crore is expected.
India Semiconductor Mission (ISM): set up in 2021 under the Ministry of Electronics and IT to build chip and display manufacturing; Micron (Sanand) and Tata Electronics (Dholera) are among the approved plants.

Air links and fertiliser

  • Viksit UDAN – PM Modi launched the next phase of UDAN at Jodhpur on 4 July 2026; it runs from 2026-27 to 2035-36 with Rs 28,840 crore.
  • Plan – 100 new airports from unused airstrips, 200 modern helipads, upkeep support and Rs 10,043 crore of viability gap funding for airlines.
  • Growth – India had 74 operational airports in 2014 and 165 by 15 July 2026; only two countries have a bigger domestic flying market.
  • Urea policy – the CCEA approved the National Investment Policy for Urea 2026 on 15 July to bring 8–9 new gas-based plants and about 10 million tonnes of extra capacity.
  • Assured returns – return on equity is fixed between 12% and 16%, and fixed costs can be shifted to rupees after four years to cut currency risk.
UDAN: 'Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik', the Regional Connectivity Scheme of the Civil Aviation Ministry launched in October 2016; the Airports Authority of India implements it.

Chemical parks, MSMEs and ships

  • BHAVYA Rasayan – on 24 July the Cabinet approved the Bharat Industrial Development Scheme – Chemicals to create 3 chemical parks, with Rs 3,030 crore for 2026-27 to 2030-31.
  • Park terms – the Centre gives a maximum of Rs 1,000 crore a park; the state adds at least Rs 500 crore and about 2,000 acres; parks are picked through a challenge route.
  • Corporate Mitra – a Corporate Affairs Ministry scheme to train young para-professionals who give low-cost compliance help to small firms in smaller (Tier-2/3) towns; phase one covers 2,000 people.
  • Shipbuilding – the Ports Ministry approved a greenfield shipbuilding cluster at Porbandar and a ship-repair yard at Vadinar, both in Gujarat.
  • Solar loan – the World Bank approved $890 million for India's National Rooftop Solar Programme.

New indices and reports

  • Investment Friendliness Index – NITI Aayog's first such index (17 July) ranked Gujarat first with 56.6, ahead of Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu; Goa and Odisha were also top performers.
  • Index design – all 28 states and 8 UTs were scored on 84 indicators under eight pillars; Uttarakhand led the hilly and north-eastern states.
  • Core industries – a new ICI series with base year 2022-23 adds iron ore as the ninth industry; electricity carries the highest weight.
  • Services index – MoSPI released the first Index of Services Production on 14 July, with base year 2024-25 and 19 sub-sectors.
  • School reports – UDISE+ 2025-26 showed lower dropout rates; in PGI 2.0 (2025-26) Chandigarh got the best grade, 'Uttam-3'.
NITI Aayog: replaced the Planning Commission on 1 January 2015; the Prime Minister is its chairperson; Vice-Chairperson Ashok Kumar Lahiri; Anurag Jain was named CEO in July 2026.

Trade, coal and company news

  • WTO fish pact – India became the 123rd member of the WTO Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies on 20 July 2026; the pact was adopted at MC12 in Geneva (2022).
  • Aaroha – the Coal Ministry released the first Indian report on scientific mine closure, covering 42 closed coal mines.
  • Five-star mines – three NTPC mines got five stars; Pakri-Barwadih in Jharkhand topped for the second year running.
  • Data centre – Reliance Industries and Meta will build a 168 MW data centre at Jamnagar, Gujarat.
  • Overseas buy – BPCL bought all of Brazil's IBV Brasil Petroleo.

June 2026Monthly notes

New price indices

  • WPI base – from 15 June the Wholesale Price Index uses 2022-23 as its base year in place of 2011-12.
  • Bigger basket – items covered rise from 697 to 957; solar, wind and nuclear power now feature in the energy group.
  • Producer Price Index – an output PPI, a trial input PPI and a quarterly Service PPI for seven services were released for the first time.
  • Transition – the WPI will continue for five years alongside the PPIs, after which the PPI takes over.
  • Compiler – the Office of the Economic Adviser in DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Jobs, housing and health cover

  • PM-VBRY – the PM released about Rs 2,400 crore of incentives under the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana.
  • Scheme basics – in force from 1 August 2025 with about Rs 99,446 crore; run through EPFO; it hopes to create more than 3.5 crore jobs.
  • Who gets what – first-time workers get up to Rs 15,000 in two parts; employers get Rs 3,000 a month per extra worker (four years in manufacturing).
  • PMAY-U 2.0 – over 2.13 lakh more urban homes were cleared, pushing approvals under the second phase beyond 16 lakh.
  • PM-JAY – West Bengal signed an MoU with the National Health Authority and became the 36th state/UT to adopt Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.
PMAY-Urban 2.0: Housing and Urban Affairs Ministry scheme for 2024-25 to 2028-29; its target is homes for 1 crore families from the urban poor and middle class through four verticals – BLC, AHP, ARH and interest subsidy.

Farm credit, food and exports

  • KCC norms – RBI made Kisan Credit Card rules uniform: a crop season now means 12 months for short crops but 18 for long ones; no collateral needed up to Rs 3 lakh against pledged produce.
  • Smart warehousing – Pralhad Joshi launched an AI and IoT based system in 215 Central Warehousing Corporation godowns on 18 June.
  • BHARATI – APEDA's export accelerator finished its first batch of 100 agri-food start-ups; target US$50 billion of such exports by 2030.
  • RISA – TRIFED's new premium label for tribal textiles and crafts, with a store in Delhi's Rajiv Gandhi Handicrafts Bhawan.
  • Senehjori – Jyotiraditya Scindia launched this mission to turn Assam's Muga silk into a global luxury brand.
Kisan Credit Card: started in 1998 on the R.V. Gupta Committee's advice to give farmers timely short-term loans for seeds, fertilisers and other inputs.

Investment and market reforms

  • Bonds for foreigners – new 15, 30 and 40-year government securities and Sovereign Green Bonds were added to the Fully Accessible Route (FAR), opened by RBI in 2020.
  • FEMA change – foreign individuals (PROI) may now buy listed Indian shares via the portfolio route; the single-investor cap rose from 5% to 10% and the combined cap to 24%.
  • ATF fund – a Rs 10,000 crore stabilisation fund will give oil marketing companies interest-free advances for three years to curb airfare spikes.
  • Coal exchange – the Coal Ministry notified the Coal Exchange Rules, 2026; the Coal Controller's Organisation will register and regulate exchanges.
  • Puducherry SEZs – two new zones were approved, one of them India's first IT/ITES SEZ built by an urban local body (Oulgaret).

Energy and industry projects

  • Coal to chemicals – on 20 June the PM laid the stone for India's first commercial coal-to-ammonium nitrate plant at Lakhanpur, Jharsuguda (Odisha), costing about Rs 25,016 crore.
  • Builder – Bharat Coal Gasification and Chemicals Ltd, a joint venture of BHEL and Coal India, using BHEL's home-grown fluidised-bed gasifier.
  • Floating solar – a NISE study puts India's floating solar potential at about 102 GW if 20% of water-body area is used; Maharashtra leads.
  • Omkareshwar – on the Narmada in Madhya Pradesh, India's largest floating solar plant (278 MW so far).
  • Andaman gas – Oil India found gas in its Vijayapuram-3 well off the Andamans.

May 2026Monthly notes

New schemes cleared by the Cabinet

  • Cotton mission – the Mission for Cotton Productivity gets Rs 5,659.22 crore for 2026-27 to 2030-31.
  • Cotton targets – raise lint yield from about 440 to 755 kg per hectare by 2031 and sell Indian cotton as 'Kasturi Cotton Bharat'.
  • Coal gasification – a Rs 37,500 crore scheme to gasify about 75 million tonnes of coal and lignite, paying up to 20% of plant cost.
  • Bigger aim – 100 million tonnes of gasification by 2030 under the National Coal Gasification Mission (2021).
  • SARTHAK-PDS – approved by CCEA on 27 May as an umbrella scheme with a central outlay of Rs 25,530 crore up to March 2031.
SARTHAK-PDS: Scheme for Assistance in Ration Transport and Handling – Income with Automation in PDS; merges help for intra-state grain movement and ration-dealer margins with SMART-PDS; serves about 81.35 crore NFSA beneficiaries using AI, machine learning and blockchain.

Credit, RBI and import curbs

  • ECLGS 5.0 – a fresh Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme to ease cash stress caused by the West Asia crisis.
  • Guarantee – 100% cover for MSMEs and 90% for non-MSMEs and airlines through NCGTC; the credit flow aimed at is Rs 2.55 lakh crore.
  • RBI surplus – the central board under Governor Sanjay Malhotra approved a record Rs 2,86,588.46 crore transfer to the Centre for 2025-26.
  • Gold and silver – from 13 May the effective import duty rose from 6% to 15% (10% basic customs duty plus 5% AIDC).
  • Sugar – the government also stopped sugar exports.

Farm prices and exports

  • Kharif MSP 2026-27 – raised for 14 crops; the largest rise was for sunflower seed (Rs 622 a quintal).
  • Other rises – cotton Rs 557, nigerseed Rs 515 and sesamum Rs 500 a quintal.
  • Sugarcane – FRP for 2026-27 fixed at Rs 365 a quintal at a basic recovery of 10.25%.
  • Honey – APEDA sent a 20-tonne ODOP honey lot from Baksa district, Assam, to the USA.
  • Seafood – the EU put India back on its list for farmed seafood exports, effective September 2026.

Industry, data and North-East missions

  • Chips – the India Semiconductor Mission cleared its 12th unit, involving Crystal Matrix (Dholera) and Suchi Semicon (Surat), both in Gujarat.
  • IIP – a MoSPI committee proposed moving the Index of Industrial Production base from 2011-12 to 2022-23.
  • IBC at 10 – the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code of 2016 turned ten, with over Rs 4 lakh crore recovered for lenders.
  • Mizoram – a Ginger Mission worth Rs 189.79 crore was launched by DoNER Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia.
  • More missions – Mission Queen Pineapple for Tripura (Rs 236 crore), plus missions for Arunachal kiwi and Nagaland coffee.

Ports, airports and rail

  • Vadinar – a Rs 1,570 crore ship repair yard at Vadinar, Gujarat, by Deendayal Port Authority and Cochin Shipyard, for ships up to 300 m.
  • Nagpur airport – the Cabinet cleared its upgrade through a public–private partnership.
  • Rail lines – three multitracking projects of about 901 km across six states were approved.
  • Lothal – Phase 1 of the National Maritime Heritage Complex in Gujarat is aimed for July 2026.

April 2026Monthly notes

PM Internship Scheme recast

  • Revamp – the Corporate Affairs Ministry reworked the PM Internship Scheme; a fresh round opened in April 2026.
  • Age band – now 18–25 years (was 21–24); final-year UG and PG students can also apply.
  • Stipend – Rs 9,000 a month (earlier Rs 5,000): Rs 8,100 from the Centre by DBT and Rs 900 from the company.
  • Length – internships now last 6 or 9 months instead of 12; at least half the time must be at a real workplace.
  • Extras – a one-time grant of Rs 6,000; family income must be under about Rs 12 lakh a year.
PM Internship Scheme: started in October 2024 by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs to place 1 crore young people in top companies over five years through the PMIS portal.

Roads, rail freight and a new airport

  • Expressway – PM Modi opened the 213-km, six-lane Delhi–Dehradun Economic Corridor on 14 April; it cost over Rs 12,000 crore.
  • Quicker trips – the drive drops from over 6 hours to about 2.5 hours across Delhi, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand.
  • Animal pass – a roughly 12-km elevated stretch over forest near Rajaji National Park is billed as Asia's longest wildlife corridor.
  • WDFC done – the full 1,506-km Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (JNPT to Dadri) was completed on 31 March 2026; the 1,337-km Eastern DFC runs Ludhiana–Sonnagar.
  • Jewar – the PM opened phase 1 of Noida International Airport on 28 March; it is a PPP built by YIAPL, a unit of Zurich Airport.

Power, ports and mines

  • Arunachal hydro – the CCEA cleared the 1,720 MW Kamala (NHPC joint venture) and 1,200 MW Kalai-II (THDC joint venture) projects.
  • Lohit basin – Kalai-II in Anjaw district is the first scheme in the Lohit basin; Kamala is on a Subansiri tributary.
  • Top port – major ports moved 915 million tonnes in FY26, above target; Deendayal Port (Kandla, Gujarat) handled the most.
  • Green power – IRENA's Renewable Energy Statistics 2026 placed India 3rd in installed renewable capacity, after China and the USA, passing Brazil.
  • Mining push – SASCI 2026-27 carries a Rs 5,000 crore reward pool for state mining reforms, partly judged by the State Mining Readiness Index.

Tax, trade and statistics

  • New tax law – the Income-tax Act, 2025 took effect on 1 April 2026 in place of the 1961 Act; the Income-tax Rules, 2026 were notified by CBDT.
  • SEZ sales – till 31 March 2027, SEZ units may sell into the Domestic Tariff Area at lower duty, up to 30% of their best export value, with 20% value addition.
  • Services survey – the NSO began the first Annual Survey of Incorporated Services Sector Enterprises (ASISSE) for 2024-25, drawing on GSTN data.
  • Skills fund – MoS Jayant Chaudhary launched a Skills Outcomes Fund run by NSDC, paying on job outcomes.
  • Ship cover – the Cabinet cleared a Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool with a Rs 12,980 crore sovereign guarantee to cut reliance on foreign insurers.

Banking and digital payments

  • Licence cancelled – the RBI cancelled the banking licence of Paytm Payments Bank, using its powers in Section 22(4), Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
  • Two-step check – from 1 April 2026, digital payments need an extra factor beyond the PIN, such as an OTP or biometrics.
  • Utkarsh 2029 – RBI's medium-term strategy for April 2026 to March 2029 sets out its priorities under six pillars.
  • Payments Vision 2028 – RBI's new roadmap for the payments system, themed 'Shaping India's Payment Frontier'.
  • Mission Saksham – an RBI drive to improve governance in urban co-operative banks.
Reserve Bank of India: set up on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934, on the Hilton Young Commission's advice; nationalised in 1949; headquarters Mumbai.

Plans and reports

  • DPI@2047 – on 27 April NITI Aayog released 'DPI@2047 for Viksit Bharat', drawn up with Deloitte and the EkStep Foundation.
  • Two phases – DPI 2.0 (2025–2035) for jobs-led growth, then DPI 3.0 (2035–2047) for wider prosperity.
  • PMGSY-III – the Cabinet extended PMGSY-III up to March 2028 to link villages with markets, higher secondary schools and hospitals.
  • AgriStack – more than 9.2 crore Farmer IDs were issued by March 2026; Uttar Pradesh leads.
  • Data alignment – MoSPI asked states to move GSDP and district GDP estimates to the national base year 2022-23.

March 2026Monthly notes

BHAVYA for plug-and-play parks

  • Approval – on 18 March 2026 the Cabinet cleared the Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana (BHAVYA) with Rs 33,660 crore.
  • Target – 100 ready-to-use industrial parks of 100 to 1,000 acres; 50 in the first phase.
  • Support – as much as Rs 1 crore an acre for internal and social infrastructure, and 25% help for outside links.
  • Period – six years, 2026-27 to 2031-32, run by NICDC with states and private developers.
  • Selection – parks will be picked through a challenge route and planned on PM Gati Shakti lines.
NICDC: National Industrial Corridor Development Corporation under DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry; develops industrial smart cities along the corridors.

A ten-year plan for regional flights

  • Outlay – the revised UDAN regional air scheme gets Rs 28,840 crore for 2026-27 to 2035-36.
  • Airports – 100 new airports from unused airstrips and 200 heliports are planned.
  • VGF – airlines get viability gap funding to fly allotted routes.
  • History – UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik) began in October 2016 under the Ministry of Civil Aviation.
  • Aircraft – Pawan Hans gets HAL Dhruv helicopters and Alliance Air gets HAL Dornier planes.

Water and clean power

  • JJM extended – the Jal Jeevan Mission now runs to December 2028, with total cost of Rs 8.69 lakh crore (Centre Rs 3.59 lakh crore).
  • New focus – from laying pipes to steady supply; each village gets a 'Sujal Gram' ID and the system is mapped from source to tap.
  • Coverage – rural homes with taps rose from 17% in 2019 to about 81%.
  • Small hydro – a Rs 2,584.60 crore scheme for 2026-27 to 2030-31 targets about 1,500 MW from 1–25 MW projects.
  • Kopili – the PM opened the 120 MW Lower Kopili run-of-river project in Assam, on a Brahmaputra tributary flowing in from the south bank.
Jal Jeevan Mission: launched on 15 Aug 2019 by the Ministry of Jal Shakti; aims at a working tap giving 55 litres per person per day to every rural home.

Credit, visas and investment rules

  • CGSMFI 2.0 – a guarantee scheme through NCGTC covers bank loans to microfinance lenders; cover is 70–80% of the default, by lender size.
  • IVFRT – the immigration, visa and foreigner tracking scheme was extended five years, to March 2031, with Rs 1,800 crore.
  • FDI change – investors from countries sharing a land border with India may use the automatic route if they hold 10% or less and have no control.
  • Fast track – such proposals in electronic parts, polysilicon and ingot-wafers must be decided within 60 days.
  • Green fuels – MNRE fixed limits of 0.38 kg CO2e per kg for green ammonia and 0.44 kg for green methanol.

War shock: fuel and exports

  • Excise – petrol and diesel excise was cut by Rs 10 a litre.
  • Export levy – exports now pay diesel Rs 21.5 per litre and ATF Rs 29.5 per litre, to keep fuel at home.
  • Gas order – a Natural Gas Control Order was issued on 9 March 2026 under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955 to curb hoarding of LPG and gas.
  • RELIEF – a Rs 497 crore scheme from 19 March 2026 helps exporters hit by war freight and insurance costs; ECGC runs it under the Export Promotion Mission.
  • Oil stocks – strategic reserves of about 5.33 MMT sit at Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru and Padur; Phase II adds Chandikhol and Padur.

States' finances and jobs

  • Fiscal Health Index – NITI Aayog's second FHI (11 March 2026, data for 2023-24) again put Odisha first (73.1), with Goa and Jharkhand next.
  • Bottom – Punjab came last among the 18 major states.
  • Hill states – ten states of the North-East and Himalayas were added; Arunachal Pradesh led them.
  • Pillars – quality of spending, revenue mobilisation, fiscal prudence, debt index and debt sustainability.
  • PLFS 2025 – the annual labour survey (age 15+) showed unemployment at 3.1%.

Output milestones

  • Coal – India crossed 1 billion tonnes of coal output for the second year running; the aim is 1.5 BT by 2029-30.
  • Iron ore – NMDC became the first Indian miner to dig 50 MT of iron ore in a single year.
  • Metro – about 1,095 km of metro lines run in 26 cities, the world's third largest network.
  • Coconut – India is the top coconut grower, with about 30% of world output.
  • LPG ATM – BPCL set up India's first round-the-clock LPG cylinder ATM, 'BharatGas Insta LPG', in Gurugram.

February 2026Monthly notes

PM RAHAT for road accident victims

  • Full form – Pradhan Mantri Road Accident Victim Hospitalisation and Assured Treatment scheme, launched on 13 Feb 2026 as the first decision from Seva Teerth.
  • Cover – cashless care capped at Rs 1.5 lakh per victim for 7 days after the accident, on any road.
  • Ministry – Road Transport and Highways; hospitals are paid within 10 days of State Health Agency approval.
  • Who pays – the Motor Vehicle Accident Fund; the Centre covers hit-and-run and uninsured cases.
  • Access – linked to the 112 emergency number (ERSS) for ambulances.

Border villages and rural women

  • VVP-II – Home Minister Amit Shah launched it on 20 Feb at Nathanpur, Cachar district, Assam.
  • Scope – 1,954 border villages in 15 states and 2 UTs; Rs 6,839 crore till 2028-29; a Central Sector Scheme.
  • VVP-I – approved on 15 Feb 2023 for 662 villages along the northern (China) border.
  • Lakhpati Didi – the target was doubled to 6 crore women by March 2029 after the 3 crore goal was met early.
  • Meaning – a self-help group member whose household earns Rs 1 lakh or more a year.
Lakhpati Didi: launched in 2023 under DAY-NRLM; Ministry of Rural Development; aims to lift SHG women through skills, credit and market links.

Money for farms, startups and cities

  • AIF – the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund was doubled to Rs 2 lakh crore (was Rs 1 lakh crore); 3% interest subvention on loans not above Rs 2 crore.
  • Startup FoF 2.0 – a Rs 10,000 crore Fund of Funds approved; managed by SIDBI; the first version came in 2016.
  • Urban Challenge Fund – Rs 1 lakh crore; the Centre gives 25% of a project's cost and at least half must come from the market.
  • Startup tag – the turnover ceiling for recognition is now Rs 200 crore (was Rs 100 crore).
  • Deep tech – such startups get a Rs 300 crore limit and recognition for 20 years.

16th Finance Commission report

  • Tabled – 1 Feb 2026, for 2026-27 to 2030-31; Chairman Arvind Panagariya.
  • States' share – kept at 41% of the divisible pool, as before.
  • New yardstick – 'contribution to GDP' (10% weight) replaces 'tax and fiscal effort'.
  • Weights – income distance 42.5%, population (2011) 17.5%, area 10%, forest 10%, demography 10%.
  • Grants – revenue deficit grants stopped; total grants of about Rs 9.47 lakh crore suggested.
Finance Commission: constitutional body under Article 280, formed by the President every five years; its report and action taken are laid before Parliament under Article 281.

New base years for data

  • CPI – a new series with base 2024 (earlier 2012) was first released on 12 Feb 2026.
  • Basket – 358 items – 308 goods and 50 services – against 299 earlier; 12 divisions in place of 6.
  • Weights – taken from HCES (Household Consumption Expenditure Survey) 2023-24; OTT services and rural housing added.
  • GDP and IIP – their new base year is 2022-23 (earlier 2011-12).
  • Trade indices – DGCI&S moved export–import index base to 2022-23.

Projects and companies

  • River tunnel – Cabinet cleared the country's first tunnel carrying both road and rail under the Brahmaputra, linking Gohpur and Numaligarh, Assam; Rs 18,662 crore.
  • Kamala project – a 1,800 MW multipurpose project on the Kamala river, Lower Subansiri, Arunachal Pradesh, by NHPC.
  • Yantra India – the defence PSU (set up 2021) got Miniratna Category-I status.
  • Bharat Taxi – India's first cooperative ride-hailing app, launched by Amit Shah on 5 Feb; no commission, no surge fares.
  • Tuna pass – a free online 'Access Pass' for deep-sea fishing in the EEZ was launched at Veraval, Gujarat.
Bharat Taxi: run by Sahakar Taxi Cooperative Ltd, registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002; backed by Amul, IFFCO, NAFED and others; Ministry of Cooperation.

January 2026Monthly notes

Export Promotion Mission starts paying out

  • Credit relief – 2.75% interest subvention on export credit, up to Rs 50 lakh per exporter this year.
  • Guarantee – collateral cover with CGTMSE: 85% for micro and small, 65% for medium firms.
  • Market reach – Market Access Support for fairs and buyer meets; at least 35% MSME share.
  • New FEMA rules – RBI's export–import regulations from Oct 2026: export money to come home within 15 months (18 for rupee trade).
  • States ranking – NITI Aayog Export Preparedness Index 4.0: Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat top large states; Uttarakhand tops small states.
EPM: approved 12 Nov 2025; Rs 25,060 crore for 2025-26 to 2030-31; run by DGFT; two arms – Niryat Protsahan (finance) and Niryat Disha (market access).

Pensions, roads and the girl child

  • Atal Pension Yojana – extended to 2030-31; Rs 1,000–5,000 monthly pension after 60 for non-taxpayers joining at 18–40.
  • PMGSY-IV – 10,000+ km new rural roads cleared; phase covers 62,500 km to 2028-29.
  • BBBP at 11 – Beti Bachao Beti Padhao and Sukanya Samriddhi both began on 22 Jan 2015 at Panipat.
  • Sukanya Samriddhi – 8.2% interest; girls under 10; matures at 21.
Atal Pension Yojana: started 2015; regulated by PFRDA. PMGSY: rural roads scheme started 25 Dec 2000 under the Rural Development Ministry.

Industry and procurement schemes

  • Womaniya (GeM) – 2 lakh+ women-led firms; 4.7% of GeM orders vs 3% target.
  • Textiles – District-Led Textile Transformation: 100 export-champion and 100 aspirational districts.
  • PLI white goods – five more AC-component firms; 85 firms in total.
  • Ann Chakra – PDS route-planning tool, a Franz Edelman Award 2026 finalist.

Money, banks and regulators

  • SIDBI – Rs 5,000 crore fresh equity from the Centre in three parts.
  • Payments Regulatory Board – first meeting under RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra; replaces the old BPSS.
  • NPS payouts – PFRDA panel under M.S. Sahoo to design an assured-payout option.
  • Maritime lending – Sagarmala Finance Corporation, first NBFC for the sea sector.
  • Forex – reserves at a record of about US$709 billion.
SIDBI: 1990, under SIDBI Act 1989; HQ Lucknow. PRB: formed under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007; six members, chaired by the RBI Governor.

Minerals, power, food and ports

  • Coking coal – named a critical and strategic mineral; about 95% of steel's need is imported.
  • Grid – transmission lines passed 5 lakh circuit km (220 kV+) on 14 Jan.
  • Rice – India passed China as top rice grower (about 150 million tonnes); UP leads among states.
  • Green tug – first all-electric tug for Deendayal Port, Kandla, under the Green Tug Transition Programme.
  • Sea farming – first open-sea fish and seaweed farm pilot off Sri Vijayapuram; Mayabunder fishing harbour cleared.

Reports and corporate moves

  • CAG on states – 18 states crossed the 3% fiscal deficit line in 2023-24.
  • NITI roadmaps – decarbonising cement and aluminium; MSME green transition.
  • Brand Finance – TCS 2nd in IT Services 25 (Accenture 1st); Tata top Indian brand in Global 500.
  • Aircraft – HAL with Russia's UAC to build the SJ-100 jet; Adani with Brazil's Embraer.
