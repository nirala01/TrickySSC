MS Office is the largest and most practical chapter of Computer Knowledge. SSC asks what a shortcut does (Ctrl + Enter, Shift + F3, F12, Shift + F5), which tab or group holds a command (Watermark, Mail Merge, Format Painter), what a formula returns (=2+3*4^2, COUNTA, VLOOKUP, ROUND) and what each Office application or file extension is for (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .accdb, .pst).

Marks are lost in three places. The first is shortcuts that differ by application: Ctrl + D opens the Font dialog in Word but duplicates a slide in PowerPoint and fills down in Excel. The second is Excel order of operations and references: powers before multiplication, and $A$1 vs A$1 vs A1 when a formula is copied. The third is views and tabs: Slide Sorter vs Normal vs Reading view, and whether a command sits on Insert, Design, Layout or References. The tests here are built around exactly these three.

The three traps that cost the most marks

  • Same keys, different apps. Ctrl + D – Font dialog (Word), duplicate slide (PowerPoint), fill down (Excel). F5 – Go To (Word), start slide show (PowerPoint). Always read which application the question names.
  • Order of operations in Excel. Brackets, then powers (^), then * and /, then + and −. =2+3*4^2 is 50, not 400 or 80.
  • Which tab? Watermark – Design; Drop Cap, WordArt, SmartArt – Insert; Table of Contents, Footnote – References; Mail Merge – Mailings; Track Changes – Review.

Solved Examples — SSC CGL MS Office

Q1. The Ribbon interface, which replaced the traditional menus and toolbars, was first introduced in which version of MS Office?
(a) Office 2003
(b) Office 2007
(c) Office 2010
(d) Office 2013
The Ribbon was first introduced in Office 2007. The Ribbon is the wide strip of commands below the title bar. It replaced the menus and toolbars used in earlier versions. Office 2003 was the last version with menus and toolbars. Answer: (b)
Q2. Consider the following statements about the MS Office interface:
1. A contextual tab, such as Picture Format, appears only when a related object is selected.
2. The Backstage view (File tab) was introduced in Office 2010.
3. The Ribbon can be shown or hidden with the shortcut Ctrl + F2.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3
Statements 1 and 2 are correct; statement 3 is wrong. A contextual tab appears only when needed, e.g., Picture Format when a picture is selected. The Backstage view under the File tab was introduced in Office 2010. Backstage view holds file tasks like New, Open, Save As, Print, Share and Options. Answer: (a)
Q3. Read the Assertion (A) and Reason (R) carefully.
Assertion (A): A Word file saved in .docx format is generally smaller in size than the same file saved in .doc format.
Reason (R): The .docx format is based on Office Open XML, in which the file's contents are stored in a compressed (ZIP) package.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true, but R is false
(d) A is false, but R is true
Both A and R are true, and R correctly explains A. The x in .docx stands for XML, as it uses the Office Open XML format. A .docx file is really a ZIP package of XML parts, so it is compressed. This compression makes .docx files smaller than old binary .doc files. Answer: (a)
Q4. जब OneDrive पर सहेजी गई किसी पावरपॉइंट प्रस्तुति को कई लोग एक ही समय पर संपादित करते हैं और हर व्यक्ति दूसरों के परिवर्तन देखता है, तो इस सुविधा को क्या कहते हैं?
(a) मेल मर्ज (Mail Merge)
(b) ट्रैक चेंजेस (Track Changes)
(c) कंपेयर (Compare)
(d) को-ऑथरिंग (Co-authoring)
एक ही फ़ाइल को एक साथ, एक ही समय पर संपादित करना को-ऑथरिंग कहलाता है। इसके लिए फ़ाइल क्लाउड, जैसे OneDrive या SharePoint, पर सहेजी और साझा की जानी चाहिए। अन्य संपादकों के चित्र या आद्याक्षर दिखाते हैं कि कौन किस भाग पर काम कर रहा है। AutoSave साझा फ़ाइल को हर कुछ सेकंड में अपने-आप सहेजता रहता है। उत्तर: (d)
