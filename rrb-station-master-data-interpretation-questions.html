Data Analysis & Interpretation · CEN 08/2026 pattern

Why Data Interpretation decides the Station Master paper

Data Analysis & Interpretation is fixed at 30 marks in the RRB Station Master CBT — more than Logical Capability and Mental Reasoning, and as much as Basic Science and Maths together. The syllabus covers text, tables, bar graphs, line graphs, pie charts, scatter plots, statistical curves and distributions, Venn diagrams, data sufficiency and data arrangement.

In the 2026 RRB Section Controller papers on the same pattern, every data question stood alone: one chart or table and one question on it, with no five-question sets. That means 30 separate data sets to read in the exam, so reading a chart quickly is the skill to build.

Solved Data Interpretation questions

Q1.

The table shows the units produced by a factory.

Year20212022202320242025
Units420480450540600
By what percentage did production rise from 2021 to 2025?

  1. 40%
  2. 42.86%
  3. 45%
  4. 30%
Show answer & solution

Answer: (b) 42.86%

Rise = 600 − 420 = 180. Percentage rise = 180/420 × 100 = 42.86%.

Q2.

A family’s monthly spending of ₹40,000 is shown as a pie chart: Food 30%, Rent 25%, Education 15%, Transport 10%, Savings 20%. How much more is spent on food than on education, and what is the central angle for rent?

  1. ₹6,000 and 90°
  2. ₹6,000 and 75°
  3. ₹4,000 and 90°
  4. ₹12,000 and 90°
Show answer & solution

Answer: (a) ₹6,000 and 90°

Food − Education = 30% − 15% = 15% of ₹40,000 = ₹6,000. Central angle for rent = 25% of 360° = 90°.

Q3.

In a group of 120 students, 70 like tea, 60 like coffee and 25 like both. How many like neither?

  1. 10
  2. 15
  3. 25
  4. 35
Show answer & solution

Answer: (b) 15

Students who like at least one = 70 + 60 − 25 = 105. Neither = 120 − 105 = 15.

Q4.

Is x > y?
Statement I: x = 2y.
Statement II: y > 0.

  1. I alone is sufficient
  2. II alone is sufficient
  3. I and II together are sufficient, neither alone
  4. Even together they are not sufficient
Show answer & solution

Answer: (c) I and II together are sufficient

From I alone, x > y only if y is positive (if y = −3, x = −6 < y), so I is not enough. II alone says nothing about x. Together, x = 2y with y > 0 gives x − y = y > 0, so x > y.

Q5.

A train carries 200 passengers. 40% travel in AC coaches. Of the AC passengers 25% are women, and of the non-AC passengers 30% are women. How many women are on the train?

  1. 50
  2. 56
  3. 60
  4. 64
Show answer & solution

Answer: (b) 56

AC passengers = 40% of 200 = 80, women = 25% of 80 = 20. Non-AC = 120, women = 30% of 120 = 36. Total women = 20 + 36 = 56.

Q6.

The table shows tickets sold at a station.

MonthJanFebMarApr
General1,2001,3501,1001,450
Reserved800750900950
In which month was the share of reserved tickets in the total the highest?

  1. January
  2. February
  3. March
  4. April
Show answer & solution

Answer: (c) March

Reserved share: Jan 800/2,000 = 40%; Feb 750/2,100 ≈ 35.7%; Mar 900/2,000 = 45%; Apr 950/2,400 ≈ 39.6%. March is the highest.

How to get fast at Data Interpretation

  • Fractions to percentages by heart. 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/6 ≈ 16.7%, 3/7 ≈ 42.9% and similar values let you skip long division in percentage-change questions.
  • Read the question before the chart. With one question per chart, look only at the rows or bars the question needs.
  • Approximate where the options are far apart. When options differ by more than 5%, rounding to the nearest ten is enough.
  • Data sufficiency has a fixed method. Test statement I alone, then II alone, then both, and try a negative or zero value to break a statement.
  • Practise mixed sets daily. Each TrickySSC Station Master mock has about 30 data questions across bar, pie, line, table, Venn, scatter and caselet formats.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Data Interpretation questions are there in RRB Station Master?

Data Analysis and Interpretation carries 30 marks out of 100 in the CEN 08/2026 pattern, inside the 60-mark first section.

What types of data questions come in the Station Master CBT?

Tables, bar graphs, line graphs, pie charts, scatter plots, statistical curves and distributions, Venn diagrams, data sufficiency and data arrangement, plus text-based caselets.

Are Station Master DI questions in sets?

In the 2026 RRB Section Controller papers on the same pattern, each data question had its own chart or table, with no five-question sets. The Station Master paper may differ, as the notification calls the distribution indicative.

How can I improve speed in Data Interpretation?

Learn common fraction-to-percentage values, read the question before the chart, approximate when options are far apart, and practise mixed data sets in full mocks on a timer.
