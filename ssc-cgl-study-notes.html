Frequently asked questions

Are these SSC CGL study notes free?
Yes. Every chapter of notes on TrickySSC is free to read, needs no login and no payment. The practice tests linked from each chapter are free as well.
Are the notes available in Hindi?
Yes. Each chapter has an English version and a Hindi version, and you can switch between them with one click at the top of the page. Formulas and technical terms are kept in English inside the Hindi notes, because that is how they appear in the exam paper.
Which subject should I start with for SSC CGL?
Start with Quantitative Aptitude, and inside it with Number System. It is the base for simplification, LCM-HCF, percentage and several other chapters, so studying it first makes the rest faster.
Can I download these SSC CGL notes as a PDF?
Every notes page is print friendly. Open the chapter, press Ctrl+P (or Share → Print on a phone) and save it as a PDF. The layout, boxes and tables are designed to print cleanly on A4.
Are these notes enough to clear SSC CGL Tier-1?
The notes cover the concepts and shortcuts you need, but reading alone is not enough. Attempt the chapter test after each topic, then move to previous year papers and full-length mocks to build speed and accuracy.
How often are new chapters added?
New chapters are published regularly, subject by subject. Quantitative Aptitude is being completed first, followed by Reasoning, English and General Awareness.
