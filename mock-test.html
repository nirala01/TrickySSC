General Instructions
Please read carefully before starting the test
⏱ Time & Submission
  1. This test is sectional. Each section has its own 15-minute timer shown at the top, along with the active section's name.
  2. When a section's timer reaches zero, that section is submitted automatically and you move to the next section. On the final section, the test ends automatically.
  3. You can submit a section early using the Submit Section button. Once a section is submitted, you cannot return to it, and its remaining time is lost. On the last section the button becomes Submit Test.
  4. You may freely navigate only within the currently active section; other sections stay locked.
📊 Question Palette
  1. The Question Palette on the right side of the screen shows the status of each question:
Not Visited — You have not visited the question yet.
Answered — You have answered the question.
Skipped — You have visited but not answered the question.
Marked for Review — You have marked the question for review.
🗏 Navigating Questions
  1. Click on a question number in the Question Palette to go directly to that question. Note: this does NOT save your answer for the current question.
  2. Click Next → to save your answer and go to the next question.
  3. Click 🔖 Mark to flag a question for review. If answered, that answer will still be evaluated unless you change it.
✏️ Answering Questions
  1. Choose one answer from options A, B, C, D. Click the same option again to deselect. Click a different option to change your answer.
  2. Only questions for which answers are saved or marked for review will be considered for evaluation.

📚 Sections
  1. This test has 4 sections: Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, and English. Click a section name in the top bar to navigate to it.
  2. After Next → on the last question of a section, you will automatically go to the first question of the next section.
  3. The Mark for Review status indicates you want to revisit a question. If answered and marked, the answer will be evaluated unless the status is modified by you.
