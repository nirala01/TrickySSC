RRB Station Master vs Section Controller — How the Two Papers Compare

Same length and marking, two key differences · CEN 08/2026

RRB Station Master has its own paper for the first time under CEN 08/2026. The only exam held on the same pattern so far is the RRB Section Controller CBT of February and October 2026, which is why its papers are used as Station Master previous year papers. This page shows what is the same and what is different.

What is the same

  • Length and marking. Both are 100 questions in 120 minutes on one clock, 1 mark each, one-third of a mark off for a wrong answer.
  • Logical Capability and Mental Reasoning. The last 40 marks have the same headings and the same kinds of questions.
  • No General Awareness. Neither paper has static GK or current affairs, unlike RRB NTPC.

What is different

  • Basic Science is new. Station Master adds units, density, work and energy, friction, heat, electricity and simple machines to the first section. The Section Controller paper had none. See Station Master Maths & Basic Science.
  • Data Interpretation is fixed at 30 marks. The Station Master notification gives Data Analysis & Interpretation its own 30 marks. See Station Master Data Interpretation.

What the Section Controller papers tell a Station Master candidate

  • Every data question stood alone — one chart or table and one question — with no five-question sets.
  • Reading comprehension was most of Logical Capability: three English passages of about 190 words with five questions each, and only four or five logical reasoning questions.
  • Arithmetic templates repeated from shift to shift: mixtures with a gain percentage, work problems, coins and cards in probability, melting and recasting solids, and mean, median and range.
  • Mental Reasoning was the same every shift: letter-cluster analogies and odd-one-out, number and letter series, coding, sign interchange, ranking and arrangement puzzles.
  • The level was moderate, and the 60-mark first section took the most time.

How to use both

  1. Sit a Section Controller paper in one 120-minute stretch to see where you stand on the real pattern.
  2. Add Basic Science from Class 9–10 physics, since the real papers will not show you any.
  3. Move to the RRB Station Master mock tests, which follow the CEN 08/2026 split with science and 30 data questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the RRB Station Master paper the same as the Section Controller paper?

The length and marking are the same: 100 questions in 120 minutes with one-third negative marking, and the same Logical Capability and Mental Reasoning sections. Station Master adds Basic Science and fixes Data Analysis and Interpretation at 30 marks.

Why are Section Controller papers used for Station Master preparation?

No Station Master paper has been held on the new pattern yet. The RRB Section Controller CBT of February and October 2026 is the only exam on the same pattern, so its papers are the closest real papers.

Does the Station Master paper have General Awareness?

No. Neither the Station Master nor the Section Controller CBT has a General Awareness section.

Was RRB Station Master earlier part of RRB NTPC?

Yes. Earlier the post was filled through RRB NTPC on a different pattern. Under CEN 08/2026 it has its own notification and its own paper.
