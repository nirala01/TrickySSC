October 2026Monthly notes

Exercises with partners

  • INDRA 2026 – India–Russia drill; the naval part ran 13–18 October at Vladivostok and the Sea of Japan with INS Sahyadri; land drills in Chelyabinsk ran till 23 October.
  • AUSTRAHIND – 5th India–Australia army exercise, 19 October–1 November, Foreign Training Node, Thiruvananthapuram; about 120 troops each.
  • SIMBEX – 33rd India–Singapore naval exercise at Visakhapatnam (21–26 October), with INS Nilgiri and INS Kamorta.
  • Garuda Shakti – 11th India–Indonesia special forces exercise at Banyuwangi (27 October–6 November).
  • AIME-2026 – 2nd ASEAN–India Maritime Exercise from 28 September at Subic Bay, Philippines; INS Sahyadri and INS Kulish took part.
SIMBEX: Singapore–India Maritime Bilateral Exercise, begun in 1994 (first called Exercise Lion King); one of India's longest-running naval exercises.

More drills and shows

  • IBSAMAR IX – India–Brazil–South Africa naval exercise ended at Simon's Town; INS Tarkash represented India.
  • KONKAN 2026 – INS Trishul reached Plymouth for the India–UK naval exercise.
  • SAREX-2026 – the Coast Guard's multinational search-and-rescue exercise off Paradip, Odisha.
  • Cambrian Patrol – an Assam Regiment team won gold at the patrol contest in Wales.
  • Wings Over Ganga – IAF air show at Varanasi, over the ghats from Namo Ghat to Assi Ghat.

New ships

  • INS Samarthak – commissioned on 16 October at Naval Base Karwar in the presence of Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan.
  • Firsts – India's first indigenous Multi-Purpose Vessel and first major warship built by a private yard (L&T, Kattupalli); 107 m long, over 75% indigenous; can act as a drone mothership.
  • INS Mangrol – commissioned on 12 October in Mumbai; third of eight Mahe-class anti-submarine shallow water craft.
  • Shreyas – GRSE, Kolkata, launched the third of four Next Generation Offshore Patrol Vessels.
  • Surya – first of five Fleet Support Ships (about 43,500 t) launched at Hindustan Shipyard, Visakhapatnam.

Deals, weapons and doctrine

  • DAC – on 26 October cleared proposals worth about ₹2.12 lakh crore (about 82% from Indian industry), including 300-plus more K9 Vajra guns.
  • Contracts – the GSAT-7C satellite from NSIL (₹2,563 crore) and Varunastra heavyweight torpedoes from BDL (₹1,543 crore).
  • Nagastra – Solar Defence and Aerospace delivered 450 Nagastra-1R loitering munitions; Nagastra-2 also reached the Army.
  • Doctrine – Rajnath Singh released the Land Warfare Doctrine 2026 on 23 October, replacing the 2018 version.
  • Bharat Rice – the Army began 'Bharat Rice' for troops on 12 October, supplied through NAFED under an FCI pact.

Air Force and Army days

  • IAF Day – the 94th anniversary on 8 October, with the parade at Hindon, Ghaziabad.
  • Raised – the IAF was formed on 8 October 1932.
  • Infantry Day – 27 October; it recalls the landing of Indian troops at Srinagar airfield in 1947.
  • Territorial Army Day – marked on 9 October; the TA was raised in 1949.

September 2026Monthly notes

Army exercises

  • Yudh Abhyas 2026 – 22nd India–US edition, 15–28 September at Auli and the Mahajan Field Firing Range, Bikaner; the last one was at Fort Wainwright, Alaska.
  • Nomadic Elephant – 18th edition with Mongolia from 21 September at Pithoragarh.
  • KAZIND-2026 – 9th India–Kazakhstan drill (28 September–11 October) at Oskemen; India sent a Garhwal Rifles battalion.
  • Eager Lion – US–Jordan exercise held in the US (Fort Carson, Colorado) for the first time.
  • Operation Sohan – two terrorists killed in Udhampur, J&K (16–17 September) by the White Knight Corps, J&K Police SOG and CRPF.
Yudh Abhyas: annual India–US army exercise held since 2004, alternately in India and the US; focuses on UN Chapter VII-type operations.

Navy at sea

  • SLINEX-26 – 13th India–Sri Lanka naval drill at Visakhapatnam (17–21 September); INS Kavaratti and INS Jyoti joined SLNS Sindurala.
  • Samudra Laksamana – 4th edition with Malaysia at Lumut; INS Mysore took part.
  • Varuna – 24th India–France edition began as INS Trishul reached Toulon on 22 September.
  • Egypt – INS Trishul did a passage exercise with ENS Berenice off Alexandria.
  • ADMM-Plus – INS Kulish and INS Sahyadri reached Subic Bay, Philippines, for an ADMM-Plus activity.
Varuna: India–France naval exercise that began in 1983 and has been called Varuna since 2001.

Air power

  • Tarang Shakti 2026 – IAF's second multinational air exercise, 26 September–12 October at Jodhpur; about 40 countries, motto 'Power in Partnership'.
  • F-35 debut – US F-35 stealth jets joined an exercise in India for the first time, along with French Rafales and UAE F-16s.
  • Veer Guardian 2026 – IAF and Japan's Air Self-Defense Force at Jodhpur (9–22 September); Japan flew F-2A jets.
  • Dronathon – IAF's first such event at Pokhran (14–16 September); it released an unmanned aircraft roadmap.
  • Display teams – Surya Kiran flew its first show at Meerut; Sarang flew at the El Alamein airshow in Egypt.

Weapons and deals

  • SAT-SAAW – MoD signed a ₹811 crore deal with BDL (23 September) for 160 DRDO glide bombs, under Buy (Indian-IDDM).
  • Khagantak-243 – about 300 kg glide bomb by Nagpur's JSR Dynamics with BEL; drop-tested from a Su-30MKI at Pokhran; range over 140 km.
  • DAC nods – on 7 September: CBRN recce vehicles, trawl tanks and Sarvatra bridges for the Army; the Navy gets Arudhra radars and the IAF ground-based jammers.
  • Javelin – the Army accepted a US offer for 60 missiles (₹292 crore) through the Foreign Military Sales route.
  • AASHVAST – DG EME lab to check military drones for hidden flaws, opened by Army Chief Gen Dhiraj Seth.

Plans, ships and milestones

  • Raksha – defence diplomacy roadmap for 10 years, released by Rajnath Singh on 3 September.
  • Aero India 2027 – 16th edition on 8–12 February 2027 at Air Force Station Yelahanka, Bengaluru.
  • INS Nipun – second Nistar-class diving support vessel, commissioned on 31 August at the Naval Dockyard, Mumbai.
  • NCC feat – 20 girl cadets crossed Parang La (5,578 m), linking Spiti with Ladakh's Changthang, in 'Aparajita-Parang La 2026'.
  • Colombo Security Conclave – table-top exercise at Visakhapatnam (9–12 September) on maritime terror, trafficking and illegal fishing.
Aero India: biennial air show of the Defence Ministry, first held in 1996 at Yelahanka; the 15th edition was in February 2025.

August 2026Monthly notes

Missiles and weapons

  • Agni-4 – the medium-range ballistic missile was test-fired on 6 August 2026 from the Integrated Test Range, Chandipur (Odisha), under the Strategic Forces Command.
  • Agni-4 facts – a two-stage solid-fuel, nuclear-capable missile with a range of about 4,000 km, guided by a ring laser gyro system.
  • Javelin – the Army signed a government-to-government deal with the US for about 100 Javelin anti-tank missiles and 25 light launch units; Tata Advanced Systems and the Lockheed Martin–RTX Javelin JV will co-produce it in India.
  • Sher – the first fully indigenous AK-203 rifle was test-fired at Korwa, Amethi; it is made by Indo-Russian Rifles Pvt Ltd and will replace the INSAS.
  • Bhargavastra – Solar Defence and Aerospace's indigenous anti-drone system was shown live to the Army at Nagpur; it can kill drone swarms 2.5–6 km away.
Strategic Forces Command: tri-service command set up in 2003 under the Nuclear Command Authority; it manages and operates India's nuclear-capable missiles such as the Agni series.

New ships and craft

  • INS Mangrol – the third anti-submarine warfare shallow water craft built by Cochin Shipyard was handed to the Navy on 21 August; it runs on waterjets.
  • Its name – taken from Mangrol, a fishing port in Junagadh district, Gujarat; it also revives the name of a minesweeper that served till 2004.
  • Nipun – a Nistar-class diving support vessel, second in its class, built by Hindustan Shipyard, was delivered at Visakhapatnam on 30 July; it will serve as mother ship for the Navy's DSRV, used to save crews of stricken submarines.
  • Samarthak – the first indigenous Multi-Purpose Vessel, built by L&T at Kattupalli, was delivered on 27 August.
  • Others – NGOPV Yard 3037 'Shruti' was launched at GRSE, Kolkata, on 11 August; Goa Shipyard handed fast patrol vessel ICGS Ajeet to the Coast Guard.

Army formations and support

  • Baaz Battalion – the Army raised its first battalion built around drones in Punjab; 11 Corps (Vajra Corps) is the first to operate it.
  • Drone lease – India is leasing two MQ-1B SeaGuardian drones from General Atomics for 30 months.
  • Armenia – India and Armenia signed an MoU on joint defence research and production.
  • Rescue test bed – a shore-based test plinth for the submarine rescue vehicle's launch and recovery system opened at Visakhapatnam on 19 August.
  • MIDHANI – became the first Indian firm to get GE Aerospace's 'S400 Lab Approval'.

Exercises

  • EOD exercise – the 8th India–US Navy explosive ordnance disposal exercise was held at the Southern Naval Command, Kochi, on 10–14 August.
  • Udara Shakti 2026 – the IAF and the Royal Malaysian Air Force trained together at Subang airbase.
  • Sahyog HOP TAC – the Indian Coast Guard and the Vietnam Coast Guard exercised off Chennai; Vietnam sent ship CSB 8002.
  • Tarang Shakti 2026 – the IAF's multinational air exercise is lined up at Jodhpur in September with the US, France, Australia, the UAE, Greece and others.
  • Pitch Black – the IAF joined over 20 air forces at the Australian-hosted exercise.
Tarang Shakti: India's biggest multinational air exercise, first held in 2024 in two phases at Sulur (Tamil Nadu) and Jodhpur (Rajasthan), hosted by the Indian Air Force.

Operations and global arms news

  • Operation Trishul – it helped bring 274 fugitives back to India from 36 countries over 2019–2026.
  • KAL drone – IG Defence's long-range one-way attack drone can fly about 1,000 km carrying 50 kg of explosives.
  • AIM-424 Malice – the US Navy unveiled this long-range air-to-air missile by Raytheon, with a range above 460 km.
  • Sher timeline – the AK-203 prototype is due for Army user trials from September 2026.

July 2026Monthly notes

New warships

  • INS Mahendragiri – commissioned into the Eastern Fleet by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh on 11 July 2026 at Visakhapatnam.
  • Class – the sixth of seven Project 17A (Nilgiri-class) stealth frigates, built by Mazagon Dock, Mumbai, with over 75% local content and CODOG propulsion.
  • Name – it is named after the Mahendragiri peak in the Eastern Ghats; the seventh and last ship, Vindhyagiri, is still to join.
  • INS Malvan – the second Mahe-class anti-submarine shallow water craft was commissioned on 22 July at Karwar; over 80% of it is indigenous.
  • Malvan facts – its motto is 'Silent Close' and its crest shows a tiger-claw weapon (baghnakh).
Project 17A: seven Nilgiri-class stealth frigates (four by Mazagon Dock, three by GRSE) designed by the Navy's Warship Design Bureau; follow-on of the Shivalik class (Project 17). Lead ship INS Nilgiri joined in January 2025.

Missiles, rockets and engines

  • Pinaka LRGR – DRDO tested the long-range guided rocket at ITR Chandipur on 8 July for its 60-km minimum range, firing it from an in-service Army launcher.
  • Makers – designed by ARDE, Pune, with HEMRL, supported by DRDL and RCI.
  • Kusha – DRDO made the maiden flight test of the Kusha long-range surface-to-air missile from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island, Odisha, on 23 July.
  • Kusha role – a home-grown air-defence system with three interceptor types (roughly 150, 250 and 350–400 km) against jets, cruise missiles, drones and AWACS-type aircraft.
  • Turbojet – DRDO's GTRE, with Azad Engineering of Hyderabad, built India's first indigenous 350-kg-thrust expendable turbojet engine for cruise missiles.
DRDO: Defence Research and Development Organisation, formed in 1958 under the Ministry of Defence; HQ New Delhi; runs around 50 laboratories.

Army units and expeditions

  • Baaz Battalion – Army chief Gen. Upendra Dwivedi announced this special drone battalion to boost battlefield intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance.
  • Samudra Pradakshina – the first round-the-world voyage by an all-women crew drawn from all three services ended on 22 July 2026.
  • Voyage facts – nine women officers covered roughly 25,500 nautical miles over 314 days aboard the 50-ft Army yacht IASV Triveni, which set out in September 2025.
  • AEW&C Mk-II – DRDO's CABS signed pacts to convert six Airbus A321 aircraft and named Adani Defence as a development-cum-production partner.
  • Jammers – the Defence Ministry ordered 20 advanced GNSS jammers for the Navy from Accord Software, Bengaluru, for Rs 449 crore.

Exercises and naval visits

  • Pitch Black 2026 – the Royal Australian Air Force's exercise ran in Darwin from 20 July to 7 August; the IAF sent Rafale, C-17 and IL-78 aircraft.
  • Southern Readiness – the Indian Navy hosted 'Operation Southern Readiness 26-2', a four-day multinational drill with the Combined Maritime Forces, at Kochi (20–23 July).
  • Tiger Lightning – a US–Bangladesh exercise held at Jalalabad Cantonment, Sylhet (19–31 July).
  • Sail250 – INS Sudarshini, a sail training ship, arrived in New York on 4 July to join the Sail4th 250 and International Naval Review 250 events.
  • Singapore visit – Eastern Fleet ships Udaygiri, Shakti and Kavaratti made a three-day call at Changi Naval Base.

Campaigns and operations

  • SHANTI – India's campaign for a 2028–29 UN Security Council seat was launched in New York on 13 July.
  • Drug-free youth – MY Bharat launched a 'Drug-Free Youth Campaign for a Viksit Bharat' for people aged 15–29, with contests in dance, music, street plays, slam poetry and reels.
  • Vande Mataram camp – MY Bharat held the first Vande Mataram Camp at Brainware University, Barasat (West Bengal), with 150 youth, celebrating the national song's 150th year.
  • Operation Hard Ball – a global crackdown by the FBI, the US Justice Department, Canada's RCMP and European agencies on organised crime groups linked to India.
  • Terror list – the Home Ministry listed 23 more people as individual terrorists under Section 35 of UAPA, taking the total to 80.
MY Bharat: 'Mera Yuva Bharat', an autonomous body of the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports launched on 31 October 2023 as a digital platform for youth volunteering.

June 2026Monthly notes

Missiles and air defence

  • LRLACM – DRDO flight-tested the Long-Range Land Attack Cruise Missile on 15 June from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island, Odisha; it carries forward the Nirbhay effort and was designed by ADE, Bengaluru.
  • Rudram-II – an air-launched missile for ground targets, test-fired over Chandipur; nodal lab Research Centre Imarat (RCI), Hyderabad.
  • BMD Phase-II – DRDO tested the AD-1 and AD-2 interceptors, able to hit targets both outside and inside the atmosphere.
  • S-400 – Russia delivered the fourth squadron under the 2018 deal for five units; the last is due in 2027.
  • Netra – DRDO's airborne early warning and control system, flown on Embraer jets, got Final Operational Clearance.

New ships and craft

  • Three in one day – on 21 June in Kolkata the PM commissioned INS Dunagiri (Project 17A stealth frigate), INS Sanshodhak (Survey Vessel Large) and INS Agray (anti-submarine shallow-water craft).
  • Who built them – all three were made by GRSE, Kolkata, to designs of the Navy's Warship Design Bureau; 21 June is also World Hydrography Day.
  • ICGS Akshay – a fast patrol vessel of the Adamya class, built by Goa Shipyard, joined the Coast Guard on 27 June.
  • Hovercraft – the first of six home-built air cushion vehicles, made by Chowgule & Company, entered Coast Guard service in Goa on 18 June.
  • Sea King – the Navy retired its Sea King Mk 42B helicopters at INS Shikra, Mumbai, on 14 June after 36 years.
Project 17A: seven Nilgiri-class stealth frigates – four from Mazagon Dock (Nilgiri, Udaygiri, Taragiri, Mahendragiri) and three from GRSE (Himgiri, Dunagiri, Vindhyagiri).

Land and air systems

  • Zorawar – the indigenous light tank of DRDO and L&T, about 25 tonnes, named after General Zorawar Singh; induction planned for 2028–29.
  • C-295 – Tata Advanced Systems and Airbus flew the first C-295 made in Vadodara; the IAF is getting 56 (16 from Spain, 40 built in India).
  • Drone hub – the Defence Ministry cleared a National Military Drone Technology Hub at IIT Kanpur, worth about Rs 500 crore.
  • Kamikaze drones – SMPP handed the Army 106 jet-powered loitering munitions with a range of about 180 km.
  • Capital Dome – name of the planned multi-layer air defence shield for Delhi-NCR.

Exercises, deployments and operations

  • Khaan Quest – the 23rd edition of this multinational peacekeeping drill ran in Mongolia from 20 June to 3 July; a Jat Regiment contingent represented India.
  • India–Australia – the second Defence Ministers' Dialogue (Rajnath Singh and Richard Marles) was held on 1 June; India will join Australia's Operation Render Safe for the first time.
  • Ship visits – INS Tarkash reached Victoria, Seychelles, escorting the refitted PS Zoroaster; INS Sharda trained Sri Lankan sailors in Colombo.
  • Operation Chakravyuh – a DRI drive against narcotics in Kerala.
  • Operation DRISHTI – Army and Air Force eye surgeons held a free surgical camp at Military Hospital, Namkum (Ranchi).

Campaigns

  • PMSMA at 10 – J.P. Nadda launched a nationwide drive on 9 June for ten years of the Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan; a Rs 75 coin was released.
  • Swachh Gaon, Surakshit Jalvayu – a 1–5 June drive by the Jal Shakti and Environment Ministries.
  • Drug disposal – an online fortnight-long drive aims to destroy about 2.09 lakh kg of seized narcotics.
  • Nasha Mukt Bharat Saptah – observed from 17 to 26 June.
PMSMA: Health Ministry programme begun in 2016 to give free antenatal check-ups to pregnant women on the 9th of every month.

May 2026Monthly notes

Missile and weapon tests

  • Agni MIRV – DRDO flight-tested an advanced Agni missile with MIRV technology from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island, Odisha, on 8 May.
  • MIRV – one missile carries several warheads aimed at different targets; it was first shown in Mission Divyastra (Agni-5, March 2024).
  • Agni-1 – the short-range ballistic missile was test-fired from ITR Chandipur on 22 May under the Strategic Forces Command.
  • TARA – Tactical Advanced Range Augmentation, India's first home-made glide-weapon kit from RCI Hyderabad, had its first trial with the IAF off Odisha on 7 May.
  • Scramjet – DRDL Hyderabad ran an actively cooled full-scale scramjet combustor for over 1,200 seconds on 9 May, beating its 700-second run of January.

New ships and weapons

  • Mahendragiri – the sixth Nilgiri-class (Project 17A) stealth frigate, built by Mazagon Dock, Mumbai, was delivered on 30 April.
  • Sanghamitra – GRSE, Kolkata, launched Yard 3039, a Next Generation Offshore Patrol Vessel, on 20 May; 11 such ships are being built at GSL and GRSE.
  • ICGS Achal – the Coast Guard commissioned this Adamya-class fast patrol vessel at Goa Shipyard on 9 May.
  • ULPGM-V3 – DRDO tested its drone-launched precision guided missile near Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, in air-to-ground and air-to-air modes.
  • Rocket launcher – a 300-km 'Universal Rocket Launching System', India's first of this range, was put on show.

Exercises and talks

  • CINBAX-II – the armies of India and Cambodia trained at Camp Basil, Kampong Speu Province, Cambodia, on 4–17 May.
  • Contingent – 120 Indian soldiers, mostly from the Maratha Light Infantry; focus on counter-terrorism in UN peacekeeping.
  • PRAGATI 2026 – Partnership of Regional Armies for Growth and Transformation in the Indian Ocean Region, held at Umroi, Meghalaya.
  • Talks – the 10th India–Australia Defence Policy Talks (8 May) and the 16th India–Singapore Defence Policy Dialogue took place.

Navy outreach

  • IOS SAGAR – INS Sunayna ended its Indian Ocean Ship SAGAR voyage at Kochi on 20 May with 38 sailors from partner nations on board.
  • Port calls – Yangon (5 May) and Chattogram (8 May), with a passage exercise alongside Bangladesh's BNS Protoy.
  • Sagardhwani – the oceanographic research ship visited Cam Ranh, Vietnam, on 4–8 May.
  • Sudarshini – the sail training ship made its first Atlantic crossing and reached Antigua.

Operations and anniversaries

  • Operation RAGEPILL – the NCB made India's first seizure of Captagon: about 227.7 kg worth Rs 182 crore; a Syrian national was arrested.
  • Where – 31.5 kg of tablets hidden in a chapati machine at Neb Sarai, Delhi (meant for Jeddah) and 196.2 kg of powder at Mundra Port in a container from Syria.
  • Operation WHITE STRIKE – the NCB also seized 349 kg of cocaine worth about Rs 1,745 crore in the Mumbai area.
  • Sindoor at one – the first anniversary of Operation Sindoor was marked by a Joint Commanders' Conference at Jaipur.
  • Seoul memorial – Rajnath Singh opened the Indian War Memorial at Imjingak Park, Seoul, on 21 May, honouring India's Korean War medics of 60 Parachute Field Ambulance.
Captagon: an amphetamine-type stimulant pill trafficked mostly in West Asia and called the 'jihadi drug'; in India it is dealt with under the NDPS Act, 1985; the NCB is an MHA agency.

April 2026Monthly notes

Nuclear submarine and strategic ships

  • INS Aridhaman – India's third nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN) was quietly commissioned at Visakhapatnam on 3 April.
  • Class – it is an Arihant-class boat, joining INS Arihant and INS Arighaat.
  • Deterrence – three SSBNs let India keep one on patrol at all times; the boat can carry the 3,500-km K-4 missile.
  • INS Dhruv – India's first dedicated missile-tracking and ocean surveillance ship, built by Hindustan Shipyard with DRDO and NTRO, was deployed in the Arabian Sea.
  • RELOS – India and Russia put into effect their Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement for military support.

Joint exercises

  • Dustlik – the 7th India–Uzbekistan exercise was held at Gurumsaray Field Training Area, Namangan, on 12–25 April.
  • Meaning – 'Dustlik' is Uzbek for friendship; the first edition was at Tashkent in 2019 and the 2025 edition at Aundh, Pune.
  • Cyclone-IV – India and Egypt held their special forces exercise at Anshas, Egypt.
  • Dweep Shakti – a tri-service drill under the Andaman and Nicobar Command to defend island territories.
  • DIVEX – INS Nireekshak took part in the 4th India–Sri Lanka diving exercise at Colombo (21–27 April) and handed over two Aarogya Maitri health cubes.

Navy at sea

  • IOS SAGAR – MoS Defence Sanjay Seth flagged off INS Sunayna as Indian Ocean Ship SAGAR from Mumbai on 2 April.
  • Crew – personnel from 16 friendly foreign countries sailed with the Indian crew, under Vision MAHASAGAR.
  • Port calls – Malé, Phuket, Jakarta, Singapore, Yangon, Chittagong and Colombo.
  • INS Sudarshini – the sail training ship reached Casablanca, Morocco, on 15 April during its voyage 'Lokayan 26'.
  • INS Trikand – the guided-missile frigate called at Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, on 3 April.
MAHASAGAR: Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions – India's maritime vision announced in 2025, building on SAGAR (2015).

New ships, guns and exports

  • Dunagiri – a Project 17A (Nilgiri-class) stealth frigate built by GRSE, Kolkata, was handed to the Navy on 30 March.
  • Sanshodhak – the fourth and last Survey Vessel (Large), also from GRSE, joined the Navy on 30 March.
  • Malvan – the second anti-submarine shallow water craft from Cochin Shipyard, named after the coastal town linked to Shivaji's navy.
  • Prahaar LMG – Adani Defence and Israel Weapon Industries handed the first batch of locally made Prahaar light machine guns to the Army.
  • Exports – defence exports rose about 63% to Rs 38,424 crore in FY 2025-26.

Campaigns

  • Poshan Pakhwada – the 8th edition (9–23 April) by the Women and Child Development Ministry focused on brain growth in the first six years.
  • Nasha Mukt Bharat – the Social Justice Ministry launched the NMBA 2.0 app to track the anti-drug drive, which began on 15 August 2020.
  • Naxal-free – Home Minister Amit Shah told the Lok Sabha that Left-Wing Extremism had been ended by 31 March 2026.
  • Nari Shakti Vandan Run – runs in seven cities on 17–18 April by the Youth Affairs Ministry.
  • Sadhana Saptah – DoPT's Karmayogi capacity-building week (2–10 April) launched the UNNATI portal for training institutes.

March 2026Monthly notes

Operations in the Iran war

  • Epic Fury – the US operation against Iran, started by US Central Command on 28 Feb 2026.
  • Lion's Roar – Israel's parallel campaign (also called Roaring Lion) on Iran's leaders and nuclear sites.
  • True Promise 4 – Iran's IRGC hit back with missile and drone waves on Israel and US bases in the Gulf.
  • IRIS Dena – the Iranian frigate, returning from IFR and MILAN 2026 at Visakhapatnam, was sunk by a US submarine torpedo off Galle, Sri Lanka, on 4 March.
  • Rescue – Sri Lanka led the search; India sent a P-8I aircraft and the sail ship INS Tarangini.

Navy guards Indian shipping

  • Sankalp – Indian warships escorted Indian-flagged tankers such as Shivalik and Nanda Devi out of the Gulf of Oman.
  • Since 2019 – Operation Sankalp began on 19 June 2019 to protect Indian merchant ships in the Gulf.
  • Urja Suraksha – a new Indian Navy mission from 25 March 2026, with over five destroyers and frigates in the Gulf of Oman, keeps energy cargo moving.
  • Port of Refuge – APSEZ set up India's first refuge ports for ships in distress at Dighi (Maharashtra) and Gopalpur (Odisha).
  • Supply group – an Inter-Ministerial Group on supply chain resilience began work on 2 March 2026.

Joint exercises

  • LAMITIYE-2026 – the 11th India–Seychelles drill at the Seychelles Defence Academy, 9–20 March, was the first with all three Indian services.
  • Meaning – 'Lamitiye' is Creole for 'friendship'; the biennial drill has been held in Seychelles since 2001; INS Trikand and a C-130 joined.
  • Amogh Jwala – Southern Command's multi-domain drill at the Babina field firing range, Jhansi.
  • IMEX TTX – the Navy hosted the IONS table-top maritime exercise at Kochi with 13 Indian Ocean nations.
  • Sea Dragon – the US-led anti-submarine drill off Guam had India, Australia, Japan and New Zealand.

Deals, ships and voyages

  • ALH Mk-III – a Rs 2,901 crore deal with HAL for six maritime-role Advanced Light Helicopters for the Coast Guard (3 March).
  • SHTIL – Russia's Rosoboronexport got a Rs 2,182 crore order for vertical-launch SHTIL air-defence missiles for warships.
  • Fuel barges – Shoft Shipyard, a Thane MSME, will build four 500-tonne fuel barges for the Navy.
  • Kaundinya – INSV Kaundinya, a stitched sailing ship built the ancient way, returned to Mumbai on 2 March after sailing Porbandar to Muscat.
  • ADC-150 – DRDO and the Navy tested an air-dropped container for up to 150 kg of supplies from a P-8I off Goa; NSTL Visakhapatnam designed it.

Campaigns
