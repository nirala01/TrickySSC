Boat & Stream

Formulas, tricks and traps on one page · Quantitative Aptitude

A boat travels faster downstream and slower upstream. Treat the current as something you add or subtract — then it is ordinary speed, time and distance.

1. Key formulas

Let boat speed in still water = u, stream speed = v.

QuantityFormula
Downstream speedu + v
Upstream speedu − v
Boat speed u(D + U)/2
Stream speed v(D − U)/2
Same distance, time ratio down : up(u − v) : (u + v)
Average speed, up and down same distance(u² − v²)/u
Round trip of distance d, total time2ud/(u² − v²)

D = downstream speed, U = upstream speed.

2. Shortcuts that save time

Speed ratio shortcut If the downstream and upstream times for the same distance are in the ratio a : b, then u : v = (b + a) : (b − a).
  • When the boat travels the same distance both ways in total T hours, let upstream time be t₁ and downstream t₂ and use D = U × t₁ = D × t₂.
  • If no stream is mentioned, v = 0 and the question is plain speed–time–distance.

3. Solved examples

Example 1 A boat goes 30 km downstream in 2 hours and 18 km upstream in 2 hours. D = 15, U = 9. u = (15 + 9)/2 = 12 km/h, v = (15 − 9)/2 = 3 km/h.
Example 2 Boat speed 10 km/h, stream 2 km/h. Time to go 24 km down and back = 24/12 + 24/8 = 2 + 3 = 5 hours. Check: 2 × 10 × 24/(100 − 4) = 480/96 = 5 ✓.
Example 3 Time upstream is twice time downstream for the same distance. Ratio of down : up times = 1 : 2, so u : v = (2 + 1) : (2 − 1) = 3 : 1.

4. Traps to avoid

  1. Downstream is u + v, upstream is u − v — never swap them.
  2. Average speed for a round trip is not u; it is lower.
  3. For a boat and a floating log, the log moves at the speed of the stream alone.
  4. Check units: km/h versus m/s.
