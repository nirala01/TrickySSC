SSC CHSL Tier 2 Maths Previous Year Questions

Mathematical Abilities is Module I of Section I in CHSL Tier 2: thirty questions at +3 and −1, sharing an hour with Reasoning. It goes deeper than Tier 1 — more algebra, geometry, trigonometry and data interpretation — so it is the module where time planning matters most.

Solving the Maths questions paper by paper shows you what SSC actually repeats. Across shifts of the same cycle the question types barely change — only the numbers, words and facts do — so after a dozen papers you will recognise most questions within seconds. That recognition is what turns a 30-minute section into a comfortable one.

What the Tier II Maths section contains

AreaWhat gets asked
ArithmeticPercentage, ratio, profit and loss, interest, time and work, speed and distance — often two steps
Algebra and geometryIdentities, triangles, circles, coordinate geometry
Mensuration and trigonometry2D and 3D figures; heights, identities and values
Data interpretation and statisticsTables and charts; mean, median, mode

How to practise with these tests

Work newest first: the most recent shifts show the current pattern. Attempt each test on the timer, then read every solution — including the ones you got right, because the shortcut in the solution is often faster than your method. Keep an error log, and after every five tests check which topic keeps appearing in it.

When you can finish these tests with time to spare, move to the full Tier II papers on the SSC CHSL previous year papers page, where the Maths section has to share the clock with the other subjects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the SSC CHSL Tier 2 Maths previous year questions free?

Yes. Every Maths test on this page is free to attempt online. You only need a free TrickySSC account so that your score and attempted status are saved.

Which questions does each test contain?

Each test holds only the Mathematical Abilities questions (Q1–30) of one real SSC CHSL Tier II shift — 30 questions, exactly as they appeared in that paper.

How much time and what marking does each test use?

30 minutes, with +3 / −1 marking, matching the Mathematical Abilities section of the real SSC CHSL Tier II paper.

Can I attempt these questions in Hindi?
