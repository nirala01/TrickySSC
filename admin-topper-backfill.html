Topper backfill

Fills the Topper row on the solutions screen for papers that were attempted before it existed. Step 1 reads every saved attempt once and finds the best attempt per paper (higher marks, then less time). Step 2 writes it. Only attempts from 5 Sep 2026 on can be used, because older ones did not save per-question answers. Running it again is safe; it simply rewrites the same toppers.
