October 2026Monthly notes

First week of October

  • 1 October – International Day of Older Persons, set by the UN General Assembly in 1990.
  • 2 October – Gandhi Jayanti (157th) and the UN's International Day of Non-Violence (since 2007); also Lal Bahadur Shastri's birth anniversary.
  • 2–8 October – National Wildlife Week.
  • 4 October – World Animal Day.
  • 5 October – World Teachers' Day, marking the 1966 ILO–UNESCO Recommendation on teachers; held since 1994.

Second week of October

  • 9 October – World Post Day, the day the Universal Postal Union was founded at Bern in 1874.
  • 10 October – World Mental Health Day; India's Tele-MANAS helpline (14416) began on this day in 2022.
  • 11 October – International Day of the Girl Child; cricketer Smriti Mandhana was named a UNICEF India celebrity advocate.
  • 13 October – International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction.
  • 14 October – World Standards Day; BIS unveiled a Silver Refinery Scheme.

Mid-October

  • 15 October – World Students' Day, Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam's birth anniversary (95th); also Global Handwashing Day and the International Day of Rural Women.
  • 16 October – World Food Day, FAO's founding day (1945); 2026 theme 'Innovate today. Nourish tomorrow.'
  • 17 October – International Day for the Eradication of Poverty.
  • 20 October – National Solidarity Day, honouring soldiers of the 1962 war.
  • 21 October – Police Commemoration Day.
Hot Springs, 1959: on 21 October 1959 Chinese troops ambushed a CRPF patrol at Hot Springs in Ladakh; 10 policemen died. Police Commemoration Day honours them and all police martyrs.

End of October

  • 24 October – United Nations Day; the UN Charter came into force on this day in 1945 (81st anniversary in 2026).
  • 24 October – ITBP Raising Day (force raised in 1962) and World Polio Day.
  • 27 October – World Day for Audiovisual Heritage.
  • 29 October – World Stroke Day.
  • 31 October – Rashtriya Ekta Diwas, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's 151st birth anniversary; also World Cities Day.
Rashtriya Ekta Diwas: observed since 2014 on Patel's birthday (born 1875, Nadiad, Gujarat); his 182 m Statue of Unity at Kevadia was unveiled on 31 October 2018.

September 2026Monthly notes

First week of September

  • 2 September – World Coconut Day; 2026 theme on coconuts securing food, energy and livelihoods in uncertain times.
  • 5 September – Teachers' Day, birth anniversary of Dr S. Radhakrishnan (born 1888), India's second President.
  • 6 September – International Vulture Awareness Day; MoEFCC marked it at Panchkula, home of Asia's first vulture breeding centre.
  • 7 September – International Day of Clean Air for Blue Skies, themed 'Clean Air for Climate Action'; India calls it Swachh Vayu Diwas.
  • 8 September – International Literacy Day, observed by UNESCO since 1967; 2026 was its 60th year.

Second week

  • 11 September – National Forest Martyrs Day, recalling the Khejarli sacrifice of 1730 led by Amrita Devi Bishnoi to save Khejri trees.
  • 14 September – Hindi Diwas – on this day in 1949 the Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari as the Union's official language.
  • 15 September – Engineers' Day – Sir M. Visvesvaraya was born on this day in 1861.
  • 15 September – International Day of Democracy.
  • 16 September – World Ozone Day, marking the signing of the Montreal Protocol.
Montreal Protocol: signed on 16 September 1987 to phase out ozone-depleting substances; its Kigali Amendment (2016) targets HFCs.

Third week

  • 17 September – World Patient Safety Day; a national patient safety pledge was taken in India.
  • 17 September – Vishwakarma Jayanti, devoted to Lord Vishwakarma.
  • 18 September – World Bamboo Day, themed 'Bamboo Beyond Borders'.
  • 21 September – International Day of Peace.
  • 22 September – World Rhino Day, themed 'Keep the Five Alive' for the five rhino species.

End of September

  • 23 September – the September equinox – day and night are nearly equal; autumn begins in the northern hemisphere.
  • 23 September – the 11th Ayurveda Day; the main national event was at Nagpur.
  • 27 September – World Tourism Day; India unveiled a National Digital Tourism Stack along with Dekho Apna Desh 2.0.
  • 28 September – World Rabies Day, the death anniversary of Louis Pasteur, who made the first successful rabies vaccine.
  • 28 September – birth anniversary of Lata Mangeshkar; MP's national award in her name is given on this day.
Ayurveda Day: first observed in 2016; earlier tied to Dhanteras, it has been held on a fixed date, 23 September, since 2025.

August 2026Monthly notes

First week of August

  • 1–7 August – World Breastfeeding Week; the 2026 theme is 'Breastfeeding for a Sustainable Start in Life: Strengthen What Works'.
  • 3 August – Indian Organ Donation Day, its 16th edition; it recalls India's first heart transplant at AIIMS, New Delhi, in 1994.
  • 4 August – International Clouded Leopard Day; the cat is 'Vulnerable' on the IUCN list and is Meghalaya's state animal.
  • 7 August – National Handloom Day, recalling the Swadeshi Movement launched on 7 August 1905.
  • 9 August – ICoAS Day; the Indian Cost Accounts Service is a Group A service of the Department of Expenditure; 2026 theme on cost optimisation for Atmanirbhar Bharat.
National Handloom Day: first observed on 7 August 2015, when PM Modi launched it in Chennai; the date marks the Swadeshi Movement begun at Calcutta Town Hall in 1905 against the partition of Bengal.

Around mid-August

  • 10 August – World Lion Day; Asiatic lions live in the wild only in and around Gir, Gujarat.
  • 12 August – World Elephant Day; India held its national event in Visakhapatnam; the elephant is India's National Heritage Animal.
  • 12 August – International Youth Day; 2026 theme 'Different Contexts, Common Aspirations'; first marked in 2000 after a UN decision of 1999.
  • 14 August – Partition Horrors Remembrance Day, observed since 2021 to remember those who suffered in 1947.
  • 15 August – India celebrated its 80th Independence Day.

Third week of August

  • 19 August – World Humanitarian Day, recalling the 2003 bombing of the UN office in Baghdad that killed 22 aid workers.
  • 20 August – Sadbhavana Diwas (Harmony Day), the birth anniversary of former PM Rajiv Gandhi (born 1944).
  • 21 August – World Senior Citizens Day, to honour older people and their needs.
  • 23 August – the third National Space Day; 2026 theme 'Towards Viksit Bharat: Innovating, Collaborating and Aspiring to Global Space Leadership'.
  • Space Day host – the main 2026 event was held at Ahmedabad, hosted by ISRO's Space Applications Centre.
National Space Day: marks the soft landing of Chandrayaan-3's Vikram lander near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023, at a spot named Shiv Shakti Point; first observed on 23 August 2024.

End of August

  • 23–27 August – World Water Week in Stockholm, run by the Stockholm International Water Institute (SIWI); India was represented by the Rural Development Ministry.
  • 29 August – National Sports Day, the birth anniversary of hockey wizard Major Dhyan Chand (born 1905 in Allahabad).
  • 29 August – also the UN's International Day against Nuclear Tests, observed since 2010.
  • 30 August – International Day of the Victims of Enforced Disappearances.

July 2026Monthly notes

Early July

  • 1 July – National Doctors' Day, the birth and death anniversary of Dr B.C. Roy; 2026 theme 'Behind the Mask: Who Heals the Healers?'. It is also Chartered Accountants Day (ICAI, 1949).
  • 3 July – International Plastic Bag Free Day.
  • 4 July – International Day of Cooperatives, which falls on July's first Saturday; 2026 theme 'Cooperatives for a Peaceful World'.
  • 6 July – World Zoonoses Day, recalling Louis Pasteur's first rabies vaccination in 1885; 2026 theme 'One World One Health: Prevent Zoonoses'.
  • 1–7 July – Van Mahotsav week, started at the national level in 1950 by K.M. Munshi.

Mid July

  • 11 July – World Population Day, first marked in 1989 after the world's population reached 5 billion on 11 July 1987.
  • 15 July – World Youth Skills Day, observed since 2015; 2026 theme 'Skills for a Shared Future'.
  • 17 July – World Day for International Justice, marking the adoption of the Rome Statute of the ICC in 1998.
  • 18 July – Nelson Mandela International Day, on Mandela's birthday; first observed in 2010.
  • 20 July – International Chess Day, the founding day of FIDE in Paris in 1924.

Late July

  • 22 July – National Flag Adoption Day; the Constituent Assembly adopted the Tiranga on 22 July 1947. The same date is National Mango Day.
  • 23 July – National Broadcasting Day, marking the start of organised radio broadcasting from Bombay in 1927.
  • 24 July – Income Tax Day, recalling the first income tax brought in by James Wilson in 1860.
  • 26 July – Kargil Vijay Diwas, for India's 1999 victory in the Kargil war.
  • 27 July – CRPF Day; the force was raised on 27 July 1939 as the Crown Representative's Police.
Kargil Vijay Diwas: marks 26 July 1999, when the Army completed Operation Vijay to clear Pakistani intruders from the heights of Kargil, Ladakh; the IAF's part was Operation Safed Sagar.

End of July

  • 28 July – World Hepatitis Day, the birthday of Baruch Blumberg; 2026 theme 'Hepatitis: Let's Break It Down'; India runs the National Viral Hepatitis Control Programme (2018).
  • 28 July – World Nature Conservation Day.
  • 29 July – International Tiger Day, agreed at the St Petersburg Tiger Summit in 2010; the Royal Bengal tiger is India's national animal.
  • 30 July – World Day against Trafficking in Persons; 2026 theme 'Trapped Behind the Scam'.

June 2026Monthly notes

Early June

  • 3 June – World Bicycle Day; India ran the Asmita Khelo India women's cycling league across 200 cities.
  • 5 June – World Environment Day hosted by Azerbaijan (Baku); theme 'Inspired by Nature. For Climate. For Our Future'; India got its 100th Ramsar site.
  • 8 June – World Oceans Day and World Brain Tumour Day.
  • 12 June – World Day Against Child Labour, theme 'Red card to child labour'.
  • 14 June – World Blood Donor Day, on the birthday of Karl Landsteiner, who found the ABO blood groups.

Mid June

  • 15 June – Global Wind Day; MNRE marked it with a conference in Goa.
  • 17 June – Day to Combat Desertification and Drought, theme 'Rangelands: Recognize. Respect. Restore.'; India issued its second Bonn Challenge progress report.
  • 19 June – World Sickle Cell Day; President Murmu attended the national event at Omkareshwar, Madhya Pradesh.
  • 21 June – 12th International Day of Yoga, theme 'Yoga for Healthy Ageing', with the main event at Red Road, Kolkata.
  • 21 June – also World Hydrography Day.
Bonn Challenge: global effort started in 2011 to restore 350 million hectares of degraded land by 2030; India's pledge is 26 million hectares.

Late June

  • 26 June – International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking.
  • 27 June – UN MSME Day; India held 'Udyami Bharat' in New Delhi.
  • 29 June – 20th National Statistics Day, birth anniversary of P.C. Mahalanobis; theme on using administrative data.
  • 30 June – Hul Divas, recalling the Santhal rebellion of 1855 led by the brothers Sidho and Kanhu Murmu.

May 2026Monthly notes

Early May

  • 1 May – International Labour Day; also Maharashtra and Gujarat Day (both formed in 1960); Buddha Purnima fell on this date in 2026.
  • 3 May – World Press Freedom Day; RSF released its press freedom index just before it.
  • 8 May – World Red Cross and Red Crescent Day, the birth anniversary of Henry Dunant.
  • 11 May – National Technology Day, marking the Pokhran-II nuclear tests of 1998.
  • 12 May – International Nurses Day, Florence Nightingale's birth anniversary; the national nursing awards are given that day.

Mid May

  • 16 May – Sikkim Statehood Day (51st in 2026) and National Dengue Day.
  • 16 May – International Day of Light, recalling Theodore Maiman's first laser in 1960.
  • 18 May – International Museum Day (ICOM); 2026 theme 'Museums Uniting a Divided World'.
  • 20 May – World Metrology Day, theme 'Metrology: Building Trust in Policy Making'; it marks the 1875 Metre Convention; the e-Maap portal was launched.
  • 20 May – World Bee Day, birth anniversary of Slovenian beekeeper Anton Janša.

Late May

  • 21 May – National Anti-Terrorism Day, the death anniversary of Rajiv Gandhi (killed at Sriperumbudur in 1991).
  • 22 May – International Day for Biological Diversity; theme 'Acting Locally for Global Impact'; the national event was in Madhya Pradesh.
  • 30 May – Hindi Journalism Day; Udant Martand, the first Hindi paper, began in Calcutta on 30 May 1826 under Jugal Kishore Shukla.
  • 31 May – World No Tobacco Day of WHO; theme 'Unmasking the appeal – countering nicotine and tobacco addiction'.

April 2026Monthly notes

First week of April

  • 1 April – Utkal Divas, marking the formation of Odisha as a separate province in 1936.
  • 1 April – RBI Foundation Day; the bank began work in 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934.
  • 2 April – World Autism Awareness Day.
  • 5 April – National Maritime Day, recalling SS Loyalty's voyage to London in 1919.
  • 7 April – World Health Day, WHO's founding day; 2026 theme 'Together for health. Stand with science.'

Second week of April

  • 10 April – World Homoeopathy Day, on Samuel Hahnemann's birth anniversary.
  • 11 April – National Safe Motherhood Day, on Kasturba Gandhi's birth anniversary.
  • 12 April – International Day of Human Space Flight, for Yuri Gagarin's flight in 1961.
  • 13 April – Siachen Day, for the launch of Operation Meghdoot in 1984.
  • 14 April – Ambedkar Jayanti, birth anniversary of Dr B.R. Ambedkar (born 1891).

Third week of April

  • 16 April – World Voice Day.
  • 17 April – World Haemophilia Day.
  • 18 April – World Heritage Day (International Day for Monuments and Sites).
  • 19 April – World Liver Day.
  • 21 April – Civil Services Day; it recalls Sardar Patel's 1947 address to officers and was first observed in 2006.

Last part of April

  • 22 April – Earth Day; 2026 theme 'Our Power, Our Planet'.
  • 23 April – World Book and Copyright Day; the 2026 World Book Capital is Rabat (Morocco).
  • 24 April – National Panchayati Raj Day, for the 73rd Amendment taking effect in 1993; theme 'Sashakt Panchayat, Sarvangeen Vikas'.
  • 25 April – World Malaria Day; 2026 theme 'Driven to End Malaria: Now We Can. Now We Must.'
  • 30 April – Ayushman Bharat Day; the scheme was launched in 2018.
World Book Capital: a UNESCO title held for a year from 23 April; Rabat is the 26th city to hold it.

March 2026Monthly notes

Early March

  • 3 March – World Wildlife Day; 2026 theme on medicinal and aromatic plants for health, heritage and livelihoods.
  • 4 March – National Safety Day, for the founding of the National Safety Council in 1966.
  • 7 March – Jan Aushadhi Diwas, for low-cost generic medicines under PMBJP.
  • 8 March – International Women's Day; UN theme 'Rights. Justice. Action. For ALL Women and Girls'.
  • 10 March – CISF Raising Day; the force was set up in 1969.

Mid March

  • 12 March – World Kidney Day, held on the second Thursday of March.
  • 13 March – World Sleep Day; theme 'Sleep Well, Live Better'.
  • 15 March – World Consumer Rights Day; theme 'Safe Products, Confident Consumers'.
  • 20 March – International Day of Happiness, World Oral Health Day and World Sparrow Day.
  • 21 March – International Day of Forests (theme 'Forests and Economies'), World Poetry Day (UNESCO, 1999), Nowruz and World Puppetry Day.

Late March

  • 22 March – World Water Day, theme 'Water and Gender'; also Bihar Diwas.
  • 23 March – World Meteorological Day, for the WMO founded in 1950; theme 'Observing Today, Protecting Tomorrow'.
  • 23 March – Shaheed Diwas, for Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev, hanged in 1931.
  • 24 March – World TB Day, for Robert Koch's discovery of the TB germ in 1882.
  • 27 March – World Theatre Day, begun by the International Theatre Institute.
World Water Day: a UN day held since 1993 and coordinated by UN-Water; the UN World Water Development Report is released around it.

February 2026Monthly notes

Early February

  • 1 Feb – Indian Coast Guard Day; the force was raised in 1977.
  • 2 Feb – World Wetlands Day – theme 'Wetlands and Traditional Knowledge: Celebrating Cultural Heritage'.
  • 4 Feb – World Cancer Day – theme 'United by Unique' (2025 to 2027).
  • 10 Feb – World Pulses Day.
  • 11 Feb – World Unani Day, on the birth anniversary of Hakim Ajmal Khan; also Day of Women and Girls in Science.

Mid February

  • 12 Feb – National Productivity Day, on the founding of the National Productivity Council (1958).
  • 13 Feb – World Radio Day – theme 'Radio and Artificial Intelligence'.
  • 13 Feb (India) – National Women's Day, on the birth anniversary of Sarojini Naidu.
  • 20 Feb – World Day of Social Justice.
  • 20 Feb (India) – statehood day of Arunachal Pradesh and Mizoram (1987).

Late February

  • 21 Feb – International Mother Language Day – theme 'Youth Voices on Multilingual Education'; proclaimed by UNESCO in 1999.
  • 24 Feb – Central Excise Day.
  • 28 Feb – National Science Day, marking C.V. Raman's discovery of the Raman Effect (1928).
  • 2026 theme – 'Women in Science: Catalyzing Viksit Bharat'.
  • Last day of Feb – Rare Disease Day.
International Mother Language Day: honours those who died in Dhaka on 21 Feb 1952 for the Bengali language; first observed in 2000.

January 2026Monthly notes

Days in the first half of January

  • 9 Jan – Pravasi Bharatiya Divas – diaspora day; recalls Gandhi's return from South Africa on 9 Jan 1915.
  • 10 Jan – World Hindi Day – theme 'Hindi: From Traditional Knowledge to Artificial Intelligence'; recalls the first World Hindi Conference, Nagpur, 1975.
  • 12 Jan – National Youth Day – Swami Vivekananda's birthday; theme 'Ignite the Self, Impact the World'; Chetna Mahotsav held at Bilaspur.
  • 15 Jan – Army Day – K.M. Cariappa took command on 15 Jan 1949; 2026 parade held in Rajasthan.
  • 16 Jan – National Startup Day – celebrates the start-up ecosystem.

Days in the second half of January

  • 19 Jan – NDRF Raising Day – for the National Disaster Response Force.
  • 23 Jan – Parakram Diwas – Netaji's birth anniversary, observed since 2021.
  • 24 Jan – National Girl Child Day and International Day of Education – education theme 'The Power of Youth in Co-creating Education'.
  • 25 Jan – National Voters' Day (16th) and National Tourism Day – voters' theme 'My India, My Vote'.

Facts behind the days

  • Voters' Day – marks ECI's founding on 25 Jan 1950 (Article 324); observed since 2011; tagline 'Citizen at the Heart of Indian Democracy'.
  • Hindi Day – official since 2006.
  • Beating Retreat – held on 29 Jan at Vijay Chowk to close Republic Day events.
  • UN year – 2026 is the International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists.
