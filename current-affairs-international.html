October 2026Monthly notes

Swiss President in Delhi

  • Guy Parmelin – Switzerland's President paid his first state visit to India on 5–7 October 2026, at PM Modi's invitation.
  • Welcome – ceremonial reception at Rashtrapati Bhavan; talks with the PM at Hyderabad House, where MoUs were exchanged.
  • Summit – he joined the 2nd India–EFTA Prosperity Summit in New Delhi on 7 October.
  • Anniversary – the trip marked one year of the India–EFTA TEPA, which took effect on 1 October 2025.
  • Top partner – within EFTA, Switzerland is India's biggest trade and investment partner.
EFTA: European Free Trade Association, formed in 1960; members – Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein; secretariat in Geneva. Under TEPA the bloc has pledged US$100 billion of investment and 1 million jobs in India over 15 years.

Visitors and trade talks

  • Poland – PM Donald Tusk was in India on 21–23 October; the two sides want to double trade by 2030.
  • Liechtenstein – President Murmu hosted Hereditary Prince Alois and Princess Sophie on 27 October.
  • Mexico – India and Mexico opened negotiations for a Preferential Trade Agreement and signed its Terms of Reference (28 October).
  • New Zealand FTA – live from 20 October 2026; New Zealand scraps duties on every tariff line for Indian goods and will facilitate US$20 billion of investment.
  • NZ visas – 5,000 temporary employment visas and 1,000 working holiday visas a year; dairy, sugar and edible oils are kept outside the deal.

Brazil vote and a river treaty

  • Lula da Silva – won Brazil's presidential runoff on 25 October with about 51.8%, securing a fourth term.
  • Rival – Senator Flávio Bolsonaro of the Liberal Party, son of ex-President Jair Bolsonaro, got about 48.2%.
  • Comeback – Lula trailed in the first round (4 October); no one had won from second place since direct polls returned in 1989.
  • Ganga Waters Treaty – the India–Bangladesh pact signed on 12 December 1996 for 30 years runs out on 12 December 2026.
  • Farakka – the treaty governs lean-season sharing at the Farakka Barrage in West Bengal; Bangladesh wants it renewed.

UN resolutions on maps and children

  • Correct the Map – UNGA adopted it on 4 September 2026 by 164 votes to 1, with 6 abstentions.
  • Lone 'no' – the United States; the text is non-binding.
  • Aim – push equal-area maps such as the Equal Earth projection (designed in 2018) instead of the 1569 Mercator map, which makes Africa look small.
  • Mover – Togo, for the African Group of 54 states, backed by the African Union.
  • Child marriage day – UNGA also named 27 November the International Day for the Elimination of Child, Early and Forced Marriage, on a Sierra Leone-led text co-sponsored by India.

Hunger, law and passports

  • Global Hunger Index 2026 – out on 14 October; India 100th of 122 with a score of 24.1 ('serious'), tied with Ethiopia.
  • Neighbours – Sri Lanka (53), Nepal (72) and Bangladesh (84) did better; Pakistan was 106th.
  • Rule of Law Index 2026 – the World Justice Project put India 88th of 143; Denmark led.
  • Henley Passport Index – October edition: India 84th with visa-free entry to 55 places; Singapore first.
  • Living Planet Report 2026 – WWF and ZSL recorded a 73% average fall in tracked wildlife populations between 1970 and 2022.
Global Hunger Index: brought out by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany); scores use four indicators – undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting and under-five mortality; a higher score means worse hunger.

Cities, universities and climate

  • Global Cities Index 2026 – Oxford Economics scored 1,000 cities on economics, human capital, quality of life, environment and governance.
  • Leaders – New York first for the third year running, followed by London and Paris.
  • Indian cities – Delhi best at 268th; Bengaluru 311th and Mumbai 330th.
  • THE World University Rankings 2027 – Oxford tops for the 11th straight year; IISc Bengaluru is India's best at joint 254th.
  • Emissions Gap Report 2026 – UNEP's 17th edition, titled 'The Point of Return', sees about 2.8°C warming under present policies.

September 2026Monthly notes

BRICS summit in New Delhi

  • 18th BRICS Summit – met on 12–13 September 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, under India's 2026 chairship.
  • Theme – 'Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability'.
  • New Delhi Declaration – a 140-point text adopted unanimously on day one; it backed UN Security Council reform and condemned the Pahalgam terror attack.
  • Two decades – the meet marked 20 years of the grouping (2006–2026); two commemorative stamps came out on 12 September.
  • Next host – China takes the chair in 2027 and will host the 19th summit.
New Development Bank: the BRICS lender; the idea came up at the 2012 New Delhi summit and the founding pact was signed at Fortaleza, Brazil, in 2014; HQ Shanghai.

BRICS at a glance

  • India as host – 2026 was India's fourth turn; earlier summits were in 2012 (New Delhi), 2016 (Goa) and 2021 (virtual).
  • Name – the word BRIC was coined in 2001 by Goldman Sachs economist Jim O'Neill.
  • First summit – held in 2009 at Yekaterinburg, Russia; South Africa came in during 2010.
  • Members – 11 now, the latest being Indonesia (January 2025).
  • Weight – the bloc holds about 49.5% of global population and nearly 40% of world GDP.

Leaders in Delhi for BRICS

  • Xi Jinping – his first trip to India in about seven years (last in 2019); talks with Modi on 12 September, where Modi stressed the 'three mutuals' – respect, sensitivity and interests.
  • Vladimir Putin – met Modi on 11 September; Modi gave him a Russian translation of the Thirukkural.
  • Masoud Pezeshkian – his first India visit as Iran's President – also the first by any Iranian President since Hassan Rouhani in February 2018.
  • Malaysia – agreed to an Indian Consulate General at Kota Kinabalu in Sabah.
  • Vietnam and Philippines – India and Vietnam will mark 2027 as their Year of Friendship; the Philippines joined the CDRI.
CDRI: Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure; launched by India at the UN Climate Action Summit, New York, in September 2019; secretariat in New Delhi.

Other visits

  • Belgium – PM Bart De Wever visited on 2–4 September, the first Belgian PM visit in about 20 years; both sides aim to double trade in five years.
  • Diamond link – the visit recalled the old diamond trade tying Antwerp with Mumbai and Surat.
  • Sri Lanka – Rajnath Singh made his first visit as Defence Minister (8–10 September); one of three MoUs covers upgrading six L70 air-defence guns used by Sri Lanka's air arm.
  • Aga Khan V – Prince Rahim Aga Khan V, 50th Imam of the Shia Ismaili Muslims, made his first India visit as Imam (9–15 September).
  • Heritage tie – the Aga Khan Trust for Culture restored Sunder Nursery and Humayun's Tomb in Delhi.

Red Sea flashpoints

  • Houthi gains – Yemen's Houthis seized the port of Mocha (10 September) and Perim Island (11 September).
  • Bab-el-Mandeb – strait between Yemen and Djibouti–Eritrea linking the Gulf of Aden with the Red Sea; the name means 'Gate of Tears'.
  • Mocha – historic coffee port that gave its name to Mocha coffee.
  • Houthis – Iran-backed group, also called Ansar Allah; took Sana'a in 2014.
  • Petroline – Saudi East–West oil pipeline from Abqaiq to Yanbu on the Red Sea, a bypass for the Strait of Hormuz.

Greenland deal and UK nations

  • Greenland pact – the US, Denmark and Greenland signed a defence agreement on 22 September 2026 in New York, on the UNGA sidelines.
  • Signatories – Donald Trump, Danish PM Mette Frederiksen and Greenland PM Jens-Frederik Nielsen.
  • What it does – updates the 1951 defence agreement; lets the US expand Pituffik Space Base and open defence areas at Narsarsuaq (south) and Mestersvig (east).
  • Sovereignty – Denmark's sovereignty is reaffirmed and no territory moves to the US.
  • Cardiff Agreement – signed on 14 September in Cardiff by the leaders of Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, seeking constitutional change in the UK.
Greenland: world's largest island; self-governing part of the Kingdom of Denmark (home rule 1979, self-government 2009); capital Nuuk.

India at UNGA-81

  • Session – the 81st UN General Assembly opened on 8 September; high-level week began on 22 September.
  • Address – External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar spoke in the general debate on 26 September.
  • V4 + India – first meeting of India with the Visegrad Group (Poland, Hungary, Slovakia, Czech Republic) foreign ministers on 21 September.
  • Shipping group – India and Liberia launched the 'Group of Friends on Safety and Security of Shipping and Seafarers' on 25 September; Liberia runs the biggest ship registry by gross tonnage.
  • G4 and FIPIC – G4 ministers met on 22 September; the second FIPIC foreign ministers' meeting followed on 23 September.
FIPIC: Forum for India–Pacific Islands Cooperation; launched in November 2014 during PM Modi's visit to Fiji; India plus 14 Pacific island nations.

Reports and rankings

  • Global Gender Gap 2026 – WEF ranked India 131st of 145, the same as last year; parity score 64.5%.
  • Gender gap details – Iceland topped for the 17th year; India's best sub-index was political empowerment (67th).
  • TTDI 2026 – WEF's Travel and Tourism Development Index, made with Zurich Insurance, put India 31st of 110, up nine places.
  • TTDI leader – Japan came first, followed by the US, Spain, Australia and France.
  • GII 2026 – WIPO's Global Innovation Index kept India at 38th of 139; Switzerland stayed on top.

Health and world one-liners

  • Bhutan – WHO validated it as the first in its South-East Asia Region to wipe out human rabies spread by dogs (dog-mediated rabies); Mexico was first globally.
  • Thailand – certified for eliminating rubella at the 79th WHO South-East Asia Regional Committee in Dili, Timor-Leste.
  • IAEA – Timor-Leste became the 182nd member at the 70th General Conference in Vienna (14–18 September).
  • India–NZ FTA – set to enter into force on 20 October 2026.
  • Paris temple – PM Modi opened (by video) France's first traditional stone Hindu temple, built by BAPS, on 6 September.

August 2026Monthly notes

PM Modi in Tashkent

  • State visit – Narendra Modi made a two-day trip to Uzbekistan on 29–30 August 2026, his fourth visit there, as guest of President Shavkat Mirziyoyev.
  • Upgrade – the two leaders lifted ties to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership; 11 pacts were exchanged and 16 outcomes listed.
  • Trade aim – both sides want two-way trade to touch US$5 billion by 2030.
  • Energy – India and Uzbekistan will set up a long-term framework for uranium supply for Indian reactors.
  • Onward trip – from Tashkent the PM flew to Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, for the SCO leaders' meet.

What India offered Uzbekistan

  • Buddhist heritage – India will help conserve the ancient monasteries at Fayaz Tepa and Kara Tepa at Termez.
  • Twin regions – Gujarat and the Samarkand region agreed to become partner regions.
  • Language – 100 Hindi scholarships will carry the name of Lal Bahadur Shastri, and an ICCR Sanskrit chair will open at Tashkent State University of Oriental Studies.
  • Green grant – US$1 million for tree planting around the shrinking Aral Sea.
  • Payments – NPCI International (NIPL) signed a pact with NIPC, which runs Uzbekistan's interbank card network, to link the two payment systems.
Tashkent Declaration: peace pact signed on 10 January 1966 by PM Lal Bahadur Shastri and Pakistan's President Ayub Khan after the 1965 war, with Soviet mediation; Shastri died in Tashkent the next night.

West Asia security pacts

  • Mecca pact – Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkey signed the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement on 7 August 2026.
  • Core clause – an armed attack on any one of the three will count as an attack on all of them.
  • Signatories – Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, President Recep Tayyip Erdogan and PM Shehbaz Sharif signed it; it grows out of the Saudi–Pakistan defence pact of September 2025.
  • Red Sea alliance – at a Riyadh meeting on 30 July 2026, 14 nations backed a Saudi-led Multinational Maritime Defence Alliance.
  • Its aim – to keep the Bab al-Mandab Strait, Red Sea and Gulf of Aden open; Saudi Arabia will lead it and host its headquarters.

US sanctions on Russia and Iran

  • Graham Act – on 7 August 2026 the US Senate passed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 by 86 votes to 11.
  • Tariff power – the President may impose tariffs of up to 100% on goods from the five biggest importers of Russian crude and gas, a list that includes India and China.
  • Iran clause – the bill keeps the Iran Sanctions Act of 1996 alive until 2031.
  • Named after – the late Lindsey Graham, a Republican senator who died on 11 July 2026; Democrat Richard Blumenthal co-led the bill.
  • Operation Economic Outcast – US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent began this drive against Iran on 24 August 2026, hitting five sectors: gold, shipping, aviation, technology and digital assets.

Places in news

  • Naoero – the Pacific island state of Nauru formally took the name Republic of Naoero; its country code moves from NRU to NRO and its people are now called dei-Naoero.
  • No referendum – President David Adeang said a vote was not needed because Naoero is the island's own old name; Parliament had cleared the change in May.
  • Maldives – the Favara–UPI corridor went live on 30 July 2026, letting people in the Maldives send money instantly to UPI accounts in India; Favara is the Maldives' instant payment system.
  • Colombia – a magnitude 7.4 earthquake shook the Chocó region in the west of the country.
  • Taiwan – President Lai Ching-te proposed paying each citizen NT$10,000 as a share of gains from the AI and chip boom.
Naoero (Nauru): coral island in the central Pacific just south of the Equator; independent since 1968; about 12,000 people, among the world's smallest nations; colonised by Germany and later run by Australia; no official capital, government sits in Yaren district.

Reports and indices

  • SOFI 2026 – the UN's State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World report focused on the high cost of a healthy diet.
  • Hunger level – about 7.8% of people, roughly 645 million, faced hunger in 2025; global hunger fell for the third year in a row.
  • Africa first – for the first time Africa (309 million) had more hungry people than Asia (292 million).
  • Diet cost – a healthy diet cost an average PPP $4.28 per person per day in 2025, up from $3.44 in 2021.
  • Work From Anywhere Index – the International Workplace Group's fourth edition put Bangkok on top; Mumbai, at 50th, was the only Indian city.
SOFI report: annual UN flagship report on hunger, brought out jointly by FAO, IFAD, UNICEF, WFP and WHO; FAO, the lead agency, is based in Rome.

July 2026Monthly notes

Modi's tour: Indonesia

  • Dates – PM Modi was in Indonesia on 6–8 July 2026, the first stop of a three-nation tour that also took him to Australia and New Zealand.
  • Highest honour – President Prabowo Subianto gave him the Bintang Republik Indonesia Adipurna, Indonesia's top civil and military award (instituted 1959), at Merdeka Palace, Jakarta.
  • Heritage help – India will help restore the Prambanan Hindu temple complex in Yogyakarta, a UNESCO site; both leaders went there on 8 July.
  • Culture year – 2026–27 is being marked as the Tagore–Dewantara Year of cultural and educational diplomacy.
  • Education and digital – IIM Bengaluru will open its first overseas campus in the Singhasari SEZ, and Indonesia will build an open commerce network on the lines of India's ONDC.

Australia and New Zealand legs

  • Melbourne summit – Modi and Anthony Albanese held the 3rd India–Australia Annual Summit on 9 July 2026; it produced 18 listed outcomes.
  • Defence pact – the two sides adopted a Joint Declaration on Defence and Security Cooperation and a maritime security roadmap.
  • Uranium and tech – steps to put the 2014 civil nuclear deal into practice were settled, opening the way for Australian uranium; a cyber and critical-tech partnership called PACTS was also launched.
  • New Zealand – no Indian PM had gone there for 40 years before the 10–11 July stop in Auckland (Rajiv Gandhi went in 1986); PM Christopher Luxon was the host.
  • Strategic Partnership – ties were raised to a Strategic Partnership with a 'Roadmap to 2030'; both want two-way trade doubled by 2030, and Wellington agreed to become a Global Biofuels Alliance member.

Japan summit and President's Europe trip

  • 16th India–Japan Summit – PM Sanae Takaichi made her first official visit to India on 1–3 July 2026; talks took place at Hyderabad House, New Delhi.
  • Outcomes – a joint roadmap on economic security and pacts on AI, energy and critical minerals; 2027, the 75th year of ties, will be the 'India–Japan Year of Shared Horizons'.
  • Next summit – the 17th summit will be held in Japan in 2027.
  • President abroad – Droupadi Murmu went to Moldova (20 July) and North Macedonia (21–22 July) – the first visits ever by an Indian President to either country.
  • Romania – her 23–25 July stay was the first Indian presidential visit there since 1994; 2028 will be the 'India–Romania Year of Innovation'.

Trade, UN bid and global deals

  • India–UK CETA – the free trade pact signed on 24 July 2025 took effect on 15 July 2026, together with the Double Contribution Convention on social security.
  • UNSC bid – EAM S. Jaishankar opened India's campaign for a non-permanent Security Council seat for 2028–29 at UN headquarters, New York, on 13 July.
  • SHANTI – the campaign slogan stands for Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity; the vote is due in June 2027 and Tajikistan is the rival for the Asia-Pacific seat.
  • US–Saudi deal – the US and Saudi Arabia signed a '123' civil nuclear cooperation pact in Riyadh on 22 July 2026.
  • Undersea cable – a cable of over 3,600 km will link India with Malaysia and Singapore, landing at Machilipatnam and Chennai; service is planned for late 2029.
UN Security Council: 15 members – 5 permanent with veto (China, France, Russia, UK, US) and 10 non-permanent members elected by the General Assembly for two-year terms; India has served eight times, most recently in 2021–22.

Global reports

  • World Investment Report 2026 – in UNCTAD's report of 7 July, India rose to 11th among FDI destinations (13th in 2024) as inflows grew about 44% to $38.9 billion.
  • Outward FDI – Indian firms' investment abroad rose 47% to $35.66 billion, making India the 18th largest source; the US stayed the top destination.
  • World Bank groups – in the FY2027 list India stays lower-middle income, where it has been since 2009, with 2025 per-capita GNI of $2,760; Sri Lanka, Vietnam and the Philippines moved up a group.
  • Liveability – the EIU's 2026 index put Copenhagen first; New Delhi, at 120th, was the best-placed Indian city.
  • Happy cities – Jaipur, at 6th, was the sole Indian name in Time Out's top 10; Bath in England came first.
UNCTAD: UN Trade and Development, set up in 1964 with its seat in Geneva; has published the World Investment Report every year since 1991; Secretary-General Rebeca Grynspan.

June 2026Monthly notes

PM's visits to France and Slovakia

  • France leg – PM Modi was in France on 13–14 June (Nice) and again on 16–19 June (Evian and Paris); he met President Emmanuel Macron at Villa Kerylos in Nice on 14 June.
  • Innovation Roadmap 2030 – the two sides adopted this plan to steer joint work on critical technologies, research and start-ups.
  • More from Nice – a joint AI working group, a new Economic Security Dialogue and a mechanism aimed at doubling two-way trade within five years.
  • Slovakia – on 14–16 June Modi became the first Indian PM to go to Slovakia since it became independent in 1993; ties were raised to a Comprehensive Partnership.
  • Hosts in Bratislava – Prime Minister Robert Fico and President Peter Pellegrini.

Seychelles state visit

  • When – 27–29 June; Modi was Guest of Honour at the golden jubilee of Seychelles' National Day.
  • Talks – held with President Patrick Herminie at State House, Victoria (Mahé), in the year marking 50 years of diplomatic ties.
  • Outcomes – 19 in all, including a UPI rollout pact between NPCI International Payments Ltd (NIPL) and Seychelles' central bank, Jan Aushadhi medicine supply and an extradition treaty.
  • Aid and credit – a Rs 1,250 crore line of credit, a fast patrol vessel as a gift, and Seychelles joining the CDRI.
  • Honour – Herminie presented Modi the new environmental award 'Guardian of the Blue Horizon'.
CDRI: Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure, started by India at the UN Climate Action Summit in 2019; secretariat in New Delhi.

Neighbourhood diplomacy

  • Myanmar – President Min Aung Hlaing, on his first trip abroad since taking office in April 2026, toured India from 30 May to 3 June, starting at Bodh Gaya.
  • What Myanmar promised – its territory will not be used against India; both sides want faster work on the Kaladan project and the India–Myanmar–Thailand Trilateral Highway.
  • Nepal – Foreign Minister Shishir Khanal came on 5–7 June, the first such visit after Nepal's new government took charge in March 2026.
  • UPI–NPI link – India's UPI was connected to Nepal's National Payments Interface for person-to-person remittances; 72 health and 12 heritage projects rebuilt after the 2015 quake were handed over.
  • China – the 35th WMCC meeting on border affairs took place in Beijing; both sides will now get ready for SR-level talks (25th round) that China will host.

Trade pacts and UN elections

  • India–Oman CEPA – took effect on 1 June 2026 (signed in Muscat on 18 December 2025); about 99.38% of India's exports by value enter Oman duty-free.
  • India–UK CETA – set to start on 15 July 2026 along with the Double Contribution Convention on social security.
  • UN Security Council – on 3 June the UNGA chose Austria, Portugal, Kyrgyzstan, Trinidad and Tobago and Zimbabwe for 2027–28; Kyrgyzstan won a seat for the first time while Germany lost out.
  • UNGA President – Bangladesh Foreign Minister Khalilur Rahman won the post for the 81st session, defeating Andreas Kakouris of Cyprus by 99 votes to 91.
  • New Development Bank – Uzbekistan became its 10th member and first from Central Asia on 5 June.
New Development Bank: lender created by BRICS countries in 2014 and working since 2015; headquarters Shanghai; current President Dilma Rousseff.

Global reports

  • Global Peace Index 2026 – by the Institute for Economics and Peace; India 127th of 163 (115th in 2025); Iceland most peaceful, Russia last.
  • SIPRI Yearbook 2026 – India has about 190 nuclear warheads (12 deployed) against China's 620 and Pakistan's 170; India was the 5th largest military spender in 2025.
  • QS World University Rankings 2027 – IIT Delhi leads India at 118th, followed by IIT Bombay (134) and IIT Madras (170); MIT is first; 52 Indian universities feature.
  • Energy Transition Index 2026 – released by the WEF; India moved up two places to 70th of 120; Sweden, Finland and Denmark hold the top three spots.
  • Ship recycling – UNCTAD figures show India topped the world in ship recycling in 2025, with a 35.4% share; most work happens at Alang–Sosiya in Gujarat.

Relief and other news

  • Operation Amistad – begun on 26 June for earthquake-hit Venezuela; two IAF C-17s flew an Army field hospital and over 35 tonnes of supplies; 'amistad' means friendship in Spanish.
  • BHISHM cubes – portable hospital kits were part of the Venezuela aid.
  • UPI in Cambodia – NPCI joined hands with ACLEDA Bank to bring UPI to Cambodia.
  • Joint Crediting Mechanism – India and Japan cleared rules for this carbon-credit scheme, which rests on the Paris Agreement's Article 6.2.

May 2026Monthly notes

PM's five-nation tour: UAE and the Netherlands

  • Tour – between 15 and 20 May Narendra Modi went to Abu Dhabi (UAE), The Hague (Netherlands), Gothenburg (Sweden), Oslo (Norway) and Rome (Italy).
  • Oil reserve pact – in Abu Dhabi on 15 May, ISPRL and ADNOC agreed that ADNOC's crude kept in India's strategic reserves can go up to 30 million barrels.
  • Storage sites – ADNOC already stores oil at Mangaluru; new space is being looked at in Visakhapatnam and Chandikhol (Odisha).
  • Other UAE deals – a 15-year LNG supply deal with Indian Oil and a framework for a strategic defence partnership.
  • The Hague – on 16 May, India and the Netherlands (PM Rob Jetten) became strategic partners with a Roadmap 2026–2030.

Nordic leg and Italy

  • Sweden – in Gothenburg on 17 May, ties with PM Ulf Kristersson were raised to a Strategic Partnership, guided by a Joint Action Plan 2026–2030.
  • Norway – in Oslo on 18 May, India and Norway (PM Jonas Gahr Støre) formed a Green Strategic Partnership; it was the first Indian PM visit there in about 40 years.
  • India–Nordic Summit – the 3rd summit met in Oslo on 19 May and set up a Green Technology and Innovation Strategic Partnership.
  • Italy – in Rome on 20 May, Modi and Giorgia Meloni lifted ties to a Special Strategic Partnership.
  • Trade goal – India–Italy trade, about €14.25 billion in 2025, is to reach €20 billion by 2029.
India–Nordic Summit: meeting of India with Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland and Iceland; first held in Stockholm in 2018, the 2026 (third) edition was hosted by Oslo.

Leaders who came to India

  • Vietnam – President To Lam made a state visit on 5–7 May; ties became an Enhanced Comprehensive Strategic Partnership.
  • Vietnam targets – trade of about US$16 billion is to rise to US$25 billion by 2030; Vietnam joined the Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (IPOI).
  • Payments – NIPL and Vietnam's NAPAS will link QR payments; India will set up a site centre at the My Son temple complex (UNESCO site).
  • Cyprus – President Nikos Christodoulides visited on 20–23 May; ties moved from a Comprehensive to a Strategic Partnership.
  • Cyprus outcomes – a Defence Cooperation Roadmap 2026–2031, a counter-terrorism working group, and 18 May 2026 named the first India–Cyprus Space Day.

Quad and other diplomacy

  • Quad ministers – the Quad foreign ministers met at Hyderabad House, New Delhi, on 26 May under S. Jaishankar.
  • Who came – Marco Rubio (USA), Toshimitsu Motegi (Japan) and Penny Wong (Australia).
  • Outcomes – an Indo-Pacific maritime surveillance initiative, a critical minerals framework, energy security work and the group's first joint infrastructure project, a port in Fiji.
  • Ethiopia – India signed the bilateral protocol for Ethiopia's entry into the WTO.
  • Russia – Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov called on the PM on 14 May.

Reports and global health

  • Press freedom – India slipped to 157th of 180 in the World Press Freedom Index 2026 of Reporters Without Borders (151st in 2025); Norway topped it for the tenth year running.
  • Remittances – the World Migration Report 2026 of the IOM says India got about US$137.67 billion in 2024, the only country above US$100 billion; Mexico came second.
  • Asian universities – India had the most institutions (128) in the THE Asia University Rankings 2026; IISc Bengaluru was India's best at 43rd.
  • Special 301 – the US Trade Representative again placed India in its Priority Watch List on IP protection.
  • Ebola – WHO declared the outbreak in the DR Congo and Uganda a Public Health Emergency of International Concern.

April 2026Monthly notes

India and New Zealand seal a trade deal

  • Signing – the India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement was inked at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 27 April 2026.
  • Who signed – Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal for India and Trade and Investment Minister Todd McClay for New Zealand.
  • Capital pledge – Wellington will help bring US$20 billion of FDI into India across 15 years.
  • Zero duty – every Indian export line gets duty-free entry into the New Zealand market.
  • Kept out – India left dairy (milk, butter, cheese), sugar and some edible oils outside tariff cuts; roughly 30% of lines are excluded.
India–NZ FTA: talks first opened in 2010, were relaunched in March 2025 and wrapped up in December 2025; it is India's seventh trade pact in five years after Mauritius, UAE, Australia, EFTA, the UK and Oman.

Korean President's state visit

  • Visit – South Korean President Lee Jae Myung was in India on 19–21 April, the first visit by a Korean head of state in eight years.
  • Trade goal – the two sides want yearly trade to climb from about US$27 billion to US$50 billion by 2030.
  • CEPA – negotiations to upgrade the bilateral Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement will restart and be fast-tracked.
  • Memberships – Korea joined the International Solar Alliance and the Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative; India will join the Global Green Growth Institute.
  • Friendship year – 2028–29 will be observed as the Year of India–Korea Friendship.

Neighbours and Europe

  • Austria – Chancellor Christian Stocker toured India on 14–17 April, the first such visit from Vienna in about four decades.
  • Germany – Rajnath Singh held talks with German counterpart Boris Pistorius in Berlin (21–23 April); a Defence Industrial Cooperation Roadmap was signed.
  • 75 years – India and Germany unveiled a logo for 75 years of diplomatic ties, which began in 1951.
  • Bhutan – the two countries signed the tariff protocol of the 1,020 MW Punatsangchhu-II run-of-river plant in Wangdue Phodrang district.
  • Bangladesh – Foreign Minister Khalilur Rahman visited New Delhi on 7 April; Dhaka will buy 200 broad-gauge coaches from India.

War in West Asia and the oil cartel

  • Islamabad talks – the USA and Iran held their first direct top-level talks since 1979 in Islamabad on 11–12 April; after about 21 hours there was no deal.
  • Teams – Vice President JD Vance headed the US side and Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf led Iran; Pakistan acted as mediator.
  • Lebanon truce – a 10-day Israel–Lebanon ceasefire, announced by Donald Trump, began on 17 April.
  • Hormuz – Britain and France chaired a Paris meeting of about 40 nations, India among them, on 17 April on keeping the Strait of Hormuz open.
  • OPEC exit – on 28 April the UAE said it would quit OPEC and OPEC+ from 1 May 2026, ending a membership held since 1967.
OPEC: Organisation of the Petroleum Exporting Countries, founded in 1960 at Baghdad by Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia and Venezuela; headquarters Vienna (Austria). OPEC+ (2016) adds Russia and other outside producers.

Global rankings and reports

  • IMF ranking – the April 2026 World Economic Outlook placed India 6th by nominal GDP (about US$4.15 trillion), behind Japan and the UK.
  • Why – a weaker rupee and the new GDP base year; the IMF expects India back at 4th by 2027.
  • SIPRI – India was the world's 5th biggest military spender in 2025, at US$92.1 billion (up 8.9%), overtaking the UK.
  • Top three – the USA, China and Russia led; world military spending touched about US$2,887 billion, an 11th straight yearly rise.
  • Food crises – the 10th Global Report on Food Crises (FAO, WFP and partners) found Nigeria had the most people in acute hunger, followed by DR Congo and Sudan; famine was confirmed in Gaza and Sudan during 2025.

March 2026Monthly notes

Carney's reset trip to India

  • Dates – Canadian PM Mark Carney was in Mumbai and New Delhi from 27 Feb to 2 Mar 2026 – the first bilateral visit by a Canadian PM since 2018.
  • Uranium – Cameco agreed a C$2.6 billion (about US$1.9 billion) deal with the Department of Atomic Energy for nearly 22 million pounds of uranium over 2027–2035.
  • Trade pact – the two sides signed the Terms of Reference for a CEPA and want it finished within 2026.
  • Target – two-way trade of US$50 billion by 2030.
  • Security – a new India–Canada Defence Dialogue was agreed.
Cameco: a uranium mining company based in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan (Canada); one of the largest uranium suppliers in the world.

Finland's president comes calling

  • Visit – President Alexander Stubb paid a state visit on 4–7 March 2026, his first as President, covering Delhi and Mumbai.
  • New label – ties were lifted to a Strategic Partnership in Digitalisation and Sustainability.
  • Raisina – he was chief guest and keynote speaker at the 11th Raisina Dialogue.
  • Trade – both aim to double trade by 2030, riding on the India–EU FTA.
  • 6G – a joint task force on 6G and a working group on digital ties were set up.

War in West Asia: the global picture

  • Start – the US and Israel began joint strikes on Iran on 28 Feb 2026; Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei died in the first wave.
  • Hormuz – traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, the link between the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman, was badly hit.
  • Force majeure – Qatar, Bahrain and Kuwait invoked this clause on gas exports; it frees a party from a contract when events beyond its control block supply.
  • Oil – crude crossed US$100 a barrel within the first week of fighting.
  • Evacuation – India moved its citizens out of Iran overland through Armenia.

Indonesia, Sri Lanka and other moves

  • Indonesia – from 28 March 2026 children under 16 cannot hold accounts on 'high-risk' apps such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Roblox.
  • Region first – Indonesia is the first Southeast Asian nation with such a curb; Australia started the world's first in Dec 2025.
  • Blue bond – Sri Lanka's first blue bond (LKR 3 billion) was listed both at NSE International Exchange, GIFT City and in Colombo.
  • Uzbekistan – India and Uzbekistan opened an Indo-CIS pharma and nutraceutical corridor linking Mumbai with Tashkent.
  • Hexagon – Israel's PM proposed a six-nation 'Hexagon' bloc naming India, Greece and Cyprus; none has signed up.

Where India ranked

  • Happiness – World Happiness Report 2026: India 116th of 147 (118th last year); Finland top for the ninth year in a row.
  • Publisher – Oxford's Wellbeing Research Centre with Gallup and UN SDSN; Costa Rica's 4th spot is a Latin American best.
  • Terrorism – Global Terrorism Index 2026 (Institute for Economics and Peace): Pakistan worst hit for the first time; India 13th.
  • Arms – SIPRI: India was the second largest arms importer in 2021–25 (8.2% share); Ukraine was first.
  • Billionaires – Hurun Global Rich List 2026: India third with 308; Mumbai (95) lost Asia's top billionaire city title to Shenzhen.
SIPRI: Stockholm International Peace Research Institute – an independent Swedish think tank set up in 1966; tracks arms transfers and military spending.

World Air Quality Report 2025

  • Publisher – Swiss firm IQAir released the 8th edition on 24 March 2026, using data from 9,446 cities in 143 countries.
  • Countries – India was sixth most polluted by PM2.5; Pakistan led, then Bangladesh and Tajikistan.
  • Worst city – Loni (Ghaziabad, UP) topped the world with an annual PM2.5 of 112.5 µg/m³.
  • Capital – New Delhi stayed the most polluted capital for the eighth straight year.
  • Spread – two-thirds (66) of the 100 most polluted cities worldwide are Indian; Meghalaya's Byrnihat ranked third.

February 2026Monthly notes

India and France move to a 'special' tier

  • Visit – President Emmanuel Macron was in India from 17 to 19 Feb 2026 – his fourth trip – and also joined the AI Impact Summit.
  • New label – relations were lifted to a Special Global Strategic Partnership, steered by the Horizon 2047 Roadmap.
  • Innovation year – the two leaders opened the 2026 India–France Year of Innovation in Mumbai on 17 Feb.
  • Helicopters – an assembly line for Airbus H125 helicopters started at Vemagal, Kolar district, Karnataka.
  • Oldest partner – France became India's first strategic partner back in 1998.
Horizon 2047 Roadmap: agreed in 2023 on 25 years of the India–France strategic partnership; three pillars – security and sovereignty, planet, and people.

Modi in Israel after nine years

  • Dates – PM Narendra Modi made a state visit to Israel on 25–26 Feb 2026; his first was in July 2017.
  • Upgrade – ties became a Special Strategic Partnership for Peace, Innovation and Prosperity.
  • Parliament – Modi spoke in the Knesset, Israel's parliament, in Jerusalem.
  • Tech pact – a Critical and Emerging Technologies Partnership was announced for AI, quantum and critical minerals.
  • Trade talks – the first round of India–Israel FTA negotiations ran in New Delhi from 23 to 26 Feb.

Guests from Brazil and Seychelles

  • Lula – Brazil's President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva paid a state visit from 18 to 22 Feb – his second state visit to India.
  • Minerals – India and Brazil signed an MoU on rare earths and critical minerals – exploration, processing and recycling.
  • Herminie – Seychelles President Patrick Herminie made his first state visit, 5–10 Feb, after taking office in Oct 2025.
  • SESEL – both sides adopted a Joint Vision for Sustainability, Economic Growth and Security through Enhanced Linkages.
  • Aid – India announced a US$175 million package for Seychelles – a US$125 million credit line plus US$50 million in grants.
Seychelles: island nation in the western Indian Ocean; marks 50 years of independence and of ties with India in 2026; a key partner under India's MAHASAGAR vision.

A two-day stop in Malaysia

  • Trip – Modi's first foreign visit of 2026 was to Malaysia on 7–8 Feb, hosted by PM Anwar Ibrahim.
  • Status – the two countries have been Comprehensive Strategic Partners since Aug 2024.
  • Payments – India's UPI is to be linked with Malaysia's PayNet for QR-code payments across the border.
  • Business – the 10th India–Malaysia CEO Forum met in Kuala Lumpur on 7 Feb.
  • Diplomacy – India will open a new consulate in Malaysia.

Tariffs, chips and courts: India and the US

  • Deal outline – an interim trade framework was released on 7 Feb 2026; the US reciprocal tariff on most Indian goods drops to 18%.
  • Penalty gone – the extra 25% duty linked to Russian oil purchases was withdrawn by executive order.
  • Bigger goal – it is a step towards a full Bilateral Trade Agreement, whose talks began on 13 Feb 2025.
  • Court ruling – on 20 Feb the US Supreme Court held (6–3) that the IEEPA, 1977 gives the President no power to levy tariffs.
  • Pax Silica – India signed the US-led Pax Silica Declaration on 20 Feb during the AI Impact Summit.
Pax Silica: launched by the US State Department on 12 Dec 2025 to build a trusted 'silicon stack' – minerals, chips and AI; signatories include Japan, South Korea, Israel, UAE, UK and Australia.

Gaza board, BRICS and the UN

  • Board of Peace – India sat in its first meeting (Washington, 19 Feb) only as an observer, not a member.
  • Its job – the Board, chaired by Donald Trump, oversees Gaza's recovery under UN Security Council Resolution 2803.
  • BRICS centre – on 4 Feb India became part of the BRICS Centre for Industrial Competencies; the National Productivity Council is the India Centre.
  • BRICS chair – India chairs BRICS in 2026; the first Sherpa meeting was held in New Delhi on 9–10 Feb.
  • UN budget – India paid its full 2026 UN regular budget dues on time and entered the UN 'honour roll'.
BCIC: set up with UNIDO as a one-stop help centre for manufacturers and MSMEs of BRICS members, centred on Industry 4.0 skills; DPIIT guides India's role.

Where India ranked

  • Corruption – Corruption Perceptions Index 2025 – India 91st of 182 (score 39), up from 96th; Denmark first for the eighth straight year.
  • Publisher – the corruption index comes from Transparency International, based in Berlin.
  • Networks – Network Readiness Index 2025 – India 45th of 127, up four places; USA first.
  • Who makes it – the readiness index is prepared by the Portulans Institute, Washington DC.
  • Passports – in the Feb edition of the Henley Passport Index, India moved up to 75th; Singapore stayed on top.

Elsewhere in the world

  • Greenland – France became the first EU country to open a consulate general in Nuuk (6 Feb).
  • Egypt – about 30 inscriptions in Tamil-Brahmi, Prakrit and Sanskrit (1st–3rd century CE) were identified in six tombs of the Valley of the Kings.
  • Spain – it announced a social-media ban for children under 16.
  • Walmart – it became the first retailer worth US$1 trillion in market value.
  • Russia – it moved to block WhatsApp while promoting its own app, MAX.

January 2026Monthly notes

India and Europe close an 18-year negotiation

  • When and where – announced on 27 Jan 2026 at the 16th India–EU Summit, New Delhi, a day after the EU leaders attended Republic Day.
  • Talk history – talks began in 2007, stalled, and were relaunched on 17 June 2022.
  • Size – a free trade zone of about 2 billion people, roughly a quarter of world GDP; EU is India's largest trading partner.
  • Nickname – Ursula von der Leyen called it the "mother of all deals"; PM called it India's biggest FTA.
  • Tariff cuts – EU car duties to fall from 110% to 10% over time; wine duties to about 20%.
European Union: 27 member states; Commission President Ursula von der Leyen; European Council President António Costa; seat in Brussels.

Three-hour visit, a dozen outcomes: UAE

  • Visitor – UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, 19 Jan 2026; fifth India visit in ten years, third as President.
  • Trade goal – more than double bilateral trade to US$200 billion by 2032.
  • Energy – HPCL–ADNOC Gas deal: 0.5 MTPA LNG for 10 years from 2028.
  • Tech and space – supercomputing cluster in India (C-DAC with G42) under the IndiaAI Mission; IN-SPACe–UAE Space Agency tie-up.
  • Other – UAE investment in Dholera SIR (Gujarat); LoI on defence partnership; FAB and DP World in GIFT City; a 'House of India' in Abu Dhabi.

German Chancellor's first Asia stop

  • Friedrich Merz (Chancellor since May 2025) visited 12–13 Jan 2026 – Ahmedabad first, then Bengaluru.
  • Ahmedabad – Sabarmati Ashram and International Kite Festival with PM Modi; 27 MoUs and announcements signed.
  • Anniversaries – 75 years of ties, 25 years of strategic partnership.
  • Key outcomes – visa-free transit through Germany for Indians; green ammonia deal between AM Green and Germany's Uniper; Lothal maritime heritage tie-up with Bremerhaven museum.

Europe's single currency gets a new user

  • Bulgaria – adopted the euro on 1 Jan 2026 as the 21st euro-area member, 19 years after joining the EU.
  • Changeover – lev (in use since 1881) and euro ran together in January; euro sole tender from 1 Feb; fixed at 1.95583 lev = 1 euro.
  • Before it – Croatia (2023) was the 20th; the euro was launched in 1999 with 11 countries.
  • Schengen – Bulgaria became a full Schengen member in January 2025.

America steps out of 66 bodies

  • Order – on 7–8 Jan 2026 the US announced exit from 66 bodies (31 UN-linked, 35 others) under Executive Order 14199.
  • India link – list includes the International Solar Alliance, which the US had joined as 101st member in Nov 2021.
  • Others on list – IPCC, IRENA, IUCN and the Global Counterterrorism Forum.
  • India's stand – India said ISA will carry on its solar work with its member countries.
International Solar Alliance: India–France initiative launched at COP21 Paris (2015); framework opened for signature at Marrakesh (2016); headquarters Gurugram; about 125 member countries.

Countries in the news

  • Europe – Denmark became the first country to end its centuries-old letter delivery; France passed a bill to bar social media for under-15s from September 2026.
  • Peru – Satipo and Nauta gave legal rights to Amazonian stingless bees, a first for insects.
  • Australia – Finke River (Northern Territory) seen as the world's oldest river still flowing; bushfire disaster declared in Victoria.
  • Spain – joined the Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (IPOI).
  • NASA veteran – Sunita Williams retired after 27 years; she once ran a marathon on the ISS treadmill; Padma Bhushan 2008.
IPOI: Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative, unveiled by PM Modi at the East Asia Summit, Bangkok (2019); seven pillars including maritime security and ecology.

Global bodies and milestones

  • EU carbon tax – CBAM charges began on 1 Jan 2026 for imported steel, aluminium, cement and fertiliser.
  • UNSC 2026–27 entrants – Bahrain, Colombia, DR Congo, Latvia, Liberia. Staying till end-2026: Denmark, Greece, Pakistan, Panama, Somalia.
  • UN years – 2026 is the International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists; also the ASEAN–India Year of Maritime Cooperation.
  • Artefacts – Smithsonian's Asian Art museum to return three temple bronzes taken from Tamil Nadu, including a Chola Nataraja.
  • Aviation – Akasa Air joined IATA, the fifth Indian airline to do so.

Where India stands: January rankings
