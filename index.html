Frequently asked questions

Are SSC previous year papers free on TrickySSC?

Yes. Every SSC CGL, CHSL, GD and MTS previous year paper and every chapter-wise PYQ test is free for registered students, with solutions in Hindi and English.

How many mock tests are free?

SSC CGL Mock 1–4 are free. SSC CHSL, SSC GD and SSC MTS Mock 1–2 are free. RRB NTPC 2026-27 Mock 1–3 are free. The rest unlock with a ₹49 one-year pass.

What does the ₹49 pass include?

One exam's full mock series for one year: SSC CGL 100 mocks (Tier I + II), SSC CHSL 80 mocks (Tier I + II with Refresher mocks), SSC GD 60 mocks, SSC MTS 60 mocks or RRB NTPC 2026-27 60 mocks, each with subject-wise tests.

What are the combo offers?

Two exams for ₹79 instead of ₹98: SSC CGL + SSC CHSL (180 mocks), SSC CHSL + SSC GD (140 mocks) or SSC MTS + SSC GD (120 mocks). All Mocks Pass for ₹99: every SSC CGL, CHSL, GD, MTS and RRB NTPC mock (360 mocks), and every new mock series added during the year joins the pass automatically. Students who already hold a pass can upgrade by paying only the difference: ₹49 more with one exam pass, ₹19 more with two. Every combo and pass is valid for one year.

What is the difference between chapter tests and PYQ chapter tests?

Chapter tests are fresh practice questions written for one chapter, at Level 1 and Level 2. PYQ chapter tests are the real questions SSC asked in CGL, CHSL and GD papers, sorted by chapter, with the exam, date and shift under every question.

Does TrickySSC have RRB NTPC mock tests for 2026-27?

Yes. TrickySSC has a 60-mock RRB NTPC Mock Test 2026-27 series for Graduate CBT-1, now live: 100 questions in 90 minutes (General Awareness 40, Mathematics 30, Reasoning 30) with 1/3 negative marking, sections in the same order as the real exam, and every question tagged by chapter. Mock 1–3 are free.

Are the tests available in Hindi and English?

Yes. Previous year papers, mock tests, chapter tests and solutions are available in both Hindi and English.

SSC CHSL previous year papers cover Tier I and Tier II with Computer, and SSC GD previous year papers follow the 80-question CBE. Each paper shows the exam date and shift, with solutions in Hindi and English, so you can practise exactly what SSC asked.

SSC mock tests on the real pattern

Every SSC CGL mock test, SSC CHSL mock test, SSC MTS mock test and SSC GD mock test follows the latest pattern — section timing, number of questions and negative marking — with a section-wise score report after each attempt.

RRB NTPC Mock Test 2026-27 for Graduate CBT-1
