What is SSC CHSL?

SSC CHSL (Staff Selection Commission Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination) is one of India's biggest government recruitment examinations for 12th-pass candidates. Conducted every year by the Staff Selection Commission, it fills clerical and data-entry posts across central government ministries, departments and offices.

Through SSC CHSL, candidates are recruited as Lower Division Clerk (LDC) / Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Data Entry Operator (DEO) and Data Entry Operator Grade 'A'. Because the minimum qualification is only Class 12, the exam attracts several lakh applicants each year, which makes cut-offs high and accuracy far more valuable than raw attempts.

Success in SSC CHSL comes from speed, accuracy and repeated exposure to real questions. TrickySSC helps aspirants prepare through SSC CHSL previous year papers, online mock tests, chapter-wise practice, detailed solutions and exam-oriented resources.

Tier II Examination

Tier II decides the final merit in SSC CHSL. It is a single objective paper split into three sections, of which only Section I and Section II count for merit; Section III (Computer Knowledge Test and Typing Test / DEST) is qualifying in nature.

Tier II Marking & Weightage

  • Each question carries 3 marks; 1 mark is deducted for every wrong answer.
  • Merit is decided on Section I + Section II = 360 marks.
  • Maths + Reasoning · 180 marks (50%)
  • English + General Awareness · 180 marks (50%)
  • Computer Knowledge Test and Typing Test / DEST · qualifying only

Since both merit sections carry equal weight, a weak English or General Awareness section costs exactly as much as weak Maths — balance is the whole game in CHSL Tier II.

SSC CHSL Syllabus

SSC CHSL tests candidates across four major areas.

Quantitative Aptitude

Number System, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Time-Speed-Distance, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry and Data Interpretation.

General Intelligence & Reasoning

Analogy, Classification, Coding-Decoding, Series, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Ranking, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Matrix, Paper Folding & Cutting, Mirror Images and Non-Verbal Reasoning.

English Language

Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement, Fill in the Blanks, Idioms & Phrases, One Word Substitution, Synonyms, Antonyms, Spelling Correction and Active-Passive / Direct-Indirect Narration.

General Awareness

History, Geography, Indian Polity, Economics, General Science, Current Affairs, Static GK, Government Schemes, Awards, Sports and National & International Events.
