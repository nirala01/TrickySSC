SSC CHSL Tier 1 All India Challenge Mock — Free All India Mock Test Every Saturday

One new full-length SSC CHSL Tier 1 paper every Saturday while the exam is on, taken by registered students across India in the same window. It follows the real pattern, 100 questions in 60 minutes, and on Monday every student gets an All India rank, percentile and a complete analysis. Registration is free and you do it only once.

How the All India Challenge Mock works

Register once

Log in and press Register. That one click puts you in every SSC CHSL Tier 1 Challenge Mock that follows. Registration for a Saturday closes on Friday at 11:59 pm.

Take the paper on Saturday

The paper opens at 9 am and stays open till midnight. Start any time up to 11 pm. You get one attempt and the test cannot be paused.

Submit

You see “Your test has been submitted successfully”. Marks and solutions stay closed so the paper is fair for those who start later in the day.

Result on Monday

At 9 am on Monday your marks, All India rank, percentile, analysis and solutions open on their own. The paper then becomes a free mock for everyone.

Why you should attempt it

A rank among real CHSL candidates

Everyone takes the same new paper in the same window, once. Your All India rank and percentile tell you where you stand, not just how many marks you scored.

Exam-day practice while the exam is on

Tier 1 is a speed test: 100 questions in 60 minutes. One full paper every week, under the same clock and marking, keeps your pace ready for your own shift.

Real exam pattern and order

English Language, General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude and General Awareness, 25 questions each, in the CHSL order, with +2 and −0.5 marking.

A full analysis, not only a score

Section-wise rank, comparison with the topper and the average, time spent on each question, chapter-wise strong and weak areas, and where you lost marks.

Fresh current affairs

General Awareness in every paper carries current affairs of the latest months along with static GK, the way the Tier 1 paper does.

Solutions and a scorecard to share

Every question comes with a step-by-step solution in Hindi and English. Your scorecard with marks and All India rank can be shared in one tap.

Rules

  • Only students who registered before Friday 11:59 pm can take that Saturday's paper in the live window.
  • One attempt per student. The test cannot be paused; if your connection drops, open the paper again and choose Resume.
  • The last entry is 11 pm on Saturday, so that every paper finishes by midnight.
  • Marks, rank and solutions open on Monday at 9 am for everyone together.
  • From Monday 9 am the paper is a free mock for all. Attempts made then are not counted in the All India rank.
  • Students with equal marks share the same rank.

Results and leaderboard

The first result comes out on Monday, 30 Nov 2026 at 9 am. The leaderboard, and the paper as a free mock, will appear here.

Keep practising between two Saturdays

One paper a week shows where you stand. To move up the rank list you need more timed papers in between.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SSC CHSL Tier 1 All India Challenge Mock?

It is a free, full-length SSC CHSL Tier 1 mock test held on TrickySSC every Saturday while the Tier 1 exam is on. Registered students take the same new paper in the same window, and every student gets an All India rank, percentile and a full analysis of the attempt.

Is the All India Challenge Mock free?

Yes. Registration, the paper, the result, the All India rank, the analysis and the solutions are all free. You only need a free TrickySSC login.

How do I register?

Log in and press Register once. Registration is one time: after it you are in every SSC CHSL Tier 1 Challenge Mock that follows, with no need to register again.

What is the last date to register for a Saturday's challenge?

Registration for a Saturday's paper closes on Friday at 11:59 pm. If you register after that, you are in from the next Saturday's Challenge Mock.

At what time can I take the mock?

The paper is open on Saturday from 9 am to 12 midnight. It is 60 minutes long, so the last entry is 11 pm. You get one attempt.

When does the result come?

The result opens by itself on Monday at 9 am: marks, All India rank, percentile, section-wise rank, comparison with the topper and the average, time analysis, chapter-wise analysis and full solutions.

Why is the score not shown right after I submit?

The paper stays open for the whole of Saturday. Holding the score and solutions until Monday keeps the paper fair for a student who starts in the evening, and gives one rank list for everyone.

What is the pattern of the paper?

The SSC CHSL Tier 1 pattern: 100 questions for 200 marks in 60 minutes, 25 questions each from English Language, General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude and General Awareness. Each correct answer carries 2 marks and each wrong answer costs 0.5 marks. There is one clock for the whole paper.

Is the paper available in Hindi?

Yes. The paper is in English and Hindi. The English Language part is in English only, as in the real exam.

Can I take the paper if I missed the Saturday window?

Yes. From Monday 9 am the same paper is open to everyone as a free mock with solutions. Such an attempt is not counted in the All India rank, but your analysis shows where your score would have stood among the live candidates.

My SSC CHSL exam is on a Saturday. Can I still take the challenge?

Yes. The paper is open from 9 am to 12 midnight, so you can take it after your shift, any time up to 11 pm.

Till when will the SSC CHSL Tier 1 Challenge Mock run?

Every Saturday from 28 November to 26 December 2026, five papers in all, covering the SSC CHSL 2026 Tier 1 exam window of 30 November to 31 December 2026.

Is this the same as the SSC CGL Tier 2 All India Challenge Mock?

No. That is a separate challenge held every Sunday on the SSC CGL Tier 2 pattern, with its own registration. You can register for one or for both.

The All India Challenge Mock is a practice test by TrickySSC and is not conducted by, or connected with, the Staff Selection Commission. The pattern follows the SSC CHSL 2026 notification; check ssc.gov.in for the official exam schedule. The rank shown is the rank among students who took this mock on TrickySSC.
