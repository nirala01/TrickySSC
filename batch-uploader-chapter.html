1 Drop every chapter-test CSV

Select or drag in all files at once — as many sets, levels and chapters as you like. Files pair into tests by their filename, minus the language suffix: NumberSystem_L3_Set13_EN.csv and …_HI.csv become one test. Level comes from L1/L2/L3 (no marker means Level 1) and the test number from Set13 / Test13. Exam, subject and chapter are read out of the CSV's own columns; everything is editable below. A figures .docx is optional — only chapters with diagrams need one.
