Why Chapter Wise Practice Works for SSC CGL

A full-length mock tells you that you scored 132. It does not tell you that 14 of those lost marks came from mensuration and time-and-work. Chapter wise tests answer that question directly, because every test has exactly one variable in it.

It separates "I do not know this" from "I am too slow at this"

Attempt a chapter once without the timer and once with it. If the untimed score is high and the timed score drops, the chapter is a speed problem and the fix is more repetitions of the same shapes. If both are low, it is a concept problem and the fix is going back to the theory first. A mixed paper cannot tell these two apart.

It matches how SSC actually repeats questions

SSC recycles question patterns heavily — the same idiom, the same algebraic identity shape, the same coding-decoding rule, year after year. Practising fifty questions from one chapter in a single sitting is what makes those patterns visible, in a way that meeting one such question every third mock never will.

It gives you something useful to do with twenty minutes

A full mock needs an uninterrupted hour and a clear head. A chapter test fits into a break. Through the long middle stretch of preparation, that difference is what keeps the daily streak alive.

How to Plan Your Chapter Wise Preparation

  1. Start with Quantitative Aptitude. It takes the longest to build and decides the most selections. Follow the arithmetic order — percentage, then profit and loss, discount, interest, ratio and average — because each one reuses the last.
  2. Run English vocabulary and General Awareness daily. Ten to fifteen minutes each. These are memory subjects; frequency beats duration.
  3. Use Reasoning to push accuracy towards full marks. Analogy, classification, series and coding-decoding first, then the non-verbal chapters most candidates skip.
  4. Level 1 untimed, Level 2 timed. Only move a chapter to Level 3 once Level 2 is consistently strong.
  5. Read every solution, including for the questions you got right. A correct answer reached the long way is still a timing problem, and the shortcut is in the solution panel.
  6. Convert chapters into a paper. Once four or five chapters in a subject are solid, attempt a previous year paper, then a full mock test with locked sectional timing to check the speed survives.

SSC CGL Chapter Wise Test — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a chapter wise test in SSC CGL preparation?
A chapter wise test contains questions from a single chapter only — for example just Percentage, or just Coding-Decoding. It isolates one topic so you can see exactly which chapter is costing you marks, instead of finding out from a full-length paper where every section is mixed together.
How many chapters are there in the SSC CGL syllabus?
TrickySSC splits SSC CGL Tier I into 97 chapters — 35 in Quantitative Aptitude, 28 in General Intelligence & Reasoning, 20 in General Awareness and 14 in English Language. Tier II adds separate modules for Computer Knowledge, Statistics and Finance & Economics.
How many questions are in each SSC CGL chapter test?
Fifty questions per test, with an optional 30-minute timer. Marking follows the real exam: +2 for a correct answer, −0.5 for a wrong one and 0 for an unattempted question. Each chapter has three difficulty levels with 15 to 20 tests per level.
Are the SSC CGL chapter wise tests free?
Yes. Chapter wise practice is open to everyone right now, and you do not need to log in to attempt a chapter test.
Are SSC CGL chapter wise tests available in Hindi?
Yes. Questions, options, detailed solutions and shortcut tricks are available in हिंदी wherever the Hindi set has been uploaded for that chapter, and you choose English or Hindi before the test starts.
Which subject should I start chapter wise practice with?
Start with Quantitative Aptitude, because it takes the longest to build and decides most selections. Run English vocabulary and General Awareness daily in short sessions alongside it, and use Reasoning to push your accuracy towards full marks.
Where do the chapter test questions come from?
They are built from SSC previous year questions plus model questions written in the same pattern, then tagged chapter by chapter. That is the same bank that powers our previous year paper section and mock test series.
What is the difference between chapter wise tests and mock tests?
A chapter test covers one topic and fixes concept gaps. A full mock test covers all four sections under locked sectional timing and tells you your speed, order of attempt and likely rank. Use chapter tests while learning, and mock tests in the final weeks.
