SSC CGL Tier 2 All India Challenge Mock — Free All India Mock Test Every Sunday

One new full-length SSC CGL Tier 2 paper every Sunday, taken by registered students across India in the same window. It runs on the new 30-30-40-20-15 sectional timing, and on Tuesday every student gets an All India rank, percentile and a complete analysis. Registration is free and you do it only once.

How the All India Challenge Mock works

Register once

Log in and press Register. That one click puts you in every Challenge Mock that follows. Registration for a Sunday closes on Saturday at 11:59 pm.

Take the paper on Sunday

The paper opens at 9 am and stays open till midnight. Start any time up to 9:45 pm. You get one attempt and the test cannot be paused.

Submit

You see “Your test has been submitted successfully”. Marks and solutions stay closed so the paper is fair for those who start later in the day.

Result on Tuesday

At 9 am on Tuesday your marks, All India rank, percentile, analysis and solutions open on their own. The paper then becomes a free mock for everyone.

Why you should attempt it

Practice on the new sectional timing

From SSC CGL 2026, Maths gets a fixed 30 minutes and you can no longer borrow time from Reasoning. No previous year paper was held this way, so a timed mock is the only real practice.

A rank among serious Tier 2 candidates

Everyone takes the same new paper in the same window, once. Your All India rank and percentile tell you where you stand, not just how many marks you scored.

Real exam level

Each paper is written to the level of the SSC CGL Tier 2 papers: 30 Maths, 30 Reasoning, 45 English, 25 General Awareness and 20 Computer questions.

A full analysis, not only a score

Section-wise rank, comparison with the topper and the average, time spent on each part, chapter-wise strong and weak areas, and where you lost marks.

Fresh current affairs

General Awareness in every paper carries current affairs of the latest months along with static GK, the way the Tier 2 paper does.

Solutions and a scorecard to share

Every question comes with a step-by-step solution in Hindi and English. Your scorecard with marks and All India rank can be shared in one tap.

Rules

  • Only students who registered before Saturday 11:59 pm can take that Sunday's paper in the live window.
  • One attempt per student. The test cannot be paused; if your connection drops, open the paper again and choose Resume.
  • The last entry is 9:45 pm on Sunday, so that every paper finishes by midnight.
  • Marks, rank and solutions open on Tuesday at 9 am for everyone together.
  • From Tuesday 9 am the paper is a free mock for all. Attempts made then are not counted in the All India rank.
  • Students with equal marks share the same rank.

Results and leaderboard

The first result comes out on Tuesday, 1 Dec 2026 at 9 am. The leaderboard, and the paper as a free mock, will appear here.

Keep practising between two Sundays

One paper a week shows where you stand. To move up the rank list you need more timed papers in between.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SSC CGL Tier 2 All India Challenge Mock?

It is a free, full-length SSC CGL Tier 2 Paper I mock test held on TrickySSC every Sunday. Registered students take the same new paper in the same window, and every student gets an All India rank, percentile and a full analysis of the attempt.

Is the All India Challenge Mock free?

Yes. Registration, the paper, the result, the All India rank, the analysis and the solutions are all free. You only need a free TrickySSC login.

How do I register?

Log in and press Register once. Registration is one time: after it you are in every All India Challenge Mock that follows, with no need to register again.

What is the last date to register for a Sunday's challenge?

Registration for a Sunday's paper closes on Saturday at 11:59 pm. If you register after that, you are in from the next Sunday's Challenge Mock.

At what time can I take the mock?

The paper is open on Sunday from 9 am to 12 midnight. It is 2 hours 15 minutes long, so the last entry is 9:45 pm. You get one attempt.

When does the result come?

The result opens by itself on Tuesday at 9 am: marks, All India rank, percentile, section-wise rank, comparison with the topper and the average, time analysis, chapter-wise analysis and full solutions.

Why is the score not shown right after I submit?

The paper stays open for the whole of Sunday. Holding the score and solutions until Tuesday keeps the paper fair for a student who starts in the evening, and gives one rank list for everyone.

Which timing does the paper follow?

The sectional timing brought in with SSC CGL 2026 for Tier 2 Paper I: Mathematical Abilities 30 minutes, Reasoning 30 minutes, English 40 minutes, General Awareness 20 minutes and Computer Knowledge 15 minutes. Each part has its own clock and you cannot go back to a part once its time is over.

Is the paper available in Hindi?

Yes. The paper is in English and Hindi. The English Language and Comprehension part is in English only, as in the real exam.

Can I take the paper if I missed the Sunday window?

Yes. From Tuesday 9 am the same paper is open to everyone as a free mock with solutions. Such an attempt is not counted in the All India rank, but your analysis shows where your score would have stood among the live candidates.

What happens if my internet drops during the test?

Open the same paper again on the same device and choose Resume: you continue from the same question with the same answers and the same time left. The test cannot be paused in the live challenge.

Does the challenge cover Tier 2 Paper II (Statistics) and Paper III (Finance and Economics)?

No. The All India Challenge Mock is Paper I, which every post needs. Separate mock tests for Paper II (JSO) and Paper III (AAO) are available on TrickySSC.

The All India Challenge Mock is a practice test by TrickySSC and is not conducted by, or connected with, the Staff Selection Commission. The pattern and sectional timing follow the SSC CGL 2026 notification; check ssc.gov.in for the official exam schedule. The rank shown is the rank among students who took this mock on TrickySSC.
