Bills of Exchange is a “parties, dates and entries” chapter. SSC asks which section defines what (Section 4 promissory note, 5 bill, 6 cheque), who the drawer, drawee and payee are, how to find the due date with three days of grace, what noting charges and dishonour mean and which entry the drawer or drawee passes.

Marks are lost in three places. The first is the due date: add the term, then three days of grace, then shift for a holiday. The second is who accepts: the drawee accepts a bill, but a promissory note needs no acceptance. The third is entries on dishonour — the drawee becomes the debtor again along with noting charges. The tests here are built around exactly these three.

The three traps that cost the most marks

  • Due date in three steps. Term + 3 days of grace; if that day is a public holiday the bill is due on the preceding business day, but on the next day if it is a sudden emergency holiday.
  • Note vs bill. A promissory note is a promise by the debtor and needs no acceptance; a bill is an order by the creditor and must be accepted by the drawee.
  • No grace for cheques. A cheque is payable on demand, so it gets no days of grace.

Solved Examples — SSC CGL AAO Bills of Exchange

Q1. The Negotiable Instruments Act, which governs bills of exchange, promissory notes and cheques in India, was enacted in:
(a) 1872
(b) 1881
(c) 1882
(d) 1934
The Negotiable Instruments Act was enacted in 1881. It came into force on 1 March 1882 and extends to the whole of India. Section 13 of the Act treats promissory notes, bills of exchange and cheques as negotiable instruments. 1872 is the year of the Indian Contract Act, not the Negotiable Instruments Act. Answer: (b)
Q2. A bill dated 15 March 2026 is drawn payable 3 months after date. Its due date is:
(a) 15 June 2026
(b) 18 June 2026
(c) 17 June 2026
(d) 18 July 2026
The due date is 18 June 2026. The term is in months, so 3 calendar months from 15 March 2026 gives 15 June 2026. Days of grace are added to a time bill: 15 June + 3 days = 18 June 2026. 18 June 2026 is a Thursday, not a holiday, so no further change is needed. 15 June 2026 is wrong because it ignores the days of grace. Answer: (b)
Q3. Read the Assertion (A) and Reason (R) carefully.
Assertion (A): A promissory note does not require acceptance.
Reason (R): In a promissory note, the person who signs the instrument is himself the person who undertakes to pay.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true, but R is false
(d) A is false, but R is true
Both A and R are true, and R correctly explains A. A bill of exchange is an order to another person, so the drawee must accept it to become liable. A promissory note is a promise, not an order, so no other person needs to accept it. The person who signs the note is already bound by his own promise. Answer: (a)
Q4. आहार्यी (Drawee) की पुस्तकों में, उसके द्वारा स्वीकृत विपत्र के अनादरण पर रोज़नामचा प्रविष्टि क्या होगी?
(a) आहर्ता का खाता नाम (Dr.) — देय विपत्र खाते से
(b) देय विपत्र खाता (Bills Payable A/c) नाम (Dr.) — आहर्ता के खाते से
(c) प्राप्य विपत्र खाता नाम (Dr.) — आहर्ता के खाते से
(d) आहर्ता का खाता नाम (Dr.) — प्राप्य विपत्र खाते से
सही प्रविष्टि है: देय विपत्र खाता नाम (Dr.) — आहर्ता के खाते से। अनादरण पर विपत्र पर आहार्यी का दायित्व समाप्त हो जाता है, इसलिए देय विपत्र खाता नाम होता है। आहर्ता के प्रति उसका मूल ऋण फिर से जीवित हो जाता है, इसलिए आहर्ता का खाता जमा (Cr.) होता है। आहर्ता का खाता नाम — देय विपत्र खाते से, यह विपत्र स्वीकार करते समय की प्रविष्टि है। उत्तर: (b)

How to Prepare Bills of Exchange for SSC CGL AAO

  1. Practise due dates with a calendar. Count months by date, not by 30 days, and check the weekday.
  2. Draw the three parties. Drawer → drawee → payee on one line makes every party question easy.
  3. Write entries in both books. Drawer’s and drawee’s books side by side for acceptance, discounting and dishonour.
  4. Learn sections 4, 5, 6 and 9. Note, bill, cheque and holder in due course.
  5. Read the solutions, not just the answer. Each solution shows the working line by line.
