Who can apply

Educational qualification

  • Passed Class 10 (matriculation) or an equivalent examination from a recognised board or university, on or before the closing date in the notification.

Age limit

18 to 23 years on the cut-off date fixed in the notification (the 2026 notification used 1 January 2026). Relaxation follows the standard central government rules — 3 years for OBC, 5 years for SC and ST, and the specified relaxations for ex-servicemen and other reserved groups.

How to apply

  1. Go to ssc.gov.in and complete One Time Registration if you have not already.
  2. Log in with your registration ID and password.
  3. Open the Constable (GD) 2027 application link and fill in your personal, educational and communication details, including state and force preferences.
  4. Upload your photograph and signature to the stated specifications, or use Aadhaar-based authentication during OTR to simplify the ID check at the centre.
  5. Pay the fee if it applies to you, and confirm the payment status inside your login.
  6. Preview the form, submit it, and save a printout.

Applications are accepted only through the SSC portal and the official mySSC mobile application. Apply several days early — the portal slows badly in the final 48 hours.

How to prepare from here

With the CBE tentatively three to five months away, there is time to finish the syllabus properly rather than skimming it.

  • Start with previous year papers. GD repeats its question types heavily. Ten real shifts teach you more about the pattern than any syllabus PDF.
  • Fix the weak part, not the weak subject. Part-wise tests tell you whether the problem is series or coding, percentage or time and work.
  • Move to full mocks about six weeks out, and treat the 60-minute clock as non-negotiable.
  • Give GK fifteen minutes a day rather than a cram week. Current affairs from the last eight to ten months carries most of the weight, with sports, awards and women achievers favoured in recent papers.
  • Run three or four times a week from now so the PET is a formality.

Frequently asked questions

When will the SSC GD 2027 notification be released?

SSC's examination calendar for 2026–27 lists the Constable (GD) 2027 notification for late 2026, with applications open for about a month afterwards. As of 24 October 2026 it had not been published; check ssc.gov.in for the official notice.

When is the SSC GD 2027 exam?

The calendar lists the computer-based examination tentatively for January–March 2027. The exact dates and your city and shift are announced through the notification, the exam-city slip and the admit card.

How many vacancies will SSC GD 2027 have?

Vacancies are announced only with the notification. The 2026 cycle had 25,487 posts (CISF 14,595, CRPF 5,490, SSB 1,764, Assam Rifles 1,706, ITBP 1,293, BSF 616, SSF 23) and 2025 had 53,690.

What is the age limit for SSC GD 2027?

18 to 23 years on the cut-off date fixed in the notification, with relaxation of 3 years for OBC, 5 years for SC/ST and as per rules for ex-servicemen and other categories.

What is the SSC GD 2027 exam pattern?

One computer-based examination of 80 questions for 160 marks in 60 minutes — General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Knowledge & General Awareness, Elementary Mathematics and English/Hindi, 20 questions each, with 0.25 marks deducted for every wrong answer. PET/PST, a medical examination and document verification follow.

What was the SSC GD 2026 cut-off?

Overall male cut-offs out of 160 in the 12 October 2026 result: UR 146.178, OBC 145.557, EWS 144.206, SC 140.95, ST 135.03. 1,97,408 candidates were shortlisted for PET/PST.
