SSC CGL Maths Syllabus 2026 — Chapter by Chapter

Arithmetic — the highest-return block

Percentage, profit and loss, discount, interest, ratio, average, age, mixture, time and work, and time–speed–distance together account for roughly half the Tier I maths section. They also feed each other: once percentage is fluent, profit and loss, discount and interest all get faster. If you are starting chapter wise practice from zero, start here.

Advanced maths — algebra, trigonometry, geometry, mensuration

This block is where most candidates lose marks and where toppers gain them. Algebraic results and trigonometric identities are pattern-based and highly repeatable, so Level 1 and Level 2 practice pays off quickly. Geometry and mensuration need formula recall plus figure reading, which is exactly what timed chapter tests train.

Number system and simplification

Short, fast-scoring and very repeatable across shifts. Divisibility rules, remainder theorem, unit digit, LCM and HCF questions can be finished in under thirty seconds each once the standard shapes are familiar — free marks you should never leave in the paper.

Data interpretation and miscellaneous

DI in Tier I is usually a small table, bar or pie set worth two to three questions, and it is largely percentage and average work in disguise. Co-ordinate geometry, probability and basic statistics appear occasionally in Tier I and more regularly in Tier II Paper I.

How to Use Chapter Wise Practice for SSC CGL Maths

  1. Fix the chapter order. Percentage → profit and loss → discount → interest → ratio → average. Each one reuses the previous one, so this order cuts your learning time.
  2. Attempt Level 1 without the timer the same day you finish the concept. The goal there is correctness and formula recall, not speed.
  3. Move to Level 2 with the 30-minute clock on. Level 2 is real Tier I standard — this is the score that tells you whether the chapter is exam-ready.
  4. Read every solution, including the ones you got right. Our solutions carry the shortcut trick alongside the full method; a right answer reached the long way is still a timing problem.
  5. Keep an error notebook per chapter. If two of your last three mistakes in geometry were circle theorems, that is your next Level 3 test, not a new chapter.
  6. Convert chapter strength into paper strength. Once four or five chapters are at Level 2, attempt a previous year paper, then a full mock test with sectional timing to check that the speed survives under section pressure.

SSC CGL Maths Chapter Wise Test — FAQs

How many maths chapters are covered in SSC CGL chapter wise practice?
Thirty-five maths chapters, covering the complete SSC CGL Quantitative Aptitude syllabus from number system and percentage through to mensuration, trigonometry and data interpretation.
How many questions does each SSC CGL maths chapter test have?
Fifty questions per test, with an optional 30-minute timer. Marking follows the real exam: +2 for a correct answer, −0.5 for a wrong one, 0 for an unattempted question.
Are the SSC CGL quant chapter tests free?
Yes — chapter wise practice is open to everyone right now, and you do not need to log in to attempt a chapter test.
How many maths questions come in SSC CGL Tier I?
Twenty-five questions worth 50 marks, with a locked 15-minute sectional clock. Tier II Paper I carries thirty maths questions worth 90 marks.
Which maths chapters carry the most weight in SSC CGL?
Arithmetic as a block is the largest — percentage, profit and loss, interest, ratio, average, time and work, and time–speed–distance. Within advanced maths, geometry, mensuration and trigonometry each contribute two to three questions per shift.
Are the chapter tests available in Hindi?
Yes. Questions, options, solutions and tricks are available in हिंदी wherever the Hindi set has been uploaded for that chapter, and you pick the language when you start the test.
What is the difference between Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3?
Level 1 is basic practice right after learning a concept, Level 2 matches real SSC CGL Tier I difficulty, and Level 3 is harder — Tier II standard and tough-shift questions.
Is chapter wise practice better than solving previous year papers?
They do different jobs. Chapter tests fix one topic at a time; previous year papers show you the real difficulty and question style. Use chapter tests while learning, then previous year papers and full mock tests to test the whole section under time.
