National and International Organizations in SSC is a fact chapter. The paper asks where a body has its headquarters, when and where it was founded, who its members are and which report or index it publishes. WHO in Geneva, the New Development Bank in Shanghai, the Human Development Report from UNDP — each is one line of a list turned into a question. This bank is built from the real questions SSC CGL, CHSL, GD, MTS and RRB NTPC asked in 2022–2026, with only the lasting part kept: who heads a body now, recent summits and a country’s rank in an index belong to current affairs and are not asked here.

Marks are lost in three places. The first is cities that host several bodies: Geneva has WHO, ILO, WTO, WMO and ITU; Rome has FAO, IFAD and WFP; Vienna has IAEA, UNIDO and OPEC; Washington has the IMF and World Bank. The second is membership: Maldives is not in BIMSTEC, Iran is not in SAARC, Pakistan is not in ASEAN. The third is publisher of a report: Global Gender Gap and Energy Transition come from WEF, World Competitiveness from IMD, Corruption Perceptions from Transparency International. The tests here are built around exactly these three.

The three traps that cost the most marks

  • One city, many bodies. Geneva: WHO, ILO, WTO, WMO, ITU, WIPO, UNHCR. Rome: FAO, IFAD, WFP. Vienna: IAEA, UNIDO, OPEC. Washington DC: IMF and World Bank. Paris: UNESCO and OECD. SSC fills the options with the other bodies from the same city.
  • Who is not a member. Maldives is in SAARC but not BIMSTEC; Iran is not in SAARC; Pakistan is not in ASEAN; South Africa joined BRICS only in 2010–11. “Which is NOT a member” is the most common pattern.
  • Who publishes which report. Human Development Report – UNDP; Global Gender Gap and Energy Transition – WEF; World Competitiveness – IMD; Corruption Perceptions – Transparency International; Economic Freedom – Heritage Foundation; Global Peace and Terrorism – Institute for Economics & Peace.

Solved Examples — SSC CGL Organizations

Q1. The Atlantic Charter of August 1941, which laid down the ideas that later shaped the United Nations, was issued jointly by:
(a) Franklin D. Roosevelt and Winston Churchill
(b) Franklin D. Roosevelt and Joseph Stalin
(c) Winston Churchill and Joseph Stalin
(d) Harry S. Truman and Clement Attlee
The Atlantic Charter was issued jointly by US President Franklin D. Roosevelt and British PM Winston Churchill. It was announced on 14 August 1941 after their meeting aboard warships at Placentia Bay, Newfoundland. It set out common goals for a post-war world, such as self-determination and a wider, permanent system of general security. The term "United Nations" was first used officially in the Declaration by United Nations of 1 January 1942. Answer: (a)
Q2. Consider the following statements about the World Health Organization (WHO):
1. Its headquarters is located in Geneva, Switzerland.
2. Its constitution came into force on 7 April 1948.
3. Its headquarters is located in New York, USA.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3
Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only. WHO is the UN specialised agency for international public health. Its headquarters is in Geneva, Switzerland, not in New York. The WHO constitution came into force on 7 April 1948. Answer: (b)
Q3. Read the Assertion (A) and Reason (R) carefully.
Assertion (A): BIMSTEC is often seen as an alternative platform to SAARC for regional cooperation in South Asia.
Reason (R): Pakistan is a founding member of BIMSTEC.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true, but R is false
(d) A is false, but R is true
A is true, but R is false. BIMSTEC is widely viewed as an alternative to SAARC, whose summit process has stalled since 2014. Pakistan is not a member of BIMSTEC at all, so R is false. Five of the seven BIMSTEC members are also SAARC members. Answer: (c)
Q4. भारतीय अंतरिक्ष अनुसंधान संगठन (ISRO) के पहले अध्यक्ष कौन थे?
(a) होमी जे. भाभा
(b) विक्रम साराभाई
(c) सतीश धवन
(d) यू. आर. राव
विक्रम साराभाई ISRO के पहले अध्यक्ष थे। उन्हें भारतीय अंतरिक्ष कार्यक्रम का जनक माना जाता है। चंद्रयान-2 और चंद्रयान-3 के 'विक्रम' लैंडर का नाम उन्हीं के नाम पर रखा गया है। होमी जे. भाभा ने भारत के परमाणु ऊर्जा कार्यक्रम का नेतृत्व किया और वे परमाणु ऊर्जा आयोग के पहले अध्यक्ष थे। उत्तर: (b)
