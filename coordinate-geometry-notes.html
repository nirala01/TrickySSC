Co-ordinate Geometry

Coordinate geometry turns shapes into numbers. A handful of formulas — distance, section, slope and line — cover almost every SSC question.

1. Key formulas

Points A(x₁, y₁) and B(x₂, y₂):

QuantityFormula
Distance AB√((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)
Midpoint((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)
Internal division m : n((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m + n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m + n))
Slope of AB(y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)
Parallel linesequal slopes
Perpendicular linesm₁m₂ = −1
Centroid of a triangle((x₁ + x₂ + x₃)/3, (y₁ + y₂ + y₃)/3)
Area of triangle½ |x₁(y₂ − y₃) + x₂(y₃ − y₁) + x₃(y₁ − y₂)|

Line through two points(y − y₁) = m(x − x₁)
Intercept formx/a + y/b = 1
Distance from (x₁, y₁) to ax + by + c = 0|ax₁ + by₁ + c| / √(a² + b²)

2. Shortcuts that save time

Collinear points Three points are collinear if the triangle area formula gives 0, or if the slopes of AB and BC are equal.
  • For the line ax + by + c = 0, the slope is −a/b.
  • x-axis: y = 0; y-axis: x = 0. Distance of (p, q) from the x-axis is |q|, from the y-axis is |p|.
  • Diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other — equate midpoints to find the fourth vertex.

3. Solved examples

Example 1 Distance between (1, 2) and (4, 6) = √(9 + 16) = 5.
Example 2 Point dividing (2, 3) and (8, 9) in 1 : 2: x = (1×8 + 2×2)/3 = 4, y = (1×9 + 2×3)/3 = 5 → (4, 5).
Example 3 Area of the triangle (0, 0), (4, 0), (0, 3) = ½ |0 + 4(3 − 0) + 0| = 6.
Example 4 Distance of the origin from 3x + 4y − 10 = 0 = 10/5 = 2.

4. Traps to avoid

  1. Keep the order of subtraction the same for x and y in the slope.
  2. In the section formula, m goes with the second point.
  3. Area can come out negative inside the bars — take the positive value.
  4. Slope of a vertical line is not defined; of a horizontal line it is 0.
