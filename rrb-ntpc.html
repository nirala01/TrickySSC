What is RRB NTPC?

RRB NTPC (Railway Recruitment Boards, Non-Technical Popular Categories) is the Indian Railways recruitment for its non-technical posts. It is notified in two parts: the Graduate level, for posts such as Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor, Senior Clerk cum Typist and Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist, and the Undergraduate (10+2) level, for posts such as Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Clerk cum Typist and Trains Clerk. It is among the largest recruitments in the country by number of applicants, and CBT-1 is held in many shifts with marks normalised across them.

The current Graduate-level cycle, CEN 06/2026, notifies 3,548 vacancies — Goods Train Manager 2,750, Senior Clerk cum Typist 371, Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist 300 and Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor 127. Online applications ran from 8 October to 6 November 2026, and CBT-1 is expected in December 2026. The tests on TrickySSC are built for this Graduate-level CBT-1.

RRB NTPC Selection Process

  1. CBT-1 — 100 questions, 100 marks, 90 minutes. A screening stage: it shortlists candidates for CBT-2, normally 15 times the vacancies, community-wise.
  2. CBT-2 — 120 questions, 120 marks, 90 minutes (General Awareness 50, Mathematics 35, Reasoning 35). The merit list is prepared from these marks.
  3. Document verification of the shortlisted candidates.
  4. Medical examination to the standard fixed for the post.

Marks are normalised across shifts in both CBTs. The four posts of CEN 06/2026 have no aptitude test (CBAT) stage.

Objective multiple choice, 90 minutes (120 for eligible PwBD candidates with a scribe), +1 for a correct answer and −1/3 for a wrong one. There is no sectional timing and no separate section: the questions of the three subjects come in mixed order on one clock. Every TrickySSC mock and previous year paper follows the same order and timing.

RRB NTPC Syllabus

General Awareness

Current events of national and international importance, games and sports, art and culture of India, Indian literature, monuments and places of India, general science and life science up to Class 10, history of India and the freedom struggle, physical, social and economic geography of India and the world, Indian polity and governance, the Constitution, economy, basics of computers and computer applications, and static GK.

Mathematics

Number system, decimals and fractions, LCM and HCF, ratio and proportion, percentage, mensuration, time and work, time and distance, simple and compound interest, profit and loss, elementary algebra, geometry and trigonometry, and elementary statistics.

General Intelligence & Reasoning

Analogies, completion of number and alphabetical series, coding and decoding, mathematical operations, similarities and differences, relationships, analytical reasoning, syllogism, jumbling, Venn diagrams, puzzles, data sufficiency, statement-conclusion, statement-courses of action and decision making.

RRB NTPC Graduate 2026-27 (CEN 06/2026) — Key Facts

  • Vacancies: 3,548 Graduate-level posts across four posts.
  • Eligibility: a bachelor’s degree in any stream; age 18 to 33 years as on 1 January 2027, with relaxation as per the rules.
  • Application: 8 October to 6 November 2026; correction window 9 to 18 November 2026.
  • Fee: ₹500 (₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT-1); ₹250 for SC, ST, PwBD and women candidates (fully refunded after appearing).
  • CBT-1: expected December 2026. The exam city slip comes about 10 days before the exam and the e-call letter 4 days before it.

How to Prepare for RRB NTPC CBT-1

  • Start with previous year papers. NTPC repeats its question types and static GK facts from shift to shift; a few real shifts show you the pattern faster than any syllabus PDF. All of them are free on the RRB NTPC PYQ page.
  • Win time on General Awareness. It is 40 of the 100 questions and each one takes seconds, so the time saved there is the time you get for Maths. Revise GA and current affairs every day.
  • Fix the weak subject. Use the subject-wise PYQ and mock pages to see whether Maths, Reasoning or GA is pulling the score down, then practise that subject on its own clock.
  • Respect the negative marking. One-third of a mark goes for every wrong answer, so skip a pure guess.
  • Move to full mocks in the last weeks and sit each one in a single 90-minute stretch. The RRB NTPC mock series has 60 papers on the same 100-question pattern.

What is the RRB NTPC CBT-1 exam pattern?

CBT-1 is one computer-based paper of 100 questions for 100 marks in 90 minutes: General Awareness 40, Mathematics 30 and General Intelligence & Reasoning 30, asked in mixed order with no sectional timing. One-third of a mark is deducted for every wrong answer.

What is the selection process for RRB NTPC Graduate 2026-27?

Under CEN 06/2026 the stages are CBT-1, CBT-2, document verification and medical examination. CBT-1 screens candidates for CBT-2, and the merit list is prepared from CBT-2 marks.

Who can apply for RRB NTPC Graduate posts?

Candidates with a bachelor's degree in any stream, aged 18 to 33 years as on 1 January 2027, with age relaxation as per the rules.
