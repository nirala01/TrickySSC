RRB Station Master Maths & Basic Science — Syllabus and Solved Questions

30 marks of the 60-mark first section · CEN 08/2026 pattern

Basic Science and Mathematics make up 30 marks of the RRB Station Master CBT. This page sets out what the notification includes, which maths templates repeated in the 2026 RRB Section Controller papers on the same pattern, and nine solved questions you can check yourself.

Where Basic Science & Maths sits in the paper

The first section of the RRB Station Master CBT, Basic Science, Mathematical and Analytical Capability, carries 60 of the 100 marks. Half of it (30 marks) is Basic Science and Mathematics; the other half is Data Analysis & Interpretation. Every question is worth 1 mark and a wrong answer costs one-third of a mark.

PartMarksWhat it covers
Mathematicsabout 22Number system, ratio and proportion, averages, percentages, profit, loss and discount, time, speed and distance, time and work, algebra, linear equations, AP, LCM and HCF, geometry and trigonometry, area and volume, basic probability and statistics
Basic Scienceabout 8Units and measurements, mass, weight and density, work, power and energy, kinetic energy, friction, speed, velocity and relative velocity, heat and temperature, basic electricity, levers and simple machines

The split between science and maths is TrickySSC’s reading of the syllabus and is used in the mock tests; the notification gives 30 marks for the two together. The RRB Section Controller paper, held on the same pattern in 2026, had no science at all, so Basic Science is the one part of the Station Master paper that has no real previous paper behind it.

What the maths questions look like

In the Section Controller shifts of 2026 the same arithmetic templates came back from shift to shift: mixtures sold at a gain, ‘A alone, B and C together’ work problems, coins and cards in probability, melting and recasting solids, and mean, median and range. The questions are moderate — one or two steps — so speed matters more than tricks.

Solved Maths questions

Q1.

A milkman mixes water with pure milk and sells the mixture at the cost price of milk. He gains 25%. What is the ratio of water to milk in the mixture?

  1. 1 : 5
  2. 1 : 4
  3. 1 : 3
  4. 4 : 1
Show answer & solution

Answer: (b) 1 : 4

Selling at cost price with a 25% gain means the cost of what he sells is 100/125 = 4/5 of the selling price. So in every 5 litres sold, 4 litres are milk and 1 litre is water. Water : milk = 1 : 4.

Q2.

A can complete a piece of work in 12 days. B and C together can complete it in 8 days. In how many days will A, B and C together complete it?

  1. 4 days
  2. 4⅔ days
  3. 4⅘ days
  4. 5 days
Show answer & solution

Answer: (c) 4⅘ days

One day’s work of A = 1/12, of B and C together = 1/8. Together = 1/12 + 1/8 = 2/24 + 3/24 = 5/24. Time = 24/5 = 4.8 days = 4⅘ days.

Q3.

One card is drawn at random from a well-shuffled pack of 52 cards. What is the probability that it is a king or a heart?

  1. 17/52
  2. 4/13
  3. 1/4
  4. 1/13
Show answer & solution

Answer: (b) 4/13

There are 13 hearts and 4 kings, but the king of hearts is counted in both. Favourable cards = 13 + 4 − 1 = 16. Probability = 16/52 = 4/13.

Q4.

A solid metal sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into a solid cylinder of base radius 4 cm. What is the height of the cylinder?

  1. 12 cm
  2. 16 cm
  3. 18 cm
  4. 24 cm
Show answer & solution

Answer: (c) 18 cm

Volume stays the same. (4/3)π × 6³ = π × 4² × h ⇒ (4/3) × 216 = 16h ⇒ 288 = 16h ⇒ h = 18 cm.

Q5.

For the data 7, 3, 9, 3, 8, 6, 5, what is the sum of the median and the range?

  1. 11
  2. 12
  3. 13
  4. 9
Show answer & solution

Answer: (b) 12

In order: 3, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9. The median is the 4th value, 6. The range is 9 − 3 = 6. Median + range = 12.

Solved Basic Science questions

Q6.

A block of mass 600 g has a volume of 750 cm³. What is its density, and will it float in water (density 1 g/cm³)?

  1. 0.8 g/cm³, it floats
  2. 1.25 g/cm³, it sinks
  3. 0.8 g/cm³, it sinks
  4. 1.25 g/cm³, it floats
Show answer & solution

Answer: (a) 0.8 g/cm³, it floats

Density = mass / volume = 600 / 750 = 0.8 g/cm³. It is less than the density of water, so the block floats.

Q7.

A body of mass 2 kg is moving at 10 m/s. How much work must be done to bring it to rest?

  1. 20 J
  2. 50 J
  3. 100 J
  4. 200 J
Show answer & solution

Answer: (c) 100 J

By the work–energy theorem, the work needed equals the kinetic energy: ½mv² = ½ × 2 × 10² = 100 J.

Q8.

In a lever, an effort of 50 N applied 60 cm from the fulcrum balances a load placed 20 cm from the fulcrum on the other side. What are the load and the mechanical advantage?

  1. 150 N, 3
  2. 100 N, 2
  3. 150 N, 1/3
  4. 16.7 N, 3
Show answer & solution

Answer: (a) 150 N, 3

Effort × effort arm = load × load arm ⇒ 50 × 60 = L × 20 ⇒ L = 150 N. Mechanical advantage = load / effort = 150 / 50 = 3.

Q9.

Resistors of 6 Ω and 3 Ω are connected in parallel across a 12 V battery. What current is drawn from the battery?

  1. 1.33 A
  2. 4 A
  3. 6 A
  4. 9 A
Show answer & solution

Answer: (c) 6 A

Parallel resistance = (6 × 3)/(6 + 3) = 2 Ω. Current = V/R = 12/2 = 6 A.

How to prepare this part

  • Arithmetic first. Percentages, ratio, averages, profit and loss, time and work, and speed carry most of the 22 maths marks. They are the same chapters as SSC and RRB NTPC, so earlier practice counts.
  • Science from Class 9–10 physics. The topics in the notification are all Class 9–10 mechanics, heat and electricity. Learn the formulas (density, work, power, kinetic energy, Ohm’s law, series and parallel resistance, lever rule) and do numericals, not theory.
  • Mensuration and statistics at a basic level. Recasting solids and mean, median, mode and range come up often and take under a minute each once the formulas are fixed.
  • Keep time for the data questions. This part and Data Interpretation share one 60-mark section; finish the quick maths and science questions first and then take the charts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many marks do Maths and Basic Science carry in RRB Station Master?

Basic Science and Mathematics together carry 30 marks out of 100, inside the 60-mark first section that also has 30 marks of Data Analysis and Interpretation. TrickySSC mocks use about 22 maths and 8 science questions.

What Basic Science topics are in the RRB Station Master syllabus?

Units and measurements, mass, weight and density, work, power and energy, kinetic energy, friction, speed and relative velocity, heat and temperature, basic electricity, and levers and simple machines.

Did the RRB Section Controller paper have science questions?

No. The Section Controller CBT of 2026, held on the same pattern, had mathematics and data questions in its first section but no science. Basic Science is new in the Station Master paper.

Is there negative marking in the Station Master maths section?

Yes. Every question carries 1 mark and one-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer.

The pattern and syllabus on this page follow CEN 08/2026, which calls its section-wise distribution indicative.
