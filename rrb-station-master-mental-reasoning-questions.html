RRB Station Master Mental Reasoning — Syllabus and Solved Questions

20 marks · CEN 08/2026 pattern

Mental Reasoning is the 20-mark third section of the RRB Station Master CBT: analogy, series, coding-decoding, and ranking and arrangement. It is the most predictable section and the fastest to score in. Here is what it covers and seven solved questions.

What Mental Reasoning contains

Mental Reasoning is the third section of the RRB Station Master CBT and carries 20 marks. The notification lists analogy, series completion, coding-decoding, and ranking and arrangement problems. In the 2026 RRB Section Controller papers on the same pattern the section looked the same in every shift: letter-cluster analogies and odd-one-out, number and letter series, coding, sign interchange, ranking and single-question arrangement puzzles.

These are the same question types as SSC and RRB NTPC reasoning, which makes this the quickest section to score in. Aim to finish it in 15–18 minutes and save the time for the 60-mark first section.

Solved Mental Reasoning questions

Q1.

ACE : BDF :: GIK : ?

  1. HJL
  2. HIK
  3. IJL
  4. GHJ
Show answer & solution

Answer: (a) HJL

Each letter moves one step forward: A→B, C→D, E→F. So G→H, I→J, K→L, giving HJL.

Q2.

Find the odd one out: 27, 64, 125, 144, 216

  1. 27
  2. 64
  3. 144
  4. 216
Show answer & solution

Answer: (c) 144

27, 64, 125 and 216 are cubes (3³, 4³, 5³, 6³). 144 is 12², not a cube.

Q3.

Complete the series: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ?

  1. 95
  2. 125
  3. 127
  4. 128
Show answer & solution

Answer: (c) 127

Each term is double the previous plus 1: 3×2+1 = 7, 7×2+1 = 15, …, 63×2+1 = 127.

Q4.

In a certain code, TRAIN is written as USBJO. How is STATION written in that code?

  1. TUBUJPO
  2. TUBTJPO
  3. RSZSHNM
  4. TUBUIPO
Show answer & solution

Answer: (a) TUBUJPO

Each letter is replaced by the next one: T→U, R→S, A→B, I→J, N→O. So S→T, T→U, A→B, T→U, I→J, O→P, N→O = TUBUJPO.

Q5.

If ‘+’ means ‘×’, ‘×’ means ‘−’, ‘−’ means ‘÷’ and ‘÷’ means ‘+’, what is the value of 8 + 3 × 12 − 4 ÷ 5?

  1. 24
  2. 26
  3. 29
  4. 31
Show answer & solution

Answer: (b) 26

Replace the signs: 8 × 3 − 12 ÷ 4 + 5. By BODMAS: 24 − 3 + 5 = 26.

Q6.

In a row of students, Ravi is 14th from the left end and 20th from the right end. How many students are in the row?

  1. 32
  2. 33
  3. 34
  4. 35
Show answer & solution

Answer: (b) 33

Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 14 + 20 − 1 = 33.

Q7.

Five trains P, Q, R, S and T leave a station one after another. Q leaves immediately after P. S leaves before P. T leaves last. R does not leave first. Which train leaves first?

  1. P
  2. Q
  3. R
  4. S
Show answer & solution

Answer: (d) S

T is fifth. P cannot be first because S leaves before it, and Q cannot be first because it comes right after P. R is not first either. So S leaves first. (The rest can be S, P, Q, R, T or S, R, P, Q, T — the clues fix only the first and the last train.)

How to prepare Mental Reasoning

  • Learn the alphabet positions (A = 1 … Z = 26) and their reverse (A = 26 … Z = 1). Letter analogies, series and coding all depend on them.
  • Know squares to 30 and cubes to 15 for number series and odd-one-out questions.
  • For sign interchange, rewrite the expression first, then apply BODMAS; most errors come from changing signs in your head.
  • Draw arrangements. A quick line or circle diagram for ranking and seating questions avoids misreading ‘immediately after’ and ‘left of’.

Frequently Asked Questions

What topics come in RRB Station Master Mental Reasoning?

Analogy, series completion, coding-decoding, and ranking and arrangement problems, as listed in CEN 08/2026. The 2026 Section Controller papers also had odd-one-out, sign interchange and short arrangement puzzles.

How many marks does Mental Reasoning carry?

20 marks out of 100, with one-third of a mark deducted for every wrong answer.

Is Station Master Mental Reasoning like SSC reasoning?

Yes. The question types are the same as SSC and RRB NTPC reasoning, so SSC and NTPC reasoning practice applies directly.

How much time should I spend on Mental Reasoning?

About 15 to 18 minutes of the 120, so that most of the time goes to the 60-mark first section with maths, science and data questions.
