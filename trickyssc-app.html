What is the TrickySSC app?

TrickySSC (also searched as “Tricky SSC”) is a free SSC exam preparation platform with mock tests, PYQs and chapter-wise practice on the web. The TrickySSC app is its Android version: pick your exam once, then practise full-length mocks, previous year papers and chapter-wise tests, and see exactly where you lose marks after every test.

You sign in with Google or your mobile number, and everything you attempt is saved to your account, so your Dashboard shows your overall performance for the exam you are preparing for. The app needs an internet connection to load tests and save your results.

What you get in the app

🎯

Mock tests

Full-length SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD and RRB mocks in the real exam pattern, with free tests in every exam to try first.

Year-wise PYQs of the exams, with the number of tests shown for each year, plus chapter-wise PYQs.

One screen with score, accuracy, quality of attempt, painful questions, chapter-wise result and comparison with toppers.

📈

Dashboard & Exam Readiness

Overall performance for the exam you pick, with chapter analysis and a Practice button on weak chapters.

📰

Current affairs

Current affairs for SSC and railway exams with practice questions, kept in the same app.

🏆

The weekly challenge papers open for practice after the results, with solutions.

How to get started

Install the TrickySSC app from Google Play once it is live (mid-December 2026). Until then, open trickyssc.com in your browser.

Sign in with Google or your mobile number, then choose your exam. You can switch exams any time.

Try the free tests and PYQs to check the level, and unlock a pass only if you like what you see.

Study your analysis after every test, then fix weak chapters with the Practice button.

TrickySSC app – frequently asked questions

What is the TrickySSC app?

The TrickySSC app is the Android app of TrickySSC (trickyssc.com), a free SSC exam preparation platform. It brings mock tests, previous year papers (PYQs), chapter-wise tests, current affairs and detailed test analysis for SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, SSC MTS, SSC GD, RRB NTPC and RRB Station Master into one app.

Is the TrickySSC app on Google Play? When will it launch?

The app is currently in closed testing on Google Play and is planned to go public by mid-December 2026. This page is updated the moment it goes live. Until then the whole TrickySSC website works in any mobile or desktop browser.

Is the TrickySSC app free to download?

Yes, installing the app is free. Every exam has free tests so you can try the quality first, and the PYQs are free. Full mock series are unlocked with a pass, which you buy inside the app through Google Play. The Full Access pass for all mock series is priced at ₹99; current prices for each exam are always shown in the Plans tab of the app.

Which exams does the TrickySSC app cover?

SSC CGL (Tier 1 and Tier 2), SSC CHSL (Tier 1 and Tier 2), SSC MTS, SSC GD Constable, RRB NTPC, RRB NTPC UG and RRB Station Master. Most new courses show up in the app by themselves; if one is missing, update the app (see the update question below).

A course or feature is not visible in my app. How do I update the app?

Some new courses and features need the latest version of the app. To update: 1) Open the Google Play Store. 2) Tap your profile picture, then Manage apps & device. 3) Open Updates available, find TrickySSC and tap Update. You can also search TrickySSC in the Play Store and tap Update. 4) When it finishes, close the app completely and open it again. 5) If a new course still does not show, tap your exam name at the top of the app and pick the course from the list. The app also shows an Update now popup when a new version is available. Still stuck? Write to contact@trickyssc.com with the course name.

Is there a TrickySSC APK download?

TrickySSC does not share APK files. The only official way to install the app will be the Google Play Store listing for com.trickyssc.app. Please avoid APKs from other websites, because they are not published by us and can be unsafe.

Is the TrickySSC app available for iPhone (iOS)?

Not at the moment. The app is for Android. iPhone users can use the full TrickySSC website in Safari or Chrome with the same tests and the same account.

Do I need a separate account for the app?

No. You sign in with Google or with your mobile number (OTP). Your passes are linked to your TrickySSC account, so use the same login that you use on the website.

Are the tests in Hindi and English?

Yes. Before a test starts you choose Hindi or English wherever both versions are available, and the solutions are written to be easy to follow.

What analysis do I get after a test?

You get a one-screen Test Analysis with your score, accuracy, subject-wise and chapter-wise performance, the questions that cost you the most, a comparison with top scorers, solutions for every question and your leaderboard rank. The Dashboard tab then shows your overall performance for the exam you pick, including Exam Readiness and Chapter Analysis.

Is the All India Challenge Mock available in the app?

The live All India Challenge is held on the website: SSC CGL Tier 2 every Sunday and SSC CHSL Tier 1 every Saturday, with an All India rank. In the app, the challenge papers open for practice two days after each exam, once the results are out. Details are on the challenge pages of the website.

Do I need internet to use the app?

Yes. Tests, solutions and your results are loaded and saved online, so you need an internet connection.

How can I join the app testing or get a launch notification?

Follow the Tricky SSC channel on Telegram (t.me/trickyssc), where the Google Play launch will be announced first. For questions about testing, write to us through the Contact page.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Use the report-an-issue option inside the app, or email contact@trickyssc.com. After a finished test the app also lets you rate it and leave a suggestion, both optional.

How can I delete my account and data?

Open the account deletion page on our website (trickyssc.com/delete-account.html) and follow the steps. Our Privacy Policy explains what data we keep.

Is TrickySSC connected to the Staff Selection Commission or the Railway Recruitment Boards?

No. TrickySSC is an independent exam preparation platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected with any government body. Always check the official notification on ssc.gov.in or the official RRB websites for exam dates, syllabus and rules.
