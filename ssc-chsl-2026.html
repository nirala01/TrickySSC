Who can apply

Educational qualification

  • Passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination from a recognised board, on or before 7 October 2026.
  • For Data Entry Operator posts in the Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India, Class 12 in the Science stream with Mathematics as a subject.

Age limit

18 to 27 years as on 1 August 2026. Relaxation follows the standard central government rules — 3 years for OBC, 5 years for SC and ST, and the specified relaxations for PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved groups.

SSC CHSL 2026 syllabus

Tier 1 is a single 60-minute paper with 25 questions from each of the four subjects below. Tier 2 goes deeper into the same four subjects and adds a 15-question Computer Knowledge module.

English Language

  • Spot the error, fill in the blanks, sentence improvement
  • Synonyms, antonyms, spelling, idioms and phrases, one word substitution
  • Active/passive voice, direct/indirect narration, parajumbles
  • Cloze passage and reading comprehension

General Intelligence (Reasoning)

  • Analogy, classification, series (number, letter, figure)
  • Coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, ranking
  • Syllogism, Venn diagrams, statement and conclusion
  • Mirror/water image, paper folding, embedded figures, matrix, dice

Quantitative Aptitude

  • Number system, LCM-HCF, simplification, surds, percentage, ratio, average
  • Profit & loss, discount, simple and compound interest, mixture
  • Time & work, time-speed-distance, algebra, trigonometry
  • Geometry, mensuration, data interpretation (tables, bar, pie, line)

General Awareness

  • Indian history, culture, geography, polity and Constitution
  • Economy, scientific research, general science
  • Static GK: awards, books, important days, organisations
  • Current affairs of the last 8–10 months

How to prepare from here

The Tier 1 exam date has not been announced yet, so use this period to complete the syllabus and build speed before the exam schedule is released. Finish it properly rather than skimming it.

  • Start with previous year papers. CHSL repeats its question types heavily. Ten real papers teach you more about the pattern than any syllabus PDF.
  • Fix the weak chapter, not the weak subject. Chapter-wise tests tell you whether the problem is percentages or time and work, which a full mock cannot.
  • Move to full mocks about six weeks out, and treat the 60-minute clock as non-negotiable. CHSL mock tests are coming soon on TrickySSC; until then attempt the previous year papers as full timed tests.
  • Give General Awareness fifteen minutes a day rather than a cram week. Current affairs from the last eight to ten months carries most of the weight.

How to apply

  1. Go to ssc.gov.in/login and complete One Time Registration if you have not already.
  2. Log in with your registration ID and password.
  3. Open the SSC CHSL 2026 application link and fill in your personal, educational and communication details.
  4. Upload your photograph and signature to the stated specifications, or use Aadhaar-based authentication during OTR to simplify the ID check at the centre.
  5. Pay the fee if it applies to you, and confirm the payment status inside your login.
  6. Preview the form, submit it, and save a printout.

Applications are accepted only through the SSC portal and the official mySSC mobile application. Nothing submitted through any other route is accepted. Apply several days early — the portal slows badly in the final 48 hours.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to apply for SSC CHSL 2026?

Online applications close at 11:00 PM on 7 October 2026. The fee can be paid until 11:00 PM on 8 October 2026, and the correction window runs from 14 to 16 October 2026.

How do I apply online for SSC CHSL 2026?

Log in at ssc.gov.in/login with your One Time Registration ID and password, open the SSC CHSL 2026 application link, fill in the form, upload your photograph and signature, pay the fee if it applies to you and submit before 11:00 PM on 7 October 2026. Applications are accepted only through the SSC portal and the mySSC app.

How many vacancies are there?

Approximately 2,536 tentative vacancies. The number may be revised, and post-wise and category-wise details are published separately.

When is the Tier 1 exam?

Not announced. The notification says the Tier 1 and Tier 2 schedules will be notified separately, so watch the SSC notice board.

What is the age limit?

18 to 27 years as on 1 August 2026, with the usual relaxations for reserved categories.

I am waiting for my Class 12 result. Can I apply?

You must have passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination on or before 7 October 2026. Read the eligibility clause in the notification carefully before you pay the fee.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. Tier 1 deducts 0.50 marks per wrong answer. Tier 2 deducts 1 mark per wrong answer in every scored module.

Are SSC CHSL previous year papers free on TrickySSC?

Yes. Every CHSL previous year paper on the site is a free online test with solutions in English and Hindi. You will need a free TrickySSC account so your score, rank and section-wise analysis are saved.

Where can I practise SSC CHSL chapter-wise tests?

TrickySSC has chapter-wise tests for every Tier 1 subject — Quantitative Aptitude, English, Reasoning and General Awareness. The Tier 1 syllabus is common to SSC CHSL and SSC CGL, so the same chapter tests work for both. Each chapter has a 25-question test with an optional 15-minute timer and solutions in English and Hindi.
