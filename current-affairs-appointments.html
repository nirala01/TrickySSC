October 2026Monthly notes

Six Governors named

  • Reshuffle – announced by President Murmu on 12 October 2026.
  • Transfers – Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar moved from Keralam to Tamil Nadu; Om Prakash Mathur from Sikkim to Keralam.
  • New Governors – Braj Mohan Ram (Odisha), Dr Sudha Yadav (Karnataka), Dr Dhal Singh Bisen (Uttarakhand) and Dushyant Gautam (Sikkim).
  • Karnataka – Sudha Yadav succeeds Thaawarchand Gehlot and is the state's second woman Governor after V.S. Ramadevi.
  • Odisha – Braj Mohan Ram takes over from Hari Babu Kambhampati.
Governor: appointed by the President under Article 155 and holds office during the President's pleasure (Article 156); must be at least 35 years old.

Three new Supreme Court judges

  • Oath – CJI Surya Kant swore in three judges on 6 October; working strength rose to 37 against 38 sanctioned.
  • Names – Justices Sunita Agarwal, Devendra Kumar Upadhyaya and Aparesh Kumar Singh.
  • Earlier posts – Chief Justices of the Gujarat, Delhi and Telangana High Courts respectively.
  • Women – Justice Agarwal's arrival gives the Court three women judges.
  • Collegium – the CJI-led collegium had recommended them on 28 September.
Supreme Court judges: appointed by the President under Article 124(2); they retire at 65; Parliament fixes the Court's strength.

Banks and PSUs

  • HDFC Bank – Anup Bagchi picked as MD & CEO, subject to RBI approval; the bank's first outside choice for the post.
  • Kotak Mahindra Bank – Anup Kumar Saha will be MD & CEO from 1 January 2027, after Ashok Vaswani.
  • New India Assurance – Lavanya R. Mundayur named CMD until May 2029.
  • ONGC – Sunil Kumar to be chairman from 7 December 2026.
  • RBI – Sudhakar Malli became an Executive Director from 1 October.

Posts abroad and in sport

  • Morocco – Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri of the Authenticity and Modernity Party became the country's first woman Prime Minister.
  • Adobe – Indian-origin Anil Chakravarthy, an IIT-BHU alumnus, takes over as President and CEO on 1 December 2026; Shantanu Narayen becomes executive chair.
  • ICC – Jay Shah was re-elected unopposed as ICC Chairman for a second term.
  • OCA – shooter Rajeshwari Kumari joined the Olympic Council of Asia's Athletes Commission (2026–30).
  • IOCINDIO – INCOIS Director T.M. Balakrishnan Nair was elected its chair.

Deaths

  • Yasuo Fukuda – former Prime Minister of Japan, died at 90.
  • Jogen Chowdhury – painter and former Rajya Sabha MP, died in Kolkata at 87.
  • Singeetham Srinivasa Rao – film director of about 60 films, died at 94.
  • Cheruvayal Raman – Kerala's 'living paddy gene bank' farmer and Padma Shri (2023), died at 74.
  • Eva Marie Saint – Oscar-winning actress of 'On the Waterfront', died at 102.

September 2026Monthly notes

High Court chiefs

  • Eight at once – on 5 September the President named chiefs for eight High Courts under Article 217(1); all 25 High Courts now have regular chiefs.
  • Patna – Justice Valluri Kameswar Rao, from the Delhi High Court.
  • Calcutta – Justice Ravindra V. Ghuge, from the Bombay High Court.
  • Punjab and Haryana – Justice Ashwani Kumar Mishra, who was its acting chief.
  • Chhattisgarh – Justice Krushna Ram Mohapatra, from the Orissa High Court.
Article 217: HC judges are appointed by the President after consulting the CJI and the state Governor; they retire at 62.

Governor and national bodies

  • Andaman and Nicobar – former UP Deputy CM Dinesh Sharma became the 14th Lieutenant Governor, sworn in at Sri Vijaya Puram on 15 September.
  • Predecessor – Admiral (retd) D.K. Joshi, LG since October 2017.
  • CBSE – Mandeep K. Bhandari (IAS 2001, AGMUT) named chairperson on 25 September, taking over from Lokhande Prashant Sitaram, who held extra charge.
  • Ram Mandir – Air Marshal (retd) Jitendra Mishra became the first CEO of Ayodhya's Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, picked by the temple trust for three years.
  • Safai Karamcharis – Bhagwat Prasad Makwana now heads the National Commission for Safai Karamcharis.
NCSK: set up in 1994 under the NCSK Act, 1993; the Act lapsed in 2004 and the panel now functions without statutory backing under the Social Justice Ministry.

Boards and committees

  • CBFC – the film board was reconstituted with 18 members for three years under the Cinematograph Act, 1952; last done in 2017.
  • FCRA panel – MP Sanjay Jaiswal heads the 31-member JPC on the FCRA Amendment Bill, 2026.
  • UNGA – Bangladesh's Khalilur Rahman took charge as President of the 81st session on 8 September.
  • CPA – Refilwe Mtshweni-Tsipane is the Commonwealth Parliamentary Association's President for 2026.

Indians on global posts

  • APPU – Dr Vinay Prakash Singh, an Indian Postal Service officer, won a second term unopposed as Secretary General of the Asian-Pacific Postal Union at its Bangkok Congress (14th) on 4 September; term 2027–2030.
  • ICA – IFFCO chairman Dileep Sanghani got an at-large seat on the global board of the International Cooperative Alliance.
  • Disney – Indian-origin Karandeep Anand became The Walt Disney Company's first Chief Technology Officer.
  • Apple – John Ternus took over as Apple's CEO from 1 September 2026.
  • Shakti Sharma – Air Vice Marshal Shakti Sharma became the first woman non-medical officer to reach two-star rank; she is Assistant Chief of Air Staff (Education).

August 2026Monthly notes

Bangladesh's new President

  • Oath – Mirza Fakhrul Islam Alamgir was sworn in as Bangladesh's 23rd President at Bangabhaban, Dhaka, on 21 August 2026.
  • Vote – MPs elected him a day earlier with 255 votes; his rival, Col (retd) Oli Ahmad of the Jamaat-led alliance, got 88.
  • Contest – it was the first contested presidential poll in Bangladesh since 1991.
  • Ceremony – Acting Speaker Kayser Kamal administered the oath in the presence of PM Tarique Rahman.
  • Profile – a 78-year-old BNP veteran and its longest-serving secretary general; he was Local Government minister before.
President of Bangladesh: elected by members of the Jatiya Sangsad for five years; largely a ceremonial head of state who appoints the Prime Minister and the Chief Justice.

Indians in firsts

  • GSI chief – Varsha Ashok Aglawe took charge as GSI's 54th Director General, the first woman to lead it in its 176 years.
  • Her career – a palaeontologist who joined GSI in 1992; she replaced Asit Saha and earlier headed GSI's Eastern Region at Kolkata.
  • Zambia – Delhi-born Abha Nayar Patel is the first Indian-origin woman judge on Zambia's Supreme Court, where she was appointed in May 2026.
  • IAF – Squadron Leader Bhawana Kanth became the first woman in the IAF to qualify as a Fighter Combat Leader, clearing the course at TACDE, Gwalior, in August.
  • Army – Captain Shivani became the first woman officer to serve as Aide-de-Camp to the Chief of the Army Staff.
Geological Survey of India: founded in 1851 to find coal for the railways; Thomas Oldham was its first head; an attached office of the Ministry of Mines with headquarters in Kolkata.

Secretary-level changes at the Centre

  • Higher Education – Deepti Gaur Mukerjee was named Secretary, Department of Higher Education, in place of Vineet Joshi.
  • Panchayati Raj – Vineet Joshi moved to head the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.
  • Corporate Affairs – Pallavi Jain Govil became Corporate Affairs Secretary.
  • Youth Affairs – Sunil Paliwal took over as Secretary, Department of Youth Affairs.
  • Waterways – Sushil Kumar Lohani was made Chairman of the Inland Waterways Authority of India.

Panels and posts

  • NEET probe – the Supreme Court set up a high-powered committee under retired SC judge Justice R. Subhash Reddy to look into police action during the NEET protests.
  • Sports awards panel – Justice (Retd) Arun Kumar Mishra chaired the selection committee for the National Sports Awards 2025.
  • Kanth's record – Bhawana Kanth, from Darbhanga in Bihar, was among India's first three women fighter pilots in 2016.
  • Earlier first – in 2019 she became the first Indian woman cleared to fly day combat missions, on the MiG-21 Bison.

July 2026Monthly notes

Union government changes

  • Education Ministry – Pralhad Joshi took over the Education Ministry as additional charge on 25 July 2026 after Dharmendra Pradhan resigned.
  • NITI Aayog CEO – the ACC on 22 July named Anurag Jain, a 1989-batch IAS officer and Chief Secretary of Madhya Pradesh, as CEO for two years.
  • MP Chief Secretary – Ashok Barnwal was picked to take over from him in Madhya Pradesh.
  • Exam task force – Nandan Nilekani heads the new high-powered task force on exam reforms.
  • DRDO – Dr Jagannath Nayak became Director General of Missiles and Strategic Systems.

Commissions, courts and bodies

  • Minorities panel – Harjit Singh Grewal became Chairperson of the National Commission for Minorities, with Munawari Begum as Vice-Chairperson.
  • Term – the NCM chair and members serve three-year terms.
  • Legal services – CJI Surya Kant nominated Justice B.V. Nagarathna as Chairperson of the Supreme Court Legal Services Committee.
  • World chess – Viswanathan Anand took charge as interim President of FIDE.
  • Biofuel fellows – the Global Biofuels Alliance named India's Ruchi Agarwal and Abhishek Sahu among its first Global Biofuel Champion Fellows.
National Commission for Minorities: statutory body under the NCM Act, 1992, with a chairperson, a vice-chairperson and five members; six notified minorities – Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis and Jains.

New leaders abroad

  • UK – Andy Burnham, former Mayor of Greater Manchester, became Prime Minister on 20 July 2026 after Keir Starmer resigned; he is the UK's seventh PM in about ten years.
  • AI minister – the UK made Indian-origin MP Kanishka Narayan its first cabinet-level AI minister.
  • Peru – Keiko Fujimori was elected President of Peru.
  • Lithuania – Mindaugas Sinkevičius became Prime Minister.
  • ECOWAS – Senegal's President Bassirou Diomaye Faye took over as chair of the West African bloc from Sierra Leone's Julius Maada Bio.

Global posts and roles

  • UN rights chief – Volker Türk of Austria got a second term as UN High Commissioner for Human Rights.
  • AI for Good – Mukesh Ambani and Sunil Bharti Mittal are founding members of the UN-backed AI for Good Global Commission, led by the ITU and co-chaired by Paul Kagame and Marc Benioff.
  • EU Council – Ireland took the rotating six-month chair of the EU Council on 1 July 2026.
  • Space flight – NASA astronaut Anil Menon, of Indian origin, left for the ISS on Soyuz MS-29.
  • UNSC race – India named its 2028–29 Security Council campaign 'SHANTI'; elections are due in June 2027.

June 2026Monthly notes

Defence and security

  • Army Chief – Gen Dhiraj Seth took over as the 31st Chief of the Army Staff on 30 June, succeeding Gen Upendra Dwivedi.
  • Background – an Armoured Corps officer and NDA alumnus – the first from that arm to lead the Army since 1997; he had been Vice Chief since April 2026.
  • CDS – Gen N.S. Raja Subramani assumed office as Chief of Defence Staff on 31 May; he also heads the Department of Military Affairs as Secretary.
  • Intelligence Bureau – IPS officer Mahesh Dixit was named Director.
  • DRDO – Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh was given additional charge as DRDO chairman after Dr Samir V. Kamat.

Judiciary and law officers

  • Five SC judges – sworn in on 2 June, taking the court to 37 judges against a sanctioned 38; four were High Court chief justices – Sheel Nagu, Shree Chandrashekhar, Sanjeev Sachdeva and Arun Palli.
  • V. Mohana – senior advocate Venkita Subramani Mohana became only the second woman raised straight from the Bar to the Supreme Court, after Indu Malhotra (2018).
  • Solicitor General – Tushar Mehta got a third straight term, from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2029.
  • Patna High Court – Justice Meenakshi Madan Rai was made Chief Justice.
  • Collegium – Justice P.S. Narasimha joined the Supreme Court Collegium after Justice J.K. Maheshwari retired.
Supreme Court judges: appointed by the President under Article 124(2); an ordinance in 2026 raised the sanctioned strength to 38 including the Chief Justice.

States and Parliament

  • Karnataka – D.K. Shivakumar took oath as Chief Minister on 3 June after Siddaramaiah resigned on 28 May; G. Parameshwara became Deputy CM.
  • Oath – administered by Governor Thaawarchand Gehlot at Lok Bhavan, Bengaluru.
  • Rajya Sabha – polls for 24 seats in 10 states ended unopposed; Mallikarjun Kharge was among those returned.
  • Deve Gowda – the nearly 50-year parliamentary career of former PM H.D. Deve Gowda ended on 25 June.
  • George Kurian – resigned as Minister of State for Minority Affairs and Fisheries.

Other national posts

  • NSC – Prof Saibal Chattopadhyay became Chairperson of the National Statistical Commission.
  • Labour statistics panel – economist Prof Alakh N. Sharma heads a high-level technical expert committee.
  • WHO India – Dr Yvan Hutin was appointed WHO Representative to India.
  • NSCS – Lt Gen Rajiv Ghai became Military Adviser in the National Security Council Secretariat.
  • Agro-biodiversity panel – the National Biodiversity Authority reconstituted its expert committee with Dr P.L. Gautam as chair.

Global posts and elections

  • FATF – India's Vivek Agrawal was elected Vice-President, the first time India holds the post.
  • World Bank – economist Neelkanth Mishra became an Executive Director for three years.
  • ITLOS – Prof Bimal N. Patel won election to the International Tribunal for the Law of the Sea as a judge for 2026–35.
  • Polls abroad – Nikol Pashinyan won in Armenia and Abiy Ahmed in Ethiopia; Mette Frederiksen began a third term as Denmark's PM.
  • BCB – Tamim Iqbal became the youngest president of the Bangladesh Cricket Board.

May 2026Monthly notes

Assembly election results

  • Count – results for West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Assam and Puducherry came on 4 May.
  • West Bengal – the BJP won and Suvendu Adhikari took oath on 9 May as the state's first BJP Chief Minister, ending 15 years of TMC rule.
  • Tamil Nadu – actor C. Joseph Vijay's TVK won 108 of 234 seats and formed a coalition; he took oath on 10 May.
  • Kerala – the Congress-led UDF won and V.D. Satheesan became Chief Minister.
  • Assam – Himanta Biswa Sarma took oath on 12 May for a second straight term; the NDA also kept Puducherry.

Defence chiefs

  • CDS – Lt Gen N.S. Raja Subramani (retd) was named the next Chief of Defence Staff after Gen Anil Chauhan, whose term ended on 30 May.
  • Profile – commissioned into 8th Garhwal Rifles in 1985; the CDS post was created in 2020 (first holder Gen Bipin Rawat).
  • Navy – Vice Admiral Krishna Swaminathan, an electronic warfare expert, was named the next Chief of the Naval Staff after Adm Dinesh K. Tripathi (retired 31 May).
  • Earlier post – Flag Officer Commanding-in-Chief, Western Naval Command.

Media and film posts

  • Prasar Bharati – lyricist Prasoon Joshi became Chairman.
  • What it runs – Doordarshan and All India Radio.
  • CBFC – Shashi Shekhar Vempati became Chairperson for three years, taking over from Joshi.
  • IFFI – filmmaker Ashutosh Gowariker is Festival Director of the 57th International Film Festival of India, Goa (IFFI began in 1952).

Other appointments

  • UIDAI – Saurabh Vijay, a 1998-batch IAS officer of Maharashtra cadre, is the new CEO.
  • NITI Aayog – Dr Joram Aniya and Dr R. Balasubramaniam were made full-time members.
  • US Fed – the Senate confirmed Kevin Warsh as the 17th Chair of the Federal Reserve on 13 May (54–45); he took over from Jerome Powell.
  • Uganda – Jayant Chaudhary attended President Yoweri Museveni's swearing-in as India's special envoy.

April 2026Monthly notes

NITI Aayog reshaped

  • Vice-Chair – economist Ashok Kumar Lahiri, a former Chief Economic Adviser, became NITI Aayog Vice-Chairperson by an order of 24 April.
  • Replaced – he took over from Suman K. Bery; the post carries the rank of a Cabinet Minister.
  • Kept on – former Cabinet Secretary Rajiv Gauba stays as a full-time member.
  • New members – economist K.V. Raju, IISER Bhopal director Gobardhan Das, DST Secretary Abhay Karandikar and AIIMS Delhi director M. Srinivas.
  • Chair – the Prime Minister heads NITI Aayog.
NITI Aayog: National Institution for Transforming India, a non-statutory policy think tank that replaced the Planning Commission on 1 January 2015; its Governing Council includes all Chief Ministers.

Courts and key posts in India

  • First woman CJ – Justice Lisa Gill was sworn in as Chief Justice of the Andhra Pradesh High Court on 25 April, the first woman in the post.
  • Route – she came from the Punjab and Haryana High Court and succeeded Justice Dhiraj Singh Thakur; CJs are appointed under Article 217.
  • DGCA – Veer Vikram Yadav became Director General of Civil Aviation, succeeding Faiz Ahmed Kidwai.
  • Selectors – the BCCI extended chief selector Ajit Agarkar's term by one year.
  • Wrestling coach – Georgia's Shako Bentinidis was named coach of India's men's freestyle team.

Indian envoys

  • UK – Secretary (East) Periasamy Kumaran was named High Commissioner to the United Kingdom.
  • China – Vikram Doraiswami, until now in London, moves to Beijing as Ambassador.
  • Belgium and EU – Pranay Verma, earlier High Commissioner in Dhaka, will be Ambassador to Belgium and the European Union.
  • Bangladesh – former Railway Minister Dinesh Trivedi was named High Commissioner to Bangladesh.

Leaders abroad

  • Myanmar – former junta chief Min Aung Hlaing was elected President by Parliament on 3 April with 429 of 584 votes.
  • Hungary – Péter Magyar's Tisza party won the 12 April election by a landslide, ending Viktor Orbán's 16-year rule.
  • Vietnam – Communist Party chief To Lam also took over as President.
  • Djibouti – Ismail Omar Guelleh was re-elected for a sixth term.
  • Iraq – Kurdish politician Nizar Amedi became President.

Global posts

  • Apple – hardware chief John Ternus will take over as Apple CEO on 1 September 2026; Tim Cook becomes executive chairman.
  • Australia – Lt Gen Susan Coyle became the first woman to lead the Australian Army.
  • Arab League – Egypt's Nabil Fahmy was named Secretary-General.
  • BWF – P.V. Sindhu began her term on the Badminton World Federation Council (2026–29).
  • Infosys – Carlos Alcaraz was signed as the IT firm's global brand ambassador.

March 2026Monthly notes

Governors reshuffled

  • Date – President Droupadi Murmu changed Governors and LGs in nine states and UTs on 5 March 2026.
  • West Bengal – R.N. Ravi, moved from Tamil Nadu after C.V. Ananda Bose resigned.
  • Bihar – Lt Gen (retd) Syed Ata Hasnain, replacing Arif Mohammed Khan.
  • Nagaland – ex-Bihar Assembly Speaker Nand Kishore Yadav.
  • Tamil Nadu – Kerala Governor Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar holds extra charge.

More Lok Bhavan changes

  • Maharashtra – Jishnu Dev Varma, shifted from Telangana.
  • Telangana – Shiv Pratap Shukla, shifted from Himachal Pradesh.
  • Himachal Pradesh – Kavinder Gupta, earlier LG of Ladakh.
  • Delhi LG – former diplomat Taranjit Singh Sandhu.
  • Ladakh LG – Vinai Kumar Saxena, moved from Delhi.
Governor: appointed by the President under Article 155; holds office during the President's pleasure (Article 156), usually for five years.

New leaders abroad

  • Nepal – Balendra Shah ('Balen'), 35, ex-Kathmandu mayor and rapper, was sworn in as PM on 27 March 2026.
  • RSP – his Rastriya Swatantra Party won 182 of 275 seats in the 5 March poll held after the 2025 Gen Z protests.
  • Firsts – Nepal's youngest elected PM and the first from the Madhesi community; he beat K.P. Sharma Oli in Oli's own seat.
  • Iran – Mojtaba Khamenei, 56, was named the third Supreme Leader on 8–9 March after his father was killed.
  • Selector – the 88-member Assembly of Experts, a clerical body, chooses Iran's Supreme Leader.

Other posts

  • Chile – José Antonio Kast took office as President of Chile.
  • China envoy – Vikram Doraiswami, until now High Commissioner in London, was named India's Ambassador to China.
  • NCBC – Sadhvi Niranjan Jyoti was named Chairperson of the National Commission for Backward Classes.
  • Census face – sand artist Sudarsan Pattnaik became brand ambassador of Census 2027.

February 2026Monthly notes

New heads of government abroad

  • Bangladesh – Tarique Rahman of the BNP was sworn in as the 11th Prime Minister on 17 Feb after the 12 Feb election.
  • Majority – the BNP-led alliance won 212 seats in the 300-member Jatiya Sangsad.
  • Background – son of Ziaur Rahman and Khaleda Zia; he replaced the interim set-up of Muhammad Yunus.
  • Netherlands – Rob Jetten became Prime Minister, the youngest in the country's history.
  • Thailand – Anutin Charnvirakul returned as Prime Minister.

More leaders and posts

  • Barbados – Mia Mottley won a third straight term as Prime Minister.
  • Bulgaria – Andrey Gyurov was named Prime Minister.
  • Japan – Sanae Takaichi is Japan's first woman Prime Minister.
  • Board of Peace – Bulgarian diplomat Nickolay Mladenov is its High Representative for Gaza.
  • UN AI panel – Prof. B. Ravindran of IIT Madras joined the UN Independent International Scientific Panel on AI.

Posts in India

  • Manipur CM – Yumnam Khemchand Singh (BJP) became Chief Minister after President's Rule ended on 4 Feb.
  • Deputies – Nemcha Kipgen became Manipur's first woman Deputy CM; Losii Dikho is the other Deputy CM.
  • GIFT City – banker Uday Kotak was named Chairman of GIFTCL, the company that runs GIFT City.
  • Thailand envoy – Punit Agarwal is India's new Ambassador to Thailand.
  • Railways – Hari Shankar Verma became Member (Operations and Business Development), Railway Board.

January 2026Monthly notes

Security and vigilance chiefs

  • CVC – Praveen Vashisht (IPS 1991) made Vigilance Commissioner.
  • BSF – Praveen Kumar (IPS 1993) is Director General.
  • ITBP – Shatrujit Kapoor (IPS 1990) is Director General.
  • NIA – Rakesh Agarwal given additional charge as DG.
Central Vigilance Commission: set up 1964; statutory since CVC Act 2003; one CVC + up to two VCs; appointed by the President; term 4 years or age 65.

Other key posts

  • Maharashtra – Sunetra Pawar is the state's first woman Deputy CM.
  • Air Force – Air Marshal Nagesh Kapoor is Deputy Chief of the Air Staff.
  • Nuclear regulator – A.K. Balasubramanian heads the AERB.
  • RBI board – Aruna Sundararajan is a government nominee on the new Payments Regulatory Board.

High Court chiefs

  • Kerala – Soumen Sen (moved from Meghalaya).
  • Meghalaya – Revati Mohite Dere.
  • Sikkim – A. Muhamed Mustaque.
  • Uttarakhand – Manoj Kumar Gupta.
  • Patna – Sangam Kumar Sahoo.

Global roles and foreign leaders

  • UN – D.B. Venkatesh Varma chairs the UN Secretary-General's Advisory Board on Disarmament Matters, 2026–27.
  • Venezuela – Delcy Rodríguez made acting President.
  • Africa – Yoweri Museveni re-elected in Uganda for a seventh term; Mamady Doumbouya elected President of Guinea.
  • Yemen – Shaya Mohsen al-Zindani is the new PM.
  • Afghan mission – Taliban's Mufti Noor Ahmad Noor is acting chargé d'affaires in Delhi.
