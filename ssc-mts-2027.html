SSC MTS 2027 Vacancy

Vacancies are announced with the notification and are often revised upwards before the result. The 2025 cycle opened with a smaller tentative figure and ended with nearly 8,000 posts after revision. Posts are allotted state-wise and by age group (18–25 and 18–27), so the cut-off that matters is the one for your state, category and age group.

Who can apply

Educational qualification

  • Passed Class 10 (matriculation) or an equivalent examination from a recognised board, on or before the closing date in the notification.

Age limit

18 to 25 years for Multi-Tasking Staff, and 18 to 27 years for Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) and some MTS posts, on the cut-off date fixed in the notification. Relaxation follows the central government rules — 3 years for OBC, 5 years for SC and ST, 10 years for PwBD (more for reserved PwBD) and the specified relaxations for ex-servicemen.

Exam pattern

Computer-based examination — 90 questions, 270 marks, two sessions of 45 minutes

Objective multiple choice, computer based, +3 for each correct answer. Session I carries no negative marking; in Session II 1 mark is deducted for every wrong answer. Both sessions are compulsory, 45 minutes each (60 minutes for eligible scribe candidates). The paper is offered in Hindi, English and 13 regional languages, and marks are normalised across shifts.

How to apply

  1. Go to ssc.gov.in and complete One Time Registration if you have not already.
  2. Log in with your registration ID and password.
  3. Open the MTS and Havaldar application link of the new cycle and fill in your personal, educational and communication details, including the posts and region you want.
  4. Upload your photograph and signature to the stated specifications.
  5. Pay the fee if it applies to you, and confirm the payment status inside your login.
  6. Preview the form, submit it, and save a printout.

Applications are accepted only through the SSC portal and the official mySSC mobile application. Apply several days early — the portal slows badly in the final 48 hours.

How to prepare from here

The notification has not come yet, which is time you can use to finish the syllabus properly.

  • Bank Session I. Maths and Reasoning carry 120 marks with no negative marking; aim to finish the 40 questions comfortably inside 45 minutes.
  • Fix one section at a time. Section tests in maths, reasoning, GK and English show exactly where marks leak.
  • Move to full mocks about six weeks out, with both 45-minute sessions on the clock.
  • Give GK fifteen minutes a day. Art and culture, history and polity carry most static questions; current affairs is a few questions in every shift.
  • Havaldar aspirants: walk the PET distance against the clock every week.

Frequently asked questions

When will the SSC MTS 2027 notification be released?

SSC had not published the notification for the next Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) Examination as of 8 November 2026. Applications usually stay open for about a month after the notice. Check ssc.gov.in for the official notification.

When is the SSC MTS 2027 exam?

The exam dates come with the notification and the admit card. For comparison, the 2025 cycle was notified on 26 June 2025 and its computer-based examination was held from 4 to 20 February 2026.

How many vacancies does SSC MTS have?

Vacancies are announced with the notification and are often revised later. The 2025 cycle ended with nearly 8,000 posts across Multi-Tasking Staff and Havaldar after SSC revised its tentative vacancy list.

What is the age limit for SSC MTS?

18 to 25 years for Multi-Tasking Staff, and 18 to 27 years for Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) and some MTS posts, on the cut-off date in the notification, with relaxation of 3 years for OBC, 5 years for SC/ST and as per rules for other categories.

What is the SSC MTS exam pattern?

One computer-based paper of 90 questions for 270 marks in two 45-minute sessions: Numerical & Mathematical Ability and Reasoning in Session I with no negative marking; General Awareness and English in Session II with 1 mark deducted for each wrong answer. Havaldar candidates also take a qualifying PET and PST.

What is the salary of SSC MTS?

Multi-Tasking Staff and Havaldar are Pay Level 1 posts (₹18,000–56,900) under the 7th Pay Commission, plus dearness, house rent and transport allowances that depend on the city of posting.
