What is SSC MTS?

The Staff Selection Commission conducts the Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldar examination to fill Group ‘C’ non-gazetted, non-ministerial posts in central government ministries, departments and offices across India. Multi-Tasking Staff do office support work — keeping records, running the dak, maintaining files and premises — while Havaldars in the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) and the Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN) also take a physical test. Both are Pay Level 1 posts and need only a Class 10 pass.

The 2025 cycle was notified on 26 June 2025, ended with nearly 8,000 posts after SSC revised its tentative vacancies, and held its computer-based examination from 4 to 20 February 2026. As of 8 November 2026, the notification for the next exam has not been released, so its computer-based exam will fall in 2027.

SSC MTS Selection Process

  1. Computer-Based Examination — one paper, 90 questions, 270 marks, two sessions of 45 minutes, offered in Hindi, English and 13 regional languages. Marks are normalised across shifts.
  2. Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test — for Havaldar only; qualifying.
  3. Document verification and the final merit list, prepared state-wise and category-wise from the normalised CBE marks, with separate lists for the 18–25 and 18–27 age groups.

Objective multiple choice, +3 for every correct answer. Session I has no negative marking; in Session II 1 mark is deducted for each wrong answer. Each session lasts 45 minutes (60 minutes for eligible scribe candidates), and both sessions are compulsory. SSC MTS Syllabus

Numerical & Mathematical Ability

Integers and whole numbers, LCM and HCF, decimals and fractions, relationships between numbers, fundamental arithmetical operations and BODMAS, percentage, ratio and proportion, work and time, direct and inverse proportion, averages, simple interest, profit and loss, discount, area and perimeter of basic figures, distance and time, lines and angles, simple graphs and data, squares and square roots.

Reasoning Ability & Problem Solving

Alpha-numeric series, coding and decoding, analogy, following directions, similarities and differences, jumbling, problem solving and analysis, non-verbal reasoning based on diagrams, age calculations, calendar and clock.

General Awareness

The environment and its application to society, current events, everyday science, and India and its neighbouring countries — especially sports, history, culture, geography, the economic scene, general polity including the Indian Constitution, and scientific research.

English Language & Comprehension

Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms and their correct usage, spot the error, fill in the blanks, spelling, idioms and phrases, one-word substitution, sentence improvement, active and passive voice, direct and indirect narration, sentence rearrangement, cloze passage and reading comprehension.

Eligibility, Physical Standards and Salary (in brief)

  • Education: Class 10 (matriculation) from a recognised board. Age: 18–25 years for MTS; 18–27 years for Havaldar and some MTS posts, with the usual relaxations.
  • Havaldar PET: walking 1,600 m in 15 minutes (male) or 1 km in 20 minutes (female). PST: 157.5 cm height and 76 cm chest with 5 cm expansion for men; 152 cm and 48 kg for women, with relaxations as per the notification.
  • Pay: Level-1, ₹18,000–56,900 plus allowances.

Fee, how to apply and the 2025 cycle’s dates are on the SSC MTS 2027 page.

How to Prepare for SSC MTS 2027

  • Bank Session I. Maths and Reasoning carry 120 marks with no negative marking — never leave a question there blank, and practise until the 40 questions fit comfortably inside 45 minutes.
  • Play Session II for accuracy. With −1 a wrong answer, attempt a GK or English question only when you can narrow it down to one option.
  • Give GK fifteen minutes a day rather than a cram week; art and culture, history and polity carry the most static questions, and current affairs is a few questions in every shift.
  • Move to full mocks about six weeks out. The SSC MTS mock series runs the two sessions on separate 45-minute clocks with the real marking, and the maths, reasoning, GK and English tests let you fix one section at a time.
  • Havaldar aspirants: walk the PET distance against the clock every week from now.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SSC MTS preparation on TrickySSC free?

Are the SSC MTS tests available in Hindi and English?

Yes. Every mock can be attempted in English or Hindi, chosen when you start. In the Hindi paper Q1–65 are in Hindi and the English section (Q66–90) stays in English, as in the real exam.

What is the SSC MTS exam pattern?

One computer-based paper of 90 questions for 270 marks in two sessions of 45 minutes. Session I: Numerical & Mathematical Ability (20) and Reasoning Ability & Problem Solving (20), no negative marking. Session II: General Awareness (25) and English Language & Comprehension (25), with 1 mark deducted for each wrong answer. Every question carries 3 marks.

Is there a descriptive paper in SSC MTS?

No. Since the 2023 cycle the MTS examination has one computer-based paper only. Havaldar candidates additionally take a qualifying PET and PST.

When will the SSC MTS 2027 notification be released?

As of 8 November 2026 SSC had not released the notification for the next MTS and Havaldar exam. Check ssc.gov.in for the official notice; the TrickySSC SSC MTS 2027 page is updated when it appears.

How are the TrickySSC MTS mocks timed?

Exactly like the real paper: Session I (Maths and Reasoning) runs for 45 minutes and is submitted before Session II (General Awareness and English) opens for its own 45 minutes. You move freely between the two sections inside the open session.
