Why chapter-wise PYQs across all SSC exams?

Solving a full paper tells you your score. Solving the same questions chapter-wise tells you which chapters cost you marks — and shows the exact patterns SSC repeats year after year. CGL, CHSL, GD, MTS and CPO are set by the same commission from the same syllabus, so a chapter's questions from every exam together give you the complete picture of how it is asked. These tests are built only from real previous-year questions, so the difficulty is the real difficulty.

Questions are added automatically whenever a new paper of any SSC exam is uploaded to TrickySSC. Each chapter's tests are fixed in paper order, so Test 3 is always the same Test 3 — new questions open a new test at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Are the questions really from previous SSC papers?
Yes. Every question comes from a SSC CGL, SSC CHSL and SSC GD paper hosted on TrickySSC, and the exam, date and shift are shown under the question in the test.
Why are CGL, CHSL and GD questions together in one test?
The chapter is the same; only the exam differs. Seeing every way SSC has asked a chapter — across exams and years — is the fastest way to master it. The label under each question tells you which exam and shift it came from.
How many questions are in one test?
25. A new chapter shows its first test once 10 questions have been tagged; that test grows to 25, after which every further test is a full 25.
Can I take the tests in Hindi?
Yes — the हिंदी button opens the same test in Hindi wherever the Hindi paper is available, with a View in English toggle on every question.
Is there negative marking?
Tests use +2 for a correct answer and −0.5 for a wrong one, like the chapter tests. Unattempted questions score zero.
