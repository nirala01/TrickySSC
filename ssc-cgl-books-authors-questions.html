Books and Authors in SSC is a pure fact chapter. There is nothing to work out: the paper asks who wrote a book, whose autobiography a title is, or which book a famous song, character or idea comes from. Every question in these tests is based on a book or author that SSC has actually asked in a CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD or RRB NTPC paper between 2022 and 2026, and only static facts are used — books released in the last few months belong to current affairs and are left out.

Marks are lost in three places. The first is one author, many books: Nehru, Tagore, Tharoor, Kalam and Premchand each have several titles, and SSC offers the wrong one of the same author. The second is look-alike autobiographies: sportspersons’ and film stars’ memoirs with similar titles — ‘Playing It My Way’ against ‘Playing to Win’, ‘Unbreakable’ against ‘Unfinished’. The third is era and language: which Mughal or Sultanate book was in Persian or Turkish, which classic was Sanskrit, Tamil or Awadhi, and which freedom-era book was first written in Gujarati or Marathi. The tests here are built around exactly these three.

Books and Authors gives zero or one of the 25 General Awareness questions in a typical SSC CGL Tier I shift, and together with awards and important days, static GK accounts for one to two questions in most shifts. The three traps that cost the most marks

  • One author, many books. Nehru wrote The Discovery of India, Glimpses of World History and An Autobiography; Kalam wrote Wings of Fire (with Arun Tiwari) and Turning Points; Tharoor wrote The Great Indian Novel (fiction) and An Era of Darkness (non-fiction). SSC puts a second book of the same author among the options.
  • Look-alike sports autobiographies. Playing It My Way is Sachin Tendulkar, 281 and Beyond is VVS Laxman, Playing to Win is Saina Nehwal, Ace Against Odds is Sania Mirza, Unbreakable is Mary Kom and The Race of My Life is Milkha Singh. The options are always four players of the same sport or era.
  • Language of the original. The Baburnama was written in Chagatai Turkish, the Akbarnama in Persian, Ramcharitmanas in Awadhi, Hind Swaraj in Gujarati and Gita Rahasya in Marathi (in Mandalay jail). Statement questions often hide the wrong language in one line.

Solved Examples — SSC CGL Books and Authors

Q1. Which novel by R.K. Narayan, set in the fictional town of Malgudi, won him the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1960?
(a) The Guide
(b) Swami and Friends
(c) The English Teacher
(d) The Financial Expert
The Guide (1958), the story of Raju, a tourist guide who comes to be taken for a holy man, won Narayan the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1960 and was filmed as ‘Guide’ (1965) with Dev Anand. Swami and Friends (1935) was his first novel and introduced Malgudi; The English Teacher and The Financial Expert are other Malgudi novels. Answer: (a)
Q2. Consider the following statements about ‘Hind Swaraj’:
1. Mahatma Gandhi wrote it in 1909 while sailing from London to South Africa.
2. It was originally written in Gujarati.
3. It was first serialised in the journal ‘Young India’.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3
Gandhi wrote Hind Swaraj in Gujarati in November 1909 aboard the SS Kildonan Castle, as a dialogue between a Reader and an Editor. It was first serialised in ‘Indian Opinion’, his journal in South Africa, not in ‘Young India’ — statement 3 is wrong. Gandhi translated it into English himself in 1910. Answer: (b)
Q3. Read the Assertion (A) and Reason (R) carefully.
Assertion (A): India’s National Song ‘Vande Mataram’ is taken from the Bengali novel ‘Anandamath’ by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee.
Reason (R): ‘Anandamath’ is set against the backdrop of the Sannyasi Rebellion of the late 18th century.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true, but R is false
(d) A is false, but R is true
Anandamath (1882) contains ‘Vande Mataram’, and its story is set during the Sannyasi Rebellion in Bengal — both statements are true. But the setting of the novel does not explain why the song was taken from it, so R does not explain A. Tagore sang the song at the 1896 Calcutta session of the Congress. Answer: (b)
Q4. प्रेमचंद का कौन-सा उपन्यास गरीब किसान होरी और उसकी पत्नी धनिया की कठिनाइयों को दर्शाता है?
(a) गबन
(b) निर्मला
(c) गोदान
(d) सेवासदन
गोदान (1936) प्रेमचंद का अंतिम पूर्ण उपन्यास है, जिसके मुख्य पात्र किसान होरी, उसकी पत्नी धनिया और पुत्र गोबर हैं; शीर्षक गाय के दान की होरी की अधूरी इच्छा की ओर संकेत करता है। गबन रमानाथ और जालपा के गहनों के मोह पर, निर्मला दहेज और बेमेल विवाह पर तथा सेवासदन समाज में स्त्रियों की स्थिति पर आधारित है। उत्तर: (c)

How to Prepare Books and Authors for SSC CGL

  1. Learn by author, not by book. Write each author once with all their asked titles — Premchand: Godan, Gaban, Nirmala, Mansarovar. When SSC offers a second book of the same author, you will spot it at once.
  2. Keep a separate autobiography sheet. Title – person – co-writer, split into sports, politics and films. This one list covers the most-asked part of the chapter.
  3. Tie old books to their history. Learn the Mughal chronicles with the emperor, the freedom-struggle books with the leader and the year, and the music treatises with the period — the history chapters then revise them for you.
  4. Read the solutions, not just the answer. Each solution here carries six or seven related facts — reading them is how 200 questions cover the books SSC keeps asking.
  5. Check yourself on real questions. One test a day keeps the earlier lists fresh, and the Books and Authors previous year questions show exactly how SSC words them.
