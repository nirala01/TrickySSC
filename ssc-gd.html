What is SSC GD Constable?

The Staff Selection Commission conducts the Constable (GD) examination to recruit General Duty constables for the Central Armed Police Forces — Border Security Force (BSF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) and Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) — along with the Secretariat Security Force (SSF), Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles and, in some cycles, Sepoy in the Narcotics Control Bureau and constables in the National Investigation Agency. Because the minimum qualification is Class 10 and the vacancy count runs into tens of thousands, it is the largest uniformed-services recruitment in India by applicants.

The 2026 cycle notified 25,487 vacancies (CISF 14,595, CRPF 5,490, SSB 1,764, Assam Rifles 1,706, ITBP 1,293, BSF 616, SSF 23), held its computer-based examination from 27 April to 30 May 2026 and declared the result on 12 October 2026 with 1,97,408 candidates shortlisted for PET/PST. The next cycle, SSC GD 2027, is listed in SSC’s 2026–27 calendar with the CBE tentatively in January–March 2027.

SSC GD Selection Process

  1. Computer-Based Examination (CBE) — 80 questions, 160 marks, 60 minutes, offered in Hindi, English and 13 regional languages. Marks are normalised across shifts.
  2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — a timed run; qualifying only.
  3. Physical Standard Test (PST) — height and chest measurement; qualifying only.
  4. Detailed Medical Examination (DME) and review medical examination.
  5. Document verification and the final merit list, prepared from the normalised CBE marks, category-wise, state-wise and force-wise.

SSC GD Syllabus

General Intelligence & Reasoning

Analogies, similarities and differences, spatial visualisation and orientation, visual memory, discrimination, observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning and figural classification, number and alphabet series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, order and ranking, mirror images, paper folding and embedded figures.

General Knowledge & General Awareness

India and its neighbouring countries, sports, history, culture, geography, economic scene, general polity, the Indian Constitution, scientific research and current events of national and international importance.

Elementary Mathematics

Number systems, computation of whole numbers, decimals and fractions, relationships between numbers, fundamental arithmetical operations, percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount, mensuration, time and distance, ratio and time, time and work.

English / Hindi

Basic comprehension and writing ability — error spotting, fill in the blanks, synonyms, antonyms, spelling, idioms, one-word substitution, sentence improvement, voice, narration, cloze passage and reading comprehension in English, or the equivalent vyakaran, shabd gyan and gadyansh in Hindi.

Eligibility, Physical Standards and Salary (in brief)

  • Education: Class 10 (matriculation) from a recognised board. Age: 18–23 on the notification’s cut-off date, with the usual relaxations.
  • PET: 5 km in 24 minutes (male) or 1.6 km in 8½ minutes (female). PST: 170 cm / 157 cm height, 80 cm chest with 5 cm expansion for men, with category and regional relaxations.
  • Pay: Level-3, ₹21,700–69,100 plus allowances.

Fee, the full standards table, how to apply and the 2026 vacancy split by force are on the SSC GD 2027 notification page.

How to Prepare for SSC GD 2027

  • Start with previous year papers. GD repeats its question types heavily; ten real shifts teach you the pattern faster than any syllabus PDF. All of them are free on the SSC GD PYQ page.
  • Fix the weak part, not the weak subject. Use the part-wise PYQ and mock pages to find whether it is series, blood relations, percentage or GK that leaks marks.
  • Give GK fifteen minutes a day rather than a cram week; it is the only part that cannot be solved by method.
  • Move to full mocks about six weeks out and treat the 60-minute clock as non-negotiable. The SSC GD mock series is built on the 80-question pattern with +2 / −0.25 marking.
  • Run from day one. The PET follows the CBE within weeks; three or four runs a week from the start makes it a formality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SSC GD preparation on TrickySSC free?

Yes. All SSC GD previous year papers (PYQ) and their subject-wise tests are completely free to attempt online. In the SSC GD Mock Test Series, Mock 1 and Mock 2 are free; the full series of 50 mocks and every subject-wise mock test unlocks with the SSC GD pass at Rs 49 for one year.

Are the SSC GD tests available in Hindi and English?

Yes. Every paper and mock can be attempted in English or Hindi, chosen when you start. In the Hindi paper, Part D is the Hindi language section, exactly as in the real exam.

What is the SSC GD exam pattern?

A single computer-based examination of 80 questions for 160 marks in 60 minutes: 20 questions each from General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Knowledge & General Awareness, Elementary Mathematics, and English/Hindi, with 0.25 marks deducted for every wrong answer. It is followed by PET/PST, a medical examination and document verification.

Who can apply for SSC GD Constable?

Indian citizens who have passed Class 10 (matriculation) from a recognised board and are 18 to 23 years old on the date fixed in the notification, with the usual age relaxation for reserved categories and ex-servicemen.

What is the SSC GD Constable salary?

Constable (GD) is a Pay Level-3 post on the 7th CPC matrix, Rs 21,700 to 69,100 per month, plus dearness allowance, house rent allowance, transport allowance, ration money and the other allowances applicable to the force and posting.

What was the SSC GD 2026 cut-off?

In the result declared on 12 October 2026, the male cut-offs out of 160 were UR 146.178, OBC 145.557, EWS 144.206, SC 140.95 and ST 135.03 (overall figures; state- and force-wise cut-offs vary). 1,97,408 candidates were shortlisted for PET/PST.
