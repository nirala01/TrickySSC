Memorise the table of standard angles and the three Pythagorean identities. With them, nearly every SSC trigonometry question becomes substitution or simplification.

1. Concepts in brief

The six ratios in a right triangle

For an angle θ in a right-angled triangle, name the sides relative to θ: opposite (across from θ), adjacent (next to θ) and hypotenuse (longest side).

RatioDefinitionReciprocal
sin θopposite / hypotenusecosec θ
cos θadjacent / hypotenusesec θ
tan θopposite / adjacentcot θ

Where the identities come from

Pythagoras says opposite² + adjacent² = hypotenuse². Divide by hypotenuse² and you get sin²θ + cos²θ = 1. Divide by adjacent² instead and you get tan²θ + 1 = sec²θ. So all three identities are one fact in three forms.

Find all ratios from one

Given one ratio, draw the triangle, find the third side by Pythagoras, and read off any other ratio. tan θ = 5/12 gives sides 5, 12, 13, so sin θ = 5/13 and cos θ = 12/13.

3. Shortcuts that save time

Sin 0, 30, 45, 60, 90 sin takes √0/2, √1/2, √2/2, √3/2, √4/2 → 0, 1/2, 1/√2, √3/2, 1. cos is the same list in reverse.
  • If A + B = 90°, then tan A · tan B = 1 and sin A = cos B.
  • Product of tan 1° × tan 2° × … × tan 89° = 1 (pairs multiply to 1; tan 45° = 1).
  • If sin θ + cosec θ = 2, then θ = 90°; sin θ + cosec θ is never between −2 and 2.

4. Solved examples

Example 1 sin²30° + cos²60° = 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2.
Example 2 sin 25° / cos 65° = 1, because cos 65° = sin 25°.
Example 3 If tan θ = 3/4, then sin θ = 3/5, cos θ = 4/5 (3-4-5 triangle).
Example 4 Max value of 3 sin θ + 4 cos θ is √(9 + 16) = 5.

More solved examples (exam level)

Example 5 If sin θ = 3/5, the sides are 3, 4, 5, so tan θ + sec θ = 3/4 + 5/4 = 2.
Example 6 (1 − sin²θ) sec²θ = cos²θ × 1/cos²θ = 1.
Example 7 tan 15° × tan 75° = tan 15° × cot 15° = 1.
Example 8 If sec θ + tan θ = 2, then sec θ − tan θ = 1/2 (their product is 1). Adding: 2 sec θ = 5/2, so sec θ = 5/4.

5. Practice questions (with answers)

Tap a question to see its answer.

Q1. Find sin 60° cos 30° + cos 60° sin 30°.

Answer: 1 (3/4 + 1/4).

Q2. Simplify cosec²θ − cot²θ.

Answer: 1.

Q3. If tan θ = 1 and θ is acute, find θ.

Answer: 45°.

Q4. Find the minimum value of 9 sin²θ + 16 cos²θ.

Answer: 9 (it equals 9 + 7 cos²θ).

Q5. Find sin 37° / cos 53°.

Answer: 1 (37° + 53° = 90°).

6. Traps to avoid

  1. Don't mix up sin 30° = 1/2 with cos 30° = √3/2.
  2. sin²θ means (sin θ)², not sin(θ²).
  3. tan 90° is not defined — questions with it usually have another way in.
  4. Check which ratio is asked: the exam often gives sin and asks cosec or cot.
