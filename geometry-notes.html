Geometry is a set of theorems you must recall in a second. This sheet lists the ones SSC asks most — triangles, centres and circles — in the form they are used.

1. Concepts in brief

Lines and angles

  • Angles on a straight line add to 180°; angles round a point add to 360°.
  • Vertically opposite angles are equal.
  • With parallel lines and a transversal: corresponding angles are equal, alternate angles are equal, and co-interior angles add to 180°.

Polygons

Interior angles of an n-sided polygon add to (n − 2) × 180°. Exterior angles always add to 360°, so each exterior angle of a regular polygon is 360°/n.

Congruent and similar

Congruent triangles are identical in shape and size (tests: SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS). Similar triangles have the same shape but different sizes: corresponding angles are equal and sides are in one fixed ratio. Area goes as the square of that ratio, volume as the cube.

Pythagorean triplets to recognise

3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25, 9-40-41 and their multiples (6-8-10, 9-12-15, …).

4. Solved examples

Example 1 In a triangle, ∠A = 70°. ∠BIC = 90° + 35° = 125°; ∠BOC = 140°; ∠BHC = 110°.
Example 2 In a right triangle with legs 6 and 8, hypotenuse 10: circumradius = 5, inradius = (6 + 8 − 10)/2 = 2.
Example 3 Two circles of radii 9 and 4 have centres 13 apart. Direct common tangent = √(169 − 25) = 12; transverse = √(169 − 169) = 0 (they touch externally).
Example 4 A tangent PT = 12 from an external point; the secant cuts the circle at A and B with PA = 8. PB = 144/8 = 18, so chord AB = 10.

More solved examples (exam level)

Example 5 Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 150°. Each exterior angle = 30°, so the number of sides = 360/30 = 12.
Example 6 In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 4, DB = 6 and AE = 6. By the basic proportionality theorem 4/6 = 6/EC, so EC = 9.
Example 7 In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, ∠A = 70°. The opposite angle ∠C = 110°.
Example 8 A chord of length 16 cm lies in a circle of radius 10 cm. Distance from the centre = √(100 − 64) = 6 cm.

5. Practice questions (with answers)

Tap a question to see its answer.

Q1. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find them.

Answer: 40°, 60°, 80°.

Q2. Each exterior angle of a regular hexagon?

Answer: 60°.

Q3. A point is 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm. Length of the tangent from it?

Answer: 12 cm.

Q4. Two similar triangles have sides in the ratio 3 : 5. Ratio of areas?

Answer: 9 : 25.

Q5. What is the angle in a semicircle?

Answer: 90°.

6. Traps to avoid

  1. Centroid divides the median 2 : 1 from the vertex, not from the side.
  2. For an obtuse triangle, orthocentre and circumcentre lie outside the triangle.
  3. Angle at centre is double — don't halve instead of doubling.
  4. Secant–tangent: use the whole secant (PB), not just the chord.
