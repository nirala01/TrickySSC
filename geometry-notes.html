Geometry is a set of theorems you must recall in a second. This sheet lists the ones SSC asks most — triangles, centres and circles — in the form they are used.

1. Triangle facts

TopicFact
Angle sum180°; exterior angle = sum of the two opposite interior angles
CentroidMeeting point of the medians; divides each median 2 : 1 from the vertex
IncentreMeet of angle bisectors; centre of the incircle; ∠BIC = 90° + A/2
CircumcentreMeet of perpendicular bisectors; ∠BOC = 2A
OrthocentreMeet of altitudes; ∠BHC = 180° − A
Right trianglecircumradius = hypotenuse/2; inradius = (a + b − c)/2
SimilarityArea ratio = (side ratio)²
Basic proportionalityA line parallel to one side divides the other two sides in the same ratio
Angle bisector theoremBD/DC = AB/AC

2. Circle theorems

TheoremStatement
Angle at centreTwice the angle at the circumference on the same arc
Angle in a semicircle90°
Same segmentAngles in the same segment are equal
Cyclic quadrilateralOpposite angles add to 180°; exterior angle = interior opposite angle
TangentPerpendicular to the radius at the point of contact
Two tangents from a pointEqual in length
ChordsIntersecting inside: PA × PB = PC × PD
Secant–tangentPT² = PA × PB
Direct common tangent√(d² − (r₁ − r₂)²)
Transverse common tangent√(d² − (r₁ + r₂)²)

3. Solved examples

Example 1 In a triangle, ∠A = 70°. ∠BIC = 90° + 35° = 125°; ∠BOC = 140°; ∠BHC = 110°.
Example 2 In a right triangle with legs 6 and 8, hypotenuse 10: circumradius = 5, inradius = (6 + 8 − 10)/2 = 2.
Example 3 Two circles of radii 9 and 4 have centres 13 apart. Direct common tangent = √(169 − 25) = 12; transverse = √(169 − 169) = 0 (they touch externally).
Example 4 A tangent PT = 12 from an external point; the secant cuts the circle at A and B with PA = 8. PB = 144/8 = 18, so chord AB = 10.

4. Traps to avoid

  1. Centroid divides the median 2 : 1 from the vertex, not from the side.
  2. For an obtuse triangle, orthocentre and circumcentre lie outside the triangle.
  3. Angle at centre is double — don't halve instead of doubling.
  4. Secant–tangent: use the whole secant (PB), not just the chord.
