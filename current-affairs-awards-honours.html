October 2026Monthly notes

Nobel science prizes 2026

  • Medicine – Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel, for light-gated ion channels and optogenetics (switching cells on with light).
  • Physics – Francis Halzen alone, for the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and finding high-energy neutrinos from space.
  • IceCube – a cubic-kilometre sensor array buried in the ice at the South Pole; Halzen proposed the idea in 1988.
  • Chemistry – Henri B. Kagan (France) and Kenso Soai (Japan), for non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis.
  • Why it matters – the work explains how one mirror-image (chiral) form of a molecule can come to dominate – key in making medicines.

Nobel Literature, Peace and Economics

  • Literature – Canadian poet and classicist Anne Carson, born in Toronto in 1950; Alice Munro (2013) was an earlier Canadian winner.
  • Peace – Navi Pillay of South Africa, of Indian Tamil descent, 'for her efforts to promote peace and international law'.
  • Pillay – UN High Commissioner for Human Rights (2008–14) and earlier a judge at the ICC and the Rwanda tribunal.
  • Economics – Robert E. Hall and Michael Woodford, for the microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation.
  • Prize – 12 million Swedish kronor per prize; awarded on 10 December, Nobel's death anniversary.
Nobel Prize: set up by Alfred Nobel's will and first given in 1901; the Peace Prize is presented in Oslo, the rest in Stockholm; the Economics prize was created by Sweden's central bank in 1968.

Asian and global honours

  • Ramon Magsaysay 2026 – 68th set of awards, announced on 31 August: Tommy Koh (Singapore), Runa Khan (Bangladesh) and Bo Kyi (Myanmar).
  • Who they are – Koh helped negotiate the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea; Runa Khan founded the NGO Friendship; Bo Kyi defends political prisoners.
  • Sakharov Prize 2026 – the European Parliament honoured the International Criminal Court and its officials.
  • Oskar Klein Medal – string theorist Ashoke Sen became its first Indian winner.
  • Legion of Honour – France made Bharti group's Sunil Bharti Mittal a Chevalier.
Ramon Magsaysay Award: Asia's top honour for public service, named after the seventh President of the Philippines; first given in 1958; awarded by a foundation in Manila.

Art, music and design

  • Lalit Kala Akademi – 20 national awards at the 65th National Exhibition of Art, chosen from 6,175 works by 2,495 artists.
  • Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar – the Sangeet Natak Akademi honoured 106 young artists for 2024 and 2025.
  • Red Dot – the archaeological experiential museum at Vadnagar, Gujarat, won a 'Best of the Best' design award in Berlin.
  • Wildlife photo – Sitaram Raul won a category at Wildlife Photographer of the Year 2026 with 'Shifting Sand'.
Lalit Kala Akademi: India's national academy of fine arts, set up on 5 August 1954; headquarters at Rabindra Bhavan, New Delhi.

Other honours

  • Smit Machchhar – the Indian pilot got the UAE's Order of Zayed II (first class) for protecting 174 passengers; Israel also plans a civilian heroism award for him.
  • Reema Nanavaty – SEWA director received the Hillary Rodham Clinton Award.
  • SBI – named World's Best Consumer Bank 2026 for a second year, at the IMF annual meetings.
  • Vadnagar link – Vadnagar in Mehsana district is PM Modi's hometown and an old Buddhist site.

September 2026Monthly notes

Cinema honours

  • Phalke Award – veteran Kannada actor Anant Nag received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for 2024 from President Murmu on 22 September.
  • Venue – the 72nd National Film Awards were held at Ekta Nagar (Kevadia), Gujarat, beside the Statue of Unity – the first time outside Delhi.
  • Top film – 'Article 370' was named Best Feature Film.
  • Anant Nag – debuted in Hindi with 'Ankur'; known for 'Malgudi Days'; got the Padma Bhushan in 2025.
  • TIFF – 'Angh' by Theja Rio of Nagaland won the Toronto festival's Platform Prize.
Dadasaheb Phalke Award: India's highest film honour, started in 1969; first given to Devika Rani; it carries a Swarna Kamal and a shawl.

Teachers, Parliament and Hindi

  • Teachers' Award – 82 teachers got the National Teachers' Award from the President on 5 September at Vigyan Bhawan.
  • Sansad Ratna – the 16th awards (9 September) were given by Prime Point Foundation; begun in 2010 on Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam's suggestion.
  • Kalam Jan Seva Puraskar – the first one went to the National Commission for Scheduled Castes.
  • Rajbhasha Kirti – given by Amit Shah on Hindi Diwas in Navi Mumbai; the Ministry of Defence came first among large ministries.
  • Rajbhasha Gaurav – honours original or translated Hindi books; a book on AI, 'Yantrabuddhi', won in its category.

Clean air and green honours

  • Swachh Vayu Survekshan – fifth awards on 7 September under NCAP; among million-plus cities Lucknow was first (198/200), Indore second, Jabalpur third.
  • Other tiers – Rourkela topped cities of 3–10 lakh; Kalinganagar topped the smallest group.
  • Eco Warrior – the 4th award's lifetime honour went to retired IFS officer Brij Kishore Singh for curbing sandalwood smuggling.
  • Green Highways – NHAI's 2026 awards for roadside, median and land-parcel planting.
  • COOL Leaders – first award of UNEP's Cool Coalition went to the UAE, Singapore and Chinese business leader Dong Mingzhu.

Science, maths and farming

  • SASTRA Ramanujan Prize – 2026 winner Sarah Peluse of Stanford; for mathematicians aged 32 or below; US$10,000.
  • AGU honours – Neelima Satyam (IIT Indore) got the Devendra Lal Memorial Medal; Roxy Mathew Koll (IITM Pune) a climate communication prize.
  • ICAR farmer award – Praveen Dehari of Kanker, Chhattisgarh, for a low-cost egg incubator.
  • FAO 100 Women Heroes – Lalrintangi, a strawberry grower from Mizoram, will be honoured at the World Food Forum in Rome.
  • Lata Mangeshkar Award – MP's national award for 2026 went to composer Shantanu Moitra.
SASTRA Ramanujan Prize: given since 2005 by SASTRA University, Thanjavur, around Ramanujan's birthday (22 December), which India marks as National Mathematics Day.

Adventure, media and youth

  • Tenzing Norgay awards 2024 – land adventure – Baljeet Kaur and Skalzang Rigzin; water – Rimo Saha; lifetime – Shiv Prasad Chamoli.
  • Prize – each winner gets a statuette, certificate and ₹15 lakh, presented with the National Sports Awards.
  • CCNow awards – Roli Srivastava, founder of 'The Migration Story', was among Journalists of the Year; Fiona Harvey of The Guardian got the lifetime award.
  • Pune Festival – the 38th festival's lifetime award went to actor Mohan Joshi.
  • Youth Parliament – PM SHRI JNV Ukhrul-1, Manipur, won the 27th National Youth Parliament Competition for Navodaya schools.

August 2026Monthly notes

National honours

  • Lokmanya Tilak National Award 2026 – given to National Security Adviser Ajit Doval by Amit Shah in Pune, on Tilak's 106th death anniversary (1 August); the Lokmanya Tilak Smarak Trust gives it.
  • Gallantry list – on the eve of Independence Day the President cleared 78 gallantry awards for the forces and CAPFs, 13 of them posthumous.
  • Top awards – they include 9 Kirti Chakras (7 posthumous) and 19 Shaurya Chakras.
  • Jeevan Raksha – Nitin Gadkari gave the Sarvottam Jeevan Raksha Padak to 12 rat-hole miners who saved 41 workers from the Silkyara tunnel in Uttarakhand in November 2023.
  • Gaj Gaurav Award 2026 – went to seven forest staff and mahouts from Jaldapara National Park, West Bengal, for saving elephants and guiding rhinos during floods.
Gallantry awards: peacetime awards in order are Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra and Shaurya Chakra; wartime awards are Param Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra and Vir Chakra; lists are announced on the eves of Republic Day and Independence Day.

National Sports Awards 2025

  • Announced – by the Sports Ministry on 18 August 2026; no one was picked for the Khel Ratna, the top sports honour.
  • Panel – the selection committee was headed by retired SC judge Arun Kumar Mishra.
  • Arjuna Award – 17 players, among them Tejaswin Shankar, Divya Deshmukh, Vidit Gujrathi, Treesa Jolly, Gayatri Gopichand and hockey's Lalremsiami.
  • Lifetime – the Arjuna (Lifetime) went to footballer I. Arumainayagam; Dronacharya (Lifetime) to Dharmendra Singh Yadav (boxing) and Virender Kumar (wrestling).
  • Promotion award – the Rashtriya Khel Protsahan Puraskar went to the Army Paralympic Node, Pune.

Science honours

  • Vikram Sarabhai Medal – Prof Annapurni Subramaniam, Director of the Indian Institute of Astrophysics, Bengaluru, got the COSPAR Vikram Sarabhai Medal 2026.
  • Her firsts – she is the first Indian woman and the fourth Indian to win it; it was given at the 46th COSPAR Assembly (Florence, 3 August).
  • About the medal – COSPAR and ISRO give it every two years for space research in developing countries; past Indian winners include U.R. Rao and Anil Bharadwaj.
  • Dirac Medal 2026 – physicist Prof Deepak Dhar shared it with Haim Sompolinsky, Marc Mézard and Bernard Derrida for work on statistical mechanics and the sandpile model.
  • Rare double – Dhar, who won the Boltzmann Medal in 2022, is only the second India-based scientist after Ashoke Sen to get the Dirac Medal.

Honours from abroad

  • Uzbekistan – President Mirziyoyev gave PM Modi the 'Oliy Darajali Do'stlik' Order, Uzbekistan's top honour for foreign leaders, in Tashkent on 30 August.
  • Nepal – President Ramchandra Poudel made India's Army Chief an honorary Nepal Army General at Sheetal Niwas, Kathmandu.
  • Liberty award – author Gurcharan Das ('India Unbound') became the first Indian to receive Liberty International's Lifetime Libertarian Award, in Kathmandu.
  • Pakistan – two Parsi doctors from Mumbai will get the Nishan-e-Imtiaz after death for keeping Jinnah's TB secret in 1946.
  • WHO network – the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission was named a regional excellence centre for drug-safety monitoring by WHO's South-East Asia Regulatory Network.

Other awards

  • Organ donation – Tamil Nadu was Best State at the National Organ Donation Awards; Assam best in the North-East and Delhi best UT.
  • Environment – the M.S. Swaminathan Environment Protection Award 2026 went to P. Pechiyammal (marine clean-up) and Govindhammal (climate-smart farming).
  • Publishing – the Publications Division won seven Federation of Indian Publishers awards.
  • Statistics – MoSPI gave the Sukhatme National Award 2026 to Prof Arup Bose of ISI Kolkata.
  • Media – lyricist and CBFC chief Prasoon Joshi got the first NBT Bhushan Samman.

July 2026Monthly notes

72nd National Film Awards

  • Top film – announced on 18 July 2026 for films of 2024; Article 370 (Hindi, directed by Aditya Suhas Jambhale) won Best Feature Film.
  • Acting – Mammootty (Bramayugam) and Kartik Aaryan (Chandu Champion) shared Best Actor; Yami Gautam (Article 370) was Best Actress.
  • Direction – Rajkumar Periasamy won Best Direction for the Tamil film 'Amaran'; Randeep Hooda won the debut director award for 'Swatantrya Veer Savarkar'.
  • Other picks – 'Srikanth' was Best Hindi Film, 'Kalki 2898 AD' won the wholesome entertainment award and 'Bhangaar' the best non-feature film.
  • Writing – a Kannada book on cinema and politics by Kenchuru Pradeep Kumar Shetty was named the best book on cinema.
National Film Awards: India's top film honours, given since 1954 by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting; the main prizes are the Swarna Kamal (Golden Lotus) and Rajat Kamal (Silver Lotus).

Science Olympiads

  • Medal haul – Indian students won 12 gold and 7 silver at the 2026 international science and maths Olympiads; HBCSE, Mumbai, runs India's Olympiad programme.
  • Physics – at the 56th IPhO in Bucaramanga, Colombia, all five Indians won gold and India shared the top rank.
  • Chemistry – at the 58th IChO in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, four golds again put India joint first.
  • Maths – India finished 7th of 117 countries at the 67th IMO in Shanghai, with 2 gold and 4 silver.
  • Biology – India took one gold and three silvers at the 37th IBO; Bhavya Gunwal of Haryana took the gold.
HBCSE: Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education, Mumbai, a centre of TIFR under the Department of Atomic Energy; nodal body for India's science Olympiads.

Telecom and governance awards

  • WSIS Prize 2026 – the Department of Telecommunications won the ITU's global award in Geneva for 'Samriddh Gram', a BharatNet-backed phygital service model for villages.
  • Champion projects – Samriddh Gram and Tata Communications' 'School of Hope and Empowerment' were India's two Champion projects.
  • Egmont award – FIU-India was runner-up for the Best Egmont Case Award at the plenary in Baku, for exposing a Rs 868 crore cross-border crypto fraud.
  • 5G labs – IIITDM Kurnool came first in all four categories of the 5G Laboratory Excellence Awards.
  • Youth Parliament – the University of Kashmir topped the 17th National Youth Parliament Competition.
ITU: International Telecommunication Union, the UN agency for information and communication technology, founded in 1865 and based in Geneva; it runs the WSIS Prizes.

Global honours and lists

  • Indonesia's honour – PM Modi received the Bintang Republik Indonesia Adipurna in Jakarta on 7 July 2026.
  • USISPF award – Sunil Bharti Mittal, V. Prem Watsa and Christopher Calio got the US-India Strategic Partnership Forum's Leadership Award 2026 in Washington.
  • Short story prize – Jameer Nazir won the Commonwealth Short Story Prize 2026; India's Sharon Aruparayil won the Asia region.
  • Heritage award – Nepal's 17th-century Jyaba Bahi monastery, restored with Indian help by INTACH, won a UNESCO Asia-Pacific heritage award.
  • Power list – Nita Ambani headed the 2026 Fortune India Most Powerful Women ranking.

Books and other honours

  • On a former CJI – 'The Voice of Justice: Justice Gavai Speaks' is by Prof. S. Shivakumar.
  • RSS centenary – 'RSS@100: A Century of Service, Unity and Sacrifice' is by Shyam Jaju and Anupam Trivedi.
  • Prison diary – 'Cell Number 14' is the prison diary of the late Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei.
  • UN medal – 651 Indian Army peacekeepers with the UN mission in DR Congo received the UN Medal.
  • Rangeland award – Sumer Singh Bhati of Jaisalmer was chosen for the Rangeland Restoration Award 2026, to be given at UNCCD COP17 in Mongolia.

June 2026Monthly notes

Honours for the Prime Minister

  • Slovakia – President Peter Pellegrini conferred the Order of the White Double Cross (First Class), the country's top decoration for foreigners, on Modi in Bratislava on 15 June.
  • Tally – reports counted it as his 33rd foreign state honour.
  • Seychelles – 'Guardian of the Blue Horizon', an environmental honour given for the first time, was presented by President Patrick Herminie.
  • Haj service – India's Haj Mission won two 'Labbaytum' awards from Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, the only country to win two.

Performing arts and cinema

  • Akademi Ratna – seven Sangeet Natak Akademi Fellows were named for 2024–25, among them Kathak dancer Ramlal Bareth and singer Rita Ganguly.
  • Akademi Puraskar – 108 artists were chosen for 2024 and 2025 (Rs 1 lakh each); 106 young artists got the Ustad Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar.
  • Fellowship value – Rs 3 lakh; the number of living Fellows is capped at 40.
  • MIFF 2026 – the 19th Mumbai International Film Festival (15–21 June) gave its Golden Conch to the Polish documentary 'Silver'.
  • Silver Conch – the top Indian documentary award went to 'Waai'.
Sangeet Natak Akademi: national academy of music, dance and drama set up in 1953 under the Ministry of Culture; its Fellowships and Awards are presented by the President.

Science and environment prizes

  • Wolf Prize – Prof Jainendra Jain became the first person of Indian origin to win it, for his work on composite fermions.
  • Global Earth Prize – three 16-year-old Indian students won with 'Plas-Stick', a tamarind-seed powder that removes microplastics from water.
  • Food Planet Prize – Andhra Pradesh Community Managed Natural Farming (APCNF) won this Swedish award.
  • IP Hall of Fame – Delhi High Court judge Justice Pratibha M. Singh became the first judge from India to enter it.
  • Prix Versailles – Navi Mumbai airport (Terminal 1) and Guwahati airport (Terminal 2) were named among the world's most beautiful airports.

Defence and sports honours

  • Investiture Phase I – President Droupadi Murmu gave 51 gallantry awards: 7 Kirti Chakra, 15 Vir Chakra and 29 Shaurya Chakra.
  • Investiture Phase II – on 29 June she gave 105 distinguished service awards, including PVSM and Sarvottam Yudh Seva Medal.
  • IMA – Lt Vishal Kumar won the President's Gold Medal along with the Sword of Honour at the Dehradun passing-out parade on 13 June.
  • Messi – Lionel Messi became the first footballer to receive Spain's Princess of Asturias Award.
  • Anand – Viswanathan Anand received the SJFI Medal, the top honour of the Sports Journalists' Federation of India.

Books in news

  • When Audit Matters – by former CAG Vinod Rai, on CAG reports that changed outcomes.
  • The Second Orbit – a memoir by astronaut Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla.
  • Agatthiyar – The Unifier – by O. Shama Bhat and M.N. Sudha, on sage Agastya as a symbol of cultural unity.
  • IIT – The Story of India's Most Prestigious Educational Ecosystem – written by Prabhat Kumar.

May 2026Monthly notes

Honours for the Prime Minister

  • Sweden – Crown Princess Victoria presented the Royal Order of the Polar Star, Degree Commander Grand Cross in Gothenburg on 17 May.
  • About it – created in 1748; Sweden's highest honour for a foreign head of government.
  • Norway – King Harald V gave the Grand Cross of the Royal Norwegian Order of Merit in Oslo on 18 May.
  • FAO – Director-General Qu Dongyu gave the Agricola Medal at FAO headquarters, Rome, for work on food security.
  • FAO visit – it was the first visit to FAO by an Indian head of government in 30 years.

Science and nature

  • Royal Society – Soumya Swaminathan, former WHO chief scientist, and IIT Kanpur Director Manindra Agrawal were elected Fellows in 2026.
  • A first – Soumya is the second Indian woman FRS; with her father M.S. Swaminathan (FRS 1973) they are India's first father–daughter pair.
  • Whitley Awards – BNHS scientist Parveen Shaikh (Indian Skimmer) and Barkha Subba (Himalayan salamander) won the 2026 'Green Oscars' in London.
  • Skimmer work – her 'Guardians of the Skimmer' project on the Chambal helped numbers grow from about 400 to about 1,000.
  • Gold award – the Whitley Gold Award went to Farwiza Farhan of Indonesia.

Books and cinema

  • International Booker – Taiwan Travelogue by Yáng Shuāng-zǐ, translated by Lin King, won on 19 May at Tate Modern, London.
  • First – the first winner translated from Mandarin Chinese; the £50,000 prize is shared by author and translator.
  • Cannes – Romanian director Cristian Mungiu's Fjord won the Palme d'Or at the 79th festival on 23 May.
  • Second Palme – Mungiu also won in 2007; this year's jury was led by South Korea's Park Chan-wook.
  • Grand Prix – went to 'Minotaur' by Andrey Zvyagintsev.

National awards

  • Nurses – President Droupadi Murmu gave the National Florence Nightingale Awards 2026 to 15 nurses on International Nurses Day (12 May).
  • Prize – Rs 1 lakh each; the awards were started by the Health Ministry in 1973.
  • Panchayat awards – winners of the National Panchayat Awards 2025 were named, ranked using PAI 2.0 (Panchayat Advancement Index); a district panchayat can get up to Rs 5 crore.
  • Grievance index – the Insurance Division of the Department of Financial Services topped Group A in DARPG's March 2026 grievance index.

April 2026Monthly notes

Screen Awards and Miss India

  • Best film – the Chetak Screen Awards in Mumbai on 5 April chose Neeraj Ghaywan's Homebound as Best Film.
  • Biggest haul – Aditya Dhar's Dhurandhar won 14 trophies, including Best Director; Ranveer Singh was Best Actor.
  • Acting and lifetime – Yami Gautam Dhar won Best Actress for 'Haq'; Dharmendra got the Lifetime Achievement award, received by Bobby Deol.
  • Miss India – Sadhvi Satish Sail of Goa was crowned Femina Miss India World 2026 on 18 April at KIIT, Bhubaneswar.
  • Firsts – the 61st edition was the first held in Odisha, and Sail is the first Goan to win; she will go to Miss World 2027.

Environment and science prizes

  • Goldman Prize – the 2026 'Green Nobel' went to six women, the first all-woman group since the award began; each gets US$200,000.
  • Winners – Iroro Tanshi (Nigeria), Borim Kim (South Korea), Sarah Finch (UK), Theonila Roka Matbob (PNG), Alannah Acaq Hurley (USA), Yuvelis Morales Blanco (Colombia).
  • Breakthrough Prize – Assam physicist Atanu Nath was honoured for his part in the Muon g-2 experiment; France's Frank Merle won the maths prize.
  • UNIDO award – Guntur Municipal Corporation, Andhra Pradesh, won UNIDO's 4th Gender Equality Mobilisation Award.
  • Cheese medal – Ladakh's Yak Churpi won gold at the Mundial do Queijo do Brasil in São Paulo.
Goldman Environmental Prize: set up in 1989 by Richard and Rhoda Goldman in San Francisco; given each year to grassroots activists from six world regions; called the 'Green Nobel'.

Honours for Indians

  • Swaminathan Award – Dr Ch. Srinivasa Rao, Director of ICAR-IARI, New Delhi, received the Prof. M.S. Swaminathan Award for climate-resilient farming work.
  • WWF award – N. Aleem Yusuf of the Malabar Botanical Garden, Kerala, for an AI app that spots about 100 invasive plants.
  • Green Icon – ex-Rajya Sabha MP J. Santosh Kumar, founder of the Green India Challenge, got the Global Green Icon Award at the House of Lords, London.
  • President's Colour – the Odisha Police received the President's Colour, the top honour for a state force.
  • Hall of Fame – Army chief Gen. Upendra Dwivedi was inducted into the US Army War College's International Hall of Fame, the third Indian so honoured.

Books in the news

  • NBCC – Arundhati Roy's memoir 'Mother Mary Comes to Me' won the US National Book Critics Circle award for autobiography.
  • Fiction prize – Nobel laureate Han Kang's 'We Do Not Part' won the NBCC fiction award.
  • Army memoir – former Army chief Gen. M.M. Naravane wrote 'The Curious and the Classified'.
  • Murals book – Sudha Murty's 'Tides of Time', on Indian history through Parliament's murals, was released by VP C.P. Radhakrishnan.
  • Symbols – T.M. Krishna's 'We, the People of India' decodes the nation's symbols.

March 2026Monthly notes

Sahitya Akademi Awards 2025

  • Announced – on 16 March 2026 for 24 languages; presented on 31 March in New Delhi.
  • English – ex-diplomat Navtej Sarna for 'Crimson Spring', a novel set around the 1919 Jallianwala Bagh massacre.
  • Hindi – Mamta Kalia for her memoir 'Jeete Jee Allahabad'.
  • Malayalam – N. Prabhakaran for the novel 'Maayaamanushyar'.
  • Prize – an engraved copper plaque, a shawl and Rs 1 lakh.
Sahitya Akademi: India's National Academy of Letters, set up in 1954, New Delhi; gives awards in the 22 Eighth Schedule languages plus English and Rajasthani.

60th Jnanpith Award

  • Winner – Tamil poet and lyricist R. Vairamuthu, chosen for 2025 (announced 14 March 2026).
  • Tamil – only the third Tamil winner after Akilan (1975) and Jayakanthan (2002), and the first for Tamil poetry.
  • Earlier honours – Sahitya Akademi Award 2003 for 'Kallikattu Ithikasam', Padma Bhushan 2014 and seven National Film Awards for lyrics.
  • Jnanpith – given by Bharatiya Jnanpith; first awarded in 1965 to G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam).

Music and journalism

  • Sangita Kalanidhi – Saraswati veena artist Jayanthi Kumaresh was chosen by the Madras Music Academy.
  • Carnatic – it is Carnatic music's top honour; Carnatic and Hindustani are the two streams of Indian classical music.
  • Goenka awards – the 20th Ramnath Goenka Excellence in Journalism Awards went to 24 journalists in 19 categories.
  • Founder – Ramnath Goenka started the Indian Express group.

98th Academy Awards

  • Night – held on 15 March 2026 at the Dolby Theatre, Los Angeles; Conan O'Brien hosted.
  • Best Picture – 'One Battle After Another', with six Oscars; Paul Thomas Anderson won Best Director.
  • Acting – Michael B. Jordan ('Sinners') and Jessie Buckley ('Hamnet') won the leads; Sean Penn and Amy Madigan the supporting awards.
  • Animation – 'KPop Demon Hunters' took Animated Feature and Original Song for 'Golden'.
  • Records – 'Sinners' had a record 16 nominations; Autumn Durald Arkapaw is the first woman to take the cinematography Oscar.

Global and Indian honours

  • Abel Prize – Germany's Gerd Faltings won for arithmetic geometry, including his proof of the Mordell conjecture; the first German winner.
  • About Abel – given by the Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters since 2003; often called mathematics' Nobel.
  • Water Prize – Kaveh Madani, head of UNU-INWEH, became the youngest Stockholm Water Prize winner.
  • History prize – William Dalrymple won the Mark Lynton History Prize for 'The Golden Road'.
  • KBE – Tata Sons chairman N. Chandrasekaran was made an honorary Knight Commander by the UK for India–UK trade.

February 2026Monthly notes

Indians honoured abroad

  • Organic fair – India was named 'Country of the Year' at BIOFACH 2026, Nuremberg.

January 2026Monthly notes

Padma list 2026 in numbers
