What is RRB Station Master (CEN 08/2026)?

The Station Master is a Level 6 post of the Indian Railways (initial pay ₹35,400, medical standard A-2), open to graduates aged 20 to 33 years. CEN 08/2026 is the first notification for this post alone: 1,215 vacancies in its vacancy table, applications from 27 October to 25 November 2026, and the CBT expected in January 2027.

Selection is by the CBT, the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) for eight times the vacancies, document verification and medical examination. The merit list gives 70% weight to the CBT and 30% to the CBAT. Pattern, syllabus and key dates are on the RRB Station Master page.

What the Section Controller Papers Show

Going through the October 2026 shifts question by question:

  • Every data question stands alone — one bar graph, line graph, pie chart, table or mixed chart, and one question on it. There are no five-question sets.
  • Reading comprehension is most of Logical Capability — three English passages of about 190 words with five questions each, on the central theme, the title, the tone, an inference or a detail. Only four or five questions are logical reasoning.
  • Arithmetic templates repeat from shift to shift — mixtures with a gain percentage, ‘A alone, B and C together’ work problems, coins and cards in probability, melting and recasting solids, mean, median and range.
  • Mental Reasoning is the same every shift — letter-cluster analogies and odd-one-out, number and letter series, coding, sign interchange, ranking and single-question arrangement puzzles.
  • The level is moderate, and the 60-mark first section takes the most time.

How to Use These Papers

  1. Sit one paper in a single 120-minute stretch before you study anything, to see where you stand.
  2. Review every wrong and every skipped question; note whether it was a concept gap, a slip or lost time.
  3. Practise the weak part for a few days: data questions, reading passages or arithmetic.
  4. Sit the next shift and compare the section-wise score.
  5. After the real papers, move to the RRB Station Master mock tests, which add Basic Science and the full 30 marks of data questions.

With one-third negative marking, attempt a doubtful question only when you can rule out at least one option.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there previous year papers of RRB Station Master?

Not on the new pattern. Station Master has its own notification and its own paper for the first time under CEN 08/2026; earlier the post was filled through RRB NTPC on a different pattern. The papers on this page are those of the RRB Section Controller CBT of February 2026 and October 2026, the exam held on the same 100-question, 120-minute pattern.

Why are Section Controller papers given for Station Master?

Both papers have 100 questions in 120 minutes with one-third negative marking, in three parts: a 60-mark mathematical and analytical section, Logical Capability with English reading comprehension, and Mental Reasoning. No other railway exam has been held on this pattern, so these are the closest real papers.

How is the Station Master paper different from the Section Controller paper?

Station Master adds Basic Science to the first section (units, density, work and energy, friction, heat, electricity, simple machines) and fixes Data Analysis and Interpretation at 30 marks. The Section Controller paper had no science. The Station Master mock tests cover both.
