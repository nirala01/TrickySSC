SSC CGL Pay Level 5 & 4 Posts: Salary, Job Profile, Promotion & FAQs

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Pay Level 5 and Level 4 are where most candidates stop reading, which is a mistake worth correcting. These posts carry entry basic pay of ₹29,200 and ₹25,500 respectively — lower than the rest of the list — but they come with the same permanent Central Government employment, the same allowance structure, the same medical and pension cover, and in several cases a promotion route that leads directly into the very posts that candidates chase at Level 7.

A Tax Assistant in CBDT who performs well and clears departmental examinations can become an Income Tax Inspector. A Senior Secretariat Assistant can rise to Assistant Section Officer and then Section Officer. The ladder is not blocked; it simply starts a rung or two lower.

Two practical differences to note before you rank these. The age limit is generally 18 to 27 years, lower than the 18–30 or 20–30 applicable to most higher posts. And several of these posts require a skill test — a Data Entry Speed Test for Tax Assistant, and a Computer Proficiency Test for certain assistant-grade posts — as specified in the notification for that cycle.

This page covers all Level 5 and Level 4 posts, then closes the four-part guide with a best-post comparison across every pay level, a consolidated promotion chart, and answers to the questions candidates ask most.

Pay Level 5 Posts (₹29,200 – ₹92,300)

Level 5 covers the Auditor and Accountant posts under C&AG, the Controller General of Defence Accounts, the Controller General of Accounts and other ministries. Approximate in-hand salary is ₹42,000 to ₹55,000 per month at entry.

These are the classic government finance jobs: regular office hours, a well-defined body of rules to master, and a promotion ladder that runs steadily upward through the accounts and audit hierarchy. For candidates with a commerce background who want a stable career rather than field work, Level 5 is a genuinely good outcome rather than a consolation prize.

1. Auditor — C&AG

Department
Offices under the Comptroller & Auditor General
Pay Level
Level 5 — ₹29,200
Age Limit
18–27 years
Work Type
Office + audit inspections
Approx. In-Hand
₹42,000 – ₹55,000

Job profile

Audits government departments and bodies — verifying expenditure against sanctions, examining financial records and vouchers, checking compliance with financial rules, and preparing audit observations for senior officers.

  • Scrutiny of vouchers, bills and expenditure records
  • Verification of sanctions and rule compliance
  • Assisting in audit inspections of departments and offices
  • Drafting audit observations and inspection notes
  • Maintaining audit records and following up on replies

Why choose this post

The C&AG audit cadre has one of the clearest promotion ladders in the Central Government, and an Auditor who takes the departmental examinations seriously can move up steadily. You also learn government financial rules thoroughly, which is knowledge that makes you useful in every subsequent role. Audit inspections involve some travel, though far less than at the AAO level.

Perks

  • Office-based work with regular hours
  • DA, HRA, TA and NPS
  • Government medical facilities and LTC
  • Departmental examinations offering accelerated advancement
  • Excellent promotion opportunities within the audit hierarchy

Promotion path

  1. Auditor
  2. Senior Auditor
  3. Assistant Audit Officer
  4. Audit Officer and above, as per departmental rules

Job profile

Maintains government accounts, prepares financial statements, verifies transactions, processes bills and vouchers, and handles reconciliation with treasuries and departments. Where the Auditor checks accounts after the fact, the Accountant produces and maintains them.

  • Compilation and maintenance of government accounts
  • Verification and processing of bills and vouchers
  • Preparation of monthly and annual financial statements
  • Reconciliation with treasuries and departmental figures
  • Handling pay bills, GPF and departmental payments where applicable

Why choose this post

Accountant posts suit commerce graduates who want their degree to be directly relevant to daily work. The role is entirely office-based with a predictable annual cycle, pressure concentrated around closing periods, and a promotion ladder that runs through Senior Accountant to Accounts Officer. Departmental examinations, where offered, can accelerate that considerably.

Accountant, Junior Accountant or Accounts Assistant? The exact designation differs between C&AG, CGA and individual ministries, and some cycles notify these as separate post codes at the same pay level. The work and promotion route are broadly similar; check the specific designation in the current notification.

Pay Level 4 Posts (₹25,500 – ₹81,100)

Level 4 posts carry a basic pay of ₹25,500 and an approximate in-hand salary of ₹35,000 to ₹45,000 per month, with the full set of Central Government allowances and benefits. The age limit is generally 18 to 27 years.

These are entry-grade posts, and the honest framing is this: they are the least paid on the list, and they are also permanent Central Government jobs with pension, medical cover, job security and a promotion route upward. For a candidate whose alternative is unstable private employment or another year of preparation with no guarantee, that is not a small thing.

5. Tax Assistant — CBDT

Department
Central Board of Direct Taxes
Pay Level
Level 4 — ₹25,500
Age Limit
18–27 years
Skill Test
Data Entry Speed Test
Approx. In-Hand
₹35,000 – ₹45,000

Job profile

Maintains taxpayer records, assists in processing returns and refunds, handles data entry and verification, prepares notices and correspondence, and provides administrative support to Inspectors and Income Tax Officers.

  • Data entry and maintenance of assessment records
  • Assisting in processing of returns and refunds
  • Preparing notices, letters and departmental correspondence
  • File and register maintenance in the ward or circle
  • Supporting Inspectors during assessment and verification work

Why choose this post

Tax Assistant is the strongest promotion story at Level 4. The route runs Tax Assistant → Senior Tax Assistant → Income Tax Inspector, which means the Level 7 post that candidates compete fiercely for is reachable from inside the department. Many serving officers took exactly this path. You are also in the Income Tax Department from day one, learning the work from the ground up.

The requirement to be aware of: a Data Entry Speed Test applies to this post, with the qualifying standard specified in the notification. Practise typing before the exam rather than after the result.

Perks

  • Clear departmental promotion route towards Income Tax Inspector
  • Office-based work with regular hours
  • DA, HRA, TA and NPS
  • Government medical facilities and LTC
  • Departmental examinations offering accelerated advancement

Promotion path

  1. Tax Assistant
  2. Senior Tax Assistant
  3. Inspector of Income Tax
  4. Income Tax Officer and above, as per departmental rules

Why Choose Pay Level 5 & 4 Posts?

  • Permanent Central Government employment with full job security
  • Regular office timings in almost all of these posts
  • Annual increments and periodic DA revisions
  • DA, HRA, TA, NPS, medical facilities and LTC
  • Clear promotion pathways, several of which reach Level 7 posts
  • Departmental examinations that can accelerate advancement significantly
  • Lower cut-offs, making them reachable in a candidate's first serious attempt

The honest counterpoint: entry pay is meaningfully lower, the age limit of 27 is tighter, and the work in the early years is routine. Whether that matters depends on your alternative. If you are 26, have attempted the exam twice and are choosing between a Tax Assistant post and another year of preparation, the post that puts you inside the department with a route to Inspector is usually the better decision than the post you might get next year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best post in SSC CGL?

There is no single best post. Assistant Audit Officer pays the most at entry. ASO in CSS has the deepest promotion ladder. Income Tax Inspector and CBI Sub Inspector offer authority and field work. Junior Statistical Officer offers analytical work and the best work-life balance.

The right answer depends on whether you want desk work, field work, investigation or analysis, and on your location constraints — not on which post ranks highest in someone else's list.

Which SSC CGL post has the highest salary?

Assistant Audit Officer and Assistant Accounts Officer at Pay Level 8, with a basic pay of ₹47,600 and an approximate in-hand salary of ₹70,000 to ₹85,000 per month depending on posting city and allowances.

What is the salary of a Pay Level 4 post?

Basic pay is ₹25,500, with an approximate monthly in-hand salary of ₹35,000 to ₹45,000 depending on city classification and applicable allowances.

Which SSC CGL post has the fastest promotion?

Promotion speed depends far more on cadre size, zone and vacancy position than on the post itself. Smaller cadres generally move faster because fewer officers compete for the same higher posts.

Two candidates in the same batch and the same post but different zones can be several years apart at their first promotion. Ask serving officers about the specific cadre and zone rather than relying on the post's general reputation.

Is there a typing or data entry test for SSC CGL posts?

Yes, for certain posts. Tax Assistant requires a Data Entry Speed Test, and several Assistant and Assistant Section Officer posts require a Computer Proficiency Test. The applicable test, its qualifying standard and whether it is qualifying in nature are specified in the official notification for each cycle.

What is the age limit for Pay Level 5 and 4 posts?

Generally 18 to 27 years, lower than the 18–30 or 20–30 limits applicable to most Level 6, 7 and 8 posts. Age relaxation applies as per government norms for reserved categories.

Can I get promoted from a Level 4 post to Inspector?

Yes. Tax Assistant in CBDT can rise to Senior Tax Assistant and then Income Tax Inspector; Tax Assistant in CBIC follows the same route to Inspector (CBIC). Timelines depend on cadre vacancy position and departmental rules, and departmental examinations can accelerate the process considerably.

Are SSC CGL posts transferable?

Most are, within the allotted cadre or zone. Central Secretariat posts typically involve transfers between ministries within Delhi rather than between cities. State cadre posts keep transfers within the allotted state. Field posts in tax, customs and enforcement departments involve periodic transfers within the zone as per departmental transfer policy.

Which post is best for work-life balance?

Junior Statistical Officer, Assistant Accounts Officer, the ASO cadres, Divisional Accountant and most Level 5 and 4 posts have regular hours and predictable schedules. Enforcement and investigation posts — CBI SI, AEO, NIA SI and Customs Preventive Officer — involve irregular hours and case-driven or shift-based work.

Do SSC CGL employees get a pension?

SSC CGL appointees are covered by the National Pension System, with contributions from both the employee and the government. Gratuity and other retirement benefits apply as per government rules.

How many posts are there in SSC CGL?

More than thirty distinct posts across five pay levels — 8, 7, 6, 5 and 4 — in ministries, departments, constitutional bodies and government organisations. The exact list and vacancy distribution is notified afresh in every recruitment cycle.

How should I decide my post preference order?

Decide the type of work first — desk, field, investigation or analysis. Then apply your location constraints. Then consider pay.

Ranking purely by pay level is the most common mistake candidates make. The monthly difference between adjacent levels is modest once allowances are applied; the difference in daily work over a thirty-year career is not.

Conclusion

Across four parts, this guide has covered every major SSC CGL post — from Assistant Audit Officer at Level 8 down to Tax Assistant and Postal Assistant at Level 4 — with the department, pay, job profile, perks and promotion route for each.

The pattern that emerges is worth stating plainly. The posts differ far more in what you will actually do than in what you will be paid. Between the highest and lowest levels, entry basic pay differs by about ₹22,000; between an Assistant Accounts Officer and a CBI Sub Inspector, the daily working life differs completely. One sits in an office reconciling accounts on a fixed schedule; the other spends years on case work and court appearances. Both are excellent jobs. They are excellent for entirely different people.

So when the preference form opens, do not fill it from a ranking list. Fill it from an honest answer to three questions: What kind of work will I still find tolerable in year fifteen? Where do I need to live? What am I willing to trade for pay? Rank accordingly, and you will be far happier than the candidate who put AAO first because a video said it was the best post.

Before any of that matters, though, there is a paper to clear. Post preference is a problem you earn the right to have.
