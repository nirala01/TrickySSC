1. Concepts in brief

Perimeter, area, volume

Perimeter is the length of the boundary of a flat shape (units: cm, m). Area is the flat space inside it (cm², m²). Volume is the space inside a solid (cm³, m³). Each extra dimension adds a power to the unit — a quick way to spot a wrong formula.

Curved and total surface area

For cylinders, cones and hemispheres, the curved surface area (CSA) is only the rounded part. The total surface area (TSA) adds the flat ends: two circles for a cylinder, one for a cone or hemisphere.

Scaling

If every length is multiplied by k, perimeter becomes k times, area k² times and volume k³ times. A 10% increase in radius gives a 21% increase in area (1.1² = 1.21).

Units

1 m = 100 cm, so 1 m² = 10,000 cm² and 1 m³ = 10,00,000 cm³. 1 litre = 1000 cm³.

4. Shortcuts and examples

Melting and recasting Volume stays constant. A sphere of radius 3 cm melted into small spheres of radius 1 cm gives (3/1)³ = 27 spheres.
Example 1 Area of an equilateral triangle of side 8: (√3/4) × 64 = 16√3 cm².
Example 2 Cylinder r = 7, h = 10: volume = (22/7) × 49 × 10 = 1540 cm³.
Example 3 Cone r = 3, h = 4 → l = 5. CSA = (22/7) × 3 × 5 = 47.14 cm² approximately; volume = ⅓ × π × 9 × 4 = 12π.
Example 4 If the side of a cube is doubled, surface area becomes 4 times and volume 8 times.

More solved examples (exam level)

Example 5 A rectangle is 12 cm by 5 cm. Its diagonal = √(144 + 25) = 13 cm.
Example 6 If the radius of a circle increases by 10%, its area increases by (1.1² − 1) × 100 = 21%.
Example 7 Volume of a sphere of radius 7 cm = 4/3 × 22/7 × 343 ≈ 1437.33 cm³.
Example 8 How many 2 cm cubes can be cut from a 10 × 8 × 6 cm cuboid? 480/8 = 60.

5. Practice questions (with answers)

Tap a question to see its answer.

Q1. The circumference of a circle is 44 cm. Its area?

Answer: 154 cm² (r = 7).

Q2. A cube has volume 512 cm³. Its total surface area?

Answer: 384 cm² (side 8).

Q3. An equilateral triangle has perimeter 18 cm. Its area?

Answer: 9√3 cm² (side 6).

Q4. Curved surface area of a cylinder with r = 7 cm and h = 10 cm?

Answer: 440 cm².

Q5. A rhombus has diagonals 10 cm and 24 cm. Its area and side?

Answer: Area 120 cm², side 13 cm.

6. Traps to avoid

  1. Curved surface area ≠ total surface area — check what is asked.
  2. Use the slant height for a cone's surface area and the vertical height for its volume.
  3. Radius is half the diameter; read which one is given.
  4. Convert to the same units before multiplying (cm and m do not mix).
