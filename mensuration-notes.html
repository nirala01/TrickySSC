For a cone, slant height l = √(r² + h²). The longest diagonal of a cuboid is √(l² + b² + h²); of a cube, a√3.

3. Shortcuts and examples

Melting and recasting Volume stays constant. A sphere of radius 3 cm melted into small spheres of radius 1 cm gives (3/1)³ = 27 spheres.
Example 1 Area of an equilateral triangle of side 8: (√3/4) × 64 = 16√3 cm².
Example 2 Cylinder r = 7, h = 10: volume = (22/7) × 49 × 10 = 1540 cm³.
Example 3 Cone r = 3, h = 4 → l = 5. CSA = (22/7) × 3 × 5 = 47.14 cm² approximately; volume = ⅓ × π × 9 × 4 = 12π.
Example 4 If the side of a cube is doubled, surface area becomes 4 times and volume 8 times.

4. Traps to avoid

  1. Curved surface area ≠ total surface area — check what is asked.
  2. Use the slant height for a cone's surface area and the vertical height for its volume.
  3. Radius is half the diameter; read which one is given.
  4. Convert to the same units before multiplying (cm and m do not mix).
