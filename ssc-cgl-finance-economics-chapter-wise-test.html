SSC CGL AAO Paper III Syllabus 2026 — Chapter by Chapter

Accounting Fundamentals

Accounting Fundamentals is the “concepts and classification” chapter of the Finance & Accounts part. SSC asks which concept or convention explains a practice (going concern, matching, conservatism, consistency), who issues Accounting Standards and what each one covers, what a basic term means (drawings, voucher, trade discount, fictitious asset) and whether an item is capital or revenue.

Journal, Ledger & Trial Balance

Journal, Ledger & Trial Balance is the “recording cycle” chapter. SSC asks which account is debited and which credited for a transaction, what type an account is (personal, real, nominal), which book records a transaction (purchases book, sales returns book, journal proper), what a contra entry or imprest system is and what a trial balance can and cannot show.

Bank Reconciliation Statement

Bank Reconciliation is a “plus or minus” chapter. SSC asks why the cash book and pass book differ (cheques issued but not presented, cheques deposited but not cleared, bank charges, interest, direct deposits, dishonoured cheques), whether to add or subtract an item when starting from one balance, and what the balance as per the other book will be.

Rectification of Errors

Rectification of Errors is the “find it and fix it” chapter. SSC asks what type an error is (omission, commission, principle, compensating), whether the trial balance will still agree, which entry corrects it, what the suspense account balance is and how the error changes the net profit.

Final Accounts

Final Accounts is the largest chapter of Finance & Accounts. SSC asks where an item goes (trading account, P&L or balance sheet), how to find gross and net profit, how an adjustment is treated (closing stock, outstanding wages, provision for doubtful debts, manager’s commission) and, for clubs and societies, what goes into Receipts and Payments vs Income and Expenditure.

Depreciation, Reserves & Inventory

Depreciation is a “method and number” chapter. SSC asks which method gives a fixed or a falling charge, how much depreciation is charged in a given year, the profit or loss on selling an asset, how a provision differs from a reserve and how inventory is valued under AS-2.

Bills of Exchange

Bills of Exchange is a “parties, dates and entries” chapter. SSC asks which section defines what (Section 4 promissory note, 5 bill, 6 cheque), who the drawer, drawee and payee are, how to find the due date with three days of grace, what noting charges and dishonour mean and which entry the drawer or drawee passes.

CAG & Finance Commission

CAG & Finance Commission is the “articles and facts” chapter of Economics & Governance. SSC asks which Article deals with what (148, 149, 151, 280, 270, 275), how the CAG is appointed and removed, his term and post-retirement bar, who chaired which Finance Commission and what the latest Commission recommended.

Demand, Supply & Consumer Behaviour

Demand & Supply is the core micro-economics chapter of the AAO paper. SSC asks who defined economics how (Smith, Marshall, Robbins), what shifts or moves a curve, which goods break the law of demand (Giffen, Veblen), how elastic demand is and what the properties of indifference curves are.

Production, Cost & Markets

Production, Cost & Markets is the “curves and market forms” chapter. SSC asks how TP, AP and MP move together, what the three stages of production are, which cost curve is a rectangular hyperbola (AFC), where MC cuts AC, and which features belong to which market.

National Income

National Income is a “which aggregate, which method” chapter. SSC asks how GDP, GNP, NDP and NNP are linked (depreciation, NFIA, net indirect taxes), who estimates national income in India, what is included or excluded (transfer payments, second-hand goods, imputed rent) and what the GDP deflator shows.

Indian Economy

Indian Economy is the widest chapter of Economics & Governance. SSC asks which plan had which model or aim, when an institution was set up (Planning Commission, NITI Aayog, NABARD, CCI), what a revolution or scheme stands for, how industries and PSUs are classified and which goal or conference is which.

Economic Reforms since 1991

Economic Reforms is the “1991 and after” chapter. SSC asks what caused the 1991 crisis, what the New Economic Policy changed (delicensing, MRTP, public sector), which committee reformed which sector (Narasimham, Chelliah, Malhotra, Rangarajan), FDI vs FPI, and when and where the WTO, IMF and World Bank bodies were set up.

Poverty, Unemployment & Human Development

Poverty & Unemployment is the “measures and committees” chapter. SSC asks which committee estimated poverty how (Lakdawala, Tendulkar, Rangarajan), what the MPI and Gini coefficient measure, which type of unemployment is which (disguised, seasonal, structural, frictional), what the PLFS ratios mean and what the HDI is built from.

Money, Banking & Monetary Policy

Money & Banking is the “definitions and instruments” chapter. SSC asks what a money measure includes (M0, M1, M3), when the RBI and SBI were set up, which instrument does what (CRR, SLR, repo, reverse repo, SDF, MSF, OMO), what the MPC targets and which kind of inflation is which.

Budget, Fiscal Policy, GST & BoP

Budget & Fiscal Policy is the “articles, deficits and taxes” chapter. SSC asks which Article governs which part of the budget (112, 265, 266, 267), how each deficit is calculated, what the FRBM Act targets, how GST is structured (101st Amendment, Article 279A, CGST, SGST, IGST) and how the balance of payments is split.

Role of IT in Governance

IT in Governance is the “schemes and platforms” chapter. SSC asks what each e-governance model means (G2C, G2B, G2G, G2E), when a programme was launched (NeGP 2006, Digital India 2015, GeM 2016), who runs a platform (UIDAI, NIC, CGA for PFMS) and what the IT Act and DPDP Act do.

How to Use Chapter Wise Practice for SSC CGL AAO

  1. Start Part A with concepts and journal entries. Every later accounting chapter — BRS, rectification, final accounts — rests on them.
  2. Do the sums by hand. BRS, suspense account, depreciation and due-date questions are free marks once your hand knows the steps.
  3. Start Part B with micro theory. Demand, elasticity, cost and market forms are fixed concepts that never change.
  4. Keep a current-facts card. The latest Finance Commission, Union Budget figures, CPI and GDP base years and new laws are dated in the questions.
  5. Use wrong answers as your syllabus. After each test, list only the facts you missed and revise that list weekly.
  6. Finish with timed practice. Paper III gives 2 hours for 100 questions — about 72 seconds a question.

SSC CGL AAO Finance & Economics Chapter Wise Test — FAQs

How many Finance & Economics chapters are covered?
17 — 7 in Part A, Finance & Accounts, and 10 in Part B, Economics & Governance — with 24 tests and 600 questions in all.
What is SSC CGL Tier II Paper III?
Paper III is General Studies (Finance and Economics), taken only by candidates for the Assistant Audit Officer and Assistant Accounts Officer posts: 100 questions for 200 marks in 2 hours, with 0.5 marks deducted for each wrong answer. Part A, Finance & Accounts, has 40 questions and Part B, Economics & Governance, has 60.
Which Paper III chapters are most important?
In Part A, final accounts, accounting concepts, capital vs revenue, BRS and depreciation; in Part B, money and banking, budget and fiscal policy, the Indian economy, the CAG and Finance Commission and the theory of demand and supply.

Are the AAO tests available in Hindi?

Do the accounting chapters have numerical questions?

How should I prepare Finance & Economics for SSC CGL AAO?
