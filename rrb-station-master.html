What is RRB Station Master?

The Station Master runs a railway station: train movements, signals in coordination with the control office, and the staff and safety of the station. It is a Level 6 post of the Indian Railways (initial pay ₹35,400) with the A-2 medical standard. Until now the post was filled through the RRB NTPC Graduate-level recruitment. CEN 08/2026 is the first notification for Station Master alone, with its own paper.

The notice lists 1,215 vacancies in its vacancy table. Online applications opened on 27 October 2026 and close on 25 November 2026, and the CBT is expected in January 2027.

RRB Station Master Selection Process

  1. CBT — 100 questions, 100 marks, 120 minutes, one-third negative marking. Marks are normalised across shifts.
  2. Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) — for eight times the vacancies of each RRB. A T-score of at least 42 is needed in every test battery, for every category; there is no negative marking, and the test is given only in English and Hindi.
  3. Document verification of the shortlisted candidates.
  4. Medical examination to the A-2 standard.

The merit list is drawn from candidates who qualify in the CBAT, with 70% weight for CBT marks and 30% for CBAT marks. Minimum qualifying marks in the CBT: UR 40%, EWS 40%, OBC (non-creamy layer) 30%, SC 30%, ST 25%.

RRB Station Master Syllabus

Basic Science & Mathematics (30 marks)

Number system, ratio and proportion, averages, percentages, profit, loss and discount, time, speed and distance, power and work, algebra, linear equations, arithmetic progression, LCM and HCF, geometry and trigonometry, area and volumes, and basic probability and statistics. In science: units and measurements, mass, weight and density, work, power and energy, kinetic energy, friction, speed, velocity and relative velocity, heat and temperature, basic electricity, levers and simple machines.

Data Analysis & Interpretation (30 marks)

Reading and drawing inferences from text, tables and graphs: charts, graphs, scatter plots, pie charts, statistical curves and distributions, Venn diagrams, data sufficiency and data arrangement, including data from more than one source.

Logical Capability (20 marks)

Binary logic, syllogism, clocks and calendars, assumptions, blood relations, family tree and logic puzzles. Reading comprehension passages are given in English on history, society, literature, science, environment, abstract topics, mythology, technology and culture, with questions on the main idea, supporting ideas, application, logical structure and style.

Mental Reasoning (20 marks)

Analogy, series completion, coding-decoding, and ranking and arrangement problems.

Previous Year Papers: why Section Controller?

Station Master has its own notification and its own paper for the first time (CEN 08/2026); earlier it was filled through RRB NTPC, on a different pattern. So no Station Master paper on the new pattern exists yet. The RRB Section Controller CBT, held in February 2026 and again in October 2026, is the exam closest to it: 100 questions in 120 minutes with one-third negative marking, in the same three parts — a 60-mark mathematical and analytical section, Logical Capability with English reading comprehension, and Mental Reasoning. That is why the Station Master previous year papers page gives the Section Controller shifts as full online tests. One difference to keep in mind: the Section Controller paper had no Basic Science, which Station Master adds to its first section. The mock series covers it.

RRB Station Master 2026-27 (CEN 08/2026) — Key Facts

  • Vacancies: 1,215 in the vacancy table of the notice; RRB-wise and zone-wise numbers are in its Annexure B.
  • Eligibility: a graduate degree in any stream; age 20 to 33 years as on 1 January 2027, with relaxation as per the rules.
  • Application: 27 October to 25 November 2026; fee payment up to 27 November; correction window 28 November to 7 December 2026.
  • Fee: ₹500 (₹400 refunded after appearing in the CBT); ₹250 for women, transgender, ex-servicemen, SC, ST, minority and EBC candidates (refunded after appearing).
  • Pay and medical standard: Level 6, initial pay ₹35,400; medical standard A-2.
  • CBT: expected January 2027.

How to Prepare for the RRB Station Master CBT

  • Give Data Analysis its due. It is 30 of the 100 marks, more than in any other railway paper. Practise one chart or table a question: bar graphs, line graphs, pie charts, tables and mixed charts.
  • Read English every day. In the Section Controller papers about 15 of the 20 Logical Capability questions were reading comprehension: three passages with five questions each. The passages stay in English even if you take the paper in Hindi.
  • Revise Class 9-10 physics. Units, density, work and energy, friction, heat, electricity and simple machines: one formula and one or two steps a question.
  • Do not study General Awareness for this paper. There is none; put that time into data and reading.
  • Sit the real papers, then the mocks. Start with the Section Controller papers to see the level, then take the 60 mocks in single 120-minute sittings.
  • Respect the negative marking. One-third of a mark goes for every wrong answer, so skip a pure guess.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Station Master preparation on TrickySSC free?

The previous year papers are free to attempt online with a TrickySSC account. In the RRB Station Master Mock Test Series, Mock 1, Mock 2 and Mock 3 are free, and the Station Master pass unlocks all 60 mocks.

Are there previous year papers for RRB Station Master?

Not on the new pattern. Station Master has its own notification and paper for the first time under CEN 08/2026. The closest real papers are those of the RRB Section Controller CBT of February 2026 and October 2026, held on the same 100-question, 120-minute, three-part pattern, so these are given as the previous year papers.

What is the RRB Station Master CBT exam pattern?

One computer-based test of 100 questions for 100 marks in 120 minutes: Basic Science, Mathematical and Analytical Capability 60 (Basic Science and Mathematics 30, Data Analysis and Interpretation 30), Logical Capability 20 and Mental Reasoning 20. One-third of a mark is deducted for every wrong answer. There is no General Awareness section.

What is the selection process for RRB Station Master 2026-27?

Under CEN 08/2026 the stages are the CBT, the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) for eight times the vacancies, document verification and medical examination. The merit list gives 70% weight to CBT marks and 30% to CBAT marks.

Who can apply for RRB Station Master?

Graduates in any stream, aged 20 to 33 years as on 1 January 2027, with age relaxation as per the rules. The post needs the A-2 medical standard.

Are the tests available in Hindi and English?

Yes. Every mock and every previous year paper can be attempted in English or Hindi. The reading comprehension passages stay in English in the Hindi paper, as in the exam.

When is the RRB Station Master CBT?

The CBT for CEN 08/2026 is expected in January 2027; the exact dates come only from the official RRB notice.
