Sports, Culture & Days Asian Games 3 October 2026

India Clinches Asian Games Golds in Women's Hockey, Wrestling, and Boxing

  • The Indian Women's Hockey Team clinched the coveted gold medal at the Asian Games, winning the title after an interval of over 4 decades.
  • In Men's 65kg Freestyle Wrestling, Sujeet secured the gold medal on his Asian Games debut, delivering India's first wrestling gold of this edition.
  • In Men's 80kg Boxing, Ankush claimed the gold medal with an emphatic victory in the final bout to conclude India's boxing campaign.
  • Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated all three medalists and hailed their determination, teamwork, and contribution to Indian sports.

Government Schemes Rural Governance 3 October 2026

Over 2.42 Lakh Special Gram Sabhas Held on Gandhi Jayanti

  • On the occasion of Gandhi Jayanti, more than 2.42 lakh Special Gram Sabhas were convened across the country across rural local bodies.
  • The nationwide exercise marked the rollout of the People's Plan Campaign (Jan Yojna Abhiyan) 2026-27 under the banner 'Sabki Yojana, सबका Vikas' to draft Panchayat Development Plans for 2027-28.
  • The 16th Finance Commission recommended an allocation of ₹4,35,236 crore for rural local bodies for 2026-31, representing an 84 percent increase over the 15th Finance Commission.
  • Under the SVAMITVA scheme, 3.33 crore property cards have been generated across 2.06 lakh villages, facilitating nearly ₹1,713 crore in credit.
  • Under the Viksit Bharat Guaranteed Employment and Livelihood Mission (Gramin) launched on 1 July 2026, 125 days of wage employment are guaranteed per eligible rural household.
  • Under Jal Jeevan Mission, tap water connections have expanded from 3.23 crore households (17%) to over 16 crore households (over 80%).

Government Schemes Senior Citizens 3 October 2026

Assistive Aids Distributed under Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana in Delhi

  • Union Minister Dr. Virendra Kumar and MoS B. L. Verma distributed 662 assistive devices worth over ₹10 lakh to 114 senior citizens residing in elderly homes across Delhi-NCR.
  • The distribution camp was organized at the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre (DAIC), New Delhi on the occasion of Gandhi Jayanti.
  • The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment plans to expand PM-Divya Asha Kendras from 109 to 300 centres to speed up delivery of aids.
  • Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY) was launched on 1 April 2017 to provide physical aids and assisted-living devices for BPL/eligible senior citizens.
  • The scheme is implemented through the Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India (ALIMCO), a CPSU under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.

Economy & Banking MSME 3 October 2026

PMEGP Supports Over 8.12 Lakh Micro-Enterprises with ₹24,398 Cr Subsidy

  • Since FY 2014-15, the Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) has supported approximately 8.12 lakh micro-enterprises.
  • A total of ₹24,398.61 crore has been disbursed as Margin Money (MM) subsidy, generating sustainable employment for about 73.08 lakh persons.
  • PMEGP is a flagship credit-linked subsidy programme of the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME).
  • The scheme is administered nationally by the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) as the single nodal agency.
  • The scheme assists first-generation entrepreneurs in setting up micro-enterprises in the non-farm sector across rural and urban locations.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

Standard categories SSC asks current affairs from

Over the last several exam cycles, current affairs questions in SSC papers have clustered into a predictable set of buckets. Reading with these buckets in mind makes your notes far easier to revise:

Frequently Asked Questions

What did India win in Women's Hockey at the Asian Games?
The Indian Women's Hockey Team won the coveted gold medal at the Asian Games. This marked India's first gold medal in women's hockey at the continental event in over four decades.
What is the campaign 'Sabki Yojana, Sabka Vikas' launched on Gandhi Jayanti?
It is the People's Plan Campaign 2026-27 under the Ministry of Panchayati Raj, rolled out during 2.42 lakh Special Gram Sabhas on 2 October. It enables Gram Panchayats to formulate their participatory Gram Panchayat Development Plans (GPDP) for FY 2027-28.
Where did INS Trishul visit in Spain and what exercise did it complete earlier?
INS Trishul made a port call at Valencia, Spain on 30 September 2026 as part of its deployment to the Mediterranean and Atlantic. Prior to this, the frigate participated in the 24th edition of Exercise Varuna with the French Navy at Toulon.
What is Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana and who implements it?
Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY) is a central scheme launched on 1 April 2017 by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. It provides physical aids and assisted-living devices to senior citizens belonging to BPL and eligible categories through ALIMCO.
What are the five pillars of the Yuva Sangam initiative?
Yuva Sangam, conducted under the Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat programme by the Ministry of Education, is structured around five pillars: Paryatan (Tourism), Parampara (Tradition), Pragati (Development), Paraspar Sampark (People-to-People Connect), and Prodyogiki (Technology).
How many Centres of Excellence are India and the Netherlands establishing in agriculture?
India and the Netherlands are collaborating on the establishment of 25 Centres of Excellence (CoEs) in agriculture and horticulture, along with preparing a joint action plan for the seed sector.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
