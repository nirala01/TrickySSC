Sports, Culture & Days Asian Games 30 September 2026

Neeru Dhanda Wins Historic Trap Gold, Relay and High Jump Medals at Asian Games

  • Neeru Dhanda became the first Indian woman to win an individual Gold medal in Trap shooting at the Asian Games.
  • The Indian quartet of Kiran Pahal, Poovamma M.R., Prachi, and Vithya Ramraj won the Gold medal in the Women's 4×400m Relay.
  • India secured a Silver medal in the Women's Trap Team event, won by Manisha Keer, Neeru Dhanda, and Aashima Ahlawat.
  • Pooja clinched the Bronze medal in the Women's High Jump, securing India's first medal in high jump at the Asian Games in over two decades.

Environment & Ecology Pollution Control 30 September 2026

CAQM Approves Revised GRAP Schedule with 48 Total Measures for Delhi-NCR

  • The Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) held its 30th full meeting under the chairmanship of Rajesh Verma to approve the revised GRAP schedule.
  • The revised GRAP framework comprises a total of 48 measures distributed as: Stage-I (25), Stage-II (8), Stage-III (10), and Stage-IV (5).
  • Restrictions on BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel light motor vehicles (4-wheelers) under Stage-III have been expanded to include Sonipat, alongside Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, and Gautam Buddh Nagar.
  • An explicit ban has been placed on outside-registered BS-IV and below diesel light and medium goods vehicles (LGVs and MGVs) entering Delhi under Stage-III.
  • Appointments Committee of the Cabinet (ACC) appointed Mukesh Sharma and Trilok Singh Panwar as full-time technical members of CAQM for a tenure of 3 years or up to 70 years of age.
  • Mandatory dust portal registration for Construction & Demolition (C&D) sites (≥500 sq m) was extended beyond municipal limits to development zones and highway corridors.

  • Chairperson of CAQM: Rajesh Verma
  • Total number of pollution control measures in the revised GRAP: 48 measures
  • New NCR district added for BS-III/IV private vehicle restrictions under Stage-III: Sonipat
  • Full-time technical members appointed to CAQM: Mukesh Sharma and Trilok Singh Panwar
  • Minimum plot threshold for mandatory C&D dust web portal registration: 500 sq metres

Awards & Honours Forest & Wildlife 30 September 2026

Bhupender Yadav Presents 4th Eco Warrior Awards 2026; B.K. Singh Gets Lifetime Honour

  • Union Minister for Environment, Forest and Climate Change Bhupender Yadav chaired the 4th Eco Warrior Awards 2026 and Conclave in New Delhi.
  • The event was jointly organized by the Indian Forest Service Association (IFSA) and 'Indian Masterminds'.
  • The Lifetime Achievement Award was conferred on Brij Kishore Singh (B.K. Singh), a 1976-batch IFS officer (Karnataka cadre), known for tackling forest crimes and sandalwood smuggler Veerappan.
  • The Protected Area Management Award was given to Dr. Prakash Chand Gogineni (Similipal Tiger Reserve, Odisha) and Manish Singh (Pilibhit Tiger Reserve, Uttar Pradesh).
  • The Special Project Award was conferred upon Project Elephant and the Great Indian Bustard Conservation Project in Rajasthan.
  • The Best Use of Technology Award went to N. Jayaraj (Wildlife Warden, Hosur, Tamil Nadu) for an AI-based early warning system preventing elephant-train collisions.

Government Schemes Rural Roads 30 September 2026

NRIDA and IIM Calcutta Sign MoU for 4-Year Longitudinal Impact Study of PMGSY-IV

  • The National Rural Infrastructure Development Agency (NRIDA) under the Ministry of Rural Development signed an MoU with IIM Calcutta.
  • The partnership will conduct a 4-year longitudinal impact assessment study of rural roads built under PMGSY-IV and PM-JANMAN.
  • The MoU was signed by Aditi Singh (Director General, NRIDA) and Prof. Alok Kumar Rai (Director, IIM Calcutta) in the presence of Rural Development Secretary Rohit Kansal.
  • The study will cover 10 States/UTs, 50 districts, 150 blocks, 250 roads, and survey approximately 5,000 households.
  • The 10 covered States/UTs are: Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Chhattisgarh, J&K, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Rajasthan, Sikkim, and Uttarakhand.
  • Study phases comprise a 14-month baseline survey, a 12-month transition period, and a 22-month endline survey.

Government Schemes Panchayati Raj 30 September 2026

NITI Aayog Organizes National Workshop on 'Viksit Panchayat' in Assam

  • NITI Aayog, under its State Support Mission (SSM), organized a national workshop on 'Viksit Panchayat: Connecting People, Places, Resources and Opportunities'.
  • The workshop was held in collaboration with Assam's Golap Borbora State Institute of Panchayat and Rural Development (GBSIPRD).
  • It was inaugurated by Assam Panchayat and Rural Development Minister Atul Bora, alongside NITI Aayog Member Dr. R. Balasubramaniam.
  • Discussions centered on transitioning from traditional sectoral planning to spatial planning using GIS and geospatial tools.
  • Highlighted the integration of spatial data into Gram Panchayat Development Plans (GPDP), e-Gram Swaraj, and the Panchayat Development Index (PDI / PMI).
  • The workshop reinforced constitutional decentralized governance rooted in the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act and Part IX of the Constitution.

Four Focus Deliberation Areas

  • Decentralized and Spatial Planning: Transitioning from sectoral to geographic and resource mapping.
  • Capacity Building at Grassroots: Equipping panchayat functionaries in data interpretation and resource management.
  • Spatial Planning for Priority Sectors: Deploying geospatial maps to locate development bottlenecks.
  • Integration with GPDP: Anchoring spatial data onto e-Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Development Index.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Initiative under which the workshop was held: State Support Mission (SSM) of NITI Aayog
  • Assam institute partnered with NITI Aayog: Golap Borbora State Institute of Panchayat & Rural Development (GBSIPRD)
  • Theme of the national workshop: 'Viksit Panchayat: Connecting People, Places, Resources and Opportunities'
  • Constitutional amendment governing Panchayati Raj: 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 (Part IX)

Source: PIB release

International Relations Bilateral Tourism 30 September 2026

India and Italy Hold 2nd Joint Working Group Meeting to Deepen Tourism Cooperation

  • The 2nd India-Italy Joint Working Group (JWG) on Tourism meeting was convened to expand bilateral travel and tourism collaboration.
  • The meeting was co-chaired by Bhuvnesh Kumar (Secretary, Ministry of Tourism, India) and Emanuela Tripepi (Director General, Ministry of Tourism, Italy).
  • Key areas of cooperation finalized include film tourism, twinning of tourism destinations, sharing tourism statistics, and promoting lesser-known destinations.
  • Both nations agreed to establish targeted technical working groups and designated contact points to execute collaborative outcomes.
  • Discussions also aligned roadmap preparations for the proposed 'India-Italy Year of Culture and Tourism 2027'.

Key Areas of Bilateral Tourism Focus

  • Destination Twinning: Linking heritage and cultural sites between Indian and Italian cities.
  • Film Tourism: Streamlining permissions and incentives for international cinema production.
  • Capacity Building & B2B: Promoting industry-to-industry partnerships between tour operators.
  • Joint Celebration: Organizing the proposed India-Italy Year of Culture and Tourism 2027.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Co-chair from India: Bhuvnesh Kumar (Secretary, Ministry of Tourism)
  • Co-chair from Italy: Emanuela Tripepi (Director General, Ministry of Tourism, Italy)
  • Proposed landmark bilateral event: India-Italy Year of Culture and Tourism 2027
  • Edition of the JWG on Tourism meeting: 2nd Meeting

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

Frequently Asked Questions

What record did Neeru Dhanda set at the Asian Games?
Neeru Dhanda became the first Indian woman to win an individual Gold medal in the Trap shooting event at the Asian Games. She also won a Silver medal as part of the Women's Trap Team alongside Manisha Keer and Aashima Ahlawat.
What major changes were introduced in the revised GRAP schedule by CAQM?
The revised Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) now comprises 48 total measures across four stages. Key changes include expanding Stage-III private vehicle restrictions (BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel) to Sonipat, banning outside BS-IV diesel goods vehicles from entering Delhi, and extending mandatory web portal monitoring for C&D sites.
Who was honoured with the Lifetime Achievement Award at the 4th Eco Warrior Awards 2026?
Retired 1976-batch Karnataka-cadre IFS officer Brij Kishore Singh (B.K. Singh) received the Lifetime Achievement Award from Union Minister Bhupender Yadav for his decades of dedication to wildlife conservation and anti-smuggling operations.
Where and when will Routes Asia 2027 take place?
The 24th Routes Asia summit will be hosted at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi from 16 to 18 March 2027 by the Airports Authority of India (AAI) under the theme 'Bharat Aviation Rising: Inclusive Growth – Connecting Asia and Beyond'.
What is the objective of the MoU between NRIDA and IIM Calcutta?
NRIDA and IIM Calcutta partnered to execute a 4-year longitudinal impact assessment study covering 250 rural roads and 5,000 households across 10 States/UTs to evaluate socio-economic outcomes of roads built under PMGSY-IV and PM-JANMAN.
What was the theme of NITI Aayog's workshop on Panchayati Raj held in Assam?
The workshop was held under NITI Aayog's State Support Mission on the theme 'Viksit Panchayat: Connecting People, Places, Resources and Opportunities', focusing on spatial planning and integrating geospatial tools into Gram Panchayat Development Plans.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
