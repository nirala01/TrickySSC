Government Schemes Rural Employment 25 October 2026

VB-G RAM G: 125-Day Rural Employment Guarantee

  • VB-G RAM G stands for Viksit Bharat—Rozgar and Aajeevika Mission (Gramin), a rural employment and livelihood framework aligned with the Viksit Bharat @2047 vision.
  • The VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 came into force on 1 July 2026 and was launched across rural areas of India, replacing Mahatma Gandhi NREGA without interruption.
  • The Act guarantees 125 days of wage employment in each financial year to rural households whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work.
  • Its four priority areas are water security, basic rural infrastructure, livelihood-related infrastructure and resilience to extreme weather events.
  • The framework centres on Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans (VGPPs), prepared with active participation from Gram Panchayats and Gram Sabhas; the Gram Sabha recommends and approves plans.
  • VGPP planning uses asset mapping and gap assessment, and draws on GIS, Yuktdhara, PM Gati Shakti and other digital tools to identify local needs and reduce duplication.
  • VGPPs link planning across block, district, state and national levels and contribute to the Viksit Bharat National Rural Infrastructure Stack.
  • The VGPP framework identifies 318 categories of permissible works across its four priority areas.
  • By 24 September 2026, the mission reported more than 31.69 crore person-days, 2.83 lakh assets created and 61.65% women’s participation in 2026–27.
  • For 2026–27, interim allocations to states and Union Territories totalled ₹93,841 crore; a first instalment of ₹25,880 crore was issued as the mother sanction.

Implementation figures reported to 24 September 2026

  • Workforce and reach: 10.88 crore active workers; over 2.81 crore people from about 2.23 crore rural households received employment on demand.
  • Delivery: More than 31.69 crore person-days were provided, with 2.83 lakh assets created and 97.17 lakh works in progress.
  • Coverage: Around 2.31 lakh Gram Panchayats were working under the framework; 15.17 crore rural employment guarantee cards had been issued.

  • Q: How many days of employment does the Act guarantee per financial year? A: 125 days.
  • Q: What is the main local planning instrument under the mission? A: Viksit Gram Panchayat Plan (VGPP).
  • Q: Which bodies participate in preparing VGPPs? A: Gram Panchayats and Gram Sabhas.

Defence Submarines 25 October 2026

Indigenous AIP Energy Module Reaches Setting to Work Phase 2

  • The indigenous Air Independent Propulsion (AIP) system for Indian Navy diesel-electric submarines reached the Setting to Work Phase-2 milestone on 24 October 2026.
  • The AIP system’s energy module was developed by the Naval Materials Research Laboratory (NMRL), DRDO.
  • The ceremony took place at Larsen & Toubro’s A.M. Naik Heavy Engineering Complex in Hazira, Gujarat.
  • Defence Secretary and DRDO Chairman Rajesh Kumar Singh attended the event virtually as chief guest.
  • Indian Navy Chief of Materiel Vice Admiral B. Shivakumar and DRDO Director General (Naval Systems and Materials) Dr R. Balakrishnan attended at Hazira.
  • AIP reduces a diesel-electric submarine’s dependence on atmospheric oxygen and thereby increases its ability to remain underwater.
  • In the next phase, the energy module is to be activated for electricity generation and undergo rigorous testing under Indian Navy procedures.
  • After testing, the module is scheduled to be handed over to Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited (MDL) in early 2027.
  • MDL is to integrate the module with a Kalvari-class submarine; the system is intended to strengthen the Indian Navy’s underwater capability.
  • The release highlighted testing and validation support from the Navy and the need to maintain strict safety practices for the hydrogen and fuel-cell system.

  • Q: When is the module scheduled to be handed over to MDL? A: Early 2027.

Government Schemes Digital Agriculture 25 October 2026

India Creates 11 Crore Farmer IDs Ahead of Deadline

  • India reached the target of creating 11 crore Farmer IDs five months before the March 2027 deadline.
  • The milestone was achieved under the Digital Agriculture Mission, implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare.
  • The Farmer ID initiative gives farmers a unique digital identity intended to make access to public services more transparent and efficient.
  • The IDs are designed to support access to government schemes, bank credit, crop insurance and disaster relief, while reducing paperwork and repeat verification.
  • The Union Cabinet approved the Digital Agriculture Mission on 2 September 2024.
  • The initiative’s target of 11 crore Farmer IDs by March 2027 had been set by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
  • More than 6 crore farmers had received direct benefits under various schemes through Farmer ID-based service delivery.
  • Linking verified farmer records with agricultural services is intended to reduce fraud and duplicate verification.
  • The ministry said the digital infrastructure was also improving coordination between departments and supporting policy formulation and scheme monitoring.

Sports, Culture & Days Para Sports 25 October 2026

India Wins Record 114 Medals at Asian Para Games

  • India finished the Aichi-Nagoya 2026 Asian Para Games with a record 114 medals: 30 gold, 35 silver and 49 bronze.
  • The Games concluded in Nagoya, Japan, with the closing ceremony at Mizuho Park Athletics Stadium.
  • Japan hosted the Asian Para Games for the first time; around 4,000 athletes and staff from 45 countries and regions took part.
  • India’s 114-medal total surpassed its previous best of 108 medals at the 2023 Games in Hangzhou, China.
  • Double gold medallists Nishad Kumar and Mona Agarwal carried the Indian flag at the closing ceremony.
  • Para athletics was India’s leading medal sport, contributing 65 medals, including 22 gold.
  • India won 16 medals in para badminton, 11 in para swimming and seven in para table tennis.
  • The Indian contingent also earned six medals in para archery and five in para shooting.
  • India won two medals each in para judo and para powerlifting.
  • The sixth edition of the Asian Para Games is scheduled for Doha in 2030.

Science & Technology Air Quality 25 October 2026

AATAL Lab Opens to Analyse Delhi-NCR Air Pollution

  • Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh inaugurated a new air-pollution analysis facility for Delhi-NCR on 24 October 2026.
  • The facility is named the Integrated Atmospheric Chemistry and Aerosol Toxicity Analysis Laboratory (AATAL).
  • The release described AATAL as the world’s first integrated facility of its kind for analysing air pollution and identifying its sources in real time.
  • It was inaugurated at the Delhi branch of the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM) under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
  • AATAL was established under Mission Mausam, announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, to strengthen scientific understanding of atmospheric pollution.
  • The laboratory analyses pollutant composition, sources, their relative contributions and possible health effects, going beyond reporting an overall air-quality level.
  • It can help distinguish sources including vehicle emissions, industry, biomass and crop-residue burning, construction and road dust, and pollution transported from surrounding areas.
  • Its integrated capabilities include atmospheric measurement, chemical analysis, source identification and decision support; the laboratory is linked with AIRWISE.
  • The associated forecasting system provides air-quality forecasts for the next 72 hours at a 400-metre spatial resolution.
  • The facility is intended to provide evidence to the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) and other agencies for targeted interventions and assessment.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.
