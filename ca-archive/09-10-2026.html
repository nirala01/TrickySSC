Defence Legislation 9 October 2026

CAPF General Administration Act, 2026 Comes into Force

  • The Central Armed Police Forces (General Administration) Act, 2026 came into force on 8 October 2026.
  • The Union Ministry of Home Affairs notified 8 October as the commencement date for the Act’s provisions.
  • President Droupadi Murmu gave her assent to the Act on 9 April 2026.
  • The Act provides for 50% of Inspector General posts to be filled by Indian Police Service officers on deputation.
  • At least 67% of Additional Director General posts are to be filled by IPS officers on deputation.
  • All posts of Special Director General and Director General are to be filled by IPS officers on deputation.
  • The prescribed percentages relate to the total posts in the respective ranks.
  • IPS stands for Indian Police Service, one of India’s All India Services.

  • Q: When did the Act come into force? A: 8 October 2026.
  • Q: Which ministry notified the commencement date? A: Ministry of Home Affairs.
  • Q: When did President Droupadi Murmu assent to the Act? A: 9 April 2026.
  • Q: What share of Inspector General posts is reserved for IPS deputation? A: 50%.
  • Q: What is the minimum IPS deputation share for Additional Director General posts? A: 67%.
  • Q: Who is to fill all Special Director General and Director General posts under the Act? A: IPS officers on deputation.

Economy & Banking GST 9 October 2026

GST Council Raises Prosecution Threshold to ₹5 Crore

  • The GST Council raised the prosecution threshold from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore.
  • The Council removed tax officers’ power of arrest, according to the News On AIR report.
  • Courts will decide whether punishment is a fine, imprisonment, or both in each case.
  • Late filing, errors or delayed tax payments may still lead to recovery, interest and a proportionate penalty.
  • The Council recommended common standards for GST notices and proceedings.
  • Notices are not to be issued for amounts of ₹10,000 or below.
  • Pending notices below the ₹10,000 threshold are to be withdrawn.
  • Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman announced the decisions after the Council meeting in New Delhi.
  • No GST rate changes were made at the meeting; the rate structure was described as settled.
  • The GST Council is a constitutional body under Article 279A and is chaired by the Union Finance Minister.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Q: Which body recommended the reported GST changes? A: GST Council.
  • Q: What is the new prosecution threshold? A: ₹5 crore.
  • Q: From what amount was the prosecution threshold raised? A: ₹1 crore.
  • Q: Who chaired the meeting as Union Finance Minister? A: Nirmala Sitharaman.
  • Q: What is the monetary threshold for not issuing GST notices? A: ₹10,000 or below.
  • Q: Which constitutional article provides for the GST Council? A: Article 279A.
  • Q: Were GST rates changed at this meeting? A: No.

Economy & Banking Capital Markets 9 October 2026

SEBI Makes Credit Risk-o-Meter Disclosure Mandatory

  • SEBI introduced a mandatory Credit Risk-o-Meter to help investors assess debt-security credit risk.
  • The indicator is colour-coded to provide a visual representation of credit risk.
  • It must appear in offer documents and abridged prospectuses.
  • Private placement memorandums and advertisements issued by issuers must also display it.
  • Online bond platform providers (OBPPs) must include it in relevant disclosures.
  • The measure also applies to OBPP websites and mobile platforms.
  • The provisions take effect 45 days from the date of the SEBI circular.
  • Stock exchanges and depositories have been directed to establish the systems and processes needed for implementation.
  • SEBI stands for Securities and Exchange Board of India.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Q: Which regulator introduced the Credit Risk-o-Meter? A: SEBI.
  • Q: What does SEBI stand for? A: Securities and Exchange Board of India.
  • Q: Which securities’ risk does the indicator help investors assess? A: Debt securities.
  • Q: What form does the risk indicator take? A: A colour-coded disclosure.
  • Q: When do the new provisions take effect? A: 45 days from the circular date.
  • Q: Which entities must prepare systems for implementation? A: Stock exchanges and depositories.
  • Q: What does OBPP stand for? A: Online bond platform provider.

Science & Technology Artificial Intelligence 9 October 2026

World Development Report 2026 on AI Launched in New Delhi

  • Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw launched the World Bank Group’s *World Development Report 2026: The Promise of AI* in New Delhi.
  • The launch event was held at the Oberoi Hotel on 8 October 2026.
  • The IndiaAI Mission under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology and the World Bank Group jointly hosted the event.
  • The report examines AI’s opportunities and challenges for developing economies.
  • It offers a framework for countries to adopt, adapt and advance AI to support development.
  • The report identifies reliable electricity and connectivity, computing infrastructure, quality data, skills, education and capable institutions as key foundations.
  • The event included a state-level AI exhibition featuring solutions from states and innovators.
  • The report describes AI applications in medical screening, agricultural decision-making and education as ways to extend expertise and improve services.
  • The minister cited a Baramati sugarcane initiative that recorded a 40% yield increase alongside 30% lower water and fertiliser use.
  • The release also cited a 35% increase in TB detection and said Gujarat’s AgriStack was used by five lakh farmers.
  • The IndiaAI Mission is organised around seven pillars, including computing capacity, datasets, applications, future skills and safe and trusted AI.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.
