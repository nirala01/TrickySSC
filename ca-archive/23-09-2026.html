Awards & Honours National Awards 23 September 2026

President Conferred 72nd National Film Awards; Dadasaheb Phalke to Anant Nag

  • President Droupadi Murmu presented the 72nd National Film Awards at Ekta Nagar, Kevadia, Gujarat, near the Statue of Unity.
  • Veteran actor Anant Nag was conferred the prestigious Dadasaheb Phalke Award for the year 2024 for his outstanding contribution to Indian cinema.
  • The President commended the film Article 370 for presenting a vivid account of the critical challenges and solutions related to national integration.

  • Film Bhakshak was lauded for creating societal awareness on women's safety, while Santhali film Aangen was recognized for advancing cultural diversity.
  • President Murmu urged the film fraternity to ensure women's representation on every regional jury and protect the social security of background artists.

Defence Joint Military Exercise 23 September 2026

IAF and Japan Air Self-Defense Force Conclude Exercise Veer Guardian 2026

  • The bilateral air defence exercise Veer Guardian 2026 between the Indian Air Force (IAF) and the Japan Air Self-Defense Force (JASDF) concluded at Air Force Station Jodhpur, Rajasthan.
  • The exercise was held over 14 days, focusing on tactical interoperability and coordinated multi-aircraft combat missions.
  • The IAF fielded frontline platforms including Su-30MKI, Rafale, and the indigenous Light Combat Aircraft (LCA) Tejas.
  • The JASDF participated with its advanced F-2A fighter aircraft.
  • On the Distinguished Visitors' Day, JASDF Chief of Staff General Takehiro Morita and IAF Chief of the Air Staff Air Chief Marshal A.P. Singh flew sorties in the indigenous LCA Tejas.
  • Ground interactions covered operational discussions, mission planning conferences, aircraft maintenance drills, and aerospace safety debriefs.

Science & Technology Important Days 23 September 2026

Ministry of Ayush Celebrates 11th Ayurveda Day in Nagpur

  • The Ministry of Ayush hosted the national celebrations of the 11th Ayurveda Day on 23 September 2026 in Nagpur, Maharashtra.
  • The focal theme for the 11th Ayurveda Day is 'Ayurveda for a Healthy Future' (स्वस्थ भविष्य के लिए आयुर्वेद).
  • The first-ever comprehensive assessment report on the 'Ayush Service Sector', prepared by the Research and Information System for Developing Countries (RIS), was officially released.
  • Safety dossiers were introduced for two critical medicinal herbs: Gokshura (*Tribulus terrestris*) and Chandrasur (*Lepidium sativum*).
  • The CCRAS-AGNI (Ayurveda Gyan Naipunya Initiative) Assessment Scale web portal was launched to bolster evidence-based documentation.
  • Key policy publications released include the 'Ayush Vision 2030' report and a national status report on Ayush-related inventions.

Environment & Ecology Summits & Conferences 23 September 2026

Vice-President Inaugurates 9th India International Water Week in New Delhi

  • Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan inaugurated the 9th India International Water Week (IIWW) 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi.
  • Organized by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, the 5-day summit (22–26 September 2026) is themed 'Climate-Resilient Water Management'.
  • Marking a global transition from 'India Water Week', the event featured the Netherlands and Germany as partner countries.
  • The conference brought together 14 partner states and two partner central ministries alongside global water specialists.
  • Two landmark publications were unveiled: Sustainable Urban River Management Framework and AI and ML Driven Integrated Reservoir Sediment Management.
  • Grassroots water warriors Smt Ram Kumari (Chhindwara, MP) and Shri Sitaram Madavi (Gadchiroli, Maharashtra) were felicitated for community water conservation.

Environment & Ecology Wildlife Conservation 23 September 2026

PM Modi Commemorates World Rhino Day; Highlights Launch of Project Rhino

  • Prime Minister Narendra Modi commemorated World Rhino Day on 22 September 2026, highlighting India's strides in one-horned rhino conservation.
  • The Prime Minister announced that Assam now shelters nearly 80 percent of the world's greater one-horned rhinoceros population.
  • To strengthen species protection, Project Rhino has been launched to provide financial and technical assistance to frontline range states.
  • Project Rhino focuses on habitat management, genetic improvement, and tracking population dynamics of rhinoceroses.
  • Successful reintroduction was underscored in Dudhwa National Park (Uttar Pradesh), alongside thriving populations in Bihar and West Bengal.
  • Anti-poaching and habitat-protection initiatives in Kaziranga National Park were specially lauded for halting poaching.

Government Schemes Civil Aviation 23 September 2026

Civil Aviation Ministry Launches Challenge Mode for Aerodrome Development

  • Union Minister of Civil Aviation Kinjarapu Ram Mohan Naidu launched the Challenge Mode Portal for aerodrome development under the modified UDAN scheme.
  • A total of 21 Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) were signed between the Ministry of Civil Aviation, Airports Authority of India (AAI), and states/UTs.
  • The Challenge Mode allows state and UT administrations to competitively nominate unserved and underserved airstrips and helipads for infrastructure development.
  • Under the revised UDAN phase, the ministry aims to connect 120 new destinations and fly 4 crore additional passengers over the next 10 years.
  • Since inception in 2016, UDAN has operationalized 687 routes, 95 aerodromes, and flown over 1.71 crore passengers across 3.66 lakh flights.
  • Employment in India's aviation sector grew from 30 lakh in 2014 to 77 lakh, with a projection of reaching 2.5 crore by 2040.

Government Schemes Internal Security 23 September 2026

Union Home Minister Inaugurates 3rd National Conference of ANTF Heads in New Delhi

  • Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah inaugurated the 3rd National Conference of Heads of Anti-Narcotics Task Forces (ANTF) of States and UTs in New Delhi.
  • The Central Government set a firm deadline of 31 December 2029 to completely eradicate the illicit narcotics trade from India.
  • A multi-agency Vision Document 2026–2029 was introduced as the operational master plan to eliminate cartels and trace financial drug trails.
  • Foundation stones were laid for new Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) Zonal Complexes in Lucknow (Uttar Pradesh) and Dehradun (Uttarakhand).
  • A nationwide anti-drug TV commercial and a revamped NCB official website were launched during the conference.
  • Districts nationwide will be divided into three operational clusters: drug manufacturing/entry districts, transportation corridor districts, and consumption districts.

Economy & Banking Infrastructure 23 September 2026

NHAI and SSNNL Sign MoU to Accelerate Infrastructure Crossings in Gujarat

  • The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) signed an MoU with Sardar Sarovar Narmada Nigam Limited (SSNNL) in Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
  • The agreement establishes a simplified, time-bound approval process for national highway, canal, and pipeline crossings.
  • NHAI and SSNNL have mutually agreed to a full waiver of fees, charges, and levies on intersecting crossings.
  • The fee waiver directly reduces overall project costs, eliminates administrative bottlenecks, and optimizes the use of public funds.
  • The framework mandates standardized engineering practices and clearly defines maintenance and construction responsibilities.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

Standard categories SSC asks current affairs from

Over the last several exam cycles, current affairs questions in SSC papers have clustered into a predictable set of buckets. Reading with these buckets in mind makes your notes far easier to revise:

  • Government schemes and Cabinet decisions — full form, ministry, outlay, launch date
  • Appointments — new chairpersons, chiefs, CEOs, and heads of constitutional bodies
  • Awards and honours — instituting body, field, first recipient
  • Summits, conferences and agreements — host city, edition number, theme
  • Science, technology and space — missions, launches, indigenous technology
  • Defence — exercises, inductions, indigenous platforms
  • Economy and banking — reports, indices, rankings, RBI decisions
  • Environment and ecology — disasters, protected areas, climate reports
  • Sports — tournaments, winners, venues, important days
  • Important days and themes — date, theme of the year, observing body
