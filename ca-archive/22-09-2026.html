Defence Joint Military Exercise 22 September 2026

18th India-Mongolia Joint Military Exercise Nomadic Elephant Commences in Pithoragarh

  • The 18th edition of the India-Mongolia joint military exercise 'Nomadic Elephant' commenced at the Foreign Training Node in Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand.
  • The exercise is scheduled to run from 21 September to 3 October 2026 as an annual platoon-level bilateral drill.
  • A contingent of 45 personnel each from the Indian Army and the Mongolian Armed Forces is participating in the drill.
  • The primary focus is to boost joint military capability to conduct counter-terrorism operations in semi-urban and mountainous terrain under a United Nations mandate.
  • Drills include cordon and search operations, room intervention drills, special heliborne operations, cyber security, and deployment of drone and counter-drone systems.
  • The previous 17th edition was conducted in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia during May–June 2025.

International Relations Free Trade Agreement 22 September 2026

Historic India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement to Enter into Force on 20 October 2026

  • The India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement (FTA) is set to officially enter into force on 20 October 2026, coinciding with Dussehra.
  • The agreement was formally signed on 27 April 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, by Union Minister Piyush Goyal and New Zealand Trade Minister Todd McClay.
  • From day one, 100% of Indian exports to New Zealand will enjoy zero-duty access across all tariff lines, benefiting textiles, leather, footwear, gems, and engineering goods.
  • India has safeguarded domestic agriculture by excluding sensitive sectors such as dairy, sugar, edible oils, and bovine meat from tariff concessions.
  • The pact facilitates New Zealand's commitment to enable USD 20 billion in investment into Indian manufacturing, infrastructure, and startups.
  • Under the mobility framework, a quota of 5,000 temporary employment entry visas for skilled Indians and 1,000 working holiday visas annually has been created.
  • Indian STEM graduates in New Zealand will receive 3-year post-study work visas, while doctoral scholars will receive 4 years.
  • Both nations aim to double two-way bilateral trade to 7 billion NZD (around ₹35,000 crore) by 2030 under the Strategic Partnership Roadmap.

Economy & Banking Core Industries 22 September 2026

Index of Eight Core Industries Grows by 4.8% in August 2026 Under Base Year 2022-23

  • The Index of Eight Core Industries (ICI) recorded a year-on-year growth of 4.8% (provisional) in August 2026 compared to August 2025.
  • The growth rate is slightly lower than the revised growth rate of 5.0% recorded in July 2026.
  • The cumulative growth rate of ICI during the period April to August 2026 stood at 4.3%, up from 2.4% in the corresponding period of the previous year.
  • Sectors registering positive year-on-year expansion were Cement (12.5%), Electricity (11.6%), Iron Ore (5.5%), Steel (3.4%), and Refinery Products (2.6%).
  • Negative output growth was registered in Coal (-3.8%), Natural Gas (-4.9%), Crude Oil (-3.6%), and Fertilizers (-12.4%).
  • Under the revised base year 2022-23, Electricity carries the highest weightage (30.93%), followed by Refinery Products (22.57%) and Steel (17.58%).

Science & Technology Traditional Medicine 22 September 2026

First Meeting of Sowa-Rigpa Working Group Held Under PCIM&H in Ghaziabad

  • The first meeting of the Sowa-Rigpa Working Group was held at the Pharmacopoeia Commission for Indian Medicine & Homoeopathy (PCIM&H) in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh.
  • The meeting was chaired by Dr. Kaustubha Upadhyaya, Director of PCIM&H, under the Ministry of Ayush.
  • The working group comprises experts from premier Sowa-Rigpa institutions, including Dr. Padma Gurmet, Director of the National Institute of Sowa-Rigpa (NISR), Leh.
  • The mandate is to formulate phased action plans for drafting Formularies, Pharmacopoeias, and regulatory documents for Sowa-Rigpa medicine.
  • Standards will be established under the statutory ambit of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and rules made thereunder.
  • PCIM&H functions as the Central Drug Testing-cum-Appellate Laboratory for Indian systems of medicine and homoeopathy.

Economy & Banking Infrastructure 22 September 2026

NHAI Issues New Design Guidelines for Access-Controlled High-Speed National Highways

  • The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) released uniform design and construction guidelines for access-controlled high-speed corridors (other than expressways).
  • The guidelines apply to all upcoming 4-lane and 6-lane greenfield and brownfield high-speed access-controlled national highways.
  • This move aligns with the Viksit Bharat 2047 strategic goal of developing 50,000 kilometres of access-controlled high-speed corridors to lower national logistics costs.
  • Standardized Right of Way (ROW) is fixed at 60 metres for 4-lane highways with traffic flow up to 15,000 Passenger Car Units (PCUs).
  • A ROW width of 70 metres is mandated for highways carrying traffic flows between 15,000 and 40,000 vehicles.
  • To prevent unauthorized entry, encroachments, and stray animal accidents, a continuous 1-metre high boundary wall is mandated along the outer edge of corridors.

  • Which agency framed the new standards for high-speed access-controlled highways? NHAI.

Government Schemes Social Welfare 22 September 2026

Ministry Highlights Milestones of Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana and Elderline 14567 Under AVYAY

  • The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment reviewed the achievements of senior citizen welfare schemes under the umbrella Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY).
  • Under the Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY), over 46.32 lakh assistive living devices have been distributed to 8.53 lakh senior citizens across 589 assessment camps since 2017.
  • RVY provides free physical aids like wheelchairs, walking sticks, hearing aids, and spectacles to BPL and low-income senior citizens suffering from age-related infirmities.
  • The implementation partner for manufacturing and distributing the assistive devices is ALIMCO (Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India).
  • The Elderline (14567), India's toll-free national helpline for senior citizens launched on 1 October 2021, has answered over 29 lakh calls across states.
  • Under the geriatric caregiver training initiative launched in FY 2023-24, a total of 35,480 candidates have been trained through the National Institute of Social Defence (NISD).

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the 18th Nomadic Elephant military exercise taking place?
The 18th edition of the India-Mongolia joint military exercise 'Nomadic Elephant' is being held at the Foreign Training Node in Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, from 21 September to 3 October 2026.
When will the India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement come into effect?
The India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement will enter into force on 20 October 2026. It grants zero-duty access to 100% of Indian tariff lines from the first day while protecting sensitive Indian agricultural sectors like dairy and sugar.
What was the growth rate of India's eight core industries in August 2026?
The Index of Eight Core Industries recorded a year-on-year provisional growth of 4.8% in August 2026. Cement recorded the fastest growth at 12.5%, followed closely by Electricity at 11.6%.
What medals did Rudrankksh Patil win at the Asian Games 2026?
Rudrankksh Balasaheb Patil won a Silver medal in the Men's 10m Air Rifle Team event alongside Himanshu Dhillon and Parth Mane, and also bagged an individual Bronze medal in the Men's 10m Air Rifle competition.
What is the Elderline helpline and what is its toll-free number?
Elderline is India's national helpline for senior citizens operated under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment with toll-free number 14567. Dedicated on 1 October 2021, it provides free guidance, emotional support, and rescue assistance.
What are the new NHAI guidelines for access-controlled high-speed corridors?
NHAI's new guidelines standardize DPR preparation and design specifications for 4-lane and 6-lane non-expressway high-speed highways. It prescribes 60-metre ROW for up to 15,000 vehicles, 70-metre ROW for higher traffic, and a mandatory 1-metre boundary wall to stop unauthorized entry.

No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section.
