Economy & Banking Forex Rules 4 November 2026

RBI Simplifies Forex Reporting for Service Trade

  • RBI simplified foreign-exchange reporting for service exporters and importers, easing requirements for individuals and small-value transactions.
  • Resident individuals exporting services no longer need to submit a separate Export Declaration Form.
  • For resident individual service exporters, giving payment details to their authorised dealer bank is sufficient.
  • Other exporters also get this facility for inward remittances of up to ₹10 lakh.
  • For service imports, banks must report to IDPMS only if the importer is not a resident individual and the invoice or contract value exceeds ₹10 lakh.
  • The import-reporting test therefore requires both conditions: a non-resident-individual importer and a value above ₹10 lakh.
  • The amendments were notified on 2 November 2026 and took effect from 1 October 2026.
  • The revised rules concern foreign-exchange reporting for cross-border trade in services.

Reporting changes

  • Resident individuals exporting services: payment details to the authorised dealer bank replace the separate export declaration.
  • Other exporters: the simplified facility applies to inward remittances up to ₹10 lakh.
  • Service imports: IDPMS reporting applies only when the importer is not a resident individual and the invoice or contract exceeds ₹10 lakh.

Key terms

  • RBI means Reserve Bank of India, India’s central bank.
  • IDPMS means Import Data Processing and Monitoring System; authorised dealer banks handle the specified reporting.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Q: Which institution revised the service-trade forex reporting rules? A: Reserve Bank of India.
  • Q: What form is no longer required from resident individuals exporting services? A: A separate Export Declaration Form.
  • Q: What must a resident individual service exporter provide instead? A: Payment details to the authorised dealer bank.
  • Q: What is the remittance threshold for the facility extended to other exporters? A: Up to ₹10 lakh.
  • Q: What is the full form of IDPMS? A: Import Data Processing and Monitoring System.
  • Q: When were the amendments notified? A: 2 November 2026.
  • Q: From when did the amendments take effect? A: 1 October 2026.

Source: News On AIR report

Science & Technology Pharmaceuticals 4 November 2026

Draft Drug Rules Require Authorised Batch-Release Personnel

  • The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare proposed amendments to the Drugs Rules, 1945.
  • The proposal would require manufacturers to formally authorise a person with suitable qualifications and experience to release medicine batches.
  • Manufacturers would have to maintain prescribed records for each batch produced.
  • The proposal also introduces a prescribed format for a batch-release certificate.
  • Batch release involves reviewing a batch’s quality standards and documentation before it is released for distribution.
  • Existing manufacturing licences require details of technical staff responsible for manufacturing and testing, but have no specific provision naming the batch-release authoriser.
  • The proposed requirements aim to assign responsibility and make batch-release decisions traceable.
  • The Ministry said the changes are intended to strengthen oversight of product quality and support compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP).
  • The amendments were proposed in a PIB release dated 3 November 2026; they were presented as proposals, not as rules already in force.

Proposed requirements

  • A suitably qualified and experienced person would be formally authorised for batch release.
  • A prescribed record would be kept for every manufactured batch.
  • A standard format for the batch-release certificate would be introduced.

Why batch release matters

  • The batch is reviewed against quality requirements and its documentation before distribution.
  • The proposal addresses the absence of a specific batch-release authoriser requirement in the existing manufacturing-licence provisions.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Q: Which rules are proposed to be amended? A: The Drugs Rules, 1945.
  • Q: Which ministry proposed the amendments? A: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
  • Q: Who would be authorised to release a medicine batch? A: A person with suitable qualifications and experience.
  • Q: What records would manufacturers maintain? A: Prescribed batch-release records for each batch.
  • Q: What document would receive a prescribed format? A: The batch-release certificate.
  • Q: What is the full form of GMP? A: Good Manufacturing Practices.
  • Q: Were the changes reported as already in force? A: No, they were proposed amendments.

Source: PIB release

Government Schemes Intergenerational Engagement 4 November 2026

Vayo Samman Campaign to Build Intergenerational Engagement

  • The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment scheduled the national Vayo Samman Abhiyan launch in New Delhi for 4 November 2026.
  • Union Minister Dr Virendra Kumar was to formally launch the campaign.
  • The national intergenerational-engagement campaign is being taken up under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY).
  • Its five-part model is: Connect, Converse, Co-create, Contribute and Continue—the 5Cs.
  • The campaign aims to foster sustained interaction between senior citizens and younger generations through community participation.
  • The Vayo Saathi volunteer initiative is designed to support the welfare, care and social inclusion of senior citizens.
  • Vayo Saathi is open to citizens aged 18 years and above, including students, young professionals and community volunteers.
  • The campaign framework booklet was to outline implementation roles for states, districts and community institutions.
  • The Ministry planned MoUs with Shri Ram Chandra Mission and Adhyatma Vigyan Satsang Kendra, Jodhpur on senior citizens’ health, welfare and intergenerational engagement.
  • The release cited a projection that India’s population aged 60 and above would rise from about 10 crore in 2011 to 23 crore by 2036.

The 5Cs model

  • Connect creates opportunities for interaction; Converse encourages dialogue and sharing experiences.
  • Co-create supports joint activities; Contribute enables both generations to offer their skills and knowledge.
  • Continue aims to make engagement regular and community-led rather than a one-off activity.

Participation and activities

  • The release described 12 thematic areas, including “30 Minutes Together”, “Teach Me—I’ll Teach You” and “My Story, My India”.
  • Other examples include “Digital Grandparents/Digital Saathi”, intergenerational health and wellness, and “Silver Skills” mentoring.
  • Vayo Saathi volunteers may help older people access information, welfare services, community activities and digital support.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Q: Which ministry is leading Vayo Samman Abhiyan? A: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
  • Q: Under which scheme is the campaign being taken up? A: Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY).
  • Q: What does the campaign’s 5Cs model stand for? A: Connect, Converse, Co-create, Contribute and Continue.
  • Q: What is the minimum age to join Vayo Saathi? A: 18 years.
  • Q: Who was named to launch the campaign? A: Union Minister Dr Virendra Kumar.
  • Q: Where was the campaign launch scheduled? A: New Delhi.
  • Q: What age group is covered by the cited senior-citizen population projection? A: People aged 60 and above.

Source: PIB release

Sports, Culture & Days Shooting 4 November 2026

India Wins Three Medals at ISSF World Championships in Doha

  • India won one gold and two silver medals at the ongoing ISSF World Championships in Doha.
  • Ravinder Singh, Saurabh Chaudhary and Amanpreet Singh won team gold in the men’s 50-metre pistol event.
  • Ravinder Singh also took silver in the individual competition of that event, which is not on the Olympic roster.
  • Elavenil Valarivan, Sonam Uttam Maskar and Shambhavi Shravan Kshirsagar won silver in the women’s 10-metre air rifle team event.
  • India’s men’s 50-metre pistol team recorded a combined score of 1,659.
  • Ravinder was the team’s top scorer with 559 points; Saurabh scored 551 and Amanpreet scored 549.
  • Russia’s Anton Aristarkhov won the individual men’s 50-metre pistol gold with 561 points; Romania’s Luca Joldea took bronze.
  • India stood second in the medal table with one gold and two silvers; China led with five medals, including three golds.

India’s medal-winning teams

  • Men’s 50-metre pistol team gold: Ravinder Singh, Saurabh Chaudhary and Amanpreet Singh.
  • Women’s 10-metre air rifle team silver: Elavenil Valarivan, Sonam Uttam Maskar and Shambhavi Shravan Kshirsagar.

Individual result and standings

  • Ravinder Singh won individual silver in the men’s 50-metre pistol event.
  • Anton Aristarkhov of Russia won that individual gold; Luca Joldea of Romania claimed bronze.
  • The championships were being held in Doha, Qatar.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Q: Where were the ISSF World Championships being held? A: Doha, Qatar.
  • Q: Which men’s event brought India team gold? A: 50-metre pistol.
  • Q: Who won individual silver in the men’s 50-metre pistol? A: Ravinder Singh.
  • Q: Which Indian team won silver in women’s 10-metre air rifle? A: Elavenil Valarivan, Sonam Uttam Maskar and Shambhavi Shravan Kshirsagar.
  • Q: Who won the individual men’s 50-metre pistol gold? A: Russia’s Anton Aristarkhov.
  • Q: How many medals did India win in the reported results? A: Three—one gold and two silver.
  • Q: Which country led the medal table in the report? A: China.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.

  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.

  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

What are the RBI’s new service-export reporting rules?
Resident individuals exporting services no longer need a separate Export Declaration Form; payment details to their authorised dealer bank are sufficient. Other exporters get the same facility for inward remittances up to ₹10 lakh.
When did the RBI forex reporting amendments take effect?
The amendments were notified on 2 November 2026 and took effect from 1 October 2026. They cover reporting requirements for service exports and imports.
What is proposed under the Drugs Rules, 1945 amendment?
Manufacturers would have to authorise a suitably qualified and experienced person to release medicine batches. They would also maintain prescribed records for every batch and use a prescribed batch-release certificate format.
What is the Vayo Samman national campaign?
It is a national intergenerational-engagement campaign under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana. It aims to encourage sustained interaction between senior citizens and younger generations through community participation.
What does Vayo Saathi do, and who can join?
