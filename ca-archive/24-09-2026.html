Defence Procurement 24 September 2026

MoD Inks ₹811 Crore Contract with BDL for 160 SAT-SAAW Weapons

  • The Ministry of Defence (MoD) signed an ₹810.79 crore contract with Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) for procuring 160 Satellite Smart Anti-Airfield Weapons (SAT-SAAW) for the Indian Air Force.
  • The contract was signed in New Delhi under the Buy (Indian-IDDM) category in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh.
  • The SAT-SAAW is an indigenous air-to-surface precision-guided glide bomb designed and developed by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO).
  • The glide weapon system boasts 60% indigenous content, featuring domestic sub-systems like the Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU), Long Impact Delay Fuze, and Twin Store Carrier.
  • Capable of destroying enemy runways from stand-off ranges, the weapon will be integrated onto fighter aircraft including Jaguar, Hawk, and Sukhoi-30 MKI.
  • Deliveries of the SAT-SAAW glide bombs to the IAF are scheduled between 2027–28 and 2028–29.

Defence Joint Exercise 24 September 2026

13th India-Sri Lanka Bilateral Maritime Exercise SLINEX-26 Concludes in Visakhapatnam

  • The 13th edition of the India-Sri Lanka bilateral maritime exercise, SLINEX-26, concluded on 21 September 2026 in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.
  • The exercise was conducted over five days from 17 to 21 September 2026, comprising both Harbour and Sea phases.
  • The Sea Phase included tactical manoeuvres, surface firings, cross-deck aviation operations, and Visit, Board, Search and Seizure (VBSS) drills.
  • Special forces from both navies carried out joint slithering operations and night replenishment at sea exercises.
  • The exercise supports India's MAHASAGAR vision and its core foreign policy principle of Neighbourhood First.

Cooperative Mobility Service 'Bharat Taxi' Rolled Out in Uttar Pradesh Across 5 Cities

Economy & Banking Railways 24 September 2026

Indian Railways Completes Final Tadoki-Rowghat Section of 95 km Dallirajhara Line

  • Indian Railways completed the 17.5 km Tadoki-Rowghat section under the Raipur Division of the South East Central Railway (SECR) zone.
  • This marks the completion of the final stretch of the 95 km Dallirajhara-Rowghat broad-gauge railway project in south Chhattisgarh.
  • The newly completed single-line electrified broad-gauge track successfully underwent statutory CRS (Commissioner of Railway Safety) inspection and speed trials.
  • The railway corridor directly enables raw material transport, specifically hauling high-grade iron ore to the Bhilai Steel Plant (SAIL).
  • The project will drastically improve passenger and freight connectivity between tribal regions of south Chhattisgarh and commercial centers like Raipur and Bhilai.

Environment & Ecology Wildlife Protection 24 September 2026

DRI Seizes 86 Live Endangered Tokay Geckos in Mariani, Assam

  • Officers of the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) intercepted an illegal wildlife trafficking racket at Mariani in Assam.
  • A total of 86 live Tokay Geckos (Gekko gecko) were recovered from two traffickers at Bandar Chalia village ground.
  • The Tokay Gecko is protected under Schedule I of India’s Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972.
  • Internationally, the species is listed under Appendix II of the CITES (Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species).
  • Both accused and the rescued reptiles were handed over to the Mariani Range Forest Office for further legal prosecution.
  • Tokay Geckos are heavily poached in the Northeast due to superstitious claims of medicinal value and high demand in the illegal exotic pet trade.

Economy & Banking Market Intervention 24 September 2026

Kanda Express Delivers Buffer Stock Onion Consignment to Kolkata at ₹35/kg

  • The Kanda Express arrived in Kolkata carrying a major consignment of onion from the central government's Price Stabilisation Fund (PSF) buffer stock.
  • Onions are being supplied to consumers at a subsidized retail price of ₹35 per kilogram through NAFED and NCCF mobile vans.
  • Over 1,400 metric tonnes of buffer onion had already been dispatched to West Bengal via road routes prior to the train shipment.
  • Retail distribution is operational across 25+ key markets in Kolkata, including Kole Market, Mechua Mandi, and Shyambazar Market.
  • The central government is collaborating with West Bengal's state retail network ‘Sufal Bangla’ to broaden doorstep distribution.
  • Market interventions via rail-road hybrid logistics have stabilized or reduced retail onion prices in 17 states and Union Territories.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SAT-SAAW and who manufactures it?
SAT-SAAW (Satellite Smart Anti-Airfield Weapon) is an air-to-surface precision-guided glide bomb designed by DRDO to neutralize enemy airfields from stand-off ranges. It is manufactured by Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) for the Indian Air Force under an ₹810.79 crore contract.
Where did SLINEX-26 take place?
The 13th edition of the India-Sri Lanka bilateral maritime exercise, SLINEX-26, took place in Visakhapatnam from 17 to 21 September 2026, comprising both harbour and sea training phases.
What is the unique feature of the Bharat Taxi service launched in Uttar Pradesh?
Bharat Taxi is a cooperative ride-hailing model that functions on the principle of 'Driver is the Owner'. Unlike conventional aggregators, the revenue goes directly to the drivers' accounts without intermediary commission cuts, granting drivers ownership in the cooperative.
Why is the Tadoki-Rowghat rail section significant for the Indian steel industry?
The 17.5 km Tadoki-Rowghat railway stretch completes the 95 km Dallirajhara-Rowghat project in southern Chhattisgarh. It facilitates the seamless bulk transportation of high-grade iron ore to the Bhilai Steel Plant (SAIL).
What legal protection does the Tokay Gecko have in India?
The Tokay Gecko is an endangered reptile listed under Schedule I of India's Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972, granting it the highest level of legal protection. It is also listed under Appendix II of CITES to control international commercial trade.
What is Kanda Express?
Kanda Express is a specialized freight train initiative by the central government to transport buffer stock onions under the Price Stabilisation Fund (PSF) to major consuming centres like Kolkata, ensuring onions are available at a subsidized rate of ₹35 per kg.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
