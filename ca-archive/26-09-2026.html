Defence Maritime 26 September 2026

ICGS Shaunak Sails from Chennai for Quad-at-Sea Observer Mission

  • Indian Coast Guard Ship ICGS Shaunak departed Chennai on 25 September 2026 bound for Perth, Australia.
  • The voyage is being conducted under the maiden Quad-at-Sea Ship Observer Mission.
  • The mission features six sea riders, comprising two officers each from India and Japan, and one each from Australia and the United States.
  • This maritime outreach aligns with the Quad Foreign Ministers' joint statement issued on 26 May 2026.
  • The deployment reinforces India's maritime vision of MAHASAGAR (Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth for All in the Region).

Defence Healthcare 26 September 2026

AFMS Conducts 10th Mega Eye Surgery Camp Under Operation Drishti in Kupwara

  • Armed Forces Medical Services (AFMS) successfully conducted the 10th edition of a mega eye surgery camp under Operation Drishti.
  • The two-day camp was organized on 24–25 September 2026 at Tangdhar, Kupwara district near the Line of Control in Jammu & Kashmir.
  • Military eye surgeons performed 220 sight-restoring surgeries, predominantly advanced cataract phacoemulsification, alongside vitreoretinal and micro-invasive glaucoma procedures.
  • The camp was led by Brigadier Sanjay Kumar Mishra, Head of Ophthalmology at Army Hospital (Research & Referral), Delhi Cantt.
  • Chief of the Army Staff General Dheeraj Seth and DGAFMS Air Marshal Sandeep Thareja reviewed the facilities and interacted with patients.
  • Under the AFMS initiative, over 1.5 lakh patients have been screened and more than 3,500 surgeries completed across 10 cities; the 11th camp will take place in Srinagar.

Key Highlights of the Medical Mission

  • Patients were mobilized from border villages including Kandi, Khavarpora, Teetwal, and Tad via announcements on Radio Ruh-e-Karnah.
  • Advanced equipment for phacoemulsification and vitreoretinal surgery was transported to high-altitude frontier areas.
  • The 11th edition of the camp is scheduled at Srinagar from 27 to 29 September 2026, with the valedictory address by Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Under which operation did AFMS organize the mega eye surgery camp? — Operation Drishti
  • Where was the 10th edition of Operation Drishti held? — Tangdhar, Kupwara (Jammu & Kashmir)
  • How many surgeries were performed during the 10th camp? — 220 sight-restoring surgeries
  • Who led the medical surgical team at Tangdhar? — Brigadier Sanjay Kumar Mishra (Army Hospital R&R)
  • Who is the current Director General of Armed Forces Medical Services (DGAFMS)? — Air Marshal Sandeep Thareja
  • Where is the 11th edition of Operation Drishti scheduled? — Srinagar

Government Schemes Cooperatives 26 September 2026

National Cooperative Training Framework 2026 Consultation Held in New Delhi

  • The first national-level agenda-setting consultative meeting on the National Cooperative Training Framework (NCTF) 2026 was held at Atal Akshay Urja Bhawan, New Delhi.
  • The consultation was organized by "Tribhuvan" Cooperative University (TSU) in collaboration with the Ministry of Cooperation.
  • Minister of State for Cooperation Krishan Pal Gurjar inaugurated the agenda-setting session.
  • NCTF 2026 aims to establish a coherent, quality-assured, and outcome-oriented human resource and skill development ecosystem under the vision of "Sahkar se Samriddhi".
  • Following this national conclave, five regional consultative meetings will be organized across North, South, East, West, and North-East India.

Key Framework Objectives

  • Shifting cooperative training metrics from mere quantity of participants to quality and measurable outcomes.
  • Creating institutional linkages among national federations, state unions, and cooperative training institutes.
  • Led academically by Vice Chancellor of TSU, Prof. (Dr.) S.P. Singh.

Important Facts for Exams

  • What does NCTF stand for? — National Cooperative Training Framework
  • Which university organized the NCTF 2026 consultation? — Tribhuvan Cooperative University (TSU)
  • Where was the national consultative meeting on NCTF 2026 held? — Atal Akshay Urja Bhawan, New Delhi
  • How many regional consultations are planned under NCTF? — Five (North, South, East, West, and North-East)
  • Under which national vision is NCTF 2026 anchored? — Sahkar se Samriddhi

Government Schemes Governance 26 September 2026

DARPG Releases 49th Monthly CPGRAMS Report for States/UTs (August 2026)

  • The Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG) released the 49th monthly report on CPGRAMS for States/UTs for August 2026.
  • A total of 98,997 public grievance cases were received and 79,639 cases were disposed of by States and Union Territories during August.
  • Uttar Pradesh topped the country in grievance disposal with 28,939 cases resolved, followed by Gujarat (6,810) and Rajasthan (5,895).
  • Under the Sevottam Scheme, 1,270 training courses have been completed and 42,011 officers trained over the past five financial years (FY 2022–23 to FY 2026–27).
  • West Bengal registered the highest number of new portal user registrations (42,054 out of 1,38,801 total) and the highest complaints via Common Service Centres (CSCs).

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Quad-at-Sea Ship Observer Mission?
It is a joint maritime initiative among Quad partners (India, Australia, Japan, and the USA) to foster naval interoperability and shared maritime domain awareness. Indian Coast Guard Ship ICGS Shaunak departed Chennai for Perth carrying six observers representing all four Quad countries.
What was achieved under Operation Drishti in Tangdhar, Kupwara?
Under Operation Drishti, the Armed Forces Medical Services (AFMS) organized its 10th mega eye surgery camp near the LoC in Tangdhar, Kupwara, successfully performing 220 sight-restoring surgeries including advanced cataract, vitreoretinal, and glaucoma procedures.
Which Indian athletes won medals at the Asian Games on 25 September 2026?
Gulveer Singh won Silver in the Men's 10,000m running event, Manpreet Kaur bagged Bronze in the Women's Shot Put, and debutant Baranica Elangovan secured Bronze in the Women's Pole Vault at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.
Where was the 4th Uttar Pradesh International Trade Show held?
The 4th UP International Trade Show was held at Greater Noida. It was inaugurated by Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah on 25 September 2026, marking 12 years of the Make in India initiative.
What is the purpose of the National Cooperative Training Framework (NCTF) 2026?
NCTF 2026 is an institutional blueprint developed by Tribhuvan Cooperative University and the Ministry of Cooperation to overhaul cooperative sector training, focusing on professional skill enhancement, quality outcomes, and uniform standards across India.
Which strategic MoUs did the Ministry of Minority Affairs sign at IISc Bengaluru?
The Ministry partnered with IIM Bangalore for independent third-party monitoring of PM VIKAS, CUBE IIT Madras for PMJVK infrastructure evaluation, and FSID IISc along with NSRCEL IIMB under the PM Minority Venture Fund for incubating deep-tech minority startups.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section.
