Government Schemes Agriculture 11 October 2026

PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana Completes One Year

  • PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana (PM-DDKY) focuses on agricultural development in 100 selected districts across India.
  • The Union Cabinet approved the scheme on 16 July 2025; the Prime Minister launched it on 11 October 2025.
  • The scheme began in FY 2025–26 and has a six-year duration; its first anniversary falls on 11 October 2026.
  • Districts were selected using three indicators: low agricultural productivity, low cropping intensity and low agricultural credit disbursement.
  • Selection also considered each State and Union Territory’s share of net sown area and agricultural landholdings; every State has at least one selected district.
  • Implementation brings together 11 ministries and departments and converges 36 Central schemes, alongside State schemes and private-sector participation.
  • The scheme uses a three-tier structure: district committees prepare and implement plans, State committees review them, and national committees oversee the scheme.
  • Each District Action Plan is based on local needs and assessment, with outcome targets, annual milestones and coordinated scheme interventions.
  • Progress across the 36 schemes is assessed through 121 indicators: 74 output indicators and 47 outcome indicators; output indicators inform monthly district rankings on the PM-DDKY dashboard.
  • All 100 districts had prepared District Action Plans and submitted output-indicator data by August 2026; their average score across six objectives rose from 18.85 in April to 46.89 in August 2026.

Selection and implementation

  • The three main selection indicators are low agricultural productivity, low cropping intensity and low agricultural credit disbursement.
  • The District Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana Committee is headed by the District Collector or District Magistrate; the State committee is headed by the Chief Secretary.
  • At national level, the National Executive Committee is chaired by the Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister, while the National Monitoring Committee is chaired by the Secretary, Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

Progress and farmer engagement

  • The PM-DDKY dashboard began operating in the last week of October 2025, and monthly output-indicator rankings began in November 2025.
  • The December 2025 PM-DDKY FPO Sangam brought together more than 72 Farmer Producer Organisations from 15 States and 33 scheme districts.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Q: How many districts are covered by PM-DDKY? A: 100.
  • Q: When was PM-DDKY launched? A: 11 October 2025.
  • Q: What is the scheme’s duration? A: Six years from FY 2025–26.
  • Q: What are the three main district-selection indicators? A: Low agricultural productivity, low cropping intensity and low agricultural credit disbursement.
  • Q: How many Central schemes are converged under PM-DDKY? A: 36.
  • Q: How many indicators are used to assess progress? A: 121—74 output and 47 outcome indicators.
  • Q: Who heads the district-level scheme committee? A: The District Collector or District Magistrate.

Economy & Banking Banking 11 October 2026

RBI Raises Banks’ Daily CRR Maintenance to 99%

  • The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) raised the daily minimum maintenance requirement for banks’ Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) to 99% of the required CRR.
  • The revised daily maintenance requirement takes effect on 16 October 2026.
  • Banks currently have to maintain 90% of the required CRR on a daily basis, making the new requirement a rise from 90% to 99%.
  • The change concerns the proportion of the required CRR maintained daily; it is not a change in the CRR rate itself.
  • The RBI made the decision after reviewing liquidity conditions in the banking system.
  • Banking-system liquidity was in a surplus of about ₹3.88 lakh crore as of 8 October 2026.
  • The stated purpose of the move is to reduce surplus liquidity in the banking system.
  • CRR stands for Cash Reserve Ratio; it is a reserve requirement imposed on banks by the RBI.

  • Q: What is the full form of CRR? A: Cash Reserve Ratio.
  • Q: Which institution announced the daily CRR maintenance change? A: Reserve Bank of India.
  • Q: What is the new daily maintenance requirement? A: 99% of the required CRR.
  • Q: When does the revised requirement take effect? A: 16 October 2026.
  • Q: What was the earlier daily maintenance requirement? A: 90% of the required CRR.
  • Q: What was the reported banking-system liquidity position on 8 October 2026? A: A surplus of about ₹3.88 lakh crore.

Defence Indian Navy 11 October 2026

Indian Navy’s NGOPV Shreyas Launched in Kolkata

  • The next-generation offshore patrol vessel NGOPV Yard 3040, Shreyas, was launched on 10 October 2026.
  • The launch took place at Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE) in Kolkata.
  • Mrs Raman Kochar performed the launch in the presence of Vice Admiral Ajay Kochhar, Vice Chief of the Naval Staff.
  • NGOPVs are being built concurrently at two shipyards: GRSE, Kolkata, and Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL), Goa.
  • The vessels are designed and built indigenously for the Indian Navy.
  • Their roles include maritime surveillance, search and rescue, and protection of offshore assets.
  • They are also intended for humanitarian assistance and disaster relief (HADR) and anti-piracy operations.
  • The name Shreyas is derived from Sanskrit and means “best” or “excellent”.
  • The launch supports India’s indigenous shipbuilding effort and aligns with the visions of Aatmanirbhar Bharat and Make in India.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana?
It is a six-year initiative focused on agricultural development in 100 selected districts. It began in FY 2025–26 and brings together Central schemes with State schemes and private-sector participation.
When was PM-DDKY launched and what are its selection criteria?
The Prime Minister launched it on 11 October 2025. District selection considered low agricultural productivity, low cropping intensity and low agricultural credit disbursement, alongside land-related considerations.
How many indicators does PM-DDKY use to track progress?
It uses 121 indicators across 36 Central schemes. These include 74 output indicators and 47 outcome indicators.
What change has the RBI made to daily CRR maintenance?
From 16 October 2026, banks must maintain 99% of their required CRR daily, up from 90%. The RBI said the move followed a review of banking-system liquidity.
Where and when was the patrol vessel Shreyas launched?
Shreyas, NGOPV Yard 3040, was launched on 10 October 2026 at GRSE in Kolkata. Mrs Raman Kochar performed the launch in the presence of Vice Admiral Ajay Kochhar.
What are the roles of the Indian Navy’s NGOPVs?
The vessels are intended for maritime surveillance, search and rescue, offshore asset protection, humanitarian assistance and disaster relief, and anti-piracy operations. They are being built at GRSE in Kolkata and Goa Shipyard Limited in Goa.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section.
