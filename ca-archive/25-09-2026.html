Science & Technology Deep Tech 25 September 2026

DRDO Signs TDF Project for Indigenous 20 mK Dilution Refrigerator

  • DRDO signed its first high-value deep-tech project agreement under the Technology Development Fund (TDF) scheme with startup Zero MK India Private Limited (Alwar, Rajasthan).
  • The project aims to achieve the indigenous design and development of a 20 milliKelvin (mK) dilution refrigerator for quantum applications.
  • This is the first major contract awarded under the Rs 500 crore deep-tech fund approved by Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh.
  • Dilution refrigerators are specialized cryogenic systems that cool environments to milliKelvin levels, critical for operating quantum computing platforms.
  • The project directly supports the goals of the National Quantum Mission to foster an Atmanirbhar quantum hardware ecosystem.
  • DRDO's Solid State Physics Laboratory (SSPL) is providing technical monitoring, guidance, and project support.

  • Which startup partnered with DRDO for this project? Zero MK India Private Limited (Alwar, Rajasthan).
  • What is the total outlay of the deep-tech fund approved by Raksha Mantri? Rs 500 crore.
  • Which DRDO laboratory is mentoring and guiding this cryogenic project? Solid State Physics Laboratory (SSPL).
  • Under which national mission is this development aligned? National Quantum Mission.

Environment & Ecology BioE3 Mission 25 September 2026

Dr. Jitendra Singh Launches Commercial PLA-Based Biodegradable Plastic

  • Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh launched the commercial rollout of Polylactic Acid (PLA) based biodegradable plastic under the BioE3 framework.
  • A 100 TPA pilot facility is being established at Balrampur Chini Mills Limited (BCML) in Kumbhi, Uttar Pradesh.
  • The pilot facility is supported by a Rs 75 crore grant from BIRAC under the Department of Biotechnology (DBT).
  • The initiative converts sugarcane and biomass feedstocks into lactic acid and advanced biopolymers, promoting a 'Waste to Wealth' paradigm.
  • India's bio-economy has surged from approximately $10 billion in 2014 to nearly $195 billion.
  • The facility focuses on developing specialty PLA grades and co-polymers for commercial and high-value packaging applications.

Defence Naval Voyage 25 September 2026

INS Sudarshini Reaches Alexandria, Egypt Under Lokayan-2026 Voyage

  • Indian Navy's sail training ship INS Sudarshini made its second operational port call at Alexandria, Egypt on 23 September 2026.
  • The 10-month intercontinental training voyage Lokayan-2026 was flagged off on 20 January 2026 from Kochi, Kerala.
  • The mission encompasses 22,000 nautical miles across 18 ports in 13 countries to provide classic sail training to naval cadets.
  • During this voyage, the three-masted ship participated in France's maritime festival 'Escale à Sète' and crossed the Atlantic Ocean.
  • The voyage serves as an ambassador for India's maritime legacy, advancing the MAHASAGAR regional security and growth doctrine.

Voyage Milestones

  • Traversed the Arabian Sea, Red Sea, Suez Canal, Mediterranean Sea, and the Atlantic Ocean.
  • Crossed the Atlantic Ocean after departing Cape Verde, arriving in Antigua on 27 May 2026.
  • Surpassed the 10,000 nautical mile milestone before calling on Lisbon, Portugal as its 15th port.

Important Facts for Exams

Government Schemes Water Resources 25 September 2026

India and Netherlands Sign MoUs for Gujarat's Kalpasar Project

  • Union Jal Shakti Minister C.R. Paatil and Dutch Vice-Minister Jaap Slootmaker held a bilateral meeting at the 9th India International Water Week (IIWW) 2026 in New Delhi.
  • Two tripartite MoUs were signed between CWC, Gujarat's Kalpasar Department, and Dutch research institutes Deltares and TU Delft.
  • The collaboration supports hydraulic modeling, coastal dyke engineering, and structural design for Gujarat's Kalpasar Project.
  • The Kalpasar Project envisions a massive freshwater reservoir in the Gulf of Khambhat using waters of the Sabarmati, Mahi, Dhadhar, and Narmada rivers.
  • The technical engineering model draws insights from the Netherlands' century-old landmark sea enclosure dam, Afsluitdijk.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of DRDO's dilution refrigerator project?
The project aims to indigenously build a 20 milliKelvin dilution refrigerator with startup Zero MK India Pvt Ltd. It provides the ultralow cryogenic temperatures needed to operate and test quantum computing hardware under the National Quantum Mission.
What is the BioE3 PLA bioplastic initiative?
It is an initiative under the BioE3 framework where Balrampur Chini Mills Limited (BCML) is setting up a 100 TPA pilot facility in Kumbhi, Uttar Pradesh. Supported by a Rs 75 crore BIRAC grant, it converts sugarcane biomass into polylactic acid (PLA) biodegradable plastic.
Where and when is the BRICS Theatre Festival 2026 being held?
The BRICS Theatre Festival 2026 will take place in New Delhi from 12 to 14 October 2026. Organised by the National School of Drama, its theme is 'Shared Stories, Shared Futures'.
What is the current status of India-ASEAN trade?
Bilateral trade between India and ASEAN reached $128.38 billion in 2025-26, making up 10.55% of India's total global trade. Both sides are currently reviewing the ASEAN-India Trade in Goods Agreement (AITIGA).
What is the Lokayan-2026 mission of INS Sudarshini?
Lokayan-2026 is a 10-month intercontinental training deployment of the Indian Navy's sail training ship INS Sudarshini. Starting from Kochi in January 2026, it covers 22,000 nautical miles across 18 ports in 13 countries.
What is Gujarat's Kalpasar Project and why is the Netherlands collaborating on it?
The Kalpasar Project involves building a freshwater reservoir in Gujarat's Gulf of Khambhat by damming rivers like Sabarmati, Mahi, and Dhadhar. Dutch institutes Deltares and TU Delft signed MoUs to provide technical design and coastal dyke modeling based on their Afsluitdijk expertise.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
