Awards & Honours Cinema 21 September 2026

72nd National Film Awards to be Conferred at Ekta Nagar, Gujarat

  • The President of India will confer the 72nd National Film Awards on 22 September 2026 at Ekta Nagar (formerly Kevadia), Gujarat.
  • This marks a historic shift under the 'Ek Bharat, Shreshtha Bharat' initiative to host the awards in different parts of the country outside New Delhi.
  • The National Film Awards were instituted in 1954 (honouring films of 1953) following recommendations of the 1949 Film Enquiry Committee.
  • In the first edition, the Marathi film 'Shyamchi Aai' won the President's Gold Medal for Best Feature Film, and 'Mahabalipuram' won Best Documentary.
  • Best Actor and Actress awards were introduced in 1967-68, originally titled 'Bharat' and 'Urvashi', won by Uttam Kumar and Nargis respectively.
  • The Dadasaheb Phalke Award was instituted in 1969, with Devika Rani as its inaugural recipient.
  • In the 72nd edition, non-Schedule 8 languages were recognized, with Garhwali film 'Dholi' and Tulu film 'Imbu' bagging honors.
  • The awards are currently organized by the National Film Development Corporation (NFDC), following the Directorate of Film Festivals (DFF) merger in 2022.

Defence Joint Exercise 21 September 2026

13th Edition of India-Sri Lanka Maritime Exercise SLINEX-26 Held at Visakhapatnam

  • The 13th edition of the bilateral maritime exercise SLINEX-26 between India and Sri Lanka was conducted from 17 to 21 September 2026 at Visakhapatnam.
  • The exercise comprised a Harbour Phase followed by an operational Sea Phase.
  • Indian Navy was represented by indigenous anti-submarine warfare corvette INS Kavaratti and fleet tanker INS Jyoti.
  • Sri Lanka Navy was represented by offshore patrol vessel SLNS Sindurala.
  • The exercise operates under the operational command of the Eastern Naval Command headquartered at Visakhapatnam.
  • Initiated in 2005, SLINEX has become a premier platform for enhancing maritime cooperation and interoperability between both navies.

Government Schemes Legal Aid 21 September 2026

Department of Justice Holds DISHA 2.0 Regional Workshop in Goa

  • The Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice, organized a regional workshop under the DISHA 2.0 scheme in Goa.
  • DISHA stands for Designing Innovative Solutions for Holistic Access to Justice.
  • The workshop was hosted at the India International University of Legal Education and Research, Goa.
  • A key highlight was the showcase of top reels under the 'Nyaya Prabodh: Nyaya Ki Jagriti' campaign titled 'Know Your Rights in 90 Seconds'.
  • The workshop featured interactive sessions with beneficiaries of Tele-Law, Nyaya Bandhu (pro bono legal services), and Vidhi-Sanjeevani.

DISHA Scheme Components

  • Tele-Law: Connects citizens with panel lawyers through Common Services Centres (CSCs).
  • Nyaya Bandhu: Mobile platform connecting pro bono advocates with marginalized beneficiaries.
  • Nyaya Prabodh: Legal literacy and awareness initiative empowering citizens on legal rights.

Important Facts for Exams

  • What does the acronym DISHA stand for? Designing Innovative Solutions for Holistic Access to Justice
  • Where was the DISHA 2.0 regional workshop held on 21 September 2026? Goa
  • Which campaign under DISHA promoted legal awareness via 90-second reels? Nyaya Prabodh: Nyaya Ki Jagriti
  • Which department implements the DISHA scheme? Department of Justice, Ministry of Law and Justice

Environment & Ecology Conferences 21 September 2026

NGT International Conference Concludes in New Delhi; Souvenir 'Harit Nyayam' Released

  • Union Minister Manohar Lal highlighted that India has reached ~55% installed electricity capacity from non-fossil fuel sources, achieving its 50% target five years ahead of schedule.

Defence Air Show 21 September 2026

IAF's Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team Performs First-Ever Air Show in Meerut

  • The Indian Air Force's Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team (SKAT) performed its first-ever air show over Meerut, Uttar Pradesh on 19-20 September 2026.
  • The air show was organized under the 'Seva Sankalp Abhiyan' and witnessed by over one lakh spectators.
  • The team flew nine Hawk Mk-132 aircraft, manufactured under licence in India by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL).
  • The aircraft were modified at the Base Repair Depot (BRD), Nashik, equipped with smoke generation systems to emit saffron, white, and green smoke.
  • Formed in 1996, SKAT is Asia's only 9-aircraft aerobatic team and has performed over 800 air displays over its 30-year journey.

Important Facts for Exams

  • Where did the IAF Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team stage its first-ever air show in September 2026? Meerut, Uttar Pradesh
  • Which aircraft are flown by the Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team? Hawk Mk-132 (manufactured by HAL)
  • In which year was the Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team (SKAT) formed? 1996
  • Which Base Repair Depot modified the Hawk aircraft for tricolour smoke emission? BRD Nashik
  • SKAT is the only 9-aircraft aerobatic team in which continent? Asia

Central Silk Board Marks 77th Foundation Day; Sets 60,000 MT Target by 2030-31

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the 72nd National Film Awards ceremony being held?
The 72nd National Film Awards ceremony is being organized at Ekta Nagar (formerly Kevadia), Gujarat, on 22 September 2026. This marks a shift from hosting the awards exclusively in New Delhi under the 'Ek Bharat, Shreshtha Bharat' initiative.
What is SLINEX and which ships participated in SLINEX-26?
SLINEX is the annual bilateral maritime exercise between the navies of India and Sri Lanka. In the 13th edition (SLINEX-26) at Visakhapatnam, the Indian Navy deployed INS Kavaratti and INS Jyoti, while the Sri Lanka Navy deployed SLNS Sindurala.
What is the theme of India International Water Week 2026?
The theme of the 9th India International Water Week 2026 held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, is 'Climate Resilient Water Management'. Netherlands and Germany participated as official partner countries.
What is the DISHA scheme of the Ministry of Law and Justice?
DISHA (Designing Innovative Solutions for Holistic Access to Justice) is an initiative of the Department of Justice to advance citizen-centric justice delivery. It consolidates key outreach and legal aid schemes including Tele-Law, Nyaya Bandhu, and legal literacy campaigns like Nyaya Prabodh.
What is the Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team of the Indian Air Force?
Formed in 1996, the Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team (SKAT) is Asia's only nine-aircraft precision aerobatic team. Flying HAL-built Hawk Mk-132 aircraft, the team has completed over 800 air displays across India and foreign countries over its 30-year history.
Which state leads in silk production in India and what is India's 2030-31 production target?
Karnataka is the largest producer of raw silk in India. On the Central Silk Board's 77th Foundation Day, the government announced an ambition to raise India's silk production to 60,000 metric tonnes by 2030-31 and become the global leader by 2028-29.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
