Defence Summits 29 September 2026

Home Minister Amit Shah Inaugurates Conference on PRAHAAR Counter-Terrorism Strategy

  • Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah inaugurated the conference on 'PRAHAAR - Counter-Terrorism (CT) Policy and Strategy' in New Delhi.
  • The conference deliberated on the seven pillars of the PRAHAAR strategy: prevention, reaction, internal capacity integration, human rights, addressing conditions conducive to terrorism, synchronizing international efforts, and resilience/recovery via a whole-of-society approach.
  • The Home Minister called for establishing a concrete and institutionalized 10-year PRAHAAR mechanism extending down to the police station level.
  • Amit Shah officially launched a new Extradition Portal to streamline and expedite efforts to bring back absconding criminals and fugitives from foreign countries.
  • The Union government brought back 288 fugitive offenders from 36 countries between 2019 and 2026.
  • Under the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), the government designated the cross-border narcotics, arms, and explosives smuggling syndicate Shehzad Bhatti Network as a terrorist entity.

Environment & Ecology Summits 29 September 2026

India Showcases Forest and Landscape Restoration Milestones at FAO COFO 28 in Rome

  • India presented its landscape and forest restoration achievements at the 28th Session of the Committee on Forestry (COFO 28) held in Rome, Italy (28 September – 2 October 2026).
  • According to the statement, India's forest and tree cover stands at 8.274 crore hectares (82.74 million ha), accounting for 25.17% of the country's total geographical area.
  • As per FAO's Global Forest Resources Assessment (FRA) 2025, India ranks 9th globally in total forest area and 3rd globally in net annual forest area gain.
  • India created an additional carbon sink of 2.29 billion tonnes of CO2 equivalent by 2021, and raised its target to 3.5–4.0 billion tonnes by 2035 compared to 2005 levels.
  • Under the Bonn Challenge, India pledged to restore 2.6 crore hectares of degraded land by 2030, of which 2.176 crore hectares have already been brought under restoration.
  • India highlighted key national initiatives including the Aravalli Green Wall project (identifying 64.5 lakh hectares for restoration), the MISHTI scheme for mangrove restoration, 101 Ramsar sites, and the planting of over 3.5 billion saplings under the 'Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam' campaign.

Economy & Banking Summits 29 September 2026

Kolkata Selected to Host the 5th Edition of India Energy Week in January 2027

  • Union Minister of Petroleum and Natural Gas Hardeep Singh Puri officially announced that Kolkata will host India Energy Week (IEW) 2027.
  • The event will be held from 28 to 31 January 2027 at the New Town Convention Centre, Kolkata, marking the first time IEW travels to Eastern India.
  • The official theme for IEW 2027 is 'Fueling Innovation. Securing Energy. Shaping Tomorrow'.
  • IEW 2027 is organized under the patronage of the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas by the Federation of Indian Petroleum Industry (FIPI) in partnership with Informa Markets.
  • The inaugural edition was held in Bengaluru in 2023, while the 4th edition took place in Goa in 2026.

Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal Travels to USA for G20 Trade Ministers' Meeting

  • Union Minister of Commerce and Industry Piyush Goyal embarked on an official visit to the United States from 29 September to 5 October 2026.
  • He will participate in the G20 Trade Ministers' Meeting scheduled for 30 September and 1 October 2026 in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, on the invitation of the United States Trade Representative (USTR).

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PRAHAAR counter-terrorism strategy launched by Amit Shah?
PRAHAAR is India's 10-year counter-terrorism policy and strategy framework resting on seven pillars including prevention, response, human rights, and internal capability integration. It aims to institutionalize automated anti-terror response mechanisms down to local police station levels.
What are India's forest cover rankings according to FAO FRA 2025?
As highlighted at COFO 28 in Rome, India ranks 9th globally in total forest area and 3rd globally in net annual forest area gain. Forest and tree cover spans 82.74 million hectares, representing 25.17% of India's geographical area.
Where and when will India Energy Week 2027 take place?
India Energy Week 2027 will be hosted in Kolkata, West Bengal, from 28 to 31 January 2027 at the New Town Convention Centre. It marks the first time the premier global energy summit is held in Eastern India.
Why is Union Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal visiting the United States?
Piyush Goyal is attending the G20 Trade Ministers' Meeting held in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, on 30 September and 1 October 2026. He is also holding bilateral discussions on the India-US Bilateral Trade Agreement (BTA).
What was discussed during the India-France education ministerial meeting?
Education Minister Pralhad Joshi and French Minister Philippe Baptiste discussed academic mobility, branch campuses at GIFT City Gujarat, and updating the Mutual Recognition of Qualifications (MRQ) agreement during the Indo-French Innovation Year 2026.
Which medals did Indian athletes win at the Asian Games 2026 on 28 September?
Esha Singh won Silver in Women's 25m Pistol, Yashvir Singh won Silver and Rohit Yadav won Bronze in Men's Javelin, Murali Sreeshankar earned a Long Jump podium finish, Parul Chaudhary won Bronze in 5000m, and Pinkcy Balhara secured Bronze in Kurash (78kg).
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
