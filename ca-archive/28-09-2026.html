Economy & Banking Railways 28 September 2026

Amit Shah Flags Off India's First LNG Train and Sabarmati Projects

  • Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah launched railway infrastructure projects worth ₹1,472 crore from Sabarmati Railway Station in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
  • The Minister flagged off the country's first LNG-powered train, marking a significant milestone towards clean fuel and green transportation in Indian Railways.
  • Inaugurated a dedicated WAG-12 maintenance shed constructed at a cost of ₹700 crore to maintain 12,000 HP twin-section electric locomotives.
  • Launched the Kavach automatic train protection system on the Sabarmati–Kalol–Sabarmati section built at an outlay of ₹48 crore.
  • Announced that Indian Railways has achieved 99.6% route electrification, with only Sweden being the other country to reach 100% railway electrification globally.
  • Highlighted that railway diesel consumption has declined sharply from 293 crore litres in 2015–16 to 108 crore litres in 2024–25.

  • What is the power capacity of the WAG-12 electric locomotive? 12,000 Horsepower (HP).

Defence Air Exercise 28 September 2026

IAF Commences Operational Phase of Exercise Tarang Shakti-2026 in Jodhpur

  • The Indian Air Force (IAF) commenced the operational phase of the multilateral air exercise 'Exercise Tarang Shakti-2026' at Air Force Station Jodhpur.
  • The exercise is scheduled to be conducted from 26 September to 12 October 2026 across western airspace.
  • The official theme of the exercise is 'Strength in Synergy' (साझेदारी में शक्ति).
  • Seven friendly foreign nations are actively participating: Australia, USA, UAE, France, Germany, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh.
  • Participating foreign combat and support aircraft include F-35 Lightning II, Rafale, F-16, A400M, MRTT, C-130, C-130J, and KC-30A.
  • Side activities during the exercise include the Sekhon Marathon, aerospace industrial tours, cultural heritage visits, and yoga sessions.

Government Schemes Digital Portal 28 September 2026

Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh Launches Integrated Livestock Insurance Portal

  • Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh alias Lalan Singh launched the dedicated 'Livestock Insurance Portal' (पशुधन बीमा पोर्टल) in New Delhi.
  • The initiative is developed by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD) under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying.
  • The unified portal provides digital management for policy issuance, claim settlement, real-time monitoring, and reporting under the Livestock Insurance Scheme.
  • To ensure accessibility for farmers, the portal supports Hindi, English, and 11 regional languages (13 languages in total).
  • The platform aims to mitigate economic risks for livestock keepers and promote sustainable livelihood protection across rural India.

  • Which ministry launched the Livestock Insurance Portal? Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying.
  • Who is the current Union Minister for Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying? Rajiv Ranjan Singh alias Lalan Singh.
  • How many regional languages besides Hindi and English are supported on the portal? 11 regional languages.
  • Which department operates the Livestock Insurance Scheme? Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD).

International Relations Maritime Exercise 28 September 2026

INS Sahyadri and INS Kulish Participate in ADMM-Plus Activity at Subic Bay

  • Indian Navy stealth frigate INS Sahyadri and missile corvette INS Kulish participated in the ADMM-Plus EWG-MS Joint Cooperation Activity (JCA).
  • The maritime event was hosted at Subic Bay, Philippines, and concluded on 25 September 2026.
  • The exercise was held under the aegis of the 23rd Experts' Working Group on Maritime Security (EWG-MS).
  • The activity was hosted by the Philippine Navy and co-chaired by Japan.
  • Operations included visit board search and seizure, search and rescue (SAR), hydrographic survey, and deck landings.
  • The deployment underscores India's 'Act East Policy' and commitment to the 'MAHASAGAR' vision for maritime security.

Lalit Kala Akademi Announces 20 Winners of 65th National Exhibition of Art

Economy & Banking Mining & Steel 28 September 2026

NMDC Commissions ₹5,427-Crore Slurry Pipeline and Pellet Plant in Bastar

  • State-owned mining major NMDC Limited commissioned its integrated iron ore infrastructure project in Bastar, Chhattisgarh, built at ₹5,427 crore.
  • The project includes a 2 MTPA (million tonnes per annum) pellet plant at Nagarnar and an iron ore beneficiation plant at Bacheli.
  • A 135-km slurry pipeline connects Bacheli to Nagarnar to transport iron ore concentrate, traversing 61 villages across Bastar and Dantewada districts.
  • The pellet plant utilizes advanced Straight Grate Induration technology to convert iron ore fines and slimes into high-grade pellets.
  • The commissioning aligns with NMDC's long-term corporate target of achieving 100 million tonnes of annual iron ore production.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where was India's first LNG-powered train launched?
India's first LNG-powered train was flagged off from Sabarmati Railway Station in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, by Union Minister Amit Shah. The move forms part of Indian Railways' clean fuel initiatives alongside hydrogen-powered trains.
Which countries are participating in Exercise Tarang Shakti-2026?
Hosted by the Indian Air Force at Air Force Station Jodhpur, Exercise Tarang Shakti-2026 features seven friendly nations: Australia, the USA, the UAE, France, Germany, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh, along with several observer nations.
What is the purpose of the newly launched Livestock Insurance Portal?
Launched by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, the portal streamlines digital policy issuance, claims processing, and real-time tracking for cattle farmers across India. It supports 13 languages (Hindi, English, and 11 regional languages).
What is the route and capacity of NMDC's new slurry pipeline in Chhattisgarh?
The 135-km slurry pipeline connects Bacheli to Nagarnar in Chhattisgarh, traversing 61 villages across Bastar and Dantewada districts. It has a capacity of 15 MTPA to transport iron ore concentrate directly to the 2 MTPA pellet plant.
Where did INS Sahyadri and INS Kulish participate in the ADMM-Plus maritime exercise?
The two Indian warships participated in the 23rd ADMM-Plus Maritime Security Joint Cooperation Activity held at Subic Bay in the Philippines. The exercise was hosted by the Philippine Navy and co-chaired by Japan.
Which medals did Indian athletes win in Squash and Athletics at the Asian Games as announced on 28 September 2026?
Anahat Singh and Abhay Singh each won Silver medals in Women's and Men's Singles Squash respectively. In Athletics, Ancy Sojan bagged Silver in Women's Long Jump and Tejaswin Shankar won Bronze in Men's Decathlon.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section.
