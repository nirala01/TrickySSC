International Relations Summits 2 October 2026

G20 Trade Ministers' Meeting Held in Milwaukee Under US Presidency

  • Union Minister of Commerce and Industry Piyush Goyal attended the G20 Trade Ministers' Meeting held in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, USA.
  • The meeting was convened under the United States G20 Presidency.
  • Trade ministers agreed on a consensus document titled 'G20 Statement on Weaponization of Food through Coercive Trade Measures'.
  • India underscored its food security initiatives, noting that free foodgrains are currently provided to over 800 million citizens.
  • The Indian delegation reaffirmed strong commitment to a rules-based, non-discriminatory multilateral trading system with the World Trade Organization (WTO) at its core.
  • Piyush Goyal also toured the Rockwell Automation facility in Mequon, Wisconsin with US Trade Representative Ambassador Jamieson Greer.

Government Schemes Telecom 2 October 2026

Centre Approves ₹10,520 Crore for Amended BharatNet Program in Maharashtra

  • A multi-party agreement was signed at Sanchar Bhawan, New Delhi, to implement the Amended BharatNet Program (ABP) in Maharashtra under the state-led model.
  • The pact was executed between Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN)-DoT, Government of Maharashtra, MDIL, BSNL, and MahaIT.
  • The Central Government approved financial assistance of ₹10,520 crore for implementing the programme in the state.
  • The project will cover 28,237 Gram Panchayats, upgrading 15,171 Phase-I GPs from linear to ring topology, and covering 12,740 Phase-II and 326 new GPs.
  • Additionally, 15,799 villages across Maharashtra will receive on-demand broadband connectivity.
  • The Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) was renamed as Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN) under the Telecommunications Act, 2023.

Government Schemes Sanitation & Welfare 2 October 2026

Safaimitra Suraksha evam Samman Shivir Reaches Over 3.69 Lakh Workers

  • The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment conducted a nationwide Safaimitra Suraksha evam Samman Shivir in hybrid mode.
  • The initiative engaged more than 3,870 Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), Rural Local Bodies (RLBs), and line departments across India.
  • A total of 3,69,469 beneficiaries took part in the welfare and health campaign.
  • The camps facilitated 3,02,958 health check-ups tailored to address occupational health hazards of sanitation workers.
  • Beneficiaries downloaded 2,24,521 NAMASTE cards and received 57,967 Ayushman cards.
  • A total of 1,28,928 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) kits were distributed to sanitation personnel.

Government Schemes Social Welfare 2 October 2026

Assistive Aids Distributed to Senior Citizens Under Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana

  • Union Minister Dr. Virendra Kumar led the distribution of assistive aids to senior citizens on Swachh Bharat Diwas (2 October) at Dr. Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi.
  • The distribution was organized under the central scheme Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY).
  • A total of 662 assistive devices worth over ₹10 lakh were provided completely free of charge to 114 elderly residents.
  • RVY functions as a sub-component under the umbrella scheme Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY).
  • The scheme was originally launched on 1 April 2017 and is implemented by ALIMCO (Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India).
  • Nationally, over 10 lakh senior citizens have received over 55 lakh assistive devices, with women comprising roughly 45% of beneficiaries.

International Relations Bilateral Trade 2 October 2026

India and Jordan Hold High-Level Bilateral Talks on Textile and Apparel Sector

  • A high-level delegation from Jordan led by Zaher Al Qatarneh, Secretary General of Jordan's Ministry of Investment, met Union Textiles Secretary Neelam Shami Rao in New Delhi.
  • Discussions centered on expanding bilateral trade, joint ventures, sourcing, and investments in textiles and technical apparel.
  • In 2025, Jordan's global imports of textiles and apparel stood at approximately $2 billion.
  • India exported around $48 million worth of textile products to Jordan in 2025, accounting for a 2.4% market share.
  • India is the 4th largest supplier of textiles and apparel to Jordan.
  • Both nations also explored institutional ties in fashion and AI-driven fashion forecasting.

Important Facts for Exams

  • India's rank as a textile and apparel supplier to Jordan: 4th largest supplier.
  • India's share in Jordan's textile imports (2025): 2.4% ($48 million out of ~$2 billion).
  • Head of the Jordanian delegation: Zaher Al Qatarneh (Secretary General, Ministry of Investment).
  • Capital of Jordan: Amman; Currency: Jordanian Dinar (JOD).

Source: PIB release

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

Standard categories SSC asks current affairs from

Over the last several exam cycles, current affairs questions in SSC papers have clustered into a predictable set of buckets. Reading with these buckets in mind makes your notes far easier to revise:

  • Government schemes and Cabinet decisions — full form, ministry, outlay, launch date
  • Appointments — new chairpersons, chiefs, CEOs, and heads of constitutional bodies
  • Awards and honours — instituting body, field, first recipient
  • Summits, conferences and agreements — host city, edition number, theme
  • Science, technology and space — missions, launches, indigenous technology
  • Defence — exercises, inductions, indigenous platforms
  • Economy and banking — reports, indices, rankings, RBI decisions
  • Environment and ecology — disasters, protected areas, climate reports
  • Sports — tournaments, winners, venues, important days
  • Important days and themes — date, theme of the year, observing body

Frequently Asked Questions

Where was the 2026 G20 Trade Ministers' Meeting held?
The G20 Trade Ministers' Meeting took place in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, United States, under the US G20 Presidency. India was represented by Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal.
What is the financial outlay approved for Amended BharatNet in Maharashtra?
The Central Government has approved financial assistance of ₹10,520 crore for Maharashtra. The project will connect 28,237 Gram Panchayats and 15,799 villages via high-speed broadband.
What is the new name of the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF)?
Under the Telecommunications Act, 2023, the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) has been redesignated as Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN), managed under the Department of Telecommunications.
What is Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY)?
Launched on 1 April 2017, Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana is a 100% centrally funded scheme under the umbrella Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY). It provides free physical aids and assistive living devices to senior citizens suffering from age-related disabilities through ALIMCO.
Who won the Bronze medal in the Men's 4x400m relay at the Asian Games?
The Indian men's relay quartet consisting of Dharmveer Chaudhary, Manu TS, Jay Kumar, and Vishal TK won the Bronze medal in the 4x400m event at the Asian Games.
What is Bhashini and how many languages does it support?
Bhashini is a national language AI platform developed by the Digital India Bhashini Division under MeitY. It supports 36 Indian text languages, 23 Indian voice languages, and 35 international languages to enhance multilingual digital access.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
