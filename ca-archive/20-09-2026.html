Government Schemes Energy 20 September 2026

Biometric Aadhaar Authentication Mandatory for Subsidised Domestic LPG Refills from October 1

  • The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas announced that Biometric Aadhaar Authentication (BAA) will be mandatory for subsidised domestic LPG refills from 1 October 2026.
  • As of 19 September 2026, 27.43 crore domestic LPG consumers (89.9%) have already completed the biometric verification.
  • Consumers who do not wish to complete BAA can opt out digitally to receive non-subsidised LPG refills in 5 kg or 10 kg cylinders at market rates.
  • The implied subsidy reached ₹721 per 14.2 kg cylinder in June 2026 and stood at approximately ₹210 per cylinder in September 2026.
  • The central government provided ₹22,000 crore compensation to Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) in FY 2022-23 and is releasing ₹30,000 crore across FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27.
  • Despite government compensation, the under-recovery of public sector OMCs exceeded ₹62,000 crore as of 31 August 2026.
  • Authentication can be done via OMC delivery personnel, LPG distributorships, or self-service apps (IndaneOilOne, HelloBPCL, and HP Pay).

Defence Naval Exercise 20 September 2026

INS Trishul Visits Alexandria Port in Egypt and Participates in PASSEX Exercise

  • Indian Navy guided-missile frigate INS Trishul made an operational port call at Alexandria, Egypt from 14 to 17 September 2026.
  • INS Trishul was received and formally welcomed upon arrival by Egyptian Navy ship Al Qadir.
  • The ship's crew paid homage at the Chatby War Memorial, which houses graves of 12 soldiers and commemorates 19 Indian soldiers lost in the Eastern Mediterranean.
  • India's Ambassador to Egypt, Suresh K. Reddy, visited the frigate and interacted with the naval personnel.
  • INS Trishul conducted a Passage Exercise (PASSEX) with Egyptian Navy Ship ENS Bernice in the Mediterranean Sea.
  • The PASSEX included coordinated tactical manoeuvres, communication drills, and replenishment-at-sea approaches.

Science & Technology Rail Safety 20 September 2026

Indian Railways Sanctions ₹461 Crore for Kavach 4.0 in Lucknow and Interlocking in Solapur

  • Ministry of Railways sanctioned ₹252 crore to install Kavach 4.0 across 607.7 route kilometres of the Lucknow Division under North Eastern Railway (NER).
  • Kavach is an indigenously developed Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system designed to prevent Signal Passing at Danger (SPAD).
  • The system activates emergency braking automatically when drivers fail to respond and prevents train-to-train head-on collisions.
  • Concurrently, Indian Railways approved ₹209 crore for Electronic Interlocking (EI) systems across 7 stations in Central Railway's Solapur Division.
  • The Solapur project covers the High Density Network (HDN) 7 route, including Mohol, Mundhewadi, Bale, Solapur, Hotgi Junction, Shahabad, and Wadi Junction.
  • The Solapur signalling overhaul integrates Kavach, Automatic Block Signalling (ABS), and Centralised Traffic Control (CTC).

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

How to use this daily current affairs page effectively

  1. Read the “Today at a glance” box first. A handful of lines, under two minutes. If you are short on time on a busy day, this alone keeps you current.
  2. Then read each topic once, slowly. Do not try to memorise on the first pass — understanding what happened makes the facts stick far better than rote repetition.
  3. Revise only the Important Facts for Exams boxes. These are deliberately written in question–answer shape, so they double as a revision sheet in the last week before the exam.
  4. Attempt the practice MCQs the same day. Active recall on the day you read something is worth several passive re-readings later.
  5. Do a weekly sweep every Sunday. Open the last seven date pages from the archive and read only the fact boxes — that takes about fifteen minutes and covers a full week.
  6. Link every current affair to static GK. When you read about a summit, revise the organisation's headquarters and member capitals at the same time. SSC frequently mixes the two.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Biometric Aadhaar Authentication made mandatory for domestic LPG refills?
The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas mandated Biometric Aadhaar Authentication from 1 October 2026 to ensure subsidies reach genuine households. It eliminates duplicate connections and prevents commercial diversion of domestic cylinders.
What happens if an LPG consumer does not complete Biometric Aadhaar Authentication?
Consumers who do not finish authentication will not be able to book subsidised refills at regulated prices. However, they can opt to receive unsubsidised LPG cylinders in 5 kg or 10 kg sizes at standard market rates.
Which Egyptian naval ship conducted PASSEX with INS Trishul in September 2026?
INS Trishul conducted a Passage Exercise with Egyptian Navy Ship ENS Bernice following an operational port call in Alexandria from 14 to 17 September 2026.
What AI tools has the Ministry of Ayush developed for traditional medicine?
The Ministry developed chatbots like MAISP for citizen information, ARP Research Assistant for medical literature, e-Charak for medicinal plant trade, e-LMS for academic lifecycle management, and Yoga Saarthi and Nivesh Saarthi for guidance and investments.
What are the key technical features of Kavach 4.0 sanctioned for Lucknow Division?
Kavach 4.0 is an indigenously developed Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system. It prevents Signal Passing at Danger (SPAD), applies automatic brakes if an engineer fails to decelerate, and prevents train collisions.
What is the 100-Week Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan organised by MY Bharat?
It is a youth mobilisation initiative by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports to eradicate substance abuse. It marked its national pledge day, Rashtriya Sankalp Samaroh, across 1,067 locations on 20 September 2026.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
