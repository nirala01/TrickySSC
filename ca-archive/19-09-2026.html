Defence Aviation 19 September 2026

HAL Hands Over LCA Tejas Trainers, HTT-40 and Dhruv NG Helicopters

  • Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) handed over indigenous aircraft and helicopters to the Indian Air Force (IAF) and Pawan Hans Limited (PHL) in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
  • The IAF received 2 LCA Tejas Final Operational Clearance (FOC) two-seater trainer aircraft and 3 HTT-40 basic trainer aircraft.
  • Pawan Hans Limited was handed over 4 Dhruv Next Generation (NG) indigenous multi-role helicopters for civilian operations.
  • The handover ceremony was attended by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Civil Aviation Minister K. Rammohan Naidu.
  • The HTT-40 is an indigenous basic trainer aircraft designed and manufactured by HAL to replace aging basic trainers and meet IAF pilot training requirements.
  • The induction marks growing synergy between defence aerospace and civil aviation sectors to build an integrated aerospace ecosystem.

Government Schemes Youth Affairs 19 September 2026

100-Week Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan Launched Across India

  • The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports launched the 100-week Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan for Viksit Bharat nationwide.
  • The campaign commenced with a countrywide Rashtriya Sankalp Samaroh organized in partnership with MY Bharat - National Service Scheme (NSS).
  • The campaign covered 259 institutions across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories, drawing over 1,47,798 participants across 1,455 activities on day one.
  • The initiative is designed as a continuous 100-week or 100-Sunday campaign focusing on youth engagement, fitness, and substance abuse prevention.
  • The first phase of the campaign focuses on the theme 'Mera Swasthya, Mera Garv' (My Health, My Pride).
  • Subsequent phases will feature structured focus months including 'Fit Yuva', 'Nasha-Mukt Yuva', and 'Jeevan Kaushal Maah' (Life Skills Month).

Government Schemes Social Welfare 19 September 2026

Social Justice Ministry Reviews Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYYAY)

  • The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment reviewed the implementation of the umbrella scheme Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYYAY).
  • A total of Rs 1,089.58 crore has been released/expended under the IPSRC and SAPSRC components.
  • Under the Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSRC), over 10.76 lakh senior citizens benefited with an expenditure of Rs 953.53 crore from FY 2014-15 to FY 2025-26.
  • The State Action Plan for Senior Citizens (SAPSRC), launched in FY 2019-20, has seen releases of Rs 136.05 crore to assist States and UTs.
  • Other key sub-schemes under AVYYAY include Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY) and the Elderline national toll-free helpline (14567).
  • IPSRC provides funding to NGOs and voluntary organisations to run Senior Citizen Homes offering shelter, food, medical care, and entertainment.

Key Components of AVYYAY Umbrella Scheme

  • IPSRC: Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (shelter, food, healthcare through homes).
  • SAPSRC: State Action Plan for Senior Citizens (state-specific needs, cataract surgeries, awareness).
  • RVY: Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (physical aids and assisted-living devices for BPL seniors).
  • Elderline: National Helpline for Senior Citizens providing counselling and grievance redressal.

Important Facts for Exams

  • What does the acronym AVYYAY stand for? Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana
  • Which nodal ministry oversees AVYYAY? Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
  • In which financial year was SAPSRC introduced under AVYYAY? FY 2019-20
  • How many senior citizens benefited under IPSRC from 2014-15 to 2025-26? Over 10.76 lakh
  • What is the national toll-free helpline number for senior citizens (Elderline)? 14567

Defence Joint Warfare 19 September 2026

Joint Operational Review and Evaluation (CORE) Programme 2026 Concludes

  • The 5-day Joint Operational Review and Evaluation (CORE) Programme 2026 concluded at the Manekshaw Centre in New Delhi.
  • The programme was organized by Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff (HQ IDS) and conducted by the Centre for Joint Warfare Studies (CENJOWS).
  • Held from 14 to 18 September 2026, the review brought together senior officials from the Tri-Services, MoD, MEA, and MHA.
  • Deliberations focused on jointness, military integration, modern warfighting, and non-kinetic national security dimensions.
  • Emerging technological domains discussed included Artificial Intelligence, robotics, quantum computing, and cognitive warfare.
  • Senior military leaders including the Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) and Chief of Naval Staff (CNS) actively engaged with participants.

17th Kathakali Festival Inaugurated at Kalakshetra Foundation Chennai

  • The 17th edition of the Kathakali Festival was inaugurated at the Bharata Kalakshetra Auditorium in Thiruvanmiyur, Chennai.

International Relations Human Rights 19 September 2026

5th ITEC Human Rights Capacity-Building Programme Concludes at NHRC

  • The 5th ITEC Executive Capacity-Building Programme on Human Rights for National Human Rights Institutions (NHRIs) of the Global South concluded in New Delhi.
  • The 6-day programme was organized by the National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) in collaboration with the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA).
  • The programme trained 38 senior participants representing NHRIs from 14 countries of the Global South.
  • Participating nations included Algeria, Chile, Ecuador, Ethiopia, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Kenya, Kyrgyzstan, Malaysia, Oman, Samoa, South Africa, Tajikistan, and Zimbabwe.
  • The valedictory address was delivered by NHRC Chairperson Justice V. Ramasubramanian.
  • The ITEC (Indian Technical and Economic Cooperation) Programme was instituted by the Government of India in 1964 as a bilateral assistance programme.

Participating Global South Nations

  • Africa & Middle East: Algeria, Ethiopia, Kenya, South Africa, Zimbabwe, Oman.
  • Asia & Pacific: Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Malaysia, Samoa, Tajikistan.
  • Americas: Chile, Ecuador.

Important Facts for Exams

Environment & Ecology Ocean Cleanliness 19 September 2026

Swachh Sagar Surakshit Sagar 2026 Cleanliness Drive Held at Juhu Beach

  • Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh led a coastal cleanup drive at Juhu Beach, Mumbai, on the eve of International Coastal Cleanup Day.
  • The 2026 edition of the 'Swachh Sagar, Surakshit Sagar' (Clean Coast, Safe Sea) campaign was observed from 12 to 19 September 2026.
  • The initiative was originally launched during the 75th year of India's Independence (2022) as a 75-day inter-ministerial campaign.
  • International Coastal Cleanup Day is traditionally observed every year on the third Saturday of September.
  • The campaign leverages citizen science and volunteer reporting through the Sagar Prahari digital platform.
  • The Minister highlighted India's flagship Deep Ocean Mission and the conservation of India's extensive coastline spanning over 11,000 km.

Why daily current affairs matter in the SSC exam

In SSC CGL Tier I, the General Awareness section carries 25 questions of 2 marks each, that is 50 marks out of 200, and it must be completed in a locked 15-minute sectional window under the 2026 pattern. Unlike Quantitative Aptitude or Reasoning, General Awareness rewards recall rather than calculation, so a well-prepared candidate can finish the whole section in seven or eight minutes and carry the saved time as a psychological cushion into the rest of the paper.

Within that section, current affairs typically account for a meaningful share of questions, and the questions are almost always factual one-liners — who launched a scheme, where a summit was held, which ministry implements a programme, what a scheme's full form is. That is exactly why this page is written as short bullet facts rather than paragraphs of analysis.

  • Current affairs questions are the fastest questions in the paper — no working, no rough sheet.
  • They repeat across SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD and state-level exams in the same cycle.
  • They connect naturally with static GK: a summit question pulls in headquarters and capitals, a scheme question pulls in the ministry.
  • Reading ten minutes a day is far more effective than revising a 300-page compilation a week before the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

What aircraft were handed over by HAL to the IAF and Pawan Hans in September 2026?
HAL handed over 2 LCA Tejas Final Operational Clearance (FOC) two-seater trainers and 3 HTT-40 basic trainer aircraft to the Indian Air Force. Additionally, 4 Dhruv Next Generation (NG) helicopters were delivered to Pawan Hans Limited for civilian operations.
What is the 100-week Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan?
It is a nationwide campaign launched by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports in collaboration with MY Bharat-NSS. Designed as a 100-Sunday initiative across all States and UTs, its initial phase focuses on the theme 'Mera Swasthya, Mera Garv' to steer youth towards healthy, drug-free lifestyles.
What is Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYYAY)?
AVYYAY is an umbrella scheme implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment for the welfare, healthcare, and dignified living of senior citizens. It includes components like the Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSRC), State Action Plans (SAPSRC), Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY), and Elderline.
What is the CORE Programme organized by Headquarters IDS?
The Combined Operational Review and Evaluation (CORE) Programme is a high-level tri-services review organized by HQ Integrated Defence Staff and conducted by CENJOWS. Held at Manekshaw Centre, New Delhi, it focuses on joint operational doctrine, cognitive warfare, and emerging security technologies.
When is International Coastal Cleanup Day celebrated and what is Swachh Sagar Surakshit Sagar?
International Coastal Cleanup Day is observed globally on the third Saturday of September. Swachh Sagar Surakshit Sagar is India's flagship coastal cleanup campaign launched by the Ministry of Earth Sciences in 2022 to protect marine ecosystems and encourage community coastal waste reduction.
Who was honoured at the 38th Pune Festival?
Veteran actor Mohan Joshi was honoured with the 'Founder Suresh Kalmadi Pune Festival Lifetime Achievement Award' by Vice-President C. P. Radhakrishnan at Shree Ganesh Kala Krida Rangamanch in Pune.
Is current affairs enough to score well in SSC General Awareness?
No. Current affairs are important but static GK — History, Geography, Polity, Economics and Science — still carries the larger share of the General Awareness section. The most reliable approach is to read current affairs daily for about ten minutes and revise static GK chapter-wise alongside, using chapter-wise tests to check retention.
