October 2026Monthly notes

Science and economy conclaves

  • ESTIC 2026 – 2nd Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave, 27–29 October, Bharat Mandapam; opened by PM Modi.
  • ESTIC details – theme 'Creative Minds, Catalysing Science, Empowering Technologies'; 12 thematic areas; organised by CSIR with 13 ministries.
  • AgriVoltaics – the 7th AgriVoltaics World Conference, New Delhi, 21–23 October, hosted by MNRE – its first time in India; theme 'Powering the Global South'.
  • Kautilya Conclave – 5th Kautilya Economic Conclave, New Delhi, 3–5 October; theme 'Resilience in an Age of Flux'.
  • India Chem 2026 – 14th edition in Mumbai, 22–24 October.

Biodiversity and whaling meets

  • CBD COP17 – the UN Biodiversity Conference was held at Yerevan, Armenia, 19–30 October.
  • Agenda – progress on the Kunming–Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework – 4 goals and 23 targets for 2030, including '30x30'.
  • India – released four publications on biodiversity, biosafety and access-and-benefit-sharing.
  • Coral warning – IUCN told the meet that 96% of shallow reef ecoregions face a very high risk of collapse by 2075.
  • IWC-70 – the International Whaling Commission met at Hobart, Australia; a South Atlantic whale sanctuary plan again fell short of a three-fourths vote.
CBD: Convention on Biological Diversity, opened for signing at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit and in force since 1993; secretariat in Montreal; India's law under it is the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.

Parliaments and regional blocs

  • BRICS – the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum met in New Delhi on 26–27 October.
  • IPU – 153rd Inter-Parliamentary Union Assembly at Arusha, Tanzania; RS Deputy Chairman Harivansh led India.
  • SCO – 25th Council of Heads of Government at Dushanbe, 19–20 October; Rajnath Singh represented India.
  • BIMSTEC – the first meeting of Home Ministers, in New Delhi, adopted a Delhi Declaration on radicalisation and terror financing.
  • G20 trade – trade ministers met at Milwaukee, USA (30 September–1 October); Piyush Goyal attended.

Meets at home

  • Urban Conclave – 2nd National Urban Conclave with the 19th Urban Mobility India conference, Bhubaneswar, 23–25 October; theme 'Urban Rising'.
  • PRAHAAR – counter-terror conference in New Delhi (28 September); Amit Shah launched an Extradition Portal.
  • Chinmaya Mission – Amrit Mahotsav Global Sammelan at Bharat Mandapam (23–25 October) marked its 75 years.
  • BRICS Theatre Festival – 12–14 October in New Delhi, organised through the National School of Drama.

Coming up

  • India Energy Week 2027 – 5th edition, Kolkata, 28–31 January 2027, the first in eastern India.
  • Kashi Tamil Sangamam 5.0 – 28 November–12 December 2026, with Thirukkural as its theme.
  • Hornbill Festival – 27th edition, 1–10 December 2026, at Kisama, Nagaland.
  • World Hindu Congress – 18–20 December 2026 in Mumbai.
  • ICAR–CGIAR – Science and Partnership Week, 12–17 April 2027, Yashobhoomi, New Delhi.
Hornbill Festival: Nagaland's 'festival of festivals', held every December since 2000 at the Naga Heritage Village, Kisama, near Kohima; named after the hornbill bird.

September 2026Monthly notes

SCO summit at Bishkek

  • Dates – 31 August–1 September 2026, marking 25 years of the SCO; PM Modi attended.
  • English – a Charter protocol adds English as an official and working language beside Russian and Chinese – an old Indian proposal.
  • Next chair – Pakistan for 2026–27; Lahore named SCO tourism and cultural capital; next summit in Islamabad, 2027.
  • New centres – a security centre in Tashkent, an organised-crime centre in Bishkek and an anti-drug centre in Dushanbe.
  • India's offer – India will host the first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in 2026.
SCO: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation, founded on 15 June 2001 in Shanghai; secretariat Beijing; RATS in Tashkent; India and Pakistan joined in 2017; 10 members now.

G20 and parliamentary meets

  • Innovation ministers – met at Chapel Hill, North Carolina (1–2 September) under the US G20 presidency and adopted the 'Carolina Principles for Emerging Technologies'.
  • 6G – India later endorsed the G20 'Call to Action for 6G'; MoS Jitin Prasada represented India.
  • Energy ministers – G20 'Energy Abundance' meet in Houston (14–16 September), with Manohar Lal leading India.
  • Young MPs – 12th IPU Global Conference of Young Parliamentarians at Samarkand, Uzbekistan (4–6 September).
  • CPC – 69th Commonwealth Parliamentary Conference at Cape Town (13–19 September); Harivansh led India; Canada hosts in 2027.

Forums abroad and at home

  • BRICS judges – the BRICS Chief Justices' Forum (4–6 September) was hosted by the Supreme Court and chaired by CJI Surya Kant.
  • Manila – 14th East Asia Summit Economic Ministers' meeting and the 23rd India–ASEAN economic ministers' meeting on 21 September.
  • IAEA – 70th General Conference in Vienna, 14–18 September.
  • WHO – 79th South-East Asia Regional Committee at Dili, Timor-Leste (7–10 September).
  • BIMSTEC – 3rd conference on traditional medicine in Goa (16–17 September), run by the AYUSH Ministry with AIIA Goa.

Water and industry meets in Delhi

  • IIWW 2026 – 9th India International Water Week, 22–26 September at Bharat Mandapam; theme 'Climate Resilient Water Management'; Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan opened it.
  • Partners – Netherlands and Germany; two India–Dutch MoUs back the Kalpasar project in Gujarat.
  • Water summit – National Departmental Summit on Water Security (1–2 September), first of a new series; PM Modi spoke at the close.
  • e-FAST – 5th summit on electric freight, led by NITI Aayog (7 September); launched the PACT platform and a zero-emission truck marketplace.
  • INNOPROM – Russia's industrial fair held in India for the first time (9–11 September); co-chaired by Piyush Goyal and Anton Alikhanov.

More conclaves in India

  • Global Fintech Fest – 7th edition in Mumbai (8–11 September), opened by PM Modi.
  • APNIC 62 – the Asia-Pacific internet registry's meet came back to India after 14 years, in Mumbai, hosted by NIXI and ISPAI.
  • Drylands – Global Dryland Congress (10–12 September) by ICAR and ICRISAT adopted the 'Delhi Declaration on Drylands'.
  • WCEF 2026 – World Circular Economy Forum in Gandhinagar (15–18 September) – its first edition in South Asia, with Finland as co-host.
  • India–Germany – 4th Environment Forum (1 September) agreed a joint declaration of intent on wetlands and Ramsar sites.

Festivals and cultural meets

  • Kathakali Mahotsav – 17th edition at Kalakshetra, Chennai (18–20 September).
  • Kalakshetra – founded in 1936 by Rukmini Devi Arundale.
  • Pune Festival – 38th edition, opened on 18 September by the Vice-President, C.P. Radhakrishnan.
  • Tea research – UPASI Tea Research Foundation's centenary meet at Coonoor, Tamil Nadu – tea research there began in 1926.
  • Natural farming – national conference at Umiam, Meghalaya (9–11 September), run by Central Agricultural University, Imphal.
Kathakali: classical dance-drama of Kerala, known for green-faced characters and elaborate make-up; Kerala Kalamandalam (1930, Vallathol) is its best-known school.

August 2026Monthly notes

BRICS ministers meet in India

  • Education – the 13th BRICS Education Ministers' meeting was held at Bhubaneswar on 5–7 August.
  • Culture – the 11th Culture Ministers' meeting at Bhopal (8 August), chaired by Gajendra Singh Shekhawat; India proposed a voluntary registry of artists.
  • Environment – the 12th Environment Ministers' meeting at Bharat Mandapam (18 August), led by Bhupender Yadav, released four knowledge compendiums.
  • Youth – the BRICS Youth Summit at Gandhinagar (19–20 August) adopted the Gandhinagar BRICS Youth Declaration 2026 and backed a BRICS Youth Volunteer Network.
  • Sports – the Youth and Sports Ministers met at Visakhapatnam (22–23 August) and adopted the BRICS Sports Joint Declaration.

More BRICS tracks

  • Communications – the 12th Communications Ministers' meeting in Pune (21 August), chaired by Jyotiraditya Scindia; India and Brazil signed a telecom MoU.
  • Science – the 14th BRICS Science, Technology and Innovation ministers' meeting in Chennai (21 August), under Dr Jitendra Singh, adopted the Chennai Declaration.
  • Asset recovery – the second meeting of the BRICS network on asset recovery took place in Hyderabad, hosted by DoPT.
  • Films – at the BRICS Waves Bazaar in Mumbai, run by NFDC, the BRICS Independent Producers Alliance was launched.
  • Summit ahead – India hosts the 18th BRICS Summit as chair in 2026.
BRICS: began as BRIC in 2006, with its first summit at Yekaterinburg in 2009; South Africa joined in 2010; Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran and the UAE joined in 2024 and Indonesia in 2025; India holds the chair in 2026.

Global summits

  • UNCCD COP17 – held in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, from 17 to 28 August under the theme 'Restoring Land, Restoring Hope'.
  • Year of – the UN marks 2026 as the International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists, a Mongolian idea.
  • India's pledge – Bhupender Yadav said India restored about 2.18 crore hectares of degraded land in 2011–2020 and aims at 2.6 crore hectares by 2030.
  • SCO summit – the 26th summit, marking 25 years of the group, met at Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, on 31 August–1 September.
  • India–Russia – the 27th session of their Inter-Governmental Commission met in Moscow on 24 August, co-chaired by S. Jaishankar and First Deputy PM Denis Manturov.
UNCCD: UN Convention to Combat Desertification, adopted in Paris in 1994 and in force since 1996, with its secretariat in Bonn; India hosted COP14 at Greater Noida in 2019.

Bilateral meetings

  • Japan – Piyush Goyal held roundtables in Tokyo on chips and AI (25 August) and on start-ups (26 August), proposing a Shark Tank-style pitch format.
  • Kuwait – the first India–Kuwait Joint Defence Committee met in New Delhi on 28 August.
  • New Zealand – the second India–NZ Defence Strategic Dialogue was held in Wellington.
  • Morocco – at the 7th Joint Commission, the two sides agreed to put Moroccan languages Darija and Amazigh on the Idarati public service portal.
  • Lesotho – at the 6th joint commission, India asked Lesotho to become a member of the International Solar Alliance and CDRI.

Conclaves and expos in India

  • Medical devices – the 9th India Medical Devices conference at Vigyan Bhawan (7–8 August) was run by the Department of Pharmaceuticals with FICCI.
  • FinTech – ASSOCHAM held the 3rd India International FinTech Festival in New Delhi.
  • Handloom – the GI & Beyond 2.0 summit launched a Digital Handloom Atlas; IIHT Sutra 2026 was the first national meet of handloom technology institutes.
  • Tribal languages – at a national conference on 24 August, the Ministry of Tribal Affairs signed an MoU with CIIL and launched an AI bot named 'Jaago'.
  • Farm universities – the 36th conference of agricultural university VCs, run by DARE and ICAR, discussed farm education for Viksit Bharat@2047.

Councils and festivals

  • Zonal council – Amit Shah chaired the 31st Southern Zonal Council meeting at Mahabalipuram, Tamil Nadu.
  • Senior citizens – the National Council of Senior Citizens met for the 5th time on 14 August and released the SNEHA e-book.
  • Kargil – the 2nd Suru Summer Festival was held at Damsna, Kargil, on 26–27 August to boost tourism.
  • Ladakh – the first Black-necked Crane Festival was held to spread awareness about the bird.
Zonal Councils: set up under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956; there are five (Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern), each chaired by the Union Home Minister.

July 2026Monthly notes

BRICS meetings in India

  • Women ministers – the BRICS Women's Ministerial Meeting in Kochi (8–9 July), chaired by Annpurna Devi, adopted the track's first joint statement by consensus.
  • Health ministers – the 16th BRICS Health Ministers' Meeting in Chandigarh (22–24 July) adopted the Chandigarh Declaration on universal health coverage.
  • Mental health network – a new BRICS network of mental-health centres of excellence will be coordinated by NIMHANS, Bengaluru.
  • Labour – the 12th BRICS Labour and Employment Ministers' Meeting in Hyderabad, chaired by Mansukh Mandaviya, launched 'BRICS CONNECT' on India's proposal.
  • More meetings – Guwahati hosted BRICS anti-narcotics chiefs, Nagpur the transport ministers and New Delhi the disaster-risk-reduction ministers.
BRICS: India chairs the bloc in 2026; it began as BRIC (first summit 2009, Yekaterinburg), South Africa joined in 2010, and Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, the UAE and Indonesia have since joined.

UN and global forums

  • Codex – the 49th Codex Alimentarius Commission in Geneva (6–10 July) adopted seven texts made under India's chair or co-chair, including those on curry leaves (fresh) and coriander seeds (dried), chaired by India, plus vanilla and large cardamom.
  • Cashew – it also backed India's plan to draft a worldwide Codex standard on cashew kernels.
  • Consumer law – India, through Consumer Affairs Secretary Nidhi Khare, chaired UNCTAD's 9th expert group session on consumer protection in Geneva.
  • AI governance – the UN's Global Dialogue on AI Governance held its first meeting in Geneva on 6–7 July; MoS Kirti Vardhan Singh represented India.
  • UNCITRAL@60 – the UN trade-law commission's 60th anniversary event was held in New Delhi (24–26 July), hosted by the Supreme Court and the MEA.
Codex Alimentarius Commission: joint FAO–WHO body formed in 1963 that sets international food standards; its secretariat is in Rome; FSSAI is India's Codex contact point.

Security and trade dialogues

  • BIMSTEC security – NSA Ajit Doval chaired the fifth BIMSTEC National Security Chiefs' meeting in New Delhi on 16 July.
  • India–Japan defence – the 8th Defence Policy Dialogue was held in Tokyo on 13 July, with Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh leading India's side.
  • ASEAN trade – the 13th AITIGA Joint Committee met in New Delhi (6–10 July) to review the 2009 India–ASEAN goods pact.
  • Costa Rica – India and Costa Rica held their first Joint Economic and Trade Committee (JETCO) meeting on 6–7 July.
  • Pax Silica – at the second Pax Silica Summit in Washington (25–26 June), India signed a joint statement on trusted AI supply chains.

National conclaves and fairs

  • Bharat Tex 2026 – the third edition of this global textile show was held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 14–17 July, backed by the Textiles Ministry.
  • 5F idea – the show promoted the PM's Farm to Fibre to Factory to Fashion to Foreign vision; rural SHGs launched a 'Saras Shakti' gifting range.
  • Plastics – the 3rd Global Conclave on Plastic Recycling and Sustainability was held at Bharat Mandapam on 2–5 July.
  • E-governance – DARPG held a national conference on next-generation administrative reforms in Shillong on 13–14 July.
  • Elections and media – the Election Commission held its first all-India media conference in New Delhi.

Days, festivals and seminars

  • Namaste Day – the 3rd Namaste Day (14 July), marking three years of the NAMASTE scheme for sanitation workers, had its main event in Kolkata.
  • Saushrutam – the All India Institute of Ayurveda, New Delhi, held this international seminar on Sushruta Jayanti (15 July) to honour the 'father of surgery'.
  • Wildlife meetings – Bhupender Yadav chaired NBWL, NTCA and CAMPA meetings in Coimbatore; the pygmy hog joined the species recovery list and the MISHTI mangrove scheme was extended to 2029.
  • Rath Yatra – Lord Jagannath's chariot festival in Puri begins on Ashadha Shukla Dwitiya; the chariots are Nandighosha, Taladhwaja and Darpadalana.
  • Dree – the farm festival of the Apatani tribe of Ziro Valley, Arunachal Pradesh, was celebrated in July.
NAMASTE: National Action for Mechanised Sanitation Ecosystem, a scheme of the Ministry of Social Justice with the Housing and Urban Affairs Ministry, launched in 2023 to end hazardous manual cleaning of sewers and septic tanks.

June 2026Monthly notes

G7 summit and France

  • 52nd G7 Summit – held at Évian-les-Bains, France, on 15–17 June; India joined as an invited partner.
  • IMPACT – Modi proposed the International Mobilisation Partnership for Accelerating Connectivity and Trade, combining G7 capital, Indian talent and Global South ownership, modelled on IMEC.
  • Other ideas – a Global Skills Partnership and support for vulnerable economies hit by fuel, food and fertiliser shocks.
  • VivaTech – Modi and Macron visited Europe's largest tech and start-up show in Paris on 18 June; over 80 Indian deep-tech firms took part.
  • Bharat Innovates – the Education Ministry's showcase in Nice, opened on 14 June, was held under the India–France Year of Innovation.
G7: informal group of the USA, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan and Canada, with the EU taking part; France held the presidency in 2026.

BRICS meetings under India's chair

  • Agriculture ministers – the 16th meeting in Indore on 12–13 June unanimously adopted the Indore Declaration, a farmer-centred roadmap.
  • New steps – a BRICS digital agriculture network, a forum on farmers' seed rights and a platform on agroecology were agreed.
  • Energy ministers – India's energy track carried the motto 'Sarvesham Urjam' (Energy for All).
  • Urbanisation – the 13th BRICS Urbanisation Forum had the theme 'Cities for People'.
  • Science academies – INSA hosted the first BRICS Science Academies Forum meeting of 2026, on AI for sustainable development.

World meetings

  • ILO – at Geneva, the 114th International Labour Conference adopted Convention No. 193, covering fair work for platform (gig) workers, the first binding treaty for gig workers.
  • Our Ocean Conference – the 11th edition met in Kenya (Mombasa and Kilifi); 16 nations, not including India, signed the Mombasa Declaration against illegal fishing.
  • SCO Women's Forum – held in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, on 26 June; Annapurna Devi led India.
  • Pharmacopoeias – the 16th International Meeting of World Pharmacopoeias, coordinated by WHO, took place in Brasília.
  • SAPLING Dialogue – a South Asian food-processing policy meet held in Ahmedabad by MoFPI and the World Bank.

National conclaves and fairs

  • Rashtriya Gramin Vikas Sammelan – the first edition, 28–29 June at Pusa, New Delhi, had the theme 'Gramodaya Se Rashtrodaya'.
  • MSME Day – 'Udyami Bharat' took place in New Delhi on 27 June, chaired by Vice President C.P. Radhakrishnan.
  • Global Wind Day – at the MNRE conference in Goa on 15 June, Pralhad Joshi launched the WT-MARUT portal for wind-turbine supply chains.
  • CPA Zone II – the Commonwealth Parliamentary Association north-zone conference met in Chandigarh on 8–9 June.
  • Youth Parliament – Om Birla opened the national round of the Viksit Bharat Youth Parliament in New Delhi on 16 June.

Festivals and cultural events

  • Raja Parba – Odisha's festival honouring womanhood and the monsoon's arrival; days are Pahili Raja, Mithuna Sankranti and Bhudaha (Basi Raja), followed by Basumati Snan.
  • MIFF – the 19th Mumbai International Film Festival for documentary, short and animation films was run by NFDC.
  • Yoga Day venue – the main 2026 national event was held at Red Road, Kolkata.
  • Global Skills Challenge – an Indian team took part in WorldSkills Australia's event (23–29 June), a lead-up to the 48th WorldSkills in Shanghai.

May 2026Monthly notes

World meetings hosted by India

  • Kimberley Process – India, KP Chair for 2026, hosted the intersessional meeting in Mumbai on 11–14 May, led by Suchindra Misra.
  • About KP – started in 2003 to keep 'conflict diamonds' out of world trade.
  • APO – India hosted the 68th Governing Body meeting of the Asian Productivity Organization at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 20–22 May.
  • Space standards – India hosted the ISO subcommittee on space systems and operations (ISO/TC 20/SC 14) for the first time; ISRO and IN-SPACe took part.
Asian Productivity Organization: an inter-government body set up in 1961 to raise productivity in Asia-Pacific; headquarters Tokyo; India's nodal body is the National Productivity Council.

BRICS under India's chair

  • Chair – India holds the BRICS chairship in 2026.
  • Youth – the BRICS Youth Entrepreneurship Working Group meeting ended in Indore, Madhya Pradesh.
  • Justice – the Department of Legal Affairs hosted BRICS Justice Ministers, with stress on mediation (Mediation Act, 2023).
  • Tourism – the first BRICS Tourism Working Group meeting was held online with India in the chair.

UN and trade meetings

  • WHA – the 79th World Health Assembly met in Geneva; Health Minister J.P. Nadda led India and presented the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission.
  • ITU – at the ITU Council 2026 in Geneva (28 April–8 May), India won the right to host the Plenipotentiary Conference in 2030.
  • About ITU – founded in 1865, based in Geneva; its Plenipotentiary meets every four years.
  • India–Canada – the second round of CEPA talks was held at Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi, on 4–8 May; the next is in Ottawa in July.

Culture and wildlife events

  • Buddha relics – Amit Shah opened the Sacred Exposition of the Holy Relics of the Tathagata in Leh on Buddha Purnima.
  • Turnout – the 14-day show in Leh and Zanskar drew over 1.18 lakh devotees and closed with the Monlam Chenmo prayer at Choglamsar.
  • IBCA Summit – Bhupender Yadav released the website and logo of the first International Big Cat Alliance Summit, set for 1 and 2 June 2026 in New Delhi.
  • Delhi Declaration – to be adopted there; IBCA (2023) covers seven big cats and has its headquarters in India.
  • Yoga – a Yoga Mahotsav began the 50-day countdown to Yoga Day (21 June); the 2026 theme is 'Yoga for Healthy Ageing'.

April 2026Monthly notes

Meetings held in India

  • India Pharma – the 9th India Pharma conference (13–14 April) was run by the Department of Pharmaceuticals with FICCI and the Indian Pharmaceutical Alliance.
  • CPA Zone VII – the first meet of the Commonwealth Parliamentary Association India Region Zone VII (Goa, Gujarat, Maharashtra) was held at Panaji, 9–10 April.
  • Co-operatives – the Ministry of Cooperation held the 'Sahkar se Samriddhi' national review conference at Varanasi on 9–10 April.
  • Data summit – MoSPI's national summit on harmonising administrative data met at Bhubaneswar, 29–30 April.
  • Water Week – the 9th India International Water Week (22–26 September 2026, Bharat Mandapam) will have the theme 'Climate Resilient Water Management'.

Fairs, festivals and jubilees

  • Krishi Mahotsav – the Unnat Krishi Mahotsav was held at Raisen, Madhya Pradesh, on 11–13 April; a soil-testing mobile app was launched.
  • Van Dhan – TRIFED's Van Dhan Conclave (29 March) was part of the Bharat Tribes Fest, with the theme 'Sustainable Livelihoods for Tribal India'.
  • Phule 200 – a two-year national celebration marking 200 years since Jyotiba Phule's birth opened on 11 April 2026, running till 11 April 2028.
  • Youth parliament – the Nari Shakti Youth Parliament was held at 17 places on 12 April through the MY Bharat platform.
  • Tripura – the state marked its traditional Garia Puja.
Jyotiba Phule (1827–1890): social reformer from Maharashtra; with Savitribai Phule he opened a girls' school in Pune in 1848 and founded the Satyashodhak Samaj in 1873.

India's BRICS chairship

  • Chair – India holds the BRICS chair for 2026 and hosted a string of meetings in April.
  • Health group – the first BRICS Health Working Group meeting (15 April, virtual) added two priorities: healthy lifestyles and mental health.
  • Academics – the first BRICS Academic Research Forum met in New Delhi on 17–18 April, organised by the Observer Research Foundation with RIS.
  • BRICS-MENA – India convened the first BRICS–MENA meeting of deputy foreign ministers and envoys in New Delhi on 24 April on the West Asia conflict.
  • Members – BRICS now has Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, the UAE and Indonesia.

Global meetings

  • WTO MC14 – the 14th Ministerial Conference met at Yaoundé, Cameroon (26–30 March), only the second in Africa; Piyush Goyal led India.
  • E-commerce – the moratorium on customs duties on electronic transmissions, in place since 1998, lapsed for the first time; fisheries subsidy talks go on to MC15.
  • CMS COP15 – the Convention on Migratory Species met in Brazil's Campo Grande from 23 to 29 March, theme 'Connecting Nature to Sustain Life'.
  • SCO – Defence Ministers met at Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, on 28 April; Rajnath Singh gifted two Bhishm health cubes.
  • Indian Ocean – the 9th Indian Ocean Conference was held in Mauritius (10–12 April) by the India Foundation.
Bonn Convention: the Convention on the Conservation of Migratory Species of Wild Animals (CMS), signed in 1979; secretariat in Bonn, Germany; India is a party.

Health and other forums

  • One Health – a One Health Summit was held at Lyon, France, on 5–7 April under France's G7 presidency, with WHO.
  • WHO centres – the first Global Forum of WHO Collaborating Centres also met in Lyon (7–9 April); AIIMS New Delhi took part.
  • Fisheries congress – the 5th World Small-Scale Fisheries Congress was held at Hua Hin, Thailand (27–30 April), by FAO and TBTI Global.
  • India–Africa – EAM S. Jaishankar unveiled the logo and theme of the 4th India–Africa Forum Summit, due in New Delhi on 28–31 May 2026.
  • ASEAN – the 28th ASEAN–India Senior Officials' Meeting was held in Manila; 2026 is the ASEAN–India Year of Maritime Cooperation.

March 2026Monthly notes

11th Raisina Dialogue

  • Dates – 5–7 March 2026, New Delhi; opened by PM Modi.
  • Chief guest – Finland's President Alexander Stubb gave the opening keynote.
  • Hosts – MEA with the Observer Research Foundation; begun in 2016, it is India's top meet on geopolitics and geo-economics.
  • Name – taken from Raisina Hill, seat of the Union government in New Delhi.
  • Science diplomacy – a new Raisina Science Diplomacy track was led by the Principal Scientific Adviser, Prof. Ajay Kumar Sood.

Power and water meets

  • Electricity summit – the first Bharat Electricity Summit, 19–22 March 2026, New Delhi, was chaired by Power Minister Manohar Lal; an India–Africa meet ran alongside.
  • Next edition – Gandhinagar, 2028.
  • Water conclave – the Jal Shakti Ministry's World Water Day meet (23 March) released the 7th Minor Irrigation Census, the 2nd Census of Water Bodies and a National Water Data Policy.
  • Jal Mahotsav – C.R. Patil began it on 8 March at Rehej village, Navsari (Gujarat); it ran to 22 March under the line 'Gaon ka Utsav, Desh ka Mahotsav'.
  • Women's Day – 8 March was marked as 'Sujalam Shakti Diwas' in the festival.

Health, science and governance meets

  • Malaria – ICMR-NIMR hosted the International Malaria Conference, 7–9 March 2026.
  • Pharma – the 9th PharmaMed (27 March) was run by the Department of Pharmaceuticals with PHDCCI.
  • Districts – DARPG and the J&K government held a national governance conference in Jammu on 3 March.
  • Fisheries – Srinagar hosted the first national meet on cold-water fisheries.
  • Yoga Mahotsav – the AYUSH Ministry's event in New Delhi began the 100-day countdown to the 12th International Day of Yoga.

Fairs and festivals

  • AAHAR – the 40th international food and hospitality fair ran 10–14 March in New Delhi, organised by ITPO.
  • Orchids – the Indian Orchid Festival was held at Pakyong, Sikkim, 7–10 March, by ICAR's National Research Centre for Orchids.
  • Tribes Fest – the Tribal Affairs Ministry and TRIFED held Bharat Tribes Fest 2026 in New Delhi, with Canada taking part.
  • Handloom – 'Virasat Shakti', a handloom expo by NHDC, ran 7–14 March in New Delhi.
  • Jan Aushadhi – Janaushadhi Saptah ran 1–7 March, with free health camps by PMBI.

Global gatherings

  • ITB Berlin – at the big travel fair (3–5 March) the Tourism Ministry tied up with Netflix to promote Indian places.

India hosts the AI Impact Summit

  • First – it was the first summit of this series held in the Global South.
  • Guiding ideas – three Sutras – People, Planet, Progress – and seven working tracks called Chakras.
  • Outcome – the New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact, backed by about 88 countries and bodies, including the US and China.

AI summit series: began at Bletchley Park, UK (2023), then Seoul (2024) and Paris (2025); India announced its turn at Paris.
