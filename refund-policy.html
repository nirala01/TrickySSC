Refunds will not be granted under the following circumstances:

  6. Course, Test, or Content Expectations. Refunds will not be issued because the product does not meet personal expectations. Users are encouraged to review product descriptions and available free resources before purchasing.

  8. Usage of Product. Once a user has accessed, attempted, downloaded, viewed, or utilized a substantial portion of the purchased content, the product becomes non-refundable.

  10. Personal or Financial Reasons. Refund requests based on personal circumstances, financial difficulties, scheduling conflicts, or similar reasons are not eligible for refunds.
