Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TrickySSC ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use trickyssc.com (the "Service").

How we use information

We use the information to run the Service, save your test progress, show rankings and analysis, improve our content, and communicate important updates.

We use trusted third-party services to operate the platform, which may process limited data on our behalf, including Google Firebase (authentication and database), Google Analytics (anonymous usage statistics), and Cloudflare (security and content delivery). These providers have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children's privacy

The Service is intended for exam aspirants. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age set by applicable law without appropriate consent.

Your choices

You may request access to or deletion of your account data by contacting us at contact@trickyssc.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
