Delete Your TrickySSC Account

For the TrickySSC website and the TrickySSC Android app (com.trickyssc.app)

How to ask for deletion

  1. Send us a request from the email address or mobile number you use to sign in, so we can match it to your account.
  2. We confirm it with you, then delete the account.
Email us WhatsApp us

We acknowledge requests within 48 hours and complete deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address and mobile number.
  • Your test history: answers, scores, ranks and dashboard data.
  • Your saved exam choice and in-app preferences.

What we may have to keep

  • Payment and order records, which we are required to keep for tax and accounting purposes. They are kept only for as long as the law requires.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.

Passes you have bought

Deleting your account ends access to any pass linked to it. Passes are not refundable because of deletion. See the Refund Policy.

Only want to stop using the app?

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Your data stays until you ask for deletion as above.
