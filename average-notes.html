Average

Average = total ÷ number of items. Almost every average question is solved by working with totals, not averages — find the total, adjust it, divide again.

1. Concepts in brief

What an average tells you

The average is the single value that, if every item had it, would give the same total. Marks of 40, 50 and 60 total 150; three students each scoring 50 would also total 150, so the average is 50.

Always work with totals

Total = average × count. When someone joins, leaves or is replaced, the total changes by a known amount, while the count changes by one or stays the same. Find the old total, adjust it, and divide by the new count.

Weighted average

When groups of different sizes are combined, the bigger group pulls the average towards itself. So the combined average of 30 students averaging 60 and 20 students averaging 70 is closer to 60.

3. Shortcuts that save time

Deviation method Pick a convenient guess, add up each item's difference from it, divide by n, and add to the guess. For 48, 52, 50, 54, 46 with guess 50: deviations −2, +2, 0, +4, −4 sum to 0, so the average is 50.
  • Average of a group of equal-sized groups = average of their averages.
  • If the average of n items rises by x when one is replaced, the new item is n·x more than the one removed.
  • If one item is wrong, correct the total, then divide again.

4. Solved examples

Example 1 Average of 5 numbers is 20. One more number is added and the average becomes 22. New number = 22 × 6 − 20 × 5 = 132 − 100 = 32.
Example 2 A man goes at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Average speed = 2 × 40 × 60/100 = 48 km/h — not 50.
Example 3 Average age of 30 students is 14; including the teacher it becomes 15. Teacher's age = 15 × 31 − 14 × 30 = 465 − 420 = 45.
Example 4 Average of the first 15 odd numbers = 15.

More solved examples (exam level)

Example 5 The average of 11 results is 50. The first six average 49 and the last six average 52. The 6th result is counted twice: 6 × 49 + 6 × 52 − 11 × 50 = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56.
Example 6 A batsman scores 87 in his 17th innings and raises his average by 3. Old average x: 16x + 87 = 17(x + 3) → x = 36. New average = 39.
Example 7 30 students average 60 and 20 students average 70. Combined average = (1800 + 1400)/50 = 64.
Example 8 The average of 10 numbers is 15, but 26 was read as 62. Correct average = 15 − 36/10 = 11.4.

5. Practice questions (with answers)

Tap a question to see its answer.

Q1. Average of the first 20 natural numbers?

Answer: 10.5 ((20 + 1)/2).

Q2. The average of 7 consecutive numbers is 20. The largest?

Answer: 23 (the middle one is 20).

Q3. A new man replaces one weighing 65 kg and the average of 8 rises by 2.5 kg. His weight?

Answer: 85 kg (65 + 8 × 2.5).

Q4. Average speed for equal distances at 60 and 40 km/h?

Answer: 48 km/h.

Q5. The average of 5 numbers is 18. Removing one makes it 16. The removed number?

Answer: 26 (90 − 64).

6. Traps to avoid

  1. Average speed for equal distances is the harmonic form, not the simple mean.
  2. When a member leaves, divide the new total by n − 1, not n.
  3. Do not average percentages unless the bases are equal — use a weighted average.
  4. Check whether the question gives the old average or the new one before substituting.
