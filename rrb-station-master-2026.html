RRB Station Master 2026 (CEN 08/2026) — Exam Date, Vacancy, Eligibility & Syllabus

Everything in the official notification, in one place — last checked 18 November 2026

The Railway Recruitment Boards have notified the Station Master post on its own for the first time, under CEN 08/2026. This page puts the dates, vacancies, eligibility, fee, selection stages, paper pattern and syllabus from the notification in one place, with links to practise.

Eligibility

  • Qualification: a graduate degree from a recognised university or its equivalent, held on or before 25 November 2026. Those waiting for the final result are not eligible.
  • Age: 20 to 33 years as on 1 January 2027. Date of birth not later than 1 January 2007, and not earlier than 2 January 1994 for UR and EWS, 2 January 1991 for OBC (non-creamy layer) and 2 January 1989 for SC and ST candidates. Other relaxations are as per the notice.
  • Medical standard A-2: physically fit in all respects; distant vision 6/9, 6/9 and near vision Sn 0.6, 0.6, both without glasses; must pass the tests for colour vision, binocular vision, night vision and myopic vision.
  • Lasik: candidates who have had Lasik or any other surgery to correct refractive error are not eligible for this post.
  • PwBD: the post is not identified as suitable for any PwBD category in the notice.

Selection Process

  1. Computer Based Test (CBT) — a single stage of 100 questions; marks are normalised across shifts. Minimum qualifying marks: UR 40%, EWS 40%, OBC (non-creamy layer) 30%, SC 30%, ST 25%.
  2. Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) — candidates equal to 8 times the vacancies of each RRB are called, community-wise, on CBT merit. A T-score of at least 42 is needed in every test battery, with no relaxation for any category. There is no negative marking, and the test is in English and Hindi only. A vision certificate in the prescribed format must be produced.
  3. Document verification — candidates equal to the number of vacancies are called, on combined merit.
  4. Medical examination to the A-2 standard.

The combined merit gives 70% weight to CBT marks and 30% to CBAT marks, among those who qualify in the CBAT.

CBT Syllabus

Basic Science & Mathematics (30 marks)

Number system, ratio and proportion, averages, percentages, profit, loss and discount, time, speed and distance, power and work, algebra, linear equations, arithmetic progression, LCM and HCF, geometry and trigonometry, area and volumes, probability and statistics (both at basic level). Units and measurements, mass, weight and density, work, power and energy, kinetic energy, friction, speed, velocity and relative velocity, heat and temperature, basic electricity, levers and simple machines.

Data Analysis & Interpretation (30 marks)

Multi-source data analysis: examining, analysing and drawing inferences from text, tables and data shown as charts, graphs, scatter plots, pie charts and statistical curves and distributions; Venn diagrams, data sufficiency and data arrangement.

Logical Capability (20 marks)

Logical reasoning: binary logic, syllogism, clocks and calendars, assumptions, blood relations, family tree and logic-based puzzles. Reading comprehension: passages and questions in English on history, society, literature, science, environment, abstract topics, mythology, technology and culture, testing the main idea, supporting idea, application, logical structure and style.

Mental Reasoning (20 marks)

Analogy, series completion, coding-decoding, and ranking and arrangement problems.

The notification calls these topics illustrative, not exhaustive.

Previous Year Papers and Mock Tests

No Station Master paper exists on this pattern, because the post has its own paper for the first time; earlier it was filled through RRB NTPC. The RRB Section Controller CBT, held in February and October 2026, is on the same 100-question, 120-minute pattern, so its papers are given as the Station Master previous year papers. The Section Controller paper had no Basic Science; the RRB Station Master mock test series (60 mocks, Mock 1–3 free) covers it and follows the notification’s pattern.

How to Apply

  1. Go to rrbapply.gov.in and create an account, or log in if you already have one. Account details cannot be changed later.
  2. Choose CEN 08/2026 and one RRB. Fill in your personal, educational and community details.
  3. Upload the photograph and signature in the size the notice asks for.
  4. Pay the fee online and keep a copy of the submitted application.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for RRB Station Master 2026?

Online applications for CEN 08/2026 close at 23:59 hrs on 25 November 2026. The fee for a submitted application can be paid up to 27 November 2026, and the correction window runs from 28 November to 7 December 2026.

How many vacancies are there in RRB Station Master 2026?

The vacancy table on the first pages of CEN 08/2026 shows 1,215 posts, while the RRB-wise table in Annexure B adds up to 1,265. Vacancies can be revised by the Railways, so check your RRB's notice.

When is the RRB Station Master CBT?

The CBT is expected in January 2027. The exact dates will be given only in the official RRB notice; the city and date intimation and the e-call letter are downloaded from the RRB website.

What is the age limit for RRB Station Master?

20 to 33 years as on 1 January 2027. UR and EWS candidates should be born between 2 January 1994 and 1 January 2007; the upper limit is relaxed by 3 years for OBC (non-creamy layer) and 5 years for SC and ST candidates.

What is the qualification for RRB Station Master?

A graduate degree from a recognised university or its equivalent, held on or before 25 November 2026. Candidates waiting for their final result are not eligible.

Can a candidate who has had Lasik surgery apply?

No. The notification says candidates who have undergone Lasik or any other surgery to correct refractive error are not eligible for Station Master. The post needs the A-2 medical standard, with vision tested without glasses.

What is the RRB Station Master CBT pattern?

100 questions for 100 marks in 120 minutes: Basic Science, Mathematical and Analytical Capability 60, Logical Capability 20 and Mental Reasoning 20, with one-third of a mark deducted for every wrong answer. There is no General Awareness section.

What is the salary of an RRB Station Master?

Station Master is a Level 6 post of the 7th Pay Commission pay matrix with an initial pay of ₹35,400, plus the allowances admissible to railway staff.

Are previous year papers available for RRB Station Master?

Not on the new pattern, because Station Master has its own paper for the first time. The RRB Section Controller CBT papers of February and October 2026, held on the same pattern, are given as previous year papers on TrickySSC, along with 60 mock tests.
