SSC CGL 2026 Notification

The SSC CGL 2026 notification was released on 21 May 2026 for 12,256 tentative Group B and Group C vacancies. More than 28 lakh applications were received this cycle. The notification PDF contains complete details of:

  • Eligibility criteria and educational qualification
  • Age limit and category-wise age relaxation
  • Reservation rules
  • Post-wise vacancy details
  • Exam pattern and syllabus
  • Selection process
  • Application fee
  • Important instructions for candidates

Read the notification carefully before Tier 1 — several eligibility and post-preference details are decided at this stage and carried forward to document verification.

SSC CGL 2026 Tier 1 Exam Date

The SSC CGL Tier 1 examination will be conducted in August–September 2026. It is held as a Computer Based Test in multiple shifts over several days at examination centres across India.

Because each shift gets a different question paper, SSC applies a normalisation formula so that candidates are not penalised for sitting a comparatively tougher shift. Your normalised score, not your raw score, decides the Tier 1 cutoff.

Key change in 2026:
  • Sectional timing of 15 minutes per section has been introduced in Tier 1 — you cannot carry unused time from one section into another.
  • Tier 1 remains qualifying in nature; final merit is decided by Tier 2.

The exact date allotted to you is printed on your admit card. Until the date-wise schedule is released, treat the last week of August as your target — that is the safest planning assumption.

SSC CGL 2026 Tier 2 Exam Date

Candidates who clear the Tier 1 cutoff become eligible for Tier 2. As per the SSC examination calendar, the Tier 2 examination is scheduled for December 2026, subject to any revision by the Commission.

Only candidates who qualify Tier 1 will be issued a Tier 2 admit card. Tier 2 is the merit-deciding stage, so most of your revision effort after Tier 1 should shift to Paper 1 Mathematical Abilities and English.

SSC CGL 2026 Admit Card and City Intimation Slip

SSC releases the city intimation slip roughly a fortnight before the exam, and the region-wise admit card about 7–10 days before your exam date, through the regional SSC websites.

What the admit card contains

  • Candidate's name, roll number and registration number
  • Exam date and shift timing
  • Reporting time
  • Examination centre address
  • Instructions specific to your centre

What to carry on exam day

  • Printed admit card (with photograph pasted, if instructed)
  • Valid original photo identity proof — Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence or Passport
  • Passport-size photographs, if the admit card asks for them

SSC CGL 2026 Selection Process

The SSC CGL recruitment runs in these stages:

  • Tier 1 — Computer Based Test: qualifying stage; shortlists candidates for Tier 2.
  • Tier 2 — Computer Based Test: merit-deciding stage, including a Data Entry Speed Test / Computer Proficiency Test for the relevant posts.
  • Document Verification: shortlisted candidates are called for verification before final appointment.

There is no interview. Final selection depends entirely on your Tier 2 performance and post preferences.

How to Prepare for SSC CGL 2026

With the Tier 1 window already open, the phase for learning new concepts is largely over. What decides your score now is speed, accuracy and exam temperament. Build those with practice, not with fresh theory.

  • Solve previous year question papers regularly — they are the closest thing to the real paper.
  • Attempt at least one full-length mock test every week, and two per week in the final month.
  • Revise formulas, shortcuts and static GK notes daily rather than in long weekend sessions.
  • Analyse every mock: log which questions you got wrong and why — concept gap, silly error, or over-attempt.
  • Practise section-wise tests in 15-minute blocks to match the new sectional timing.
  • Read current affairs consistently, covering the last 8–10 months before your exam date.

A workable weekly plan

  • 2–3 hours of focused practice on Quant and Reasoning each day.
  • 45 minutes daily on English — vocabulary, error spotting, cloze test.
  • 30 minutes daily on General Awareness and current affairs.
  • One full-length mock plus a detailed analysis session every week.
  • One revision day per week for formulas and short notes.

Consistency beats intensity. Six focused days a week will take you further than one twelve-hour Sunday.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC CGL 2026 Tier 1 exam date?

The Tier 1 examination is to be conducted in August–September 2026. The exact date-wise and shift-wise schedule will be released separately on ssc.gov.in, and your individual exam date appears on your admit card.

The notification was released on 21 May 2026 for 12,256 tentative vacancies. The application window ran from 21 May to 22 June 2026, was reopened from 23 to 25 June, and the correction window was held from 1 to 3 July 2026.

When will SSC CGL Tier 2 2026 be conducted?

Tier 2 is scheduled for December 2026 as per the SSC examination calendar, subject to revision by the Commission.

Is SSC CGL Tier 1 conducted online?

Yes. Tier 1 is a Computer Based Test (CBT) conducted in multiple shifts at examination centres across India.

Is there negative marking in SSC CGL?

Yes. In Tier 1, 0.50 marks are deducted for each incorrect answer. Negative marking in Tier 2 varies by paper and is specified in the official notification.

How many questions are asked in SSC CGL Tier 1?

Tier 1 has 100 objective-type questions carrying 200 marks, to be attempted in 60 minutes, with 15 minutes allotted to each of the four sections.

When will the SSC CGL 2026 admit card be released?

Admit cards are released region-wise, generally 7–10 days before the examination, on the SSC regional websites. The city intimation slip comes out earlier, around 10–14 days before the exam.
