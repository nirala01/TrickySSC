October 2026Monthly notes

NavIC gets NVS-03

  • Launch – GSLV-F18 placed NVS-03 in geosynchronous transfer orbit on 30 October 2026 (11:51 pm) from the Second Launch Pad, Sriharikota.
  • Milestone – the 20th GSLV flight; NVS-03 weighs about 2,232 kg.
  • Series – third second-generation NavIC satellite after NVS-01 and NVS-02; it adds an L1 signal to the L5 and S signals.
  • Coverage – NavIC serves India and a belt of about 1,500 km around it.
  • Purpose – to revive NavIC, which had too few working satellites.
NavIC: Navigation with Indian Constellation, earlier called IRNSS; ISRO's regional satellite navigation system designed with seven satellites.

Defence tech trials

  • Kaveri – DRDO's Kaveri dry engine reached 48.5 kN thrust (target 46 kN); it is meant for the Ghatak unmanned combat aircraft.
  • HAPS – ADRDE Agra's high-altitude platform rose to 21 km and stayed near 20 km for over 30 minutes.
  • Parachute – DRDO's AHAP made its first live jump at Nyoma, Ladakh, from 16,000 ft.
  • Quantum link – QNu Labs, BISAG-N and IIT Gandhinagar showed India's first free-space QKD link over 5.56 km.

Space and sun studies

  • Aditya-L1 – its VELC payload gave Doppler velocity images of the solar corona; with ESA's Proba-3 it was used to study coronal mass ejections.
  • Skyroot – signed for two dedicated Vikram launches with US firm Reflect Orbital, which uses mirrors to send sunlight to Earth.
  • IN-SPACe – chose six private space firms for seed grants of up to ₹1 crore each.
  • PUFFINS – a US$20 million NASA mission on interstellar dust, led by Indian-origin scientist Dr Ramya Anche.
  • MMX – Japan's (JAXA) Martian Moons eXploration aims to bring back at least 10 g from Phobos by 2031.

Health and farm science

  • CERVAVAC – Serum Institute's HPV vaccine won WHO prequalification; it covers types 6, 11, 16 and 18.
  • Shamonda virus – WHO confirmed the first known human infection and death, in Lyon, France.
  • Pioxaniliprole – PI Industries' molecule is India's first indigenously discovered insecticide; sold as Pioxazon from rabi 2026-27.
  • New breeds – 16 indigenous breeds notified, including the Kombai dog and Nagal pig; ICAR-NBAGR, Karnal registers breeds.

New species found

  • Electric ray – ZSI named the Arabian black electric ray, Tetronarce ramanamurthyi, from the south-eastern Arabian Sea.
  • Arunachal plants – Ophiorrhiza pemae, named after CM Pema Khandu, and O. shidiensis were found in Changlang district.
  • Centipedes – three new Rhysida species take India's count in the genus to 23.
  • Fungus – Mollisia sokpayensia was described from the Sikkim Himalaya.
  • Snail – Dicharax tilariensis, a tiny land snail, was found in the Tilari area of Kolhapur, Maharashtra.

Telecom, AI and computing

  • IMC 2026 – the 10th India Mobile Congress, 7–10 October at Yashobhoomi, New Delhi; theme 'Scale Without Boundaries'.
  • 6G – the 3rd International Bharat 6G Symposium met on 7–9 October.
  • CAIK – MeitY opened the Centre for AI in Keralam at Thiruvananthapuram under the IndiaAI Mission.
  • NSAM 2.0 – MeitY's second National Strategy for Additive Manufacturing (3D printing).
  • Supercomputers – the National Supercomputing Mission (2015) has set up 40 machines with 68 petaflops.

September 2026Monthly notes

EOS-05 in geosynchronous orbit

  • Launch – ISRO's GSLV-F17 lifted off on 4 September 2026 from the Second Launch Pad, Sriharikota, and put EOS-05 (GISAT-1A) in a sub-GTO.
  • Firsts – the 19th GSLV flight; EOS-05 (2,367 kg) is India's first imaging satellite meant for geosynchronous orbit.
  • Why – it replaces GISAT-1 (EOS-03), lost in August 2021 when GSLV-F10's cryogenic stage failed.
  • Use – near-real-time watch on India – the whole landmass roughly every 30 minutes.
  • ESA link – ISRO extended its framework pact with the European Space Agency by five years, to January 2032.

Space news abroad

  • Vega-C VV30 – launched on 14 September from Kourou; its first dual launch carried ESA's FLEX and Sentinel-3C.
  • FLEX – first mission built to measure plant fluorescence – a sign of photosynthesis – from space; Sentinel-3C belongs to the EU's Copernicus programme.
  • Spectrum – Isar Aerospace's rocket reached orbit on 5 September from Andøya, Norway – the first such success by a private firm from continental Europe.
  • Roman telescope – NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope went up on a Falcon Heavy (30 August) to Sun–Earth L2; its mirror is 2.4 m, like Hubble's.
  • Paris summit – the first International Space Summit met at the Grand Palais (9–10 September); PM Modi spoke by video; ISRO–CNES TRISHNA is due in 2027.

Watching the sky from India

  • NETRA centre – foundation of ISRO's Advanced Radar Research Centre laid on 21 September at Chandrapur near Guwahati, Assam, for tracking space objects.
  • Hanle – 4th star party (7–12 September) at the Indian Astronomical Observatory; Hanle Dark Sky Reserve, India's first, lies in the Changthang Wildlife Sanctuary, Ladakh.
  • NIT Srinagar – first NIT with an astronomical observatory (14-inch telescope), guided by IIA Bengaluru.
  • OptoSAR patent – Bengaluru's GalaxEye got a US patent for syncing optical and radar sensors on one satellite – a first for an Indian startup in satellite imaging.
  • Laser links – Astrogate Labs showed India's first home-grown optical inter-satellite link at Bengaluru Space Expo; flight-ready target 2027.

Oceans, weather and earth

  • ORV Sagar Manthan – research vessel launched on 9 September at GRSE, Kolkata, for NCPOR Goa; it takes over from Sagar Kanya, in service for 43 years.
  • Doppler radar – Doppler radar number 51 for India opened at Sambalpur, Odisha, on 17 September; part of Mission Mausam.
  • Monsoon record – BSIP Lucknow traced about 25,000 years of monsoon history from sediments of Bakhira Lake (Sant Kabir Nagar, UP), an oxbow lake and Ramsar site.
  • IESO 2026 – India's best show at the Earth Science Olympiad in Turin – 9 medals (4 gold).
  • Deep-sea mining – MoES and NIOT held an industry workshop on 8 September; India holds an ISA contract for 75,000 sq km in the Central Indian Ocean Basin.
Deep Ocean Mission: MoES mission approved in 2021; it includes Samudrayaan, whose submersible Matsya-6000 is meant to carry three people to 6,000 m.

Chips, telecom and time

  • Semicon India 2026 – fifth edition, 17–19 September at Yashobhoomi, Dwarka; theme 'Building the Ecosystem – From Silicon to System'.
  • First QFN chip – Suchi Semicon and eInfochips showed India's first commercial QFN chip; lines at Mohali and Surat were opened.
  • Standard time rules – Legal Metrology (Indian Standard Time) Rules, 2026 make IST the legal time; CSIR-NPL is the national custodian.
  • Spam rules – TRAI's third amendment to the TCCCPR (18 September) adds an appeal route; action can follow 3 complaints plus AI flags (5 earlier).
  • Postal and telecom – the 7th Postal Training Centre is coming up in Shivpuri, MP; India's first integrated Telecom Manufacturing Zone is at Gwalior.

Materials, AI and energy

  • Steel slag road – a 1.85 km road in Raigarh, Chhattisgarh, built by CSIR-CRRI and Jindal Steel, set a Guinness record.
  • FQCI – non-profit set up by IISc under the National Quantum Mission to build Indian quantum processors.
  • SARAL AI – ANRF tool built by IIIT Hyderabad; CSIR-NISCPR uses it to turn papers into short videos.
  • Bhashini Sangam – the language-tech outreach was held in Kathmandu, Nepal, on 1 September.
  • Geothermal – ONGC commissioned India's first two geothermal wells in Puga Valley, Ladakh, in July 2026.

August 2026Monthly notes

Indian space tech

  • PowerBank-50 – the first India-made satellite battery to earn a 'Flight Heritage' tag; built by Hyderabad start-up TechMe2Space.
  • Its flight – it flew on Skyroot's Vikram-1 (Mission Aagman), powering a CosmoServ payload on the rocket's orbital module about 450 km up.
  • Everest engine – Astrobase Space Technologies, Bengaluru, built India's first private full-flow staged combustion engine, burning liquid oxygen and liquid methane.
  • Power – Everest is an 80-tonne class (800 kN) engine meant for a reusable medium-lift rocket.
  • Start-up count – about 440 space start-ups are on the Startup India portal; IN-SPACe has given 113 authorisations to 52 private firms.

Space news from abroad

  • Spacewalks – NASA's Indian-origin astronaut Anil Menon made his first spacewalk, a 6.5-hour job, with Jessica Meir.
  • French first – Sophie Adenot of ESA was the first woman from France to walk in space, on 18 August, alongside Menon.
  • SpaceX double – on 15 August two Falcon 9 rockets lifted off 38 minutes apart, from Cape Canaveral (Globalstar satellites) and Vandenberg (USSF-366).
  • China – LandSpace landed its reusable Zhuque-3 booster for the first time; Shenzhou-23 crew harvested rice grown aboard the Tiangong station.
  • Moon base – NASA invited ISRO to its planned base near the lunar south pole under the Artemis Accords.

Health and biotech

  • MA-104 – India's first home-grown vaccine against African Swine Fever in pigs, a live attenuated, cell-line vaccine from ICAR-NIHSAD, Bhopal.
  • RK-251 – a smart 'prodrug' from IASST Guwahati and IIT Guwahati; it stays inactive until reactive oxygen inside cancer cells switches it on and frees the drug NBDHEX.
  • ACSCeND – an AI tool from S.N. Bose National Centre and Ashoka University that sorts cancer stem-like cells into pluripotent, multipotent and unipotent states.
  • Nanobots – INST Mohali made light-driven nanobots of 50–100 nm to attack breast cancer cells.
  • Alzheimer's test – JNCASR Bengaluru built probe TZ-48 and a phone-based kit, ADxFluor, to spot amyloid-beta cheaply.
African Swine Fever: a viral disease of pigs and wild boars with very high death rate; it does not infect humans; first seen in India in Assam and Arunachal Pradesh in 2020.

Computing, chips and AI

  • Param Pragya – an AI-powered supercomputing facility that PM Modi opened on 8 August at the 57th convocation of IIT Delhi.
  • Where and how big – it is housed at the Sonipat campus of IIT Delhi, built under the National Supercomputing Mission, with up to 8 petaflops for HPC and 250 petaflops for AI.
  • Chip packaging – PM Modi opened the first chip packaging unit in South India, by ASIP Technologies, at Tarluvada, Visakhapatnam, with South Korea's APACT as partner.
  • Vulcan – Razorpay launched India's first AI foundation model for payments, to lift success rates and catch fraud.
  • IntelliSeat – a car seat from IIT Madras and Harita that senses driver fatigue and warns in time.
National Supercomputing Mission: launched in 2015 by DST and MeitY, carried out by C-DAC Pune and IISc Bengaluru; it builds the 'Param' series of supercomputers.

Nuclear skills and green industry

  • Anugyan – IIT Hyderabad and Crimson Energy Experts began the country's first nuclear technology programme run jointly by industry and academia, on 3 August.
  • Course – a three-month residential course covering small modular reactors, reactor physics, heat transfer and nuclear safety rules.
  • Goal – to help raise nuclear capacity from about 8.8 GW to 100 GW by 2047.
  • Bioplastic – Balrampur Chini Mills set up India's first large polylactic acid (PLA) plant at Kumbhi, Lakhimpur Kheri (UP), with 80,000 tonnes a year capacity.
  • Damini app – IITM Pune's app gives live lightning alerts in 17 languages.

Animals and biodiversity

  • New breeds – ICAR-NBAGR, Karnal, registered 16 new native breeds, taking the total to 262, of which 258 are indigenous.
  • Examples – cattle such as Umarda, Periyar and Koppal; Kombai dog; Sikkimi yak; Braj donkey; Kashmiri pony; Nagal pig.
  • New seahorse – Hippocampus amandavincentae, a thorny seahorse and India's fifth seahorse species, was described by Kerala University of Fisheries and Ocean Studies.
  • Its name – it honours marine biologist Amanda Vincent, founder of Project Seahorse; the first specimen came from Tuticorin harbour.
  • Mega-Earth – astronomers found GJ 523b, a dense planet about 23.5 times Earth's mass but only 2.5 times its size.

July 2026Monthly notes

Space

  • Vikram-1 – Skyroot Aerospace's rocket lifted off from Sriharikota on 18 July 2026 and placed payloads in a roughly 450-km orbit – the first orbital launch by an Indian private firm.
  • Mission name – the flight was called 'Mission Aagaman' (arrival); it carried Skyroot's SCOPE satellite and payloads of Grahaa Space, Cosmoserve and Germany's DCUBED.
  • Milestone – India is now the third nation, after the US and China, with a private-sector orbital launch.
  • Gaganyaan tests – ISRO ran an integrated main-parachute airdrop test at Sheopur (MP) and the first ground test of SOLVE, a solid-fuel sub-orbital vehicle for parachute trials, at Sriharikota.
  • Rubin Observatory – its LSST survey in Chile keeps imaging the southern sky with the biggest digital camera ever built (3.2 gigapixels); IUCAA Pune and NCRA-TIFR take part.
Skyroot Aerospace: Hyderabad start-up founded by former ISRO engineers; flew India's first private rocket, the sub-orbital Vikram-S, in November 2022 (Mission Prarambh); its rockets are named after Vikram Sarabhai.

Health

  • Qdenga – on 20–21 July CDSCO cleared Takeda's Qdenga, India's first dengue vaccine, for use from age 4 to 60.
  • How it works – a live-attenuated vaccine against all four dengue types, given as two doses three months apart; no prior dengue test is needed.
  • Awiqli – Novo Nordisk launched this once-a-week basal insulin in India for adults with either type of diabetes, cutting yearly shots from 365 to 52.
  • Parrot virus – vets led by Assam Agricultural University found Parrot Bornavirus 4 in over half of the exotic birds they checked in three states.
  • Engineering Biology – Minister Jitendra Singh unveiled a new undergraduate course in engineering biology, a first for India, and a bio-economy roadmap up to 2035.
Dengue: viral fever spread by the Aedes aegypti mosquito; it has four serotypes (DENV-1 to DENV-4); India tackles it under the National Center for Vector Borne Diseases Control.

Quantum, AI and energy

  • Quantum advantage – BITS Pilani (Goa campus) with IBM Quantum reported the first verified quantum advantage from India – a physics simulation done in about 20 seconds using 120 qubits.
  • NIDAR 2.0 – MeitY and Drone Federation India launched this national drone innovation challenge on 13 July under the SwaYaan initiative.
  • Chip centres – NIELIT will set up semiconductor centres of excellence in Ahmedabad (with Infineon) and Guwahati (with Tata Electronics).
  • Flow battery – a 100 MWh vanadium redox flow battery, the first such non-lithium storage plant in India, will come up at the Khavda solar park, Gujarat.
  • AI in schools – Punjab became the first state to make AI a core subject from Class 1 to 12.

New devices and discoveries

  • SALT – CSIR-CIMFR, Dhanbad, built a 'Smart Algal Liquid Tree' (SALT), the first mobile unit of its kind in India, which uses microalgae to take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen.
  • Ammonia sensor – CeNS Bengaluru made a vanadium oxide–vanadium sulphide sensor that works at room temperature and detects ammonia down to about 319 ppb.
  • Landslide warnings – IIT Mandi built a web-based system that issues daily monsoon forecasts for the whole Indian Himalayan region.
  • New fungi – Agharkar Research Institute, Pune, reported five new fungus species that form lichens, all in the genus Allographa, from the Western Ghats.
  • Fast charging – IACS and S.N. Bose Centre scientists made a framework-based anode that charges lithium-ion cells to 80% in about a minute.

Global space news

  • Nuclear satellite – SpaceX launched 'BOHR', billed as the first commercial nuclear-powered (betavoltaic) satellite, on a Falcon 9 rideshare.
  • Ocean satellite – China put its HY-2E ocean-watch satellite in orbit using a Long March-4B, launched from Jiuquan.
  • Reusable rocket – Japan's JAXA tested RV-X, its first reusable rocket.
  • To the ISS – Indian-origin NASA astronaut Anil Menon flew to the space station on Soyuz MS-29 from Baikonur, Kazakhstan.
  • Space economy – India's space economy target is $44 billion by 2033; 105 non-government entities had IN-SPACe approval by 14 July 2026.

June 2026Monthly notes

Space

  • Starship V3 – SpaceX flew the first third-generation Starship on Flight 12 (22 May 2026) from Starbase, Texas; it released dummy Starlink payloads and the ship splashed down in the Indian Ocean.
  • Hardware – the stack is about 124 m tall and uses Raptor 3 engines; the booster was lost and the US FAA ordered a mishap inquiry.
  • MAVEN – NASA closed its Mars orbiter mission after some six months without contact, ending more than a decade of work.
  • SOLAR-1 – NOAA's first dedicated space-weather satellite (earlier called SWFO-L1) was switched on at the Sun–Earth L1 point.
  • Kodaikanal – Indian scientists used about a century of records from the Kodaikanal Solar Observatory to track the 11-year solar cycle.

Health and biotech

  • Breast cancer – Pune's Agharkar Research Institute built biodegradable silica nanoparticles that switch off two cancer genes (MCL-1 and Survivin) using siRNA.
  • ANCHOR – IIT Madras's Sudha Gopalakrishnan Brain Centre released a highly detailed 3D map of the human brainstem from the foetal stage to adulthood.
  • Pluvicto – Novartis launched the first radioligand therapy in India, meant for advanced prostate cancer.
  • Super-vaccine – Cambridge researchers used AI to design a vaccine aimed at the whole coronavirus family.
  • Brain-eating amoeba – Kerala recorded a rise in Naegleria fowleri infections.

Energy, AI and quantum

  • Nuclear hydrogen – the Department of Atomic Energy opened the world's first hydrogen unit based on the copper–chlorine cycle, run on heat from Kalpakkam's Fast Breeder Test Reactor.
  • Varya – an Indian text-to-video AI model by Avataar, trained on IndiaAI Mission computing.
  • Majorana 2 – Microsoft's next topological quantum chip, claimed to be far more stable than Majorana 1.
  • Abhilasha Biofuels – the Technology Development Board backed Pune's Greenjoules to make a drop-in diesel substitute from crop residue.
  • Sarvam AI – the Bengaluru AI start-up reached unicorn status.

Weather and testing tools

  • Skycast – Jitendra Singh opened India's first integrated aviation weather and fog system at IGI Airport, Delhi, under Mission Mausam.
  • RMC Jammu – a new Regional Meteorological Centre in Jammu will serve J&K, Ladakh and Himachal Pradesh; another opened in Lucknow.
  • ArsenSafe – IIT Bhubaneswar developed a hand-held kit to check drinking water for arsenic.
  • GRAPES-3 – India–Japan teams used this muon telescope at Ooty to follow the Sun's magnetic effects on Earth's upper air.
  • BCON – an IITM climate station under the Bharat Climate Observation Network will come up at ARIES, Devasthal (Uttarakhand).

May 2026Monthly notes

Space

  • Mission Drishti – Bengaluru start-up GalaxEye launched the world's first OptoSAR satellite, the largest satellite built by an Indian private firm.
  • OptoSAR – it combines an optical camera with synthetic aperture radar, so it can image at night and through cloud.
  • SMILE – a joint mission of ESA and the Chinese Academy of Sciences, launched on 19 May from Kourou, French Guiana.
  • Rocket – it flew on a Vega-C, the first Vega-C flight run by Italy's Avio rather than Arianespace.
  • Aim – to watch how the solar wind strikes Earth's magnetosphere, using a soft X-ray imager and an ultraviolet aurora camera.

Weather and earth science

  • AI monsoon forecast – Dr Jitendra Singh launched IMD's first AI-based forecast of monsoon advance.
  • Who built it – IMD with IITM Pune and NCMRWF, all bodies of MoES.
  • Reach – more than 3,000 sub-districts spread over 16 states.
  • Uttar Pradesh pilot – rain forecasts at 1-km resolution, up to 10 days ahead.
  • Ancient fires – BSIP Lucknow found signs of wildfires in coal formed from Gondwana forests about 250 million years ago.

Health and biotech

  • Lormalzi – Eli Lilly launched donanemab as Lormalzi on 13 May, India's first therapy that clears amyloid plaque in early Alzheimer's.
  • Use – a 350 mg intravenous dose once a month, approved by CDSCO; one vial costs about Rs 1 lakh.
  • AI in health – IndiaAI (MeitY) and ICMR signed an MoU on responsible use of AI in healthcare.
  • Ebola watch – the Health Ministry reviewed screening at airports after WHO's emergency alert on Ebola.

Technology Day and institutions

  • Innovation rank – on National Technology Day it was noted that India stands 38th in the Global Innovation Index of WIPO.
  • Solar line – Central Electronics Ltd, a PSU under DSIR, opened a 200 MW solar module line on 14 May.
  • First cell – CEL made India's first home-grown solar cell in 1977.
  • IACS at 150 – the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science, Kolkata, founded in 1876 by Mahendralal Sarkar, got a solar-cell lab and the RETINA incubation centre.
  • Chip portal – the India Semiconductor Mission opened an Investors Support Portal.

April 2026Monthly notes

Artemis II goes round the Moon

  • Lift-off – NASA's Artemis II flew on 1 April 2026 atop the SLS rocket from Kennedy Space Center, the first crewed lunar trip since 1972.
  • Crew – commander Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch and Canada's Jeremy Hansen.
  • Farthest ever – on 6 April the crew reached 406,771 km from Earth, beating the Apollo 13 record.
  • Home – their Orion capsule 'Integrity' splashed down in the Pacific off San Diego on 10 April; the flight was a flyby with no landing.
  • Next – Artemis III, set for 2027, will test operations with commercial landers.
Artemis programme: NASA's plan to return humans to the Moon; Artemis I was uncrewed (2022). The Artemis Accords (2020) set rules for peaceful exploration; India signed them in 2023.

Fast breeder reactor goes critical

  • Milestone – the 500 MWe Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor at Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu, reached first criticality on 6 April 2026.
  • Builders – designed by IGCAR and built and run by BHAVINI under the Department of Atomic Energy.
  • Fuel and coolant – it burns uranium–plutonium mixed oxide (MOX) fuel and is cooled by liquid sodium.
  • Breeding – a uranium-238 blanket turns into plutonium-239, so it makes more fuel than it uses.
  • Second stage – India steps into stage two of its nuclear plan and will be the second country after Russia to run a commercial-scale fast breeder.
Three-stage nuclear programme: Homi Bhabha's plan – Stage 1 pressurised heavy water reactors on natural uranium, Stage 2 fast breeders on plutonium, Stage 3 thorium reactors using uranium-233.

Indian space updates

  • Vikram-1 – Telangana CM Revanth Reddy flagged off Skyroot Aerospace's Vikram-1, India's first private orbital rocket, from Hyderabad to Sriharikota.
  • Gaganyaan test – ISRO's IADT-02, its second Integrated Air Drop Test, took place at Sriharikota, dropping a dummy crew module from a Chinook helicopter.
  • Mission Mitra – four astronaut-designates began high-altitude training in Ladakh on 4 April, run by ISRO and the IAF's Institute of Aerospace Medicine.
  • Algae in orbit – DBT-backed Indian microalgae and cyanobacteria were grown on the ISS to study food and oxygen supply for astronauts.
  • Roman telescope – NASA finished assembling the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, meant to study dark energy.

Health and biology

  • Dengue shot – a DCGI expert committee backed Takeda's Qdenga (TAK-003) dengue vaccine for people aged 4–60; Takeda is a Japanese firm.
  • Gene therapy – the US FDA approved Regeneron's gene therapy for deafness caused by faults in the OTOF gene.
  • Sports genomics – Gujarat started India's first Sports Genomics Programme to study the DNA of about 10,000 athletes in five years.
  • CALM-Brain – an open database on brain structure in psychiatric disorders, built at the Rohini Nilekani Centre (NIMHANS and NCBS).
  • TB finding – Bose Institute, Kolkata, showed the sigma factor of TB bacteria stays attached to RNA polymerase while genes are read.

AI, quantum and gadgets

  • Quantum link – India showed a secure quantum communication network over 1,000 km, built by start-up QNu Labs under the National Quantum Mission.
  • Nyay Setu – a voice-first AI legal helper with mascot 'Dishika', built by the Digital India Bhashini Division and unveiled by VP Radhakrishnan.
  • Abhay – CJI Surya Kant launched the 'Abhay' chatbot at the CBI's D.P. Kohli lecture to help people spot digital arrest scams.
  • Held back – Anthropic kept its 'Mythos' AI model from public release over cyber risks, sharing it with select firms under Project Glasswing (8 April).
  • AVATAAR – Bengaluru start-up AquaAirX built India's first amphibious surveillance drone, able to fly and dive.

March 2026Monthly notes

Engines and satellites

  • CE20 – ISRO ground-tested the CE20 cryogenic engine at the ISRO Propulsion Complex, Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu, to raise the payload of LVM3.
  • Club – India is among six nations with cryogenic engines, with the US, Russia, Japan, France and China.
  • NavIC – the atomic clock on IRNSS-1F failed; NavIC covers India and up to 1,500 km around it.
  • Agnite – Chennai start-up Agnikul Cosmos tested a one-metre 3D-printed booster engine with electric-motor pumps, made of Inconel.
  • School payload – Class 11 pupils at Ariyakudi, Sivaganga (TN) sent 'Vikko Sat-1', a 600 g balloon payload, to about 22 km to study fungal spores.

Big science projects

  • LIGO-India – L&T won the DAE contract to build the gravitational-wave observatory at Aundha, Hingoli district, Maharashtra.
  • Partners – DAE and DST with the US National Science Foundation; in-principle approval came in 2016.
  • Small reactors – a 200 MW Bharat Small Modular Reactor and a 55 MW SMR at Tarapur, plus a high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (up to 5 MW) at BARC Vizag.
  • New particle – CERN's Large Hadron Collider found the Xi-cc-plus, a baryon with two charm quarks.
  • LiDAR buoy – NIOT tested India's first home-built floating LiDAR buoy off Muttom, Tamil Nadu, to read offshore winds.
LIGO-India: a laser interferometer to detect gravitational waves; it will join the two US LIGO detectors at Hanford and Livingston.

AI at work

  • Sudarshan – SEBI's in-house AI system watches online platforms for misleading investment tips.
  • FraudPro – C-DOT's AI tool flags forged or repeated ID papers used for SIM fraud; it was a GLOMO finalist at MWC 2026.
  • Saksham – India's first AI-based skill census, started in Mumbai suburban district for youth aged 18–40.
  • AI skills – the I and B Ministry, Google and YouTube will train 15,000 creators through the Indian Institute of Creative Technology.
  • G-Spider – an AI robot by Genrobotics began cleaning the Amayizhanchan canal in Thiruvananthapuram under SBM-Urban 2.0.

Labs, health and EVs

  • Moonshot – IISc Bengaluru and the Pratiksha Trust began a project to build a brain co-processor from neuromorphic chips and AI.
  • EV drive – MeitY launched a 30 kW drive unit using wide-band-gap chips, joining motor and inverter, for EVs in Chennai, built by C-DAC Thiruvananthapuram, IIT Madras and Lucas TVS.
  • Cell therapy – a Rs 60 crore recombinant cell and sensor facility opened at BRIC-RGCB, Thiruvananthapuram.
  • GLP-1 – CDSCO barred adverts for GLP-1 weight-loss drugs over rising misuse.
  • 3D printer – Kerala start-up Spacetime 4D gave ISRO the 'Aakash 300' high-heat printer for rocket parts.

New species found

  • Crab – a rare crab, Vela carli, carrying both male and female traits, was found in Silent Valley National Park, Kerala.
  • Cockroach – ZSI scientists described a new cockroach, Neoloboptera peninsularis, from Pune district.
  • Silent Valley – the park is in Palakkad district, part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, and shelters the lion-tailed macaque.
  • ZSI – the Zoological Survey of India was set up in 1916; its head office is in Kolkata.

February 2026Monthly notes

Missiles and rockets

  • SFDR – DRDO flight-tested Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet technology from ITR Chandipur, Odisha, on 3 Feb 2026.
  • Why it matters – it is vital for air-to-air missiles that must reach far, such as the planned Astra Mk-3.
  • How it works – a ramjet breathes oxygen from the air, so the missile carries less weight and holds its speed longer.
  • VSHORADS – DRDO ran three flight tests of this man-portable air defence missile from Chandipur.
  • Gaganyaan – a load test of the drogue parachute was done on DRDO's rail track rocket sled in Chandigarh.

Home-grown AI models

  • Sarvam AI – the Bengaluru startup unveiled Sarvam-30B and Sarvam-105B on 18 Feb at the AI Impact Summit.
  • Design – both are mixture-of-experts models trained from scratch for Indian languages.
  • Vikram – Sarvam's chatbot, named after Vikram Sarabhai.
  • BharatGen – the IIT Bombay-led group launched Param2 17B MoE, trained on all 22 scheduled languages.
  • Mission – these models were backed under the IndiaAI Mission.
IndiaAI Mission: approved in March 2024 with over Rs 10,000 crore; run by MeitY; offers GPUs, datasets and support for foundation models.

Space

  • Artemis II – NASA moved the crewed Moon mission to March after a hydrogen leak in a wet dress rehearsal of the SLS rocket.
  • Plan – four astronauts will loop around the Moon and return, without landing.
  • Haven-1 – US firm Vast is building it as the first commercial space station; it will fly on a Falcon 9.
  • Hope Island – Andhra Pradesh plans a satellite launch site in Kakinada Bay.
  • China – it unveiled plans for an AI data centre in space.

Health and biotech

  • nOPV2 – WHO prequalified India's novel oral polio vaccine type 2, made by Biological E.
  • Td vaccine – Td (tetanus–diphtheria) vaccines made at the Central Research Institute, Kasauli, were launched by the Health Minister.
  • Remote scan – AIIMS Delhi doctors ran a tele-robotic ultrasound on a person at Maitri station, Antarctica, in a demo by NCPOR.
  • SUJVIKA – an AI-based portal on biotech imports, released by the Department of Biotechnology as it turned 40.
  • Bio-bitumen – CSIR-CRRI and CSIR-IIP made road bitumen from paddy straw through pyrolysis.

Oceans and other tech

  • INCOIS – the Hyderabad ocean centre marked its 27th foundation day.
  • Jelly AIIP – INCOIS's AI platform to forecast jellyfish swarms; the SAMUDRA 2.0 app was also launched.
  • Blockchain – MeitY and C-DAC opened the Blockchain India Challenge for startups on 23 Feb.
  • Drones – an airport drone-testing corridor, the first for commercial use in India, is planned at Rangeilunda, Ganjam, Odisha.
  • Batteries – CeNS Bengaluru designed a fresh cathode material for zinc–iron batteries meant for grid storage.

January 2026Monthly notes

PSLV stumbles again

  • Mission – PSLV-C62 on 12 Jan 2026, ISRO's first launch of the year, from Sriharikota.
  • Cargo – DRDO's EOS-N1 ('Anvesha') hyperspectral spy satellite plus 15 co-passengers; all 16 lost.
  • Cause – roll disturbance near the end of the third stage (PS3).
  • Pattern – second PSLV failure in a row; PSLV-C61 (EOS-09) failed in May 2025, also in PS3.
  • Commercial angle – it was NSIL's ninth dedicated commercial mission.

Private space and Gaganyaan

  • Vikas engine – Godrej Aerospace handed over a human-rated Vikas engine for LVM3's L-110 stage (Gaganyaan).
  • Satellite factory – Ajista Space began India's first integrated private satellite plant at Sanand, Gujarat.
  • Firefly – Pixxel–IN-SPACe pact for India's first private earth-observation constellation.
  • Abroad – NASA's first medical evacuation brought Crew-11 home early; Russia put 52 satellites up on one Soyuz.

Missiles and rockets tested

  • Pralay – two fired back-to-back from Chandipur; 150–500 km quasi-ballistic; built by Research Centre Imarat.
  • Pinaka – first guided extended-range flight test; first export lot left Nagpur for Armenia.
  • MP-ATGM – third-generation 'fire-and-forget' anti-tank missile tested at KK Range, Ahilyanagar.
  • Suryastra – 150–300 km universal rocket launcher, Elbit (Israel) design made with NIBE Ltd, Pune.
  • Scramjet – DRDL ran a long full-scale ground test of an actively cooled scramjet.
Weapons abroad: Russia's Oreshnik – hypersonic IRBM, about Mach 10, up to 5,000 km, multiple warheads (MIRV). China's YJ-20 – hypersonic anti-ship missile. Taiwan's Narwhal – first home-built submarine.

Artificial intelligence firsts

  • Sovereign AI Park – Chennai, under a Tamil Nadu–Sarvam AI pact worth Rs 10,000 crore.
  • AI campus – CCSU Meerut is the first AI-enabled state university.
  • AI clinic – first in a government hospital at GIMS, Greater Noida.
  • OncoMark – reads cancer 'hallmarks'; built by S.N. Bose National Centre and Ashoka University.
  • Google tools – TranslateGemma (55 languages) and DeepMind's AlphaGenome.

Robots and computing

  • DRISHTI – Railways' AI alert for tampered freight-wagon doors.
  • ASC Arjun – humanoid helper for RPF at Visakhapatnam station.
  • SCOPR – first legged robot with an arm, by xTerra Robotics and IIT Kanpur.
  • Param Shakti – 3.1 petaflop machine at IIT Madras.

Labs, life and defence kit

  • Kerala – first state microbe: Bacillus subtilis.
  • New amphibian – Gegenophis valmikii, a blind caecilian from Maharashtra's Western Ghats.
  • Labs – BSL-4 facility planned at Gandhinagar; NIDHI centre at IIM Ahmedabad.
  • Defence EV – Pravaig Veer, first all-electric military vehicle.
  • DRDO – 68th Foundation Day on 1 Jan; Chairman Samir V. Kamat.
