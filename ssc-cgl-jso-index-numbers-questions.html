The three traps that cost the most marks

  • Whose weights? Laspeyres uses base-year quantities q₀; Paasche uses current-year quantities q₁.
  • Fisher’s index and the tests. Fisher satisfies the time and factor reversal tests but not the circular test; that is why it is called “ideal”.
  • Bias. Laspeyres tends to overstate and Paasche to understate price change; Fisher is their geometric mean.

Solved Examples — SSC CGL Statistics Index Numbers

Q1. Kelly's price index \(\frac{\sum p_1 q}{\sum p_0 q}\) × 100, where q are quantities of some typical period (not necessarily the base or current year), is known as:
(a) Fisher's ideal index
(b) Chain base index
(c) Simple aggregative index
(d) Fixed-weight aggregative index
The correct answer is Fixed-weight aggregative index. In Kelly's formula the weights q are fixed quantities, often the average quantities of a few years, kept constant over time. Because the weights do not change with the base or current year, Kelly's index satisfies the circular test. It also avoids collecting fresh quantity data every year, unlike Paasche's index. Answer: (d)
Q2. The prices (in ₹) of four commodities are given below.
Base year \(p_{0}\): 10, 20, 30, 40
Current year \(p_{1}\): 12, 25, 36, 47
The simple aggregative price index for the current year is:
(a) 120.63
(b) 120
(c) 83.33
(d) 20
The correct answer is 120. Simple aggregative index P₀₁ = \(\frac{\sum p_1}{\sum p_0}\) × 100. Σp₁ = 12 + 25 + 36 + 47 = 120 and Σp₀ = 10 + 20 + 30 + 40 = 100. So P₀₁ = (120/100) × 100 = 120. 120.63 is the simple arithmetic mean of price relatives (120, 125, 120, 117.5), a different method. Answer: (b)
Q3. Read the Assertion (A) and Reason (R) carefully.
Assertion (A): Laspeyres' index is used more widely in practice than Paasche's index.
Reason (R): Laspeyres' index needs only base-year quantities, so quantity data need not be collected afresh for every period.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true, but R is false
(d) A is false, but R is true
The correct answer is: Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A. A is true: most official price indices are of the Laspeyres type or a modification of it. R is true: the weights q₀ are fixed once, so only current prices have to be collected each period. Paasche's index needs current-year quantities q₁ every period, which is costly and time-consuming to obtain. Answer: (a)
Q4. निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा सूचकांक समय उत्क्राम्यता परीक्षण (Time reversal test) संतुष्ट करता है?
(a) लैस्पेयर सूचकांक
(b) मार्शल–एजवर्थ सूचकांक
(c) पाशे सूचकांक
(d) मूल्यानुपातों का सरल समांतर माध्य
सही उत्तर मार्शल–एजवर्थ सूचकांक है। मार्शल–एजवर्थ के लिए P₀₁ = Σp₁(q₀+q₁)/Σp₀(q₀+q₁) और P₁₀ = Σp₀(q₁+q₀)/Σp₁(q₁+q₀)। इनका गुणनफल ठीक 1 है, क्योंकि अवधियाँ बदलने पर भी भार (q₀ + q₁) वही रहते हैं। लैस्पेयर के लिए P₁₀ में भार q₁ होते हैं, अतः सामान्यतः P₀₁ × P₁₀ = L × (1/P) ≠ 1; पाशे भी इसी कारण असफल है। उत्तर: (b)

How to Prepare Index Numbers for SSC CGL JSO

  1. Write the four sums first. Σp₀q₀, Σp₁q₀, Σp₀q₁ and Σp₁q₁ give every weighted index in one line.
  2. Make a tests table. Which index passes which test fits on a single card.
  3. Practise base shifting. Divide every index by the new base-year index and multiply by 100.
  4. Remember the best average. The geometric mean is preferred for index numbers.
  5. Read the solutions, not just the answer. Each solution carries six or seven related facts and formulas, which is how 25 questions cover the whole chapter.
